#65

Namba wumv’ urambiwe n’ ivyaha, injiza Yesu mu mutima

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Namba wumv’ urambiwe n’ ivyaha, injiza Yesu mu mutima, Ni wab’ ugomb’ ubugingo busha, Umwinjize mu mutima.

Refrain

Shir’ amazinda none, Wemere Yesu none, Umwugururir ‘ ubu: Umwinjize mu mutima.

Couplet 2

Ni wab’ ugomba kwera derere, injiza Yesu mu mutima. Kukw ari we soko y’ ubugingo: Umwinjize mu mutima.

Refrain

Shir’ amazinda none, Wemere Yesu none, Umwugururir ‘ ubu: Umwinjize mu mutima.

Couplet 3

Namba wiyumvamw’ isanduruma, injiza Yesu mu mutima, Ni w’ ashobora kuyihwamika, Umwinjize mu mutima.

Refrain

Shir’ amazinda none, Wemere Yesu none, Umwugururir ‘ ubu: Umwinjize mu mutima.

Couplet 4

Namb’ ugomba kuj’ iyo mw ijuru, injiza Yesu mu mutima, Namb’ ugomba kuja muri za nzu, Umwinjize mu mutima.

Refrain

Shir’ amazinda none, Wemere Yesu none, Umwugururir ‘ ubu: Umwinjize mu mutima.
