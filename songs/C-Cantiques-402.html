#402

E mwine usekela

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

E mwine usekela ne kusekela, Mu mano atupondo kalonda, Mu dya babi namo kemana, Mu bakapuni monka ka shikata.

Couplet 2

Mu mukanda wa Yehova e mwatokelwa, Mu dyuba ne bufuku e mwatontokanya; E mwine bwa kiti ku mema wadimbwa, Bipangwa byakyo mu wakyomwaka;

Couplet 3

Dibula dyakyo ke kufumba. Tonso to a uba konka kufukila. Tupondo abi ke kwi bi mona, Adi tudi bwa mfumba pepu! Mulufula.

Couplet 4

Mu kidye e kyo atwa kekutalukilamo, Mu kipwilo kya bololoke babikemo! Dishinda dya bololoke wa yukabyo I Yehova. Adi dishinda dya tupondo di kafulungana.
