#162

Tunakimbilia uzuri wako

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Tunakimbilia uzuri wako Bwana Mungu, Sawa ndege aamkiavyo asubuhi, Twapaza sauti yetu tukisema: Mungu wetu tusikilize daima.

Refrain

Moyo hunyenyekea, ô Mungu wangu, Nitakuimbia kwa sauti yangu yote, {Huo ndio uzima wangu.} x2

Couplet 2

Tunakimbilia utukufu wako Baba Mungu, Shabaha yetu tunataka kufanana nawe, Tunakimbilia uso wako Baba Mungu, Utujalie utujaze nguvu zako.

Refrain

Moyo hunyenyekea, ô Mungu wangu, Nitakuimbia kwa sauti yangu yote, {Huo ndio uzima wangu.} x2

Couplet 3

Tunakimibilia mikono yako Bwana Mungu, Tushike mikono tusije tukatereza, Tunakuletea unyonge wetu Bwara Mungu, Utujalie utujaze nguvu zako.

Refrain

Moyo hunyenyekea, ô Mungu wangu, Nitakuimbia kwa sauti yangu yote, {Huo ndio uzima wangu.} x2

Couplet 4

Tunakuletea upumbavu wetu Bwana Mungu, Utujalie utujaze hekima yako, Tunakuletea kiburi chetu Bwana Yesu, Utujaze unyenyekevu wako.

Refrain

Moyo hunyenyekea, ô Mungu wangu, Nitakuimbia kwa sauti yangu yote, {Huo ndio uzima wangu.} x2

Couplet 5

Furaha gani kukuona kati yetu Bwana, Ni kama nyumba iliyo na mwamba, Furaha gani kukuona ndani yetu Bwana, Ni furaha na raha zaidi.

Refrain

Moyo hunyenyekea, ô Mungu wangu, Nitakuimbia kwa sauti yangu yote, {Huo ndio uzima wangu.} x2
