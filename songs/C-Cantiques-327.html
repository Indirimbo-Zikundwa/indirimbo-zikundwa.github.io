#327

Eyi wumi wami

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Eyi wumi wami, mwomu wekalila, Wumusakilili Yehova; Mwejima mwakukiswilaŋayi nkisu, Bayi wakavulamenaku.

Refrain

Enu aŋelu jindi mwasweja ŋovu, Enu mwamukalakelaŋa, Nyidimu Yindi Yekala Kwonsu, kwonsu, Yimusakilili Yehova.

Couplet 2

Wakwanakenaŋa muwaluwilaŋa, Wakuukaŋa nyisoŋu yeyi; Wakukuulaŋa wubuli kukisika, Wumusakilili Yehova.

Refrain

Enu aŋelu jindi mwasweja ŋovu, Enu mwamukalakelaŋa, Nyidimu Yindi Yekala Kwonsu, kwonsu, Yimusakilili Yehova.

Couplet 3

Wakuvwikaŋa chibaŋula chaluwi, Wakutiyilaŋa wushona; Wakutiyishaŋa kuwaha mukanwa, Wumusakilili Yehova.

Refrain

Enu aŋelu jindi mwasweja ŋovu, Enu mwamukalakelaŋa, Nyidimu Yindi Yekala Kwonsu, kwonsu, Yimusakilili Yehova.

Couplet 4

Wakubalumwinaŋa wukwenzi weyi Wili wawiha ne’wambuulu; Watiyilaŋa yisaka yonsu luwi: Wumusakilili Yehova.

Refrain

Enu aŋelu jindi mwasweja ŋovu, Enu mwamukalakelaŋa, Nyidimu Yindi Yekala Kwonsu, kwonsu, Yimusakilili Yehova.
