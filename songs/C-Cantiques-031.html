Kudi tshibi

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Kudi tshibi tshiunzulula Bua tuetu tubuelemu, Yesu udi unzulula, Tubuele ne disanka.

Refrain

Tshibi tshidi tshiunzulula Bua meme mbuelemu lelu, Mbuele meme, Bua meme mbuelemu.

Couplet 2

Tshibi tshidi tshiunzuluka Bua bonso babuelemu, Bua bapele ne babanji, Bonso bapate moyo.

Refrain

Tshibi tshidi tshiunzulula Bua meme mbuelemu lelu, Mbuele meme, Bua meme mbuelemu.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Tshibi tshidi tshiunzulula Bua meme mbuelemu lelu, Mbuele meme, Bua meme mbuelemu.

Couplet 4

Refrain
