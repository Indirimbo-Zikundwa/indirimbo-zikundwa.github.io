#303

Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote, awabarikie wote, Awalinde kwa amani; Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote.

Refrain

Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata mwisho wa safari yenu; Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata tuonane kule Mbingu.

Couplet 2

Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote, awalishe, awatunze Chini ya mabawa yake; Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote.

Refrain

Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata mwisho wa safari yenu; Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata tuonane kule Mbingu.

Couplet 3

Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote, awe ngabo yenu kubwa Katikati ya hatari; Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote.

Refrain

Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata mwisho wa safari yenu; Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata tuonane kule Mbingu.

Couplet 4

Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote, atukutanishe tena, Ama kwetu, ama kwake; Mungu awe nanyi sikuzote.

Refrain

Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata mwisho wa safari yenu; Mungu awe nanyi daima, Hata tuonane kule Mbingu.
