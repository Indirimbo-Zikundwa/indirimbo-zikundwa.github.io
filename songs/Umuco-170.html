#170

Igihe kiriko kirihuta cane

Umuco

Couplet 1

Igihe kiriko kirihuta cane, Mwami tegura Ishengero ryawe; Ubwami bwawe buregereje, none ambika Ishengero ryawe.

Refrain

Eh’Uraje urihuta cane, guh’ impera abakozi bawe, gwisha imvura mu mbuto zawe, ababagara babagare, abimbuzi bimbure, kukw’ igihe gishize.

Couplet 2

Igihe kiriko kirihuta cane, Mwami tunganya Ishengero ryawe; komez’ Urufatiro washizeho, raba dukeneye kwambikwa.

Refrain

Eh’Uraje urihuta cane, guh’ impera abakozi bawe, gwisha imvura mu mbuto zawe, ababagara babagare, abimbuzi bimbure, kukw’ igihe gishize.

Couplet 3

Ubwiza bwawe ni bwuzure, korera mu mpano zawe Mwami; izo washize mu bagukunda, raba dukene kwambikwa.

Refrain

Eh’Uraje urihuta cane, guh’ impera abakozi bawe, gwisha imvura mu mbuto zawe, ababagara babagare, abimbuzi bimbure, kukw’ igihe gishize.

Couplet 4

Watwigishije vyinshi Mwami Yesu, uducisha henshi Bwirukiro; komez’urufatiro rwawe Mwami, raba dukeneye kwambikwa.

Refrain

Eh’Uraje urihuta cane, guh’ impera abakozi bawe, gwisha imvura mu mbuto zawe, ababagara babagare, abimbuzi bimbure, kukw’ igihe gishize.

Couplet 5

Humur’amaso abata-bona, ukangure n’aba-sinziriye komeza urufatiro rwawe Mwami, utwambike Ubwiza bwawe Mana.

Refrain

Eh’Uraje urihuta cane, guh’ impera abakozi bawe, gwisha imvura mu mbuto zawe, ababagara babagare, abimbuzi bimbure, kukw’ igihe gishize.
