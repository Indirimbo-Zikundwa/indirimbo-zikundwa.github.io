#160

Ewe ngenzi rema!

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Ewe ngenzi rema, Wikorere umusaraba wawe, uyikurikire, Ewe ngenzi nyakuri, Niwikorere umusaraba wawe, Uyikurikire.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu yarahamagaye, Umuhamagaro umwe gusa, At’ “Uwugomba kunkurikira, Niyiyanke yikorere, Umusaraba wiwe ankurikire”.

Couplet 2

Ewe ngenzi ya kera, Wikorere umusaraba wawe, ntucobogore, Ewe ngenzi ya kera, Ntutezw’ubwoba wewe, N’umusaraba wikoreye.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu yarahamagaye, Umuhamagaro umwe gusa, At’ “Uwugomba kunkurikira, Niyiyanke yikorere, Umusaraba wiwe ankurikire”.

Couplet 3

Ewe ngenzi rema, Umwe wese n’umusaraba wiwe, ntucobogore, Ewe ngenzi rema, uhwekere, Imbere y Uhoraho Imana.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu yarahamagaye, Umuhamagaro umwe gusa, At’ “Uwugomba kunkurikira, Niyiyanke yikorere, Umusaraba wiwe ankurikire”.

Couplet 4

Imisaraba ntingana, N’intambwe ntizingana, ikorer’umusaraba, wawe, Har’abafise muto, Harabafise munini, Ikorere umusaraba wawe.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu yarahamagaye, Umuhamagaro umwe gusa, At’ “Uwugomba kunkurikira, Niyiyanke yikorere, Umusaraba wiwe ankurikire”.

Couplet 5

Umuhamagaro n’umwe, Ingorane si zimwe, ikorere umusaraba wawe, Ntuwucemwo kubiri, Nturabe uw’abandi nabo, Ikorere umusaraba wawe.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu yarahamagaye, Umuhamagaro umwe gusa, At’ “Uwugomba kunkurikira, Niyiyanke yikorere, Umusaraba wiwe ankurikire”.
