Turi mu ntambara y’iherezo

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Turi mu ntambara y’iherezo, Turi ku rugamba rukomeye, Mbega ni nde azonneshereza? N’Umwami Yesu yanesheje, {Yatsind’urupfu ku musaraba.} x2

Refrain
