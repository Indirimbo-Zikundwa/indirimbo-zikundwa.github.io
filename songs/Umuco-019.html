#19

Ewe ncuti nuze twubake

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ewe ncuti uri ngaha, nuze twubake, ku masezerano y’Imana, Yadusezeraniye.

Refrain

Kaze Yesu, kaze Yesu, nuze twibanire; mbone kwubaka ku Masezerano y’Imana.

Couplet 2

Umwami Yesu yarasize Isezerano ati: sinzobasiga nk’impfuvyi, nzoza kuri mwe vuba.

Refrain

Kaze Yesu, kaze Yesu, nuze twibanire; mbone kwubaka ku Masezerano y’Imana.

Couplet 3

Umwami yasize avuze ati: hasigaye igihe gito, ab’isi ntibabe bakimbona, ariko mwe muzombona.

Refrain

Kaze Yesu, kaze Yesu, nuze twibanire; mbone kwubaka ku Masezerano y’Imana.

Couplet 4

Isezerano ntiryatevye, ry’Umwami Yesu, ryashitse kubari bizeye, ivyasezeranijwe.

Refrain

Kaze Yesu, kaze Yesu, nuze twibanire; mbone kwubaka ku Masezerano y’Imana.

Couplet 5

Nan’ubu Yesu Arakora, n’usab’ uhishurirwe ivyo, akorera mu ba mwizeye, babatijwe na Mpwemu.

Refrain

Kaze Yesu, kaze Yesu, nuze twibanire; mbone kwubaka ku Masezerano y’Imana.
