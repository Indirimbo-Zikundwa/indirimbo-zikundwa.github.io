Mpwemu yera nyuzura

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mpwemu Yera nyuzura undemesh’ umutima, name mpimbaz’ Imana nkiriho: Kuk’uzob’ Umufasha wo kunyobora hose, Name ndongorwa nawe Mukiza.

Refrain

Nyuzura…. we Mpwemu, Mbashe kunesh’ imyampi y’umubi, nzomunesha mpagaze kuri wa Muco wawe, umwe wokutwemeza ikibi.

Couplet 2

Ndakwihereje rwose n’ivyo nzokora vyose, Ungab’ unyiganzire mb’uwawe, Mpwemu mwiza w’Ukuri Uzohor’ Unyigisha, kwizer’ Amasezerano yawe.
