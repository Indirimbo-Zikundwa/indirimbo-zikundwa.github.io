#156B

Ku kilima kule

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Ku kilima kule, kulikuwa muti, Alama ya haya sana; Msalaba wa Yesu Mwokozi wetu, Kule aliwafia watu.

Refrain

Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaikala nao lote. Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaubeba siku zote.

Couplet 2

Musalaba ule, unanionyesha Upendo wa Mungu wangu. Alituma Mutoto wake wa peke Na Yeye alinifia.

Refrain

Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaikala nao lote. Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaubeba siku zote.

Couplet 3

Musalaba ule unanionyesha Uwezo wa Mungu wangu. Kusamehe zambi za watu wowote Watakapo mukubali.

Refrain

Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaikala nao lote. Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaubeba siku zote.

Couplet 4

Nitabeba Musalaba wa Mwokozi, Nitapenda ndugu zangu. Nitasamehe makosa ya adui Nitasifu Mwokozi wangu.

Refrain

Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaikala nao lote. Naungana na Musalaba, Nitaubeba siku zote.
