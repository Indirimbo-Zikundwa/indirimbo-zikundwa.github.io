#206

Ijambo ry’imana

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ijambo ry’Imana n’Ijambo ry’Ukuri, kudahinduka; iy’Imana isezerany’ Isezerano ntirihinduka; n’Ijambo ry’Uhorah’ Imana.

Refrain

./.Naho isi n’ijuru, vyorangira vyose ./.Ijambo ry’Imana, ry’Imana; rizokwamah’ ibihe vyose (Ijambo) n’Ijambo ry’Uhorah’ Imana (Ijambo); niryo ryaremy’ isi n’ijuru (Ijambo); ni ryo ryihinduye Umuntu (Ijambo); niryo Yesu Mukiza wacu, ./.None natwe, twambar’ Ijambo, ni ryo rizoduha kudapfa./.

Couplet 2

Ivy’Imana Yavugish’ umuhanuzi wayo, umuhanuzi Malaki yavugishijwe ivyukuza kw’integuza y’Umwami, igihe c’iherezo mu bushobozi bwa Eliya.

Refrain

./.Naho isi n’ijuru, vyorangira vyose ./.Ijambo ry’Imana, ry’Imana; rizokwamah’ ibihe vyose (Ijambo) n’Ijambo ry’Uhorah’ Imana (Ijambo); niryo ryaremy’ isi n’ijuru (Ijambo); ni ryo ryihinduye Umuntu (Ijambo); niryo Yesu Mukiza wacu, ./.None natwe, twambar’ Ijambo, ni ryo rizoduha kudapfa./.

Couplet 3

Ivy’Imana Yavugishij’ umuhanuzi wayo vyarashitse vyose; non’ Umwami wacu ehe ng’Uyo agira Aze, gutwar’ Ishengero nk’uko vyari vyasezeranywe.

Refrain

./.Naho isi n’ijuru, vyorangira vyose ./.Ijambo ry’Imana, ry’Imana; rizokwamah’ ibihe vyose (Ijambo) n’Ijambo ry’Uhorah’ Imana (Ijambo); niryo ryaremy’ isi n’ijuru (Ijambo); ni ryo ryihinduye Umuntu (Ijambo); niryo Yesu Mukiza wacu, ./.None natwe, twambar’ Ijambo, ni ryo rizoduha kudapfa./.
