#147

Abana b’imana dukwiye kumenya

Izindi

Couplet 1

Abana b’Imana dukwiye kumenya, kwab’ Abamarayika biteze kubona Umugeni wa Yesu, ava mu mibabaro yiteguye kubana n’Umucunguzi.

Refrain

S/ Umwami Yesu avuga ng’ugurur’ urugi, TS/ Rw’umutima wawe maze Yinjire. Agomba kubana n’abafise imitima itagira agasembwa n’amarabaga.

Couplet 2

Tugir’uguhinduka mu mitima yacu, kuko tur’ urusengero Imana Iza Ikabamwo. Ntitwongere kurusivya ngo dushiremwo umwanda, kukw’ Imana yoza Igac’ Iduheba.

Refrain

S/ Umwami Yesu avuga ng’ugurur’ urugi, TS/ Rw’umutima wawe maze Yinjire. Agomba kubana n’abafise imitima itagira agasembwa n’amarabaga.
