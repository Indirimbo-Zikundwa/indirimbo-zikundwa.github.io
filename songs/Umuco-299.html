#299

N’igihe ki ga mukristo

Umuco

Couplet 1

N’igihe ki ga mwene Data, ko mbon’ibihe birakuze, n’igihe ki ga ncuti yanje, ko mbona Umwami ari mukuza ./.Kuko ni igihe cahanuwe, naco caramaze guseruka./.

Refrain

./.Umugeni nawe ariteguye, Umwami Yesu azohagarara avuge n’ijwi rirenga ati: Ni muze yemwe bene Data niho tuzoba dukiz’amarira./.

Couplet 2

N’igihe ki bana b’Imana, ko mbon’ijambo ry’ubuhanuzi, iryo twari twarasezeraniwe, naryo ryaramaze guseruka ./.Nuko rero ntidufyine ku gakiza twaronse k’ubuntu./.

Refrain

./.Umugeni nawe ariteguye, Umwami Yesu azohagarara avuge n’ijwi rirenga ati: Ni muze yemwe bene Data niho tuzoba dukiz’amarira./.

Couplet 3

N’igihe ki yemwe shengero, nico gusab’Imana ukwizera, kandi tukagira n’ingeso nziza, ubwenge hamwe n’ukwirinda ./.Kukw’ihanga ry’Uhoraho, tur’umuco n’umunyu w’iy’isi./.

Refrain

./.Umugeni nawe ariteguye, Umwami Yesu azohagarara avuge n’ijwi rirenga ati: Ni muze yemwe bene Data niho tuzoba dukiz’amarira./.

Couplet 4

Kandi harahiriw’umuntu, yihanganira ibimugerageza, kuko niyarama, azohabwa ca gitsibo, kimwe c’ubugingo ./.Ic’Imana yasezeraniye abazoba barabay’abahizi./.

Refrain

./.Umugeni nawe ariteguye, Umwami Yesu azohagarara avuge n’ijwi rirenga ati: Ni muze yemwe bene Data niho tuzoba dukiz’amarira./.
