Utinyuke kuyoborwa mu Nzira nziza y’Imana

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Utinyuke kuyoborwa mu Nzira nziza y’Imana. Kukw’ ishyikir’ari ryiza, ugend’ucamw’uyoboka.

Couplet 2

Ntutinye gut’ibya kera, byiguhindish’imishitsi. Kuk’Umucyo w’Uwiteka wakir’ah’ugenda hose.

Couplet 3

Urwanir’ibyiza byose n’ubihorw’uzabipfire. Ujye wubahish’Imana ng’uzabe mu cyubahiro.
