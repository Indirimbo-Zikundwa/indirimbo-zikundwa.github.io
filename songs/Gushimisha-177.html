#177

Mwen’Imana Yizerwa

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mwen’Imana Yizerwa, waterwa n’ubwoba? Mbega, ntiwatorewe Umwam’ugukunda? Ng’umeny’ Uwagutoye, uce mu Nzira ye, unyuz’ikirenge mu cye kukw ari We Shobuja.

Couplet 2

N’ubabara, wibwire kw’akubabarira; Nihagir’ugutuka, Umubabarire; Wizigir’ugukunda: Shikama kigabo, ng’uzatsind’ abanzi bawe, uberek’Urukundo.

Couplet 3

N’ubon’ibiruhanya, ukumva k’utsinzwe, we kugir’agahinda, Meny’ibigufasha; Ngibi: Senga, Wizere; Komeza Gusenga; Maz’ureb’ Umusaraba w’Umwana w’Uwiteka.
