Nzohora ndagushima

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Nzohora ndagushima, Ko We Yesu wankunze ntabizi, Nari kure yawe Mwami, Maz’umbony’uc’urampamagara.

Refrain

Ewe Yesu urankunda, Nzosenga mw’Izina ryawe, Undongore muvy’ugomba, Mb’uwawe imisi yose, Name ngendana nawe Mwami, Ibihe vyose mb’uwawe.

Couplet 2

Nzohora ngushimira, Kukw’ ata rindi shikanwa mfise, Tuma Mpwemu az’amfashe, Mpore ngushimir’imisi yose.

Refrain

Ewe Yesu urankunda, Nzosenga mw’Izina ryawe, Undongore muvy’ugomba, Mb’uwawe imisi yose, Name ngendana nawe Mwami, Ibihe vyose mb’uwawe.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Ewe Yesu urankunda, Nzosenga mw’Izina ryawe, Undongore muvy’ugomba, Mb’uwawe imisi yose, Name ngendana nawe Mwami, Ibihe vyose mb’uwawe.
