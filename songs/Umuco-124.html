#124

Uru rusago twambaye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uru rusago twambaye rwo mw’isi, turi hafi kurwamburwa; dufise Inyubakwa iri ku Mana, niho tuzoruhukira, Haleluya.

Refrain

Isi igiye gushira s’ababi s’abeza bagiye kwimburwa bose, twibaririre imifuko idashira ./.Ubutunzi budashira, buri mw’Ijuru, ahatagera n’inyenzi canke ibisuma./.

Couplet 2

Abari mur’urusago bazoru kurwamwo, nirwo uyu mubiri ubora; twiyubakirire Inzu y’uburyo bwa Mpwemu, aho tuzimukira, Haleluya.

Refrain

Isi igiye gushira s’ababi s’abeza bagiye kwimburwa bose, twibaririre imifuko idashira ./.Ubutunzi budashira, buri mw’Ijuru, ahatagera n’inyenzi canke ibisuma./.

Couplet 3

Iy’inzu yo kw’isi igiye gusamburwa, ku bumva ni bumve vuba; ariko abagize umwete kuri kazoza, bafise Inyubakwa kwa Data Imana.

Refrain

Isi igiye gushira s’ababi s’abeza bagiye kwimburwa bose, twibaririre imifuko idashira ./.Ubutunzi budashira, buri mw’Ijuru, ahatagera n’inyenzi canke ibisuma./.

Couplet 4

Urukundo, umwete nivyo dukorera m’Umwami, biduza iyo nzu yacu mw’Ijuru; ur’umunebwe mu vy’Ubwami iyo nzu ntiduga ubaka inzu, yawe mwene Data.

Refrain

Isi igiye gushira s’ababi s’abeza bagiye kwimburwa bose, twibaririre imifuko idashira ./.Ubutunzi budashira, buri mw’Ijuru, ahatagera n’inyenzi canke ibisuma./.

Couplet 5

Ubaka inzu yawe n’umwete n’ukwitanga, ehe bugira bwire ncuti; niwamburwa urusago rwawe rwo mw’isi ntuz’usange uri gusa, nawe ncuti.

Refrain

Couplet 6
