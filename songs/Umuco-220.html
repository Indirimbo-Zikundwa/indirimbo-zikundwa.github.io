#220

Ijambo ry’imana twoshobora

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ijambo ry’Imana twoshobora kuryizera gute, tudashobora guhishurirw’ ab’Imana yarirungikanye? ./.Kuko kuva na kera hose Imana yakoresha abana bayo bambay’umubiri./.

Refrain

./.Mana tugirir’ Imbabazi./. kugira ngo ntidushobore ./.Guhush’inzira./.

Couplet 2

Mu gihe ca Nowa wahagurukije umusavyi wawe Nowa kuburira ab’igihe ciwe ivyagira bishike ./.Yamara kubwo kubon’umuntu ntibashoboye gukira ivyago vyagira bishike dushoboze twoye kubon’umuntu./.

Refrain

./.Mana tugirir’ Imbabazi./. kugira ngo ntidushobore ./.Guhush’inzira./.

Couplet 3

Ni nako vyagenze mu bihe vyose vyagiye bibaho ku bahamagawe bose bagiye barongorwa nivy’Imana ishima ./.N’isi ubwayo idashobora kubatahura, uduhe Mana kwama turi maso./.

Refrain

./.Mana tugirir’ Imbabazi./. kugira ngo ntidushobore ./.Guhush’inzira./.

Couplet 4

No mur’iki gihe cacu har’ijambo ritegura Umugeni yamara hakubona ijambo ridushoboza gushikira ugutunganywa kwose ./.Tukabon’ibidakwiye kubonwa tugirire ubuntu Mana./.

Refrain

./.Mana tugirir’ Imbabazi./. kugira ngo ntidushobore ./.Guhush’inzira./.

Couplet 5

Shengero dusangiye ukwizera ijambo ry’Imana ntirikina rifise ububasha bwo gukiza ku baryizera ./.Kandi rifise ubushobozi bwo kwica ku bataryizera dushoboze Mana kwizera kugira ngo tubeho./.

Refrain

./.Mana tugirir’ Imbabazi./. kugira ngo ntidushobore ./.Guhush’inzira./.
