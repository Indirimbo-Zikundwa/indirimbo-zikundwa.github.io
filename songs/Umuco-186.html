#186

Mwami Yesu nkunda cane

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mwami Yesu nkunda cane, ijwi ryawe riranezera, iyo ngenda mur’ iy’isi, mpumurizwa nuk’ Unkunda Yesu ./.Ndakwihaye jewe nd’uwawe, Unyigishe ngendane nawe./.

Couplet 2

Mwami Yesu wanyiguze, mpura n’ibingora mu Nzira, iyo ngenda mur’ iy’isi, mpumurizwa n’ijwi ryawe Yesu ./.Rinyemeza y’uko nd’uwawe, bikamaramaza Satani./.

Couplet 3

Mwami Yesu uko ndi kose, jewe nd’uwawe Bwirukiro, mu mibabaro nd’uwawe, mu munezero nd’uwawe Yesu ./.Mu magorwa jewe nd’uwawe, mu ntambara jewe nd’uwawe./.

Couplet 4

Mwami Yesu jewe nyakira, kuko wankunze ubwa mbere, aho wampamagara Mwami, ukanyigisha kukwubaha Yesu ./.Mu ntege nke none nd’uwawe, mu nkomezi jewe nd’uwawe./.

Couplet 5

Ubu singikekeranya, wanyigishij’ Ijambo ryawe, kuko ndiho kubwawe Mwami, no ku rupfu nzob’ uwawe Yesu ./.Uruhure umutima wanje, nce mur’ iy’isi ngororoka./.
