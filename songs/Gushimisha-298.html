Mbe nk’umurase w’izuba

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mbe nk’umurase w’izuba aho ngera hose, mbonesh’ Umucyo wa Yesu mu mwijima w’isi.

Refrain

Yesu, Mucyo w’isi, mbe nk’umurase kubwawe, ngez’ Umucyo wawe mu mwijima w’iyi si!

Couplet 2

Mbe rik’umurase w’izuba weyur’ umwijima wo mu mitima y’abantu bataz’ Umukiza.

Refrain

Yesu, Mucyo w’isi, mbe nk’umurase kubwawe, ngez’ Umucyo wawe mu mwijima w’iyi si!

Couplet 3

Mbe nk’umurase w’izuba, mfash’ab’i muhira, nkwiz’ Amahoro ya Yesu mu bo duturanye.

Refrain

Yesu, Mucyo w’isi, mbe nk’umurase kubwawe, ngez’ Umucyo wawe mu mwijima w’iyi si!

Couplet 4

Mbe nk’umurase w’izuba: Mbisezeranye ntyo. Nerekan’ ingeso nziza; Ziva kuri Yesu.

Refrain

Yesu, Mucyo w’isi, mbe nk’umurase kubwawe, ngez’ Umucyo wawe mu mwijima w’iyi si!
