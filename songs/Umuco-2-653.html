Mana suka mpwemu

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Mana suka Mpwemu mw’Ishengero, Aduh’ Ubwenge buva kuri Wewe, Duhumure tubon’ ivy’Ukorera mw’Ihanga ryawe Wiguz’ Amaraso.

Refrain

Abagiriw’Ubuntu Barahiriwe, Bazotunganywa bezwe bavugutirwe, Bazohanagurw’ amosozi yabo, Yesu ni We Azob’Itara ryabo.

Couplet 2

Kwizera, ingeso nziza, ubwenge, ukwirinda, kwihangana tubikore, Dukund’ Imana hamwe na bene Data, Ni zo ntambuko z’Abana b’Imana.

Refrain

Abagiriw’Ubuntu Barahiriwe, Bazotunganywa bezwe bavugutirwe, Bazohanagurw’ amosozi yabo, Yesu ni We Azob’Itara ryabo.

Couplet 3

Abagiz’ubwenge bazokayangana, Nk’inyenyeri ibihe bidashira, N’abashikamye mu Kugororoka, Bazohabwa Igitsibo c’Ubugingo.

Refrain

Abagiriw’Ubuntu Barahiriwe, Bazotunganywa bezwe bavugutirwe, Bazohanagurw’ amosozi yabo, Yesu ni We Azob’Itara ryabo.

Couplet 4

Hahirw’abapfa bapfira mu Mwami, Ngo baruhuke ubutame bwabo, Abagiz’ Ubwenge bazokanguka, Bahabwe Ubugingo budashira.

Refrain

Abagiriw’Ubuntu Barahiriwe, Bazotunganywa bezwe bavugutirwe, Bazohanagurw’ amosozi yabo, Yesu ni We Azob’Itara ryabo.

Couplet 5

Refrain
