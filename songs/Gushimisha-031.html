#31

Mutabaz’Ubuntu bwawe

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mutabaz’Ubuntu bwawe ni jyew’ubukwiriye. Burakiz’ abandi cyane; None, nanjye bunzemo. Bunzemo, bunzemo. None, nanjye bunzemo.

Couplet 2

Data Mwiza, Mana Yera, mpora ngucumuraho. Mfit’ibyaha byinshi cyane, Umbabarir’ Umpire. Bimveho! Bimveho! Umbabarir’ Umpire.

Couplet 3

Ntumpiteho, Yesu wanjye; Nkwisunge, nkwiragize! Mfit’inzara, mfit’inyota, Ndakwinginz’ Undengere. Ndengera, ndengera ndakwinginz’ Undengere.

Couplet 4

Mwuka Wer’ump’ umutima wizey’ Umucunguzi; Yes’Unyuhagir’ ibyaha, mu Maraso Wavuye. Mboneza, mboneza mu Maraso Wavuye.

Couplet 5

Mwuka Wera, jye nd’impumyi; Ndakwinginz’ Umpumure. Ujy’Uhamy’ iby’Umukiza; Non’Umumpishurire. Nd’impumyi, nd’impumyi! Non’umumpishurire.

Couplet 6

Urukundo rwawe, Yesu, ruva ku Musaraba, Urunyerek’ ub’Unkize; Noneh’ Unyiharire. Nkiz’ubu! nkiz’ubu! noneh’Unyiharire!
