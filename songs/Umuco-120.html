#120

Emwe bakristo bene data

Umuco

Couplet 1

Emwe ba kristo bene Data, ni mwumve iyo mpanuro, iyo mpanuro nayo s’iy’indi; ja wisunga Umwami wawe, wumv’ijwi ry’Ijambo ryiwe.

Refrain

Abo mu rugo rwa Data, bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe; bama bamwubaha rwose, n’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 2

Imana igabur’ijambo ryayo, toranyamw’umugabane wawe, mw’Ishengero havugwa menshi; shaka mw’ijwi ry’Uhoraho, ryame ritsindaje muri wewe.

Refrain

Abo mu rugo rwa Data, bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe; bama bamwubaha rwose, n’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 3

Ca bugufi mugenzi wanje, shak’ic’Imana ikuronderako, ugendere mu rukundo rwayo; wame umwisunga muri vyose, nih’uzokwubaka ku rutare.

Refrain

Abo mu rugo rwa Data, bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe; bama bamwubaha rwose, n’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 4

N’utumva ncuti ijwi ry’Imana, ntumeny’ukugomba kwayo, ntuzotera intambwe na rimwe ntuzomenya inzir’ucamwo naho uhagaze ah’ariho.

Refrain

Abo mu rugo rwa Data, bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe; bama bamwubaha rwose, n’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 5

Ku rugutura amatwi ya Mpwemu uhor’umwubah’iteka, azoba mu bugingo bwawe; nawe nyene uzoba muri we, mwamane na ntaryo, haleluya.

Refrain

Abo mu rugo rwa Data, bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe; bama bamwubaha rwose, n’umunezero mwinshi.
