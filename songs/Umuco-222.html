Ndaririmba intsinzi

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ndaririmba Intsinzi Intsinzi y’Uhoraho, Intsinzi ndirimba s’iyo nabwiwe n’abantu n’iyo, n’iyo niboneye.

Refrain

Nari narumvishije ivyaw’ ugutwi ariko noneh’ ijisho (ryanje) rirakwiboneye ./.(Niyo, niyo niboneye)./.

Couplet 2

Ndaririmb’Intsinzi Intsinzi y’Umwami yabonekey’i Gologota Umwami ati : vyose birarangiye niyo, niyo niboneye.

Refrain

Nari narumvishije ivyaw’ ugutwi ariko noneh’ ijisho (ryanje) rirakwiboneye ./.(Niyo, niyo niboneye)./.

Refrain

Refrain

Nari narumvishije ivyaw’ ugutwi ariko noneh’ ijisho (ryanje) rirakwiboneye ./.(Niyo, niyo niboneye)./.
