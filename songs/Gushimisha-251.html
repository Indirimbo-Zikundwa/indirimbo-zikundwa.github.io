#251

Munsi y’Umusaraba

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Munsi y’Umusaraba w’Umukiza Yesu, ni ho nifuza rwose guhora mpagaze n’Ibuye ry’Igitare rimber’ igicucu, rinkiz’ impagarara mu Rugendo rwanjye.

Couplet 2

Uwo Musaraba we wabay’ ihuriro ry’Ukuri n’Urukundo by’Iman’ Ihoraho unyibutsa za nzozi Yakobo yarose, umbereye nk’urwego rugenza mw’Ijuru.

Couplet 3

Hirya y’Umusaraba mbonay’ umworera, n’umunwa w’i kuzimu wakamw’ umuriro, nyamar’ imbere yawo har’Umusaraba, uteze nk’amaboko ng’unkize, ntagwayo.

Couplet 4

Kur’uwo Musaraba ndasa n’ushobora, kureba Yesu Abambwe, ari jy’Apfiriye ndatangaye Mukiza ntiwar’ ukwiriye kungirir’iryo bambe jye waguhemuye!

Couplet 5

Munsi y’Umusaraba nzagumay’iteka, naho nabur’ ibyanjye nywubonamw’ inyungu, nta soni mfit’uretse iz’ibyaha byanjye nzajya nirata gusa uwo Musaraba!
