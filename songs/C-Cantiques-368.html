#368

Bayi mutiyanŋa womaku

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Bayi mutiyaŋa woma Munyichima yenuku; Chochu mwamwitiya Nzambi Itiyenu ninami. Mwenamuna mwa Tata Mudi mwakushakama, Ami kwokunukuya, Nakuyiloŋeshela.

Refrain

Mwalala, nukwinza, Chalala, chalala, Ami Yesu nukwinza. Amena, inzaŋa chi.

Couplet 2

Neyi nukuya kwenoku Nakuloŋeshelaku Himwakela ampwampuku, Hinakayilekaku. Kwokunakashakama, Nenu mwakekalaku, Nakayinjila cheŋi, Namba kayitambula.

Refrain

Mwalala, nukwinza, Chalala, chalala, Ami Yesu nukwinza. Amena, inzaŋa chi.

Couplet 3

Yami njila, yami wumi, Yami Yesu walala: Chakash’ami kwosi muntu Wukuya kudi Tata, Neyi mwaŋilukili Mwadi kwiluka Tata; Yowu namonami Yesu, Namoni Tata nawa.

Refrain

Mwalala, nukwinza, Chalala, chalala, Ami Yesu nukwinza. Amena, inzaŋa chi.
