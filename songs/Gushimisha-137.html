#137

Wowe Mana ndaguhimbaza

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Wowe, Mana, ndaguhimbaza kukw’ari nta yindi Man’ibaho; Nguhaye n’umutima wanjye Ump’imbaraga z’Umwuka wawe, mbashe gukor’ ibikunezeza, mw’Izina ry’Umwami wacu Yesu.

Couplet 2

Nuzuzw’Umwuka wawe Wera, umutima wanjy’Uwutegeke; Ujy’Unyobor’ Inzira yawe, ngo nyinyuremo ntajya nzimira; Kandi, ni nanirw’ ujy’untabara, Ugez’ubw’ Uzansohoz’i Wawe.
