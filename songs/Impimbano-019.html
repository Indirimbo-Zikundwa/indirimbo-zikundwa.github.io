#19

Ndaririmb’ urukundo rw’umukiza

Impimbano· Fr René

Couplet 1

Ndaririmb’ Urukundo rw’Umukiza, ndatur’ Ineza agira mu Mbabazi ze nyinshi zidashira, njye zampesheje Agakiza.

Refrain

Ndaririmba, Ndamushima Ndamamaz’ Imbaraga ze. Habe mw’isi no Mw’ijuru nta zina nk’iry’ Umukiza.

Couplet 2

Nari mu byaha byinshi nta Gakiza, isi nibyayo bimboshye nta Mahoro habe n’ Ibyiringiro. Ambabariye ankuramo.

Refrain

Ndaririmba, Ndamushima Ndamamaz’ Imbaraga ze. Habe mw’isi no Mw’ijuru nta zina nk’iry’ Umukiza.

Couplet 3

Nkukw’ inyoni iririmba mu gitondo, nzajya ndirimb’ Umukiza, Mu Bwiza Bwe bw’Iteka budashira. Nzajya ndirimb’Ubwiza Bwe.

Refrain

Ndaririmba, Ndamushima Ndamamaz’ Imbaraga ze. Habe mw’isi no Mw’ijuru nta zina nk’iry’ Umukiza.

Couplet 4

Njye namubonye ndushye ndemerewe, maze kuvubwo Angir’ uwe. Sinkiganyira hoya sinkiheba, kuko ndi Mubiganza bye.

Refrain

Ndaririmba, Ndamushima Ndamamaz’ Imbaraga ze. Habe mw’isi no Mw’ijuru nta zina nk’iry’ Umukiza.

Couplet 5

Yanyogororeye mw’ijwi rye rituje ati uruwanjye witinya. Naho imyambi y’Umwanzi yakurasa, Njye nd’Ingabo igukingira.

Refrain

Ndaririmba, Ndamushima Ndamamaz’ Imbaraga ze. Habe mw’isi no Mw’ijuru nta zina nk’iry’ Umukiza.
