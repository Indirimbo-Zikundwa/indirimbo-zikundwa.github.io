#39

Mukiza ndagomba Gukizwa rwose

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mukiza, ndagomba Gukizwa rwose, ndakwinginz’Uze mu mutima wanje, Uhindire hanz’ ibikwanka vyose, Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.

Refrain

{Nere derere, nere rwose,} x2 {Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.} x2

Couplet 2

Mukiz’ undabish’ Urukundo rwawe, Umfashe kuguheber’ umutima, ndakwihaye rwose, ndakwihebeye, Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.

Refrain

{Nere derere, nere rwose,} x2 {Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.} x2

Couplet 3

Mukiza, ni co ndorereye gusa, ndapfukamy’ imbere y’inyundo zawe, Amaraso yawe ni yo nizeye: Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.

Refrain

{Nere derere, nere rwose,} x2 {Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.} x2

Couplet 4

Mukiza, ndi hano mu Nyonga zawe, Yesu, ng’Undememw’ umutima musha, nta ho Wohind’ abaza kuri Wewe: Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.

Refrain

{Nere derere, nere rwose,} x2 {Unyuhagire mbone kwera rwose.} x2
