Yesu Mwungeri wanjye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yesu, Mwungeri wanjye, nd’agatam’ Undagire. Byose ndabitinyuka: Nta wuzakunyambura!

Couplet 2

Mwami, Waramfiriye kera ku Musaraba: Mbonye n’ibikomere mu biganz’Unteruje.

Couplet 3

None, njye ngusingiza, muri byose nkumvire! Urukund’ Umfitiye, ni rwo runyishimisha.

Couplet 4

Yesu, Mwungeri Mwiza, non’Unyiyegereze! Mmenyereza noneho, nzinukwe kuzimira!

Couplet 5

Mwami Yes’Unahorere, nshe mu Nzir’ifunganye; Nta cyo nanz’Untegeke: Wow’uz’ ibinkwiriye!

Couplet 6

Ngez’ah’Uri mw’Ijuru, ibijya binyobera, ni ho nzabimenyera, nshim’uko Wandagiye!
