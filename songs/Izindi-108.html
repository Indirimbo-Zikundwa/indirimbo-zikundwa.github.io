#108

Mbese wab’uz’ umurwa?

Izindi

Couplet 1

Mbese wab’uz’ Umurwa? N’ U……….murwa wera; Urarabagirana, nta joro rihaba.

Refrain

Abagenzi bajyayo babazwa mwiyisi, Bifuza gakondo yindi, N’ururembo rwiza.

Couplet 2

Waba uz’ibyuwo Murwa? Hahiriw’ Abaho; Byose n’Umunezero, N’amahoro masa.

Refrain

Ubwiza bw’Uhoraho niwo Mucy’ umurika, n’Itabaza ryaho ryaka, N’Umwana w’intama.

Couplet 3

Hariyo n’amabuye y’Igiciro cyinshi, N’uruzi rw’Ubugingo, na cyagiti cyiza.

Refrain

Nta mitwar’izabayo kubazaba bariyo, Umurwa w’Ubwiza bwinshi, Ni Gakondo yacu.

Couplet 4

Mbeg’Amahoro menshi, Tuzagiri….rayo, Umugeni yifuza, kuzabon’ Umukwe!

Refrain

Tuza…..hora….na nawe Nkitorero Mugeni; Tumuririmbire Ishimwe, Ibihe by’Iteka!
