Je negereye siyoni

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Je negereye Siyoni, n’Umugwa w’Umwami Yesu, n’Ishengero ry’Ab’Imfura, Abanditswe mw’Ijuru, hamwe n’Abagororotsi batunganijwe rwose, Ab’Umwami wacu Yesu Yezeshej’ Amaraso.

Refrain

Je negereye Siyoni, n’Umugwa w’Umwami Yesu, n’Ishengero ry’Ab’Imfura, Abanditswe mw’Ijuru.

Couplet 2

Negerey’Umwami Yesu, Ahuz’ abantu n’Imana. Negerejwe n’Amaraso, y’Isezerano Risha, Yacucagiwe ku bwanje, ngo mbone gutunganywa. Ndashimir’ Imana yanje, Yo Yanyiyegereje.

Refrain

Je negereye Siyoni, n’Umugwa w’Umwami Yesu, n’Ishengero ry’Ab’Imfura, Abanditswe mw’Ijuru.

Couplet 3

Negerey’Imana Yera, nzi ko nd’umwana wayo, Yanyogeshej’ Amaraso. Ubu ndera derere, Kwegera Umwami wanje, n’Ubuntu butangaje. Anzamura muri Mpwemu, maz’Akampezagira.

Refrain

Je negereye Siyoni, n’Umugwa w’Umwami Yesu, n’Ishengero ry’Ab’Imfura, Abanditswe mw’Ijuru.

Couplet 4

Ni nashik’iyo mw’Ijuru, nzoririmba Haleluya, nzoshimir’ Imana yanje, Ivyiza yangiriye. Nzotahur’ ivy’Urukundo, n’Ubuntu Yangiriye, nzohimbaz’ Izina ryiwe, Ibihe bidashira.

Refrain

Je negereye Siyoni, n’Umugwa w’Umwami Yesu, n’Ishengero ry’Ab’Imfura, Abanditswe mw’Ijuru.
