#30

Ai Mukunzi wanjye We

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ai Mukunzi wanjye We, Umpe kukwihishamo. iyo ntewe n’iby’isi n’ingabo za Satani, Mutabaz’ Umpish’ubwo, ba banzi be kumfata, Umber’ Ubuhungiro, Mu rupf’Uzanyakire.

Couplet 2

Nta bundi buhungiro, Mwam’ Unyirokorere; We kunsiga, Krisito, Komez’ uiy’undengera. Ni Wow’Ump’ imbaraga, Nta wundi mfit’Umfasha; Mbundikiz’ Amababa, Uhor’ Undind’ibibi.

Couplet 3

Ur’Umunyambabazi zitarangira, Yesu; Usindagiz’ urushye, n’urway’ uramukiza, Urera bihebuje, nta bwo ngutunganiye; Ndi mubi bikabije, Wow’Urakiranuka.

Couplet 4

Yesu ni Wowe Soko y’amazi y’Ubugingo, reka nkunyweho cyane, Undudubiz’ imbere. Nuk’umutima wanjye Ujye wuzur’iteka, Amazi y’iyo Soko, abe mensh’adakama.
