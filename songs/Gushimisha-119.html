#119

Yesu murutisha byose

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yesu murutisha byose: Nta kindi nkifuza. Iminsi yos’Aramfasha, ngo nkomere, ne kugwa. Iyo ndushye njya kuti We; Nta wund’ unduhura nkawe We. Yanyujuje... ibyishinno ..! N’uwanjye!

Couplet 2

Yesu murutisha byose: Amfasha naniwe. Itek’Amp’ Imigisha, mbur’uko nabivuga. Atang’izuba n’imvura ngo nez’imyak’ inkwiriye Angabira ... byose nkena ..! N’uwanjye!

Couplet 3

Yesu murutisha byose: Nzajya mukorera kukw ar’uwo kwiringirwa; Sinzamuhemukira. Nkurikira Yesu turi kumwe, nta cyo nzaba. Arankunda ..! Arandinda. N’uwanjye!

Couplet 4

Yesu murutisha byose: N’Inshuti yanjye pe. Nta wundi nabona nka We; Nzahora mwizigira. Yampay’ impan’ ihebuje, ni yo Bugingo bw’iteka. Aranyuze ..! Musingize. N’uwanjye!
