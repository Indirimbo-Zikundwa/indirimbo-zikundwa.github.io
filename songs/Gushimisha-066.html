#66

Ngwino ngukize mwangizi

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ngwino, ngukize, mwangizi: Nabony’ishavu ryawe ryose; Nyamar’ushatse ko ngukiza, ukwiriye kunyumvira. Dor’uko mpagaze ku rugi; Ndi Shobuj’ Umucunguzi. Nkuzaniy’ imigisha myinshi: Mbese, ntiwankingurira?

Couplet 2

Kera, narakuvukiye, ngucunguz’ Ubugingo bwanjye. Mbese, k’unangiw’ umutima, ubuz’uko wanshyiramo? Namaz’iminsi myinshi cyane ku rugi rwawe nkomanga. Mugenzi, ko bugiye kwira, mbese, ntiwankingurira?

Couplet 3

Rya joro ridacy’iteka ni ryir’uzantabaza s’ute? Ntutind’unyitab’ uyu munsi, ngukikire, nkurengere! Igihe cyawe kirakuze; Ntuvug’uti: Nzaz’ubundi. N’uza ku rugi rwanjy’utinze, uzakomangir’ubusa,

Couplet 4

Nubwo wumv’utanyitaho, Jye ndacyakwinginga ngukunze, nkurindire kugeza ryari? Ngwino, ndakwifuz’i wanjye; Dor’uko mpagaze ku rugi: Ndi Shobuj’ukwitayeho. Nkuzaniy’imigisha myinshi; Mbese, ntiwankingurira?
