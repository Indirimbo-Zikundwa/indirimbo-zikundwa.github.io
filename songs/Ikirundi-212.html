#212

Mw’Ijuru hariho; Isi nziza

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mw’Ijuru hariho; Isi nziza, Abera ba Yesu; Ni yo bari. Baranezererwa Guhora bamushima, Kandi batazira; Umukiza.

Couplet 2

Yemwe banyavyaha, Munyaruke Kuj’ aho Yes’ ari, Ntimutebe: Tuzoronk’ivyiza Ni twabana mw’ijuru Nawe Yesu wacu,Tutanduye.

Couplet 3

Iman’ igirira Ababayo; Neza bishimitse, N’ abahirwe! Dushishikarire, Kuj’ ah’ ubgami bgawe Bwiza budashira, Buri, Yesu.
