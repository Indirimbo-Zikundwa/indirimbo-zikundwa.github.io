#4

Dushime can’ Izina ryawe Yesu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Dushime can’ Izina ryawe Yesu Mukiza wacu: Turakwimitse ng’ utuganze, Mukama wacu Yesu, Rugira vyose.

Couplet 2

Yemwe mwishwe bamubahora, Munezerwe mw’Ijuru: Muhimbaz’ uwabapfiriye: Ni we Mukama wacu Rugira vyose.

Couplet 3

Mwa bana ba Adamu mwese, Bacunguwe na Yesu, Muhimbaze mwene Dawidi, Umwami wacu Yesu Rugira vyose.

Couplet 4

Abakuwekw ivyaha mwese, Mukaronk’ ubgo buntu Enda tumwunamire twese, Ni we Mukama wacu Rugira vyose.

Couplet 5

Namwe banyamahanga mwese, Imik’ uwo Mukiza, Ni we Mucunguzi w’abantu, Umwami wacu Yesu Rugira vyose.

Couplet 6

Twifuza cane kuzoshika Mw’ijur’ aho Yes’ ari, Ngo tumushim’ ibihe vyose, Ni we Mukama wacu Rugira vyose.
