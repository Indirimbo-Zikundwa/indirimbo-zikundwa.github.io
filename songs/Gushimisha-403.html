#403

Nyenyeri yo mu museke

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Nyenyeri yo mu museke, Ubonets’uri kw’ijuru, Ur’Umucy’ utuvira. Tumurikir’ imitima, Utweyurir’ umwijima ukunda kuba mw’isi.

Refrain

Mwami, Mwana—w’Uwiteka, muri Wowe,—har’Umucyo: Jy’Udushorera, Mukiza.

Couplet 2

Ni Wowe, Yesu, Wanshatse mu mwijima wa Satani, ubwo nari muri wo, Wowe, Mucunguzi wanjye, Wankuye mu bwenge buke kubw’ Urukundo rwawe.

Refrain

Mwami, Mwana—w’Uwiteka, muri Wowe,—har’Umucyo: Jy’Udushorera, Mukiza.

Couplet 3

Wiyambuy’ Icyubahiro, Wigira nk’umugaragu utagir’agaciro. Uhorw’ibyaha nakoze, mpeshw’ Amahoro n’Ubuntu n’urupfu rwawe, Yesu.

Refrain

Mwami, Mwana—w’Uwiteka, muri Wowe,—har’Umucyo: Jy’Udushorera, Mukiza.

Couplet 4

Mucunguzi, mbane nawe, njye nkwizeran’ umurava: Uz’intege nke zanjye; Njye nkor’iby’ Ushaka byose. Non’Unshunshumurireho Umwuka wawe Wera.

Refrain

Mwami, Mwana—w’Uwiteka, muri Wowe,—har’Umucyo: Jy’Udushorera, Mukiza.
