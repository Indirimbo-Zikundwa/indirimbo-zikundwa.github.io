#168

Mbumbatiwe nawe

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mbumbatiwe nawe, Yesu, nta kizampangara, kand’Urukund’ Umfitiye ni rwo runduhur’ubu. Numvis’amajwi meza y’abatugiy’imbere; Bahora baririmba, bashima Yesu, bati:

Refrain

Mbumbatiwe nawe, Yesu, nta kizampangara, kand’Urukund’ umfitiye ni rwo runduhur’ubu.

Couplet 2

Mbumbatiwe nawe, Yesu; Ntabwo nkiganyira. Nta cyo Satan’ antwar’ubu n’amahane ye yose. Kand’amakuba na yo ntashobora kunsinda. Ibyago by’isi byose ntibyadutandukanya.

Refrain

Mbumbatiwe nawe, Yesu, nta kizampangara, kand’Urukund’ umfitiye ni rwo runduhur’ubu.

Couplet 3

Yesu, Buhungiro bwanjye, Yesu Wamfiriye, Wowe, Rutare nshinzweho, Nzakwiringir’ iteka. Nuko, njye nihangana: Dor’iri joro ribi vuba rigiye gucya, mpereko njye mw’Ijuru!

Refrain

Mbumbatiwe nawe, Yesu, nta kizampangara, kand’Urukund’ umfitiye ni rwo runduhur’ubu.
