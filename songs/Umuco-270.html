#270

Har’umuryango wuguruye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Har’umuryango wuguruye, ntawushobora kuwugara, niyo wugaye ntawugurura, nyenewo n’umunyenkomezi.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango mwiza, ewe Mwungere mwiza, abaguciyeko bose, bafis’ubugingo budashira, kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 2

Uwo niwo intama zicamwo, iyo zigiye mu canya, niyo zigiye mu buraro, ego n’umuryango w’intama.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango mwiza, ewe Mwungere mwiza, abaguciyeko bose, bafis’ubugingo budashira, kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 3

Ayandi masho ntawucamwo, niy’agerageje arananirwa, kuko umuryango n’uw’abumva ijwi ry’Umwungere w’Intama.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango mwiza, ewe Mwungere mwiza, abaguciyeko bose, bafis’ubugingo budashira, kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 4

Uwo muryango ni Yesu Kristo, kandi n’Umwungere w’intama, ntawinjira Atari kuri we, kukw’ari we azi intama ziwe.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango mwiza, ewe Mwungere mwiza, abaguciyeko bose, bafis’ubugingo budashira, kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 5

Ni we aj’imbere y’umukuku, akaziyobora mu canya, kandi niw’azija inyuma, ngw’azirinde umwami mubi.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango mwiza, ewe Mwungere mwiza, abaguciyeko bose, bafis’ubugingo budashira, kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 6

Mwene Data emera ube intama, niyiragirwa igacurwa mu muryango, ayandi masho agejeje arananirwa.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango mwiza, ewe Mwungere mwiza, abaguciyeko bose, bafis’ubugingo budashira, kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.
