#249

Ndirimbira mu nzira

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Ndirimbira mu Nzira iyobor’imitima, I Kalvali hatemba isok’inesha, Nah’imyampi yandasa, Umwam’Arabizi, Ni W’unyobora nkamukurikira.

Refrain

Nipfuza kumureba amaso mu yandi, Ngo ndirimb’iteka Ubuntu Bwe bwinshi, Mu mihanda y’Izahabu ndangurur’ijwi, Nta maganya arashize ndi mu Bwugamo.

Couplet 2

Iy’amazi asuma aj’i Muhengeli, Umukiza afata ingabo yo mw’Ijuru, We nyine azanyobora no mur’iyi si, Bidatinze ariko nzagera mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Nipfuza kumureba amaso mu yandi, Ngo ndirimb’iteka Ubuntu Bwe bwinshi, Mu mihanda y’Izahabu ndangurur’ijwi, Nta maganya arashize ndi mu Bwugamo.

Couplet 3

Umurimo w’Imana nah’ijoro ryagwa, Umwijima ukambuza kumeny’nzira, Ni We niyegereza akamurikira, Anyonger’imbaraga akanyobora.

Refrain

Nipfuza kumureba amaso mu yandi, Ngo ndirimb’iteka Ubuntu Bwe bwinshi, Mu mihanda y’Izahabu ndangurur’ijwi, Nta maganya arashize ndi mu Bwugamo.

Couplet 4

No mu mihand’ikomeye ndeb’imisozi, Binyibutsa na none Umwami w’i Shema, Yitwaj’inkot’ityaye ngo ayobore urugamba, N’ingab’inkingira abisingirizwe.

Refrain
