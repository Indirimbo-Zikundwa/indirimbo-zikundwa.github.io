Igihe ni cashika

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Igihe ni cashika, Turangij’urugendo, Tuzoca tuzamurwa, Dusanganir’Umwami mu bicu

Refrain

Bene Data basinziriye, Bazoca bazuka, Natw’abazoba bakiriho, Tuzohindurwa mu kanya gato, {Twambikw’ Umubiri musha, Dusanganir’Umwami mu bicu.} x2

Couplet 2

Ico gihe ndakiraba, Nkabona cegereje, Kuko ibimenyetso, Vyinshi vyamaze guseruka.

Refrain

Bene Data basinziriye, Bazoca bazuka, Natw’abazoba bakiriho, Tuzohindurwa mu kanya gato, {Twambikw’ Umubiri musha, Dusanganir’Umwami mu bicu.} x2

Couplet 3

Refrain

Couplet 4

Ni twashika mw’Ijuru, Tuzobonana na Yesu, Amaso mu yandi, Tuzosa nawe dutash’ubukwe.

Refrain
