#311

Data, rab’Icibutso

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Data, rab’Icibutso c’Incungu y’Urukundo, kitwibuts’ ivyakozwe ku git’i Gologota; Kandi kuko dufise Utwitsa kuri Wewe, Duhangaye kwegera Aya meza y’Ubuntu.

Couplet 2

Data, Witegereze mu Nyonga h’uwo Mwitsa, tweho ntiturabitse, shiti turi muri We; Mwami Wirengagize uko twonon’ Ivyawe, tugasenga tunebga, tukagir’ intege nke.

Couplet 3

Hagati y’ivyo vyaha n’ingero bikwiranye, tuhakits’ Amaraso y’Umwana waw’ Ukunda; Kand’ ivyo twisabira tuvyifuriz’ abacu, Ubarinde wa Mubi, Ubahe kwihangana.

Couplet 4

Tuje ku Nyundo zawe, twisunz’ Ubuntu bgawe, iyi Ngaburo yawe idushiremw’ intege; Yiturindish’ ibibi, itumatanye Nawe; Kuba mu buja bgawe ni ko kwima nk’abami.
