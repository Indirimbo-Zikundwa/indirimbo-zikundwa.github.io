#129B

Pa buloba ndipu

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Pa buloba ndipu tshitupa tshikese, Ndi mwenyi ne mwena luendu pa buloba; Dituku dikuabu nempingana kwetu, Nenshalaku tshendelela.

Refrain

Ndi nkutekemena wewe Nzambi wanyi, Ndi ngindila bianyi dikuatshisha diebe; Mesu anyi adi matangila kuudi Wewe Tshieyemenu tshanyi.

Couplet 2

Pandi ngeyemena muntu yeye neafwe, Pangeyemena dibwe nedipandike, Pandi mweyemena mutshi neupuke, Nengeyemena tshinganyi?

Refrain

Ndi nkutekemena wewe Nzambi wanyi, Ndi ngindila bianyi dikuatshisha diebe; Mesu anyi adi matangila kuudi Wewe Tshieyemenu tshanyi.

Couplet 3

Pandi nkueyemena wewe Nzambi wanyi Tshiena mfwishibwa bundu nansha kakese; Mu dituku dibi panakubikila Kwena umbengela nansha.

Refrain

Ndi nkutekemena wewe Nzambi wanyi, Ndi ngindila bianyi dikuatshisha diebe; Mesu anyi adi matangila kuudi Wewe Tshieyemenu tshanyi.

Couplet 4

Nzambi wanyi Wewe udi bionso kundi, Wewe keudi ne diyi dia ndekelu; Tshiwudi wamba ke tshidi tshienzekibua, Ndi ndifila kuudi Wewe.

Refrain

Ndi nkutekemena wewe Nzambi wanyi, Ndi ngindila bianyi dikuatshisha diebe; Mesu anyi adi matangila kuudi Wewe Tshieyemenu tshanyi.
