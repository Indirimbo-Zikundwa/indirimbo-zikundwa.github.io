#226

Uhoraho n’umuco wanje

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Uhoraho n’umuco wanje, Amurikir’ibirenge vyanje, N’ahimbarizw’Izina ryiwe, Ni ryo ryampaye kubana nawe.

Refrain

{Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye, Kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa.} x2

Couplet 2

Ntegereje izuka ry’Abera, No kubon’Umukiza wanje. Twibanire hirya mw’Ijuru, Ibi ni vyo vyizigiro mfise.

Refrain

{Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye, Kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa.} x2

Couplet 3

Uhoraho n’umwungere wanje, Ntaco nzokena mur’iyi nzira, Nah’umwijima wonsugereza, Inkingi y’umuriro n’itara ryanje.

Refrain

{Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye, Kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa.} x2

Couplet 4

Nyenyeri yo ku Kagoroba, Suk’umuco mu bana bawe, Bahumuke babon’inzira, Ubahe n’amavuta asagutse.

Refrain

{Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye, Kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa.} x2

Couplet 5

Yes’abwira abamwizeye, Ati: “Ni Je Muco w’Ukuri, N’Umwungere yigur’intama, Utandiye wese ntaronk’ubugingo”.

Refrain

{Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye, Kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa.} x2

Couplet 6

Nyenyeri yo mu gaturuturu, Ah’ushitse nta mwiz’uhaba, Tugushimiy’Ijambo uduha, Ni ryo gari rizodushitsa mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

{Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye, Kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa.} x2
