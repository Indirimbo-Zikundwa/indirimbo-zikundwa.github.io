Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Utuzanye hano gusangira Igaburo ryera, Mwami Yesu; Nuk’uyu munsi mwiza, ngwin’ Utwibutse yuko Watubabarijwe, Udupfira.

Couplet 2

Couplet 3

Nukw iri Gaburo ritwibutsa yuko Wapfiriye kudukiza! Dukiranuke twese, tuguhimbaze rwose: Turakwihereje: Utwimemo.
