#277

Ni mpwemu yaremye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ni Mpwemu yaremye vyose, ni Mpwemu wo hambere ni we twanyoyeko ku buntu, nkuko vyari vyasezeranywe.

Refrain

Ni Mpwemu w’Isezerano, aza kw’Ijambo ry’Isezerano ./.Riri mu bana b’Isezerano niwe Bugingo Budashira./.

Couplet 2

Ni Mpwemu w’Imana Data, tunywako umutaga n’ijoro, eka ntawomuhanga namba, n’Imana ubwayo muri twebwe.

Refrain

Ni Mpwemu w’Isezerano, aza kw’Ijambo ry’Isezerano ./.Riri mu bana b’Isezerano niwe Bugingo Budashira./.

Couplet 3

Ni we ba Data bahaweko, yabarongora amanwa n’ijoro, uwo niwe Mpwemu twahawe, natwe niwe aturongora.

Refrain

Ni Mpwemu w’Isezerano, aza kw’Ijambo ry’Isezerano ./.Riri mu bana b’Isezerano niwe Bugingo Budashira./.

Couplet 4

Uwumushaka wese naze, uwumunyotewe naze anywe, ari mw’ijambo ry’isezerano, niwo muco w’igihe cacu.

Refrain

Ni Mpwemu w’Isezerano, aza kw’Ijambo ry’Isezerano ./.Riri mu bana b’Isezerano niwe Bugingo Budashira./.

Couplet 5

Yemwe ngenzi ni tureme, dufise umugabisha akomeye, atujana ahasezeranywe, ni Mpwemu Yera w’Imana.

Refrain

Ni Mpwemu w’Isezerano, aza kw’Ijambo ry’Isezerano ./.Riri mu bana b’Isezerano niwe Bugingo Budashira./.
