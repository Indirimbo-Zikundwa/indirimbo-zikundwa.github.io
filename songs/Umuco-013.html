#13

Mwami Yesu je ndagushima

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mwami Yesu je ndagushima urukundo wankunze jewe warankunze uranyiyereka kuva iy’isi ikiremwa jewe warunzi Mwami.

Refrain

./. Ewe Mukiza wanje nushimwe Haleluya./. ./. Ewe Mwami n’unshoboze Mbone gutsind’isi./.

Couplet 2

Ewe Yesu je ndagushima ubuntu bwawe wangiriye warambonye je ndi mu buja nca numva ijwi ryawe ryiza nca ndagukurikira.

Refrain

./. Ewe Mukiza wanje nushimwe Haleluya./. ./. Ewe Mwami n’unshoboze Mbone gutsind’isi./.

Couplet 3

Kandi Mwami ndagushimira ukuri kwawe wanshizemwo kugira ngo mbone kunesha mu rugendo ndimwo.

Refrain

./. Ewe Mukiza wanje nushimwe Haleluya./. ./. Ewe Mwami n’unshoboze Mbone gutsind’isi./.

Couplet 4

Kandi Yesu je ndagusavye fasha bene Data bayovye bakumenye wewe bugingo, barongore kw’isoko nziza babone kuyinywako.

Refrain

./. Ewe Mukiza wanje nushimwe Haleluya./. ./. Ewe Mwami n’unshoboze Mbone gutsind’isi./.

Couplet 5

Mwami Yesu nzama ngushima kuko wamurikiye jewe uyu Muco w’igihe cacu kugira ngo mbone vyose biva kuri wa mubi.

Refrain

./. Ewe Mukiza wanje nushimwe Haleluya./. ./. Ewe Mwami n’unshoboze Mbone gutsind’isi./.
