#268

Umwami Imana y’aburahamu

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Umwami Imana y’Aburahamu, Ni We Rutare rwatumenewe, Yihishuriye intama ziwe, Noneho zigendera mu muco.

Refrain

Abahambaye bayishakira, Mu bikubita amaso bihinda, {Ariko abato b’ubwami bwayo, Banezerwa no guhishurirwa.} x2

Couplet 2

Afise inyeriyeri ndwi mu minwe, Ni We ntsinzi y’abizeye bose, Iyo yugaye nta wugurura, Kandi yuguruye nta wugara.

Refrain

Abahambaye bayishakira, Mu bikubita amaso bihinda, {Ariko abato b’ubwami bwayo, Banezerwa no guhishurirwa.} x2

Couplet 3

Mu bari mw’isi no mw’Ijuru, Nta wuhwanye n’Uhoraho Imana, Afise ubwiza bukayangana, Aca n’imanza zigororotse.

Refrain

Abahambaye bayishakira, Mu bikubita amaso bihinda, {Ariko abato b’ubwami bwayo, Banezerwa no guhishurirwa.} x2
