#160B

Yesu wakatufwila

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Yesu wakatufwila bualu bua luse Luandi; Dî dia disanka dietu diakufika kutudi, Ne luse lua kutamba malu mabi a bantu.

Couplet 2

Mashi a Musungidi adi atutokesha; Bubi bua malu etu budi pambidi pandi, Bitualonda dî diandi, Yeye neatusungile.

Couplet 3

Yesu wakadianjila kuya kumpala kuetu, Bua kutulongoluela muaba wa disanka dia Bantu bandi babushuwa bakumutekemena.

Couplet 4

Palua dituku diandi, Yeye nealue kabidi; Neaye ne myoyo yetu ku muaba wandi muimpe, Ne tuikale kashidi ne disanka dia muoyo.
