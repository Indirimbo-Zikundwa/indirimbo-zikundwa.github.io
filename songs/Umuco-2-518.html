#518

Reka Mana yanje ndagushime

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Reka Mana yanje ndagushime, ko Wampishuriy’ Ijambo Ryawe, Ijambo rinyegejwe, Ritamenywa na bose. Iryo Jambo niryo ryaremy’isi, Ijuru be n’ibiriho vyose; Ijambo Rihishuwe, n’Ubuntu kuryizera.

Couplet 2

Reka Mana yanje ndagushime, ko Wampishuriy’ Ijambo Ryawe, Ijambo rinyegejwe, Ritamenywa na bose. Iryo Jambo niryo ryaremy’isi, Ijuru be n’ibiriho vyose; Ijambo Rihishuwe, n’Ubuntu kuryizera.

Refrain

Iryo Jambo n’Inkota... N’inkota ikarishe, aho Rishitse Satani ntiyegera na gato, Ijambo n’umuriro... Umurir’ utongora,

Refrain

{Uturir’ivy’ umubiri, bidahimbar’ Imana.} x2

Couplet 3

Ijambo ry’Imana niyo Mana, Ryuzuy’ Inkomezi n’Agakiza, Rikiza imitima, Rigakiz’ imibiri, Rizibur’ amatwi y’ibitumva, impumyi zikabona umuco, indimi z’ibiragi zigatazira Yesu.

Couplet 4

Ijambo ry’Imana niyo Mana, Ryuzuy’ Inkomezi n’Agakiza, Rikiza imitima, Rigakiz’ imibiri, Rizibur’ amatwi y’ibitumva, impumyi zikabona umuco, indimi z’ibiragi zigatazira Yesu.

Refrain

Woryitiranya n’iki? Mbe wumva risa n’iki? Mu vyaremwe nta na kimwe, kuko niryo ryaremye. Woryitondera gute? Wogerageza gute? {Oyaye ntibishoboka, kirets’ur’ uw’Imana.} x2

Couplet 5

Benshi mu bantu baseng’ Imana, barongorwa n’ubumenyi bwabo, bibwira ko bambaye, ariko bari gusa. Imana Rurema Yaravuze, yuk’ubwenge bw’umwana w’umuntu, budashobora namba, kumuha kuyimenya.

Refrain

Ijambo rihishuwe, nta muntu yorimenya, nta wogeza kuryizera, Kirets’ar’ uw’Imana. N’iragi ry’ab’Imana, babikiwe n’Imana; {Nta ruvange ruribamwo, ntaworyishushanyako.} x2

Couplet 6

Abaseng’Imana biraritse, bo bamyeho kuva mbere hose, igihe kirashitse aho bazohinyuka, igihe tugiye kwinjiramwo agahinyuza kazoboneka, ab’Imana baduzwe, ab’isi basigare.

Refrain

Uzoba uri hehe? Mbe wumva uzoduzwa? Izer’Ijambo ry’Imana, ni ryo Rizokuduza, ni ryo mababa yawe azokugurukana; {N’uryizere, n’uryizere, ni ryo Gakiza kawe.} x2
