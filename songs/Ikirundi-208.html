#208

Urugo rwacu n’Imana

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Urugo rwacu n’Imana, kandi n’inkinzo yacu. Mu marushw’ arengeranye Ni yo Mufasha wacu. Wa Mwansi wa kera Yigomber’ uburyo Bg’ uko yodutsinda, Arwanish’ urwanko: Mw isi nta wuhwanye na we.

Couplet 2

Twokwizigir’ inkomezi Zacu, twoca tuneshwa,. Imana yatoy’ intore Yo kuturasanira: Yitwa Kristo Yesu, Ni we Nyen’ ingabo, Ntahindagurika, Ni we Nyen’ inganji, Ntazobura kumunesha,

Couplet 3

Nah’ iyi si yokwuzura Ba dayimon’ isinzi; Ntitubatinye na gato Kuko batotunesha. Yesu yagomoye Ijambo n’ukuri Kutuneshereza; Kera yahangaye Wa Mwans’ ac’ aramutsinda.

Couplet 4

Hishur’ ijambo ry’ ukuri Riremesh’ Ishengero. Imbabazi zawe Mwami, Zigwire muri twese. N"iyo bodutera, Bakanyag’ ivyacu, Bakic’ umubiri, Ntibokwic’ ukuri, Kuzokwama guhangamye.
