Mutabazi wacu Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mutabazi wacu Yesu, twese tuguhimbaze; Ni Wowe Wadupfiriye, Yesu Mwana w’Intama.

Couplet 2

Yes’Ashimwe. Yes’ Ashimwe Icyubahiro kibe icy’uwo Mwana w’Intama. Duhimbaz’ Izina rye.

Couplet 3

Twar’imbohe za Satani, twari mu mubabaro; Yatuboheshej’ ingoyi, Yes’ Aratubohora.

Couplet 4

Tuguh’imibiri yacu n’imitima n’ubwenge; Kand’ibyo dufite byose bib’ibyawe, Mukiza.

Couplet 5

Tukwijejwe cyane cyane n’uk’Ushobora rwose kuturinda no kutwoza ibyo twagucumuye.
