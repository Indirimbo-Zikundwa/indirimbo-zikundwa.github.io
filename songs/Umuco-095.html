#95

Yesu akorera mw’ishengero ryiwe

Umuco

Couplet 1

Yesu akorera mw’ishengero ryiwe, uwumushaka amubonera muri ryo n’Ishengero ryuzuy’ijambo rizima, kandi Umwami ari mw’ijambo ryiwe.

Refrain

Yaje hano kw’isi nk’Umuhanuzi? Yaje hano kw’isi nk’Intumwa, yaje hano kw’isi nk’Umwigisha nk’Umwungere, kand’ari Umuvugabutumwa bwiza, Haleluya! Haleluya.

Couplet 2

Yaje nk’intumwa adushikiriz’ ijambo, nk’umuhanuzi arahanura; yaje nk’umwungere n’umwungere mwiza, nk’Umwigisha yashikij’ivyanditswe.

Refrain

Couplet 3

Refrain

Couplet 4
