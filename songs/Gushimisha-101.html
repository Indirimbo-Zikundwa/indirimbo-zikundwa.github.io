Nabony’Uburuhukiro

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Nabony’Uburuhukiro muri Kristo Yesu. Ni We musa nihishemo; Nta handi nizeye.

Refrain

Nta wundi wese nizera; Nta kindi nireguza, keretse Yesu Wabambwe, ari jyew’Azize.

Couplet 2

Mpagijwe n’Agakiza ke, nta bwoba ngifite; Yansanze, nd’umunyabyaha; Maz’Aranyakira.

Refrain

Nta wundi wese nizera; Nta kindi nireguza, keretse Yesu Wabambwe, ari jyew’Azize.

Couplet 3

Kand’iyo ndway’ Aramvura, nkaba muzima pe; Njya mpeshwa n’Amaraso ye Ubugingo bushya.

Refrain

Nta wundi wese nizera; Nta kindi nireguza, keretse Yesu Wabambwe, ari jyew’Azize.

Couplet 4

Mpumurijwe n’ljambo rye, n’Ijambo ry’Imana; Nkijijwe kubw’Izina rye, singishidikanya.

Refrain

Nta wundi wese nizera; Nta kindi nireguza, keretse Yesu Wabambwe, ari jyew’Azize.
