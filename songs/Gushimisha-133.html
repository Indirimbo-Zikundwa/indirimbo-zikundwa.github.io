Ndagukurikira Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ndagukurikira, Yesu; Nemey’Umusaraba. Jye nta shinge, nta rugero: Urampagije Wenyine.

Refrain

Ndagukurikira, Yesu, ubwo wanguz’ Amaraso; Ndakwihaye, Mwami Yesu: kera Waranyitangiye.

Icyubahiro n’iby’isi, Sinkibimaranira. Kubwa Yesu, ndabiretse: Amp’ibyiza byo mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Ndagukurikira, Yesu, ubwo wanguz’ Amaraso; Ndakwihaye, Mwami Yesu: kera Waranyitangiye.

Refrain

Ndagukurikira, Yesu, ubwo wanguz’ Amaraso; Ndakwihaye, Mwami Yesu: kera Waranyitangiye.
