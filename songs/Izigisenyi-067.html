Refrain

Araje Yesu araje araj’uwo Mwami araje Yesu.

Couplet 1

Yemwe abamuhishuriwe Araje mumuhange amaso mw’Ijambo Yesu uko yagiye ajya mwijuru niko Azagaruka aje kutujyana.

Couplet 2

Muhunge ibinyoma byo mw’iyi si muhunge abavuga ko yaje Yesu tuzamubona aje mu bicu tumusanganire tujyane nawe.

Couplet 3

Abapfuye bizeye bazazuka abazaba bariho bahindurwe imibiri ibora izatabwa twambare indi mishya yo mw’iteka.

Couplet 4

Ntagihe gisigaye ku bizera mu kanya gatoya tuzamubona isaha y’Imana y’Isiraheri yerekanye ko igihe kigeze.

Couplet 5

Abisiraheri barataha bagiye gutegerez’igihe cyabo igihe cy’amahanga gishire agakiza gasubizwe ba nyirako.

Couplet 6

Ubukwe bw’Umugeni wa Yesu buteye amatsiko buzaba mw’ijuru tuzategekana n’Umwami tuzaban’iteka n’iteka ryose.
