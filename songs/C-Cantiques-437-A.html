Kujimina pamo

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Kujimina pamo na lungenyenye Lujimina pa kumweka dyuba; Mingilo yetu ikashala pano, Tukavulukwa na bakashala.

Refrain

Bwingidi bwetu bukavulukwa, Pa mwanda wa byo twalongele pano. Twingidile Leza ke tukakoka, Tukavulukwa na bakashala.

Refrain
