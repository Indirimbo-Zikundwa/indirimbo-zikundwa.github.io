Ndakugomba Mukiza We

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Ndakugomba Mukiza We. Ntaho mbasha kwijana, Nifuza k’Umpora hafi, ukaj’ Urankomeza.

Refrain

Sinzogir’ ico ntinya, Yesu, njan’ah’ Ugomba, ntaho nzogenda ngimira, ngukurikir’ ah’Uja.

Couplet 2

Ndakugomba Mukiza We, ndiyoroheje cane, ni wewe gus’ Undemesha, nta wundi mbizigako.

Refrain

Sinzogir’ ico ntinya, Yesu, njan’ah’ Ugomba, ntaho nzogenda ngimira, ngukurikir’ ah’Uja.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Sinzogir’ ico ntinya, Yesu, njan’ah’ Ugomba, ntaho nzogenda ngimira, ngukurikir’ ah’Uja.

Couplet 4

Ndakugomba Mukiza We, Undongore mu Nzira, nshitsa kuri Yorodani, utek’ Urunjabutse.

Refrain

Sinzogir’ ico ntinya, Yesu, njan’ah’ Ugomba, ntaho nzogenda ngimira, ngukurikir’ ah’Uja.
