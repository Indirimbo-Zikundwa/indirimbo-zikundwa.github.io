#149

Nowa ku ki wubaka ubwo bwato?

Izindi

Couplet 1

Nowa ku ki wubak’ ubwo bwato? Urakor’ ivy’ ubupfapfa gose. Dor’utuy’ imusozi, kandi nta maz’ahari. Iby’ukora biratuyobeye.

Couplet 2

Uhoraho ni we yabivuze, yukw’ abagizi ba nabi bose. Abujuj’is’ ivyaha bazomarwa n’amazi, ayo maz’ azob’ar’ umwuzure.

Couplet 3

Bagir’inama yo kubazanya, bati Nowa yab’avug’ ukuri? Bati: Haba na gato, ntidukwiye kwemera, dukomeze mu vyo kwinezeza.

Couplet 4

Igihe na co gihita vuba, Nowa yubaka n’ umwete rwose. Uko bamureba ga ni ko bamuseka ga bati: Noneho Nowa yasaze.

Couplet 5

Nowa yumvir’ Iman’ Ihoraho, yinjira muri ya bwa bwato vuba. Yinjirana n’abandi bagiriw’ imbabazi, Maz’Iman’ Ibakingir’urugi.

Couplet 6

Nukw’abantu baraseka cane, bakomanga ku rugi bavuga bati: Nowa sohoka, imvur’irahita, yutubesha ng’uri mu rugendo.

Couplet 7

Ijuru risa n’iritobotse, ubwo hagw’ imvur’ itey’ ubwoba. Bati: Tura rimbutse nk’uko Nowa yavuga, dor’urupfu ruratuzengutse.

Couplet 8

Umv’ukw’ Imana Yabashubije: Nowa yajag’ abigisha rwose. Mubigir’ ibikino mwanka kwumvir’ Intumwa, ni ryo teka muciriwe none.

Couplet 9

Nukw’amaz’ aterura bwa bwato bureremba kuri wa mwuzure. Abicaye mu bwato bafisemw’ amahoro, kugez’ubwo bazosohokamwo.

Couplet 10

None natwe dufis’ ubwo bwato: N’Agakiza twahawe na Yesu. Nib’ ushak’ Amahoro ngwino wihitiremwo, Agakiza woyekurimbuka.
