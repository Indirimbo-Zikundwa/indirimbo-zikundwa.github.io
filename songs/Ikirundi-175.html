#175

Yesu n’Umwungere Wac’ Aduhoza

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yesu n’Umwungere Wac’ Aduhoza, Kw ari w’atureze Mbe twotiny’ iki? Tumukurikire Iy’ aja hose, Naho hoba habi Cank’ ari heza.

Couplet 2

Kand’ uyo Mwungere Turamuz’ ijwi, Riratunezera Iyo turyumva; Kand’ iy’ aduhannye Nta h’ayogora, Ni w’atwiharije, Ni we Ndongozi.

Couplet 3

Yavuy’ amaraso Kubgacu twese, Yaducucagiye Ayo maraso. Ac’ adushirako Ikimenyetso Ni co Mpwemu Yera Twacits’ abiwe.

Couplet 4

Yesu n’Umwungere Atuzigama, Nah’ amabingira Yoz’atontoma Ntiyodushikira, Kwaturwanira; Azoturenganya Hirya y’urupfu.
