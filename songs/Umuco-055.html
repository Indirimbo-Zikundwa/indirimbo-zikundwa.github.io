#55

Jewe mpagaze

Umuco

Couplet 1

Jewe ndahagaze mw’Izina ry’Uhoraho; ndaririmba cane Urukundo Yankunze.

Refrain

Ntazir’Izina ry’Uhoraho ndaririmba cane; ntazir’ Urukundo Yankunze Akanyigura.

Couplet 2

Jewe ndaririmba mpimbaz’Umwami wanje; ndamushima cane, sinzoreka kumuhaya.

Refrain

Ntazir’Izina ry’Uhoraho ndaririmba cane; ntazir’ Urukundo Yankunze Akanyigura.

Couplet 3

Ndirimb’Umwami wanje n’ijwi rirenga cane; nta na kimwe co mw’isi combuza kumushima.

Refrain

Ntazir’Izina ry’Uhoraho ndaririmba cane; ntazir’ Urukundo Yankunze Akanyigura.

Couplet 4

Narabonye Umuco ukayangana cane; muri wo nta kindi nabonye, atar’ Ubuntu gusa.

Refrain

Ntazir’Izina ry’Uhoraho ndaririmba cane; ntazir’ Urukundo Yankunze Akanyigura.

Couplet 5

Mbega nomushima nte ku buntu yangiriye; nta rindi shimwe mfise, akir’ubugingo bwanje.

Refrain

Ntazir’Izina ry’Uhoraho ndaririmba cane; ntazir’ Urukundo Yankunze Akanyigura.
