#148

Imana yadukuye muri egiputa

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Imana yadukuye muri Egiputa, Ngo tuyishikanir’ibimazi, Ngo tuyishikanire amashikanwa, Tugaragaze ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, Tugaragaze ubwiza bw’Iyera.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, Ubundi nabwo bukishura, Iyo hariho ukubohwa, Haba hariho n’agakiza, {Ubu twaramaze kubohorwa, Dushikanir’Imana.} x2

Couplet 2

Imana yadukuye i Babuloni, Aho twari twaboshwe nabi, Tutagikorera Imana yacu, Tutagishikanira Uhoraho, Twari twuzuye umubabaro.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, Ubundi nabwo bukishura, Iyo hariho ukubohwa, Haba hariho n’agakiza, {Ubu twaramaze kubohorwa, Dushikanir’Imana.} x2

Couplet 3

Agakiza karatanzwe rimwe risa, Nta kuganzwa n’umuntu w’inyuma, Ngo atubuze kwidegemvya nagato, Ngo dushikanire Uhoraho, Tumushikanire ibimazi.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, Ubundi nabwo bukishura, Iyo hariho ukubohwa, Haba hariho n’agakiza, {Ubu twaramaze kubohorwa, Dushikanir’Imana.} x2

Couplet 4

Refrain

Couplet 5
