#89

Yarandondey’ Asanga nanijwe n’ivyaha

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yarandondey’ Asanga nanijwe n’ivyaha, Anshira ku bitugu Anjana mu rugo: Abamarayika biwe baririmbira mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Rwa Rukundo rwiwe! Ya Maraso yiwe! Bga Buntu bganzanye mu Rugo. Mbur’ uko mbivuga ntangaye.

Couplet 2

Yasanze nkomeretse, Ansomoz’ umuti, Arampumuriz’ ati humur’ ur’uwanje. Sinaribgumv’ ijwi nk’iryo; Ndema mu mutima rwose.

Refrain

Rwa Rukundo rwiwe! Ya Maraso yiwe! Bga Buntu bganzanye mu Rugo. Mbur’ uko mbivuga ntangaye.

Couplet 3

Anyerek’ ibiganza n’inkovu z’imambo n’igitsibo c’amahwa Yambitswe mu mutwe: Nibaz’ico Yambonyemwo cotum’ Ambabarizw’ artyo.

Refrain

Rwa Rukundo rwiwe! Ya Maraso yiwe! Bga Buntu bganzanye mu Rugo. Mbur’ uko mbivuga ntangaye.

Couplet 4

None nd’ imbere yiwe, nakirwa n’umuco uva mu Nyonga hiwe, ndimbur’ imigisha Yampereye mur’iyi si: Mbur’uko mbimushimira.

Refrain

Rwa Rukundo rwiwe! Ya Maraso yiwe! Bga Buntu bganzanye mu Rugo. Mbur’ uko mbivuga ntangaye.

Couplet 5

None nduhutse neza mu mutima wanje, ndorerey’ urya musi Azogarukako, ngw’az’ antahurukan’i We Ankoy’ Amaraso yiwe.

Refrain

Rwa Rukundo rwiwe! Ya Maraso yiwe! Bga Buntu bganzanye mu Rugo. Mbur’ uko mbivuga ntangaye.
