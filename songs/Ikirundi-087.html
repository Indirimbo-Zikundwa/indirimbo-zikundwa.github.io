#87

Mbega ntugomba guturw’ ivyaha? Har’ Inkomezi mu Maraso

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mbega ntugomba guturw’ ivyaha? Har’ Inkomezi mu Maraso. Ntugomba kandi kunesh’ Umubi? Ayo Maras’ arakiza.

Refrain

Har’ inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso ya Yesu, har’ Inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso y’Umukiza.

Couplet 2

Mbega ntiwamy’ uri n’amanyama? Har’ inkomezi mu maraso. Azoguhindur’ umugwaneza, ayo Maras’ arakiza.

Refrain

Har’ inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso ya Yesu, har’ Inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso y’Umukiza.

Couplet 3

Mbega ntugomba kwera derere? Har’ inkomezi mu Maraso. Niyo gusa yoz’ico ry’ ivyaha: Ayo Maras’ arakiza.

Refrain

Har’ inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso ya Yesu, har’ Inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso y’Umukiza.

Couplet 4

Mbega ntugomba kumuyoboka? Har’ inkomezi mu Maraso. Ntugomba guhor’ Umuhimbaye? Ayo Maras’ arakiza.

Refrain

Har’ inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso ya Yesu, har’ Inkomezi zitangaje mu Maraso y’Umukiza.
