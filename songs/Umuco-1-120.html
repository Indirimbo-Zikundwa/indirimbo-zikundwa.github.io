#120

Emwe bakristo bene data

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Emwe bakristo bene Data, Nimwumve iyo mpanuro, Iyo mpanuro nayo s’iyindi, Ja wisunga Umwami wawe, Wumv’ijwi ry’Ijambo ryiwe.

Refrain

Abo mu Rugo rwa Data, Bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe, Bama bamwubaha rwose, N’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 2

Imana igabur’Ijambo ryayo, Toranyamw’umugabane wawe, Mw’Ishengero havugwa menshi, Shakamw’ijwi ry’Uhoraho, Ryame ritsindaje muri wewe.

Refrain

Abo mu Rugo rwa Data, Bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe, Bama bamwubaha rwose, N’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 3

Ca bugufi mugenzi wanje, Shak’ic’Imana ikuronderako, Ugendere mu rukundo rwayo, Wame umwisunga muri vyose, Ni ho uzokwubaka ku Rutare.

Refrain

Abo mu Rugo rwa Data, Bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe, Bama bamwubaha rwose, N’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 4

N’utumva ncuti ijwi ry’Imana, Ntumeny’ukugomba kwayo, Ntuzotera intambwe na rimwe, Ntuzomenya inzira ucamwo, N’ah’uhagaze ah’ari ho.

Refrain

Abo mu Rugo rwa Data, Bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe, Bama bamwubaha rwose, N’umunezero mwinshi.

Couplet 5

Kurugutura amatwi ya Mpwemu, Uhor’umwubah’iteka, Azoba mu bugingo bwawe, Nawe nyene uzoba muri We, Mwamane nantaryo, Haleluya!

Refrain

Abo mu Rugo rwa Data, Bumv’iteka ijwi ryiwe, Bama bamwubaha rwose, N’umunezero mwinshi.
