Mukiza Mwungeri Mwiza

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mukiza, Mwungeri Mwiza, Uturinde tw’abana bawe; Iri jor’utube hafi, ngo turamuk’ amahoro.

Couplet 2

Couplet 3

Yesu, Mwunger’Udukunda, tubabarir’ ibyaha byacu; Rind’abo dukunda bose; Nyum’ Uzatugez’i Wawe!
