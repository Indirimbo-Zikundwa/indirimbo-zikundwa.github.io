#8

Kw’Izina rya Yesu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Kw’ Izina rya Yesu Ni dupfukame, Ngo twemere Yesu Yukw ar’ Uhoraho, Ngo biter’ Imana Icubahiro, Kuko Yes’ ari We Jambo w’Imana.

Couplet 2

Uwo Jambo ni We Yaremy’ iyi si, N’ umuco n’ umwiza, N’ izuba n’ ukwezi, N’ inyenyeri na zo, N’ ibiguruka, N’ umuntu mw’ ishusho Y’ Imana yacu.

Couplet 3

Yaciye bugufi Acik’ umuntu, Maz’abanyavyaha Baca baramwica, nah’ afis’ izina Ry’ icubahiro, Hanyum’ arazuka, Atsinz’ urupfu.

Couplet 4

Bene Data, vuga Izina ryiwe Muciye bugufi, Kw ar’ Imana nkuru, N’ Umukiza wacu, Krist’ Ahoraho, N’ izina riruta Ayandi yose.

Couplet 5

Mu mitima yanyu Ni mumwimike. Rek’ anesh’ atsinde Ivy’ amasatani, Ni w’ afis’ inganji. Ni w’ azonesha Wa mwansi Satani, Ni yabatera.

Couplet 6

Bene Data, Yesu Azogaruka Mu bgiza bg’Imana N’ ubg’ ingabo zayo Azotsind’ abansi Bose bo mw isi, Kukw ari we Mwami Aganz’ abami.
