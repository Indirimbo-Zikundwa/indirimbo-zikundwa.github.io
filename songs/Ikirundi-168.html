#168

Uwo Yes’Agumbiriye, nta bgoba yogira

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Uwo Yes’Agumbiriye, nta bgoba yogira, Urukundo rwiwe rwinshi ni rwo ruturuhura. Ni mwumv’ amajwi yera y’abatugiy’ imbere, umve baririmbira neza mw’ Ijuru, bati:

Refrain

Uwo Yes’Agumbiriye nta bgoba yogira, Urukundo rwiwe rwinshi ni rwo ruturuhura.

Couplet 2

Uwo Yes’Agumbiriye yam’ Amuzigama, uburake bga Satani ntibgomuter’ ubgoba. Kand’ amarushwa na yo ntiyomucobogoza, Atewe na yo cane yama yizeye Yesu.

Refrain

Uwo Yes’Agumbiriye nta bgoba yogira, Urukundo rwiwe rwinshi ni rwo ruturuhura.

Couplet 3

Kuko Yesu Yampfiriye Azomp’ Amahoro, ni We Rutare nshinzweko nzohora mmwizigiye. Ahor’ Angumya cane mur’ iyi misi yose, ni naduga mw’ Ijuru nzomuhimbaza rwose.

Refrain

Uwo Yes’Agumbiriye nta bgoba yogira, Urukundo rwiwe rwinshi ni rwo ruturuhura.
