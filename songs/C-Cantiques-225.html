Leza wami na kutota

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Leza wami na kutota, Tata wa mwiulu wa ku ndama, Kyo nsaka ikimo kyonka, Kyobe kiswamutima pe.

Couplet 2

Dishinda nangwa dya fita, Ku bula ne mwa ku pita, Mwa ya mfumu ka mume, Kyobe kiswamutima pe.

Couplet 3

Nangwa kika kyo na temwa, Umfwa i kyo wa shidika, Sanga ami nke ki posa, Kyobe kiswamutima pe.

Couplet 4

Kisakesake ki lale, Kilakelake ki lekwe, Ne tonso tonso to nguba, Kyobe kiswamutima pe.

Couplet 5

Kupela lwendo pa ku pwa, Sanga ne mobe na twela, Nyeke kyo nsaka i kyonka, Kyobe kiswamutima pe.
