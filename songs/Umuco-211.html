#211

Sirayeli (2x), nyamusubiranyuma

Umuco

Couplet 1

Sirayeli, Sirayeli nyamusubiranyuma, igihe kigeze he bivuge namba ubibona; na wewe Laodikiya wigiz’ uwahumutse, igihe kigeze he bivuge namba ubibona?

Refrain

Eh’abana b’Ubwami, bariko barinjira, bamaze gushikira bos’ ibibanza vyabo naw’ uja irya n’ino ufatafata vyanka, uzohava ukebuka wamaze kwugaranwa ./.Injira ncuti, injira./.
