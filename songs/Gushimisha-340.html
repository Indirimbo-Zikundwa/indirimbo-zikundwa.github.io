#340

Umutima wanjye wizera

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Umutima wanjye wizera kugusaba, Mana Data; Unyumvane za mpuhwe zawe. Jye umwana wakugomeye. Ni Wowe Wenyine nizeye kw’ari Wow’Ump’Umugisha.

Couplet 2

Ndagusanze nguhungiyeho, KukwAri Wowe nizeye. Umpe n’Umwuka wawe Wera. Ump’intege ninanirwa. Ni mbon’ibyago, nkwizigire k’Unyakiran’ Imbabazi.

Couplet 3

Ku manywa mba mu Mucyo wawe; N’ijoro nkakwegamira; No mu gitondo, nkigusenga, Ujy’Untegurir’ Inzira: No mu maryama, Data Mwiza, Undinganiriz’ iby’ejo.
