#381

Dufise umwenda

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Dufise umwenda atar’uwo kwitonder’ ivy’akameremere kacu, ngo dukor’ivyo kipfuza. Ahubwo dufis’uwo gukor’ ivy’Ijambo, Rihora ridusaba.

Refrain

Umwenda w’icaha n’ivyagezwe, Umwenda w’Ijambo n’urukundo, Aragow’ uwutitondera ivyagezwe, Ahubw’ uwugendera mu rukundo, Ni we ashitsa ivyagezwe.

Couplet 2

Ivyagezwe biboneka iyo, Harih’icaha; iyo ata caha, Ivyagezwe ntibiboneka, Tugendere mu rukundo duhabwa n’Ijambo, Dushitse ivyagezwe.

Refrain

Umwenda w’icaha n’ivyagezwe, Umwenda w’Ijambo n’urukundo, Aragow’ uwutitondera ivyagezwe, Ahubw’ uwugendera mu rukundo, Ni we ashitsa ivyagezwe.

Couplet 3

Tuzoboneka ko tugendera, Mw’Ijambo; ni dupfa kuri twebwe, Ivyagezwe ntibizotuganza, Twame twihisha mw’Ijambo twama tubwirwa, Dushitse ivyagezwe.

Refrain

Umwenda w’icaha n’ivyagezwe, Umwenda w’Ijambo n’urukundo, Aragow’ uwutitondera ivyagezwe, Ahubw’ uwugendera mu rukundo, Ni we ashitsa ivyagezwe.
