#136A

Tendela Yehova aye

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Tendela Yehova Aye Mulopwe, Mutendele wa longele biyampe, Tumufwija kwitulama nyeke-ke, Kilongoji kwitukunkuja batwe.

Refrain

Bamalaika totai Ombai lunsense, Bonso badi mulu, Bandilai mpala, Byonso bidi panshi, Panopantanda, (Tendelai Yehova! x3) Totai Mulopwe!

Couplet 2

Bukudi buyampe mu mutendele, Mema a bukomo etutokeje, Lusa ne bya buntu byetupangile, Wi twitaba enka pa kumulomba.

Refrain

Bamalaika totai Ombai lunsense, Bonso badi mulu, Bandilai mpala, Byonso bidi panshi, Panopantanda, (Tendelai Yehova! x3) Totai Mulopwe!

Couplet 3

Tu mu totei nyeke, pa kutompibwa, Mwanda lusa lwandi lwitungwijanga, Lwitabijo lwetu lwituningisha, Momwa mulu nyeke tukamufwija.

Refrain

Bamalaika totai Ombai lunsense, Bonso badi mulu, Bandilai mpala, Byonso bidi panshi, Panopantanda, (Tendelai Yehova! x3) Totai Mulopwe!
