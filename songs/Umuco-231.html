#231

Uhereye kera hose

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uhereye kera hose Imana ifise imigabo, Imana isezeranira Aburahamu Sogokuru, imubwira ko uruvyaro rwiwe ruzogwira ./.Kubw’ukwizera yarashoboye kuvyara naho yarak’aheze./.

Refrain

Bene Data igihe kirakuze cane, kuko ivy’Imana yasezeranye vyarashitse, ibindi navyo birihuta, guseruka tugumye muri twebwe urukundo rw’Imana ./.Tugendere m’ukwizera, kuvuye mu guhishurirwa eh’Umwami araje./.

Couplet 2

Mu gihe c‘umusavyi w’Imana, nawe yitwa Dawidi Uhoraho yongera gutanga isezerano yuko ku gishitsi ciwe hazoturuka Umukiza ./.Umukiza w’Abisirayeli n’abanyamahanga niwe Yesu Kristo Umwami wacu./.

Refrain
