Nkunda kwibaza cane

Umuco

Couplet 1

Nkunda kwibaza cane iminsi yose ku kintu nama mbona kandi kitoroshe simfise n’ukuntu nogisigura neza n’urukundo Umwami Imana yakunze abiwe ./.Aho niho har’agakiza k’Imana./.

Refrain

None ngire nte jewe, ko nd’umuntu, Arikw’Imana yo ni nyen’inkomezi, Yesu Kristo, Mwami wanje Mana yanje, unyuzuz’urukundo rwawe ./.Nshobore kugendera mu kugomba kwawe./.

Couplet 2

Ico gikorwa nticoroshe bagenzi, kuko catanguye kuva kera cane, Imana ikirema isi n’ijuru n’ibindi vyose, arik’umuntu ikamurema mw’ishusho ryayo ./.Iyo yar’imigabo yayo yo kutwihishurira./.

Refrain

None ngire nte jewe, ko nd’umuntu, Arikw’Imana yo ni nyen’inkomezi, Yesu Kristo, Mwami wanje Mana yanje, unyuzuz’urukundo rwawe ./.Nshobore kugendera mu kugomba kwawe./.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Couplet 4
