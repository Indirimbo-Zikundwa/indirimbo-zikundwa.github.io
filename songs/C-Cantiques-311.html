#311

Ndi ngela meji

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Ndi ngela menji a musoko muakane, Udi mu diulu kudi Nzambi; Nenyeku dituku dia kunshikidilu, Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi.

Refrain

Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi bua kulu Panafika kulu kua Nzambi; Ndi musue kuenza mudimu webe Yesu, Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi.

Couplet 2

Nzambi wanyi, umpe bukole buakane, Ngenze mudimu webe bimpe; Ngitabujije bakuabo, Musungidi, Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi.

Refrain

Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi bua kulu Panafika kulu kua Nzambi; Ndi musue kuenza mudimu webe Yesu, Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi.

Couplet 3

Nentambe kusanka panamona Yesu, Nenfile bakuabo kudiye; Bu mudimu wanyi unakumuenzela, Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi.

Refrain

Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi bua kulu Panafika kulu kua Nzambi; Ndi musue kuenza mudimu webe Yesu, Nsuasua tshifulu tshia butumbi.
