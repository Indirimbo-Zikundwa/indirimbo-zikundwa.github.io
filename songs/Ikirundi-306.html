#306

Uyu musi w’akanyamuneza

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Uyu musi w’akanyamuneza N’umusi wo kuruhukako, Kandi n’umusi w’Umwam’ Imana, Mwiz’ urut’ iyind’ itandatu: Kur’ uyu musi wayo Ni tuyiririmbire, Ngo Yera, Yera, Yera, We Mana nkuru y’Ubutatu!

Couplet 2

Kur’ uyu mus’ Imana Rurema Yaremy" umurango n’umuco, Kur’ uyu mus’ Umukiza Yesu Yazuts’ ava mu mv’ i kuzimu, Kur’ uyu musi Mpwemu Yaj’ asa n’umuriro, Nuko n’umusi wera Mu buryo butatu bukuru.

Couplet 3

Bagenzi, mureke kwibagira Uyu musi mwiza w’Imana, Ntimuhebe kuwukoranako Kubg’ izina rya Yes" Umwami; N’izina ryiza ryera, Riratunezereza, N’izina ry’agakiza, Nuko turih’ icubahiro.

Couplet 4

Kand’ uyu musi n’uwo gusenga No kwumv’ ivy’ Iman’ itubgira: Ni ho dusubirizwamw’ intege Nyinshi zo gukorer’ Imana. Dushime Mpwemu Yera, Bo n’Umukiza Yesu, Na Dat’ Imana yacu, Kw ar’ Imwe mu buryo butatu.
