#174

Guh 136 AHW’AZANYOBORA HOSE

Izindi

Couplet 1

Ahw’Azanyobora hose, ni ho nzishimira kujya; Kukw ari jye Yapfiriye, akanzir’ atacumuye.

Refrain

Yes’Azanyobor’iteka, maz’ angez’i w’amahoro; Kukw ar’Inshuti y’ukuri, itigeze guhemuka.

Couplet 2

Nishimira kw’antegeka, nishimira kw’Anyobora. Nishimira kumwumvira, kukw’ari jye Yapfiriye.

Refrain

Yes’Azanyobor’iteka, maz’ angez’i w’amahoro; Kukw ar’Inshuti y’ukuri, itigeze guhemuka.

Couplet 3

Singishidikanya rwose, kuko ndi kumwe na Yesu. Nizigiye ko vub’aha, nzamureba duhanganye.

Refrain

Yes’Azanyobor’iteka, maz’ angez’i w’amahoro; Kukw ar’Inshuti y’ukuri, itigeze guhemuka.
