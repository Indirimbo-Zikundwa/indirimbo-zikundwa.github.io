#151

Izamurwa ry’umugeni

Umuco

Couplet 1

Izamurwa ry’Umugeni riri hafi gushika; Ishengero ry’Umukiza riri hafi kuzamurwa ./.Ivyerekana ko rigira rishike n’ibimenyetso yavuze./.

Refrain

Ubushizi bw’isoni, ubusambanyi n’ubushakanyi, ukwikunda gusa, n’ugukunda amahera, abirarira abibona, abatumvira abavyeyi, abanegura ivy’Imana, badakunda n’ababo, barementanya batigarura, bakunda ibibahimbara ./.Bakabirutish’ Imana./.
