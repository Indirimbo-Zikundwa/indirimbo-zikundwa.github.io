#113

Imisozi irahunga

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Imisozi irahunga, Intare zirahunga, Ibigatanya biraneshwa, Ibitandara bigasaduka, Kubw’Ihanga rihambaye, N’Ihanga ry’Abera, Rishigikiwe n’Uwanesheje.

Refrain

Raba n’Ihanga r-y’Abera, Ntirivanze n’ayandi mahanga, Aho riciye bararibisa, Abaryihanze rirabahitana, N’Ihanga rihambaye cane, Ryuzuy’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho.

Couplet 2

Umuriro urahunga, Ibiyaga vyicamwo kubiri, Iminwa y’intare ikazibwa, Ku bizeye Uhoraho, Amadayimoni arahunga, N’Ihanga ry’abahizi, Rishigikiwe n’Uwanesheje.

Refrain

Raba n’Ihanga r-y’Abera, Ntirivanze n’ayandi mahanga, Aho riciye bararibisa, Abaryihanze rirabahitana, N’Ihanga rihambaye cane, Ryuzuy’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho.

Couplet 3

Imiravyo iraravya, Imituragaro igaturagara, Isi igahind’agashitsi, Abami bakadedemezwa, Kubw’Ihanga rihambaye, N’Ihanga ritey’ubwoba, Rishigikiwe n’Uwanesheje.

Refrain

Raba n’Ihanga r-y’Abera, Ntirivanze n’ayandi mahanga, Aho riciye bararibisa, Abaryihanze rirabahitana, N’Ihanga rihambaye cane, Ryuzuy’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho.

Couplet 4

Ewe Hanga rihambaye, Urahiriwe cane, Amaso yaw’abona vyose, Abona n’ibitabonwa, Wabujij’isi amahoro, Ntawigeze akunesha, Ushigikiwe n’Uwanesheje.

Refrain

Raba n’Ihanga r-y’Abera, Ntirivanze n’ayandi mahanga, Aho riciye bararibisa, Abaryihanze rirabahitana, N’Ihanga rihambaye cane, Ryuzuy’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho.

Couplet 5

N’Ihanga rihambaye, Nta bijora bivugwamwo, Umwami waryo n’agaheta, Ryuzuy’umuco cane, Ab’umwiza barahunga, Ntako nobiririmba, N’Ihanga ryiharije rwose.

Refrain

Raba n’Ihanga r-y’Abera, Ntirivanze n’ayandi mahanga, Aho riciye bararibisa, Abaryihanze rirabahitana, N’Ihanga rihambaye cane, Ryuzuy’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho.
