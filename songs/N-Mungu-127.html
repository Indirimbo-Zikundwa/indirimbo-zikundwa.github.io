Couplet 1

Usikie Bwana Yesu anayekuita leo; Aliteswa, alikufa kwa ajili yako wewe.

Refrain

Usikie Bwana Yesu, anapiga hodi kwako; Umufungulie moyo, umukaribishe sasa.

Couplet 2

Sikuzote ulicheza, kupendeza mwili wako; Ulimusahau Yesu aliyekukufilia.

Refrain

Usikie Bwana Yesu, anapiga hodi kwako; Umufungulie moyo, umukaribishe sasa.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Usikie Bwana Yesu, anapiga hodi kwako; Umufungulie moyo, umukaribishe sasa.

Couplet 4

Refrain

Usikie Bwana Yesu, anapiga hodi kwako; Umufungulie moyo, umukaribishe sasa.
