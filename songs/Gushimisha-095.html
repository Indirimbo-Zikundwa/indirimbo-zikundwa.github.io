#95

Ibyaha byanjye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ibyaha byanjye, Yesu Yarabyikoreye, W’Atigeze gukora icyaha na kimwe; Ndamwegera, ndasenga, nd’umunyabyaha pe, nizer’ Ijambo ry’ati: Urababariwe!

Couplet 2

Maz’ibindushya byose ndabimukorera. Nta wund’utari Yesu Wabyihanganira. Ndamwegera, njy’ahw’ari, ndushye, ndendebutse, ndagenda, mpaw’intege, nsindishirijwe pe!

Couplet 3

Nukw’amaganya yanjye, nyakurwaho na We, nduhukira muri We, Amp’Amahoro ye. Imanuweli Kristo, Zina rihebuje! Rimbera nk’imibavu, ihumura neza.

Couplet 4

Icyampa nkaba nka We, Akamp’ineza ye, n’imbabazi n’ubuntu n’ubugwaneza bye. Yes’Uzampe gutura i wawe mw’Ijuru, nkajya ngusingizanya n’Abamarayika.
