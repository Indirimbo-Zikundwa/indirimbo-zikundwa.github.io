Bana b’imana

Iz'i Gisenyi

Couplet 1

Bana b’Imana mumenye ko mu minsi y’imperuka hazaba ibihe birushya abantu benshi bikunda kandi bagakunda impiya kurya no kudamarara bafit’ishusho yo kwera baguhakanisha ibyo bakora.

Refrain

Kuko muribo harimo irari ry’uburyo bwinshi bahora biga ariko ntibabasha kumenya ukuri, Abantu bameze batyo dukwiye k’ubater’umugongo.

Couplet 2

Refrain

Arikw’inyamaswa Bantu kuko zitagira ubwenge bakunda kudamarara bagatuka ibyo batazi bakurikiye Baramu baremewe guterwa no kwica.
