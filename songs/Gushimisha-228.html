#228

Bahungu bakobwa

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Bahungu, bakobwa,—Nimuze mwese i Betelehemu—Mu ruhongore; Tureb’ ibyo Data—Uri mw’Ijuru Yaduhaye twese— Mur’iri joro!

Couplet 2

Hamwe n’abashumba,—Dushim’ Umwami Uryamye mu byatsi—Nk’uworoheje. Dusang’ ababyeyi—Banezerewe; Duhuzen’ Intumwa—Kumusingiza.

Couplet 3

Nimupfukamire—Umwami Mwiza Uduh’ Ubugingo,—Mumuhimbaze! Muvuze n’impundu—Hamwe n’Intumwa; Mubwire n’abandi—Ibyo mwabonye.

Couplet 4

Twese tumushime,—Wa Mwami Mwiza, tumuhimbarize—Kukw’ Adukunda. Dufatanye twese,—Tuvuz’ impundu, ngw’abatamumenye—Baze gukizwa.
