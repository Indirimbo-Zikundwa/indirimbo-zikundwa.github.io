#66

Mu Ngoro haracar’ icicaro, naw’ urahamagawe ng’ujeyo

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mu Ngoro haracar’ icicaro, naw’ urahamagawe ng’ujeyo, abandi bagutanze kwinjira, arikw’ ahawe haracariho.

Refrain

Ihute winjire, ihute winjire, ihute winjire bigikunze.

Couplet 2

Ihute ko hatarugarira, shika mu Rug’ ugifis’ uburyo, injir’ injira mu Mazimano, usangir’ ivy’ urukundo natwe.

Refrain

Ihute winjire, ihute winjire, ihute winjire bigikunze.

Couplet 3

Umv’ uhamagara yasaraye, nyaruka, n’iki kigutebeje? Buhava bgira, wugariranwe, imyany’ izoba yuzuy’ abandi.

Refrain

Wob’ ucikiriwe, wob’ ucikiriwe, wob’ ucikiriwe bugariye.
