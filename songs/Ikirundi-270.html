Uwadupfiriye kera Araz’Ari mu bicu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Uwadupfiriye kera Araz’Ari mu bicu, Umwam’ Aramanukana N’Abera biw’isinzi: (Tumushime, tumushime)x3, Umukiz’Aragaruka.

Couplet 2

Ariyerek’abo mw’isi Ari mu Bgiza bginshi, kand’ Abamukengereye bakamubamba kera (baraboroga bamenye)x3 kw’Ari We Kristo w’Ukuri.

Couplet 3

Kand’inkovu za za mambo Araza zikiriho, Kand’ abacunguwe bose barazinezererwa: (Mukiza ni twaziraba)x3 tuzicwa n’umunezero.

Couplet 4

Nuko twese tugusenge, Yes’Ushizwe hejuru, Ubgami n’Icubahiro Ubishikire Mwami. (Urakaza! Haleluya!)x3 Ni Wewe Mwami Wenyene.
