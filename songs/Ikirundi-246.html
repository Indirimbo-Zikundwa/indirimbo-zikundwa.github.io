#246

Gologota ku giti ni ho Yesu Yabambge

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Gologota ku giti ni ho Yesu Yabambge, Yabambge kugira ngo turonke kutagipfa.

Couplet 2

Yes’Amaze kunesha, Avug’ Ijwi rirenga, Abivug’ Avyeruye, ati birarangiye.

Couplet 3

Amaze kubivuga aherakw’ arunama, Yunamishwa n’ivyaha vyari vyarancumuje.

Couplet 4

Non’antey’ imbabazi, Nihane sinongere. Simbe nkimubabaza, Aruhurw’ umutima.

Couplet 5

Abarons’ ivy’ Umuco Maze bakabivamwo, bongera kumubamba, bamutey’ akabonge.

Couplet 6

Twari twararuhijwe, twiganyira, twihevye, none turaruhutse, Yesu n’Ubukiriro.

Couplet 7

Yemw’ abarway’ ivyaha, naho mwoba ku mpfiro, ni muz’ Arabakiza, Yesu ni We Muganga.

Couplet 8

Mukiza ndakwihaye, ni Wewe Wandondeye, uko nzoba ndi kose Yesu, nzama ngushima.
