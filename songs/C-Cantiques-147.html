Mwateleka myanda

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Mwateleka myanda ya kukumya, Ya lufu lwa Yesu, Ne byo mashi andi a itike Amba etu pulushe.

Refrain

I myanda yetu a twikilwe Wa futile bubi bwetu Pa Musalabu e po a fwile, Wa futa bubi bwetu.

Couplet 2

Mwa teleka byo ba mupopele Kasha a lombela: “Tata wi balekele myanda yabo Kabayukile kyo boba”.

Refrain

I myanda yetu a twikilwe Wa futile bubi bwetu Pa Musalabu e po a fwile, Wa futa bubi bwetu.

Couplet 3
