#388

Tunganir’Imana ubudasiba

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Tunganir’Imana ubudasiba. Jy’uhora uyisenga kand’ uyumvire. Jy’ubana n’abayo, jy’ufashi indushyi, ntusibe na rimwe kuyikorera.

Couplet 2

Tunganir’Imana Jy’ubana na Yo. Nutumbira Yesu Uzaba nka We: Bose bazamenya k’ubana na We, barek’ ubugome bitabe Yesu.

Couplet 3

Tunganir’Imana, Ikuyobore. N’uterwa n’ibyago, jy’usanga Yesu. Ntiyirengagiza, umwiyambaza, uyoboke Yesu ni We Shobuja.

Couplet 4

Tunganir’Imana, tuz’umutima. Muby’ ukora byose wihutiraho. Jya wumvir’ Umwuka gwiz’urukundo: Nta son’uzagira, n’ubona Yesu.
