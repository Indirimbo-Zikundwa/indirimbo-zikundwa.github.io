#156

Ni jye Yesu Witanze

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ni jye, Yesu, Witanze kera ku Musaraba, mbitewe n’Urukundo ngo ngukiz’ibyaha byawe. Mugenzi, narakwitangiye; Wanyituy’iki wo we?

Couplet 2

Narets’Ubwiza bwanjye nari mfite mw’Ijuru, nsig’Umucyo kwa Data, nza mu mwijima w’iyi si, Naretse byose nkurengera: Wants’iki kubwaruye?

Couplet 3

Banyish’urupfu rubi, birut’uko byavugwa; Navushijw’ Amaraso ng’utazapfa nabi nawe nakwihanganiy’ibyo byose: Wanyihanganiy’iki?

Couplet 4

Navuy’i Wanjy’i bwami, nger’ah’uri mw’isi mbi, nkuzanir’Agakiza n’Ubugingo budashira. Ko nakugabiy’ Impan’ubwo, wigeze kuntur’iki?
