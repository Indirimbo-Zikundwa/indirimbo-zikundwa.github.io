#12

Ntugashake ubutunzi bw’iyi si

Impimbano· Fr René

Couplet 1

Ntugashake ubutunzi bw’iyi isi, shakashaka Ubutunzi bw’Ijuru ntiwifuz’ icyubahiro mwiyi si, shakashaka icyubahiro mw’ijuru

Refrain

MWENEDATA NAWE MUSHIKI WANJYE, NTUKARANGAZWE NIBYO UBONA MW ISI. JYA WIBUKA KW’IBI BYOSE BIZASHONGA, TWE DUFIT’ UBWAMI BWIZA MW’IJURU.

Couplet 2
