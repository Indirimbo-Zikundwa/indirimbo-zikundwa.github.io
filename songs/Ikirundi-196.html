#196

Ukwizera kwacu kurahamye pe

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Ukwizera kwacu kurahamye pe; Gushinzwe kw’Ijambo ry’Imana yacu; Nta co dutinya, dushikame gusa: Twabony’ Ubuhungiro muri Yesu.

Couplet 2

Ntutinye, na ntaryo Ndi kumwe nawe, ND’Imana yawe, nzokwama ngufasha, Nzoguh’ inkomezi zo gushikama, Nzogufat’ ukuboko, ntac’utinya.

Couplet 3

Ni nakubgira no guca mu mazi, inzuzi nta ho zizokurengera, ivyago vyose n’ibikubabaza n’ivyo Nirungikiye kukugeza.

Couplet 4

Inzira ni yaca no mu muriro, uzobumbga n’Ubuntu bganje bginshi. Imbeya nta ho zizokubabura: Nzoba nkuvugutiye ng’uz’urame.

Couplet 5

Umutima wisunz’ Umwana wanje sinzowurekuran’ Umwansi na mba. Naho Gehinomu covyur’ ingunza ntikizowunshikura mu Kuboko.
