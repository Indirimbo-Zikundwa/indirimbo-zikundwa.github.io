#294

Har’ah’abagore bazany’ abana babo

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Har’ah’abagore bazany’abana babo, Abigishwa ntibemera, barabirukana, Ariko Yes’ abibonyeArabahamagar’ ati Mukundir’ abana ngo baz’ aho ndi.

Couplet 2

Ati Ndabakira, mbarere ku bibero, Nijewe Mwungere mwiza, ntimubirukane; Kuko ni bamp’ imitima Nzobaha kuja mw ijuru: Mukundir’ abana ngo baz’ aho ndi.

Couplet 3

Dushim’ Umukiza ko yakiriy’ abana, Yamara hariho benshi bataranamwumva, Bataz’ izina rya Yesu, N’ uko yahamagay’ ati Mukundir’ abana ngo haz’ aho ndi.

Couplet 4

Icoduh’ abana bo mu mahanga yose Bakumv’ ubutumwa bgiza. bakemera Yesu; Ubahe kwumv’ ijwi ryawe. No kubon’ umuco wawe. Hamagar’ abana ngo baz’ah’uri.
