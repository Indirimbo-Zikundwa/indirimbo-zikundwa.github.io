Nzokwama nishimira

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Nzokwama nishimira cane rwose, Kurongorwa na Mpwemu w’Imana, Nzokwama nishimira kugumbirana na We, {Nkagendera musi y’ubushobozi bwiwe.} x2

Uwo Mpwemu atanga umunezero, Uwo Mpwemu atanga amahoro, Ni W’aduha gukorer’Imana, Ni W’aduha kwitandukanya n’ivyanduye, Kuk’ubushobozi bwiwe bunesh’umubiri.
