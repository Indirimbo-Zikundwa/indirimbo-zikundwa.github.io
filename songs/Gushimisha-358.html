#358

Twemere Yesu ko yaje mw’isi

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Twemere Yesu—Ko yaje mw isi kutubwir’ Amahoro Ye. Ni We Nshuti nziza. —i Gologota, Yahaw’ abazimiye. Mpora nizera,—N’ubu nizeye yuko Yesu Yampfiriye; Ku Musaraba,—Yaranesheje; Ambohora mwizeye.

Couplet 2

Inzira yanjye—Ni Yesu wanjye; Anjyan’aho Yangeneye. N’Umugiraneza;—Nta muntu n’umwe yanga guh’Agakiza mpora nizera,—N’ubu nizeye yuko Yesu Yamfiriye; Ku Musaraba,—Yaranesheje; Ambohora mmwizeye.

Couplet 3

Mwami w’Ubuntu,—Uhimbarizwe kuk’ uturarik’iteka. Tukwitabe twese,— twumv’Ijwi ryawe rihamagara hose. Twizere twese,—twe guhinyura ko Yesu Yadupfiriye; Ku Musaraba,—Yaranesheje; Abohor’abizeye.
