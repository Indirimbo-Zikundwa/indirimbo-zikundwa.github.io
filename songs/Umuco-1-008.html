Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Ncuti zanje mwakiriy’Ijambo, Muramenye ko Antikristo, Yaje kera yambay’uburyarya, Yambay’urusato rw’intama.

Couplet 2

Yaje avuga ibinyoma gusa, Ahindur’Ijambo ry’Imana, Ni we Yezebeli n’abana biwe, Bamaze gusambana n’isi.

Couplet 3

Yezebeli n’abana biwe, Niyo madini tubona ubu, Yamaze gusambana n’iyi si, Arindiriye kurimbuka.

Couplet 4

Niwe avuga yuko har’imana, Zitatu ziri mw’imana imwe, Oyaye ncuti, ntiwemere, Ivyo n’ ivya wa mugwanizi.

Couplet 5

Ijambo ry’Imana rivuga ko, Hari Imana imwe Ihoraho, Yihishuye mu buryo butatu, Ntihinduka, ni ya yind’imwe.
