#259

Uhoraho n’ingab’idukingira

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Uhoraho n’Ingab’idukingira, Ni We Mukiza, ni We Mwungere, Adukiza mu magorwa akomeye, Turi mu biganza vyiwe ntituzopfa.

Refrain

Ni We Mugabisha w’intambara, Ari imbere y’Ishengero ryiwe, Arinesherez’imbere n’inyuma, Mu kuboko kwiwe harimwo Intsinzi.

Couplet 2

Uhoraho ni We nyen’igikorwa, Uhoraho ni We azogikomeza, Amaraso yavuye ku musaraba, Arimw’intsinzi y’ibihe bidashira.

Refrain

Ni We Mugabisha w’intambara, Ari imbere y’Ishengero ryiwe, Arinesherez’imbere n’inyuma, Mu kuboko kwiwe harimwo Intsinzi.

Couplet 3

Nta nkomez’i z’umuntu zivugwa, Nta ntege nke z’umuntu zivugwa, Nta bikorwa nta kugororoka, Amaraso ya Yesu araduhagije.

Refrain

Ni We Mugabisha w’intambara, Ari imbere y’Ishengero ryiwe, Arinesherez’imbere n’inyuma, Mu kuboko kwiwe harimwo Intsinzi.
