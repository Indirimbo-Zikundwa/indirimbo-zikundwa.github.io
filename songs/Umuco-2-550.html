#550

Turi hafi kurangiza urugendo

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Turi hafi kurangiza urugendo, Ntidusuhuz’ imitima yacu, ntitwiganyire, Umwami wacu Yaduciriye Inzira, Izodushikana mu Bugingo budashira.

Refrain

Eh’ukw’imis’igenda, Niko wa musi w’Uhoraho wegereza, {N ‘umusi w’Umunezero ku Bizera, Kuko bazosanganir’ Uwabiguze mu bicu.} x2

Couplet 2

Ni h’umusaraba wac’uturemera, Twihangane tuzonezerwa duhagaz’ imbere y’Uwaneshej’ imibabaro ku bwacu, Ni ho tuzoririmb’ Indirimbo nsha z’ishimwe.

Refrain

Eh’ukw’imis’igenda, Niko wa musi w’Uhoraho wegereza, {N ‘umusi w’Umunezero ku Bizera, Kuko bazosanganir’ Uwabiguze mu bicu.} x2

Refrain

Couplet 4
