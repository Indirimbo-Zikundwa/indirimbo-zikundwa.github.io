#258

Ndabona ihanga riteye ubwoba

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ndabona ihanga riteye ubwoba, ndabona ihanga ryiharije, ryavutse murubavu rwa Yesu, ryavutse mu maraso ya Yesu, rifis’intsinzi y’umusaraba, rigize Abami n’Abaherezi ./.Ritang’ibimazi nka Abeli ritang’ibimazi nka Eliya./.

Refrain

N’abana b’Imana Ihoraho, n’ihanga ry’Imana yiharije, ryambay’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, riter’ishari ayandi mahanga, ./.uwuhagurutse kurirwanya, Asanga ariwe yirwanya, asang’ari we yiyica./.

Couplet 2

Ndabon’ihanga ritey’ubwoba, rirongorwa n’amasezerano, Balaki ararirwanya arananirwa, Balamu ararivuma arananirwa, umuvumo ku bana b’Imana, uhinduka umuhezagiro ./.Uhinduka umunezero kubw’amasezerano./.

Refrain

N’abana b’Imana Ihoraho, n’ihanga ry’Imana yiharije, ryambay’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, riter’ishari ayandi mahanga, ./.uwuhagurutse kurirwanya, Asanga ariwe yirwanya, asang’ari we yiyica./.

Couplet 3

Ndabon’ihanga ritey’ubwoba, rirongowe n’Uhoraho, imisozi yose iraribisa, n’inzuzi zose ziraribisa, n’intambwe zose ziraribisa, n’amatanure araribisa ./.Ivyo vyose biratinya kubw’inkingi y’umuriro./.

Refrain

N’abana b’Imana Ihoraho, n’ihanga ry’Imana yiharije, ryambay’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, riter’ishari ayandi mahanga, ./.uwuhagurutse kurirwanya, Asanga ariwe yirwanya, asang’ari we yiyica./.

Couplet 4

Ndabon’ihanga ritey’ubwoba, rica mu nzira ifunganye, ryikoreye imibabaro, ryikoreye umusaraba, riniha iminiho itovugwa, rica ahantu h’imbobane ./.Ku bw’urugendo ruruhije kubw’intambara zinaniza./.

Refrain

N’abana b’Imana Ihoraho, n’ihanga ry’Imana yiharije, ryambay’ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, riter’ishari ayandi mahanga, ./.uwuhagurutse kurirwanya, Asanga ariwe yirwanya, asang’ari we yiyica./.

Couplet 5

Ndabon’ihanga rihambaye, rirwana intambara ziruhije, ritsinda kubw’ukwizera, ritsinda kubw’ivyizigiro, ryuzuye urukundo ruhinguye, iryo niryo ryamaze kurama ./.Ritegereje kuruhurwa imibabaro y’igihe gito./.

Refrain
