#84

Abahanuzi barahanura.

Umuco

Couplet 1

Abahanuzi barahanura, abavuga Butumwa barabuvuga ngo twitegure; ./.Ni twumve Intumwa z’Umwami Yesu./.

Refrain

Eh’umusi urashitse ah’abizera bazoronka ivyo bizeye, n’umunsi w’akanyamuneza, ah’abizeye bazokwambikw’ impuzu zera n’ibitsibo mu mitwe yabo, ./.Nimuze rero tumwiteg’Umwami./.
