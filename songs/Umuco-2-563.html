#563

Umv’inzamba yo gutegerez’ umukwe

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Umv’inzamba yo gutegerez’ Umukwe yamaze kuvuga, Ngw’ikangure mutegerez’ Umukwe, We Mugen’ ikangure witegure nguy’ Umukw’ Araje, Isezerano rigiye gutaha.

Refrain

{Ko wihangany’ ukaguma mu Kwizera Isezerano riratashe.} x2

Couplet 2

Umwami Yesu Yatanz’ Isezerano Agiye kurishitsa, kuko Wewe ntiyivuguruza. Abihangana bakagumy’ ukwizera kugeza kw’iherezo, Ni bo bazomusanganir’ Umukwe.

Refrain

{We Mugeni ambar’ Impuzu zera Umukw’Araje ageze ku Rugi.} x2

Couplet 3

Shir’amavuta mu Matara yanyu bicitw’uyu musi, Kukw’ imburi yaramaze kuvuga, Kukw’ata gihe co kugur’ Amavuta kizoba kikiriho, Kukw’ Umukwe Azob’ Amaze kuza.

Refrain

{Abiteguye bazomusanganira, hanyum’ Urugi ruzoca, rukingwa.} x2

Couplet 4

Har’abazoja kugur’ Amavuta, Umukw’ Amaze kuza, Bazosanga bamaze kwugaranwa. Hari n’abandi bamaze kwitegura n’amatar’ araka, n’Amavuta mu macupa yabo.

Refrain

{None Mugenzi, wob’ufis’ Amavuta? N’uyagure umwany’ ukiriho!} x2
