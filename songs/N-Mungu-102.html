#102

Munganga amekuja hapa

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Munganga amekuja hapa, ndiye Bwana Yesu; Anaponyesha roho zetu kwa ugonjwa wote.

Refrain

Jina lake linapita kila Jina duniani; Jina kubwa, Jina jema, Yesu, Bwana Yesu.

Couplet 2

Anasamehe zambi zetu kama tukitubu; Anatukaribisha kwake kwa rehema kubwa.

Refrain

Jina lake linapita kila Jina duniani; Jina kubwa, Jina jema, Yesu, Bwana Yesu.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Jina lake linapita kila Jina duniani; Jina kubwa, Jina jema, Yesu, Bwana Yesu.

Refrain

Jina lake linapita kila Jina duniani; Jina kubwa, Jina jema, Yesu, Bwana Yesu.

Couplet 5

Refrain

Jina lake linapita kila Jina duniani; Jina kubwa, Jina jema, Yesu, Bwana Yesu.

Couplet 6

Refrain

Jina lake linapita kila Jina duniani; Jina kubwa, Jina jema, Yesu, Bwana Yesu.
