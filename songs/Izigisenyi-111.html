#111

Hari imisozi

Iz'i Gisenyi

Couplet 1

Hari imisozi iruhije cyane kubwo ishyaka tuyizamuke Satani yimbi ibinogo mu nzira Abera ngo tu babigwemo.

Refrain

Muze tubinesheshe ukwirinda dusaba Umwami Atuyobore bana b’Imana dufatane mu nda imisozi tuyizamuke.x2

Couplet 2

Iyo nzira yaga kandi iruhije icam’irembo rifunganye inzira yagutse kandi yoroshye nayijana mu mahonero.

Refrain

Abanzi b’Umwami n’Umusaraba bo bagenda bajandajanda inzira Ijyana mu Bwami bwa Yesu Abera nibo bayicamo.x2

Couplet 3

Muri iyo nzira intare ziraziritse ngenzi ntizibatere ubwoba amabingira y’iyi si atontomye tumenye ko dushigikiwe.

Refrain

Yewe ngenzi rema mu mitima Yesu Yaranesheje ikuzimu bitayeko Yaratsinze n’urupfu umwanzi w’Abizera bose.x2

Couplet 4

Igihugu twamaze kugitata dusanga kogiciy’ibibatsi twasanze harimo n’ivyama vyiza hamwe n’inzuzi z’Ubugingo.

Refrain

Yesu Kristo niwe Mwami waco mbere ni nawe bwiza bwaco Abizera batavug’inkuru mbi bonyene bazocinjiramo.x2

Couplet 5

Tumwizigire Uhoraho wenyene ntaziger’Aduhemukira teg’ugutwi abavugishwa nawe ntahandi harihw’agakiza.

Refrain

Tuzorya butsim’ibigatanya kuko twubatse ku rutare tuzeguker’ amarembo Y’abanzi Uhorah’ aduh’Amahoro.x2

Couplet 6

Tubere maso ubugingo bwacu Yesu Ari hafi kugaruka tuzirikir’imitima k’Uhoraho igihe duca muriy’isi.

Refrain

Dukenyeze ukwicisha bugufi tugendere mu Kwizera irembo ry’Ijuru Riruguruwe kwiherezo ry’ibiruhije.x2
