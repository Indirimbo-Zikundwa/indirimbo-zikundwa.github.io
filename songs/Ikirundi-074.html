#74

Mfis’ Umukiz’ Amvugira mw’Ijuru

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mfis’ Umukiz’ Amvugira mw’Ijuru, n’Umukunzi mwiz’ Arut’abo mw’isi, Anzigama neza kand’ Ambungabunga, icomp’ uwo Mukiz’ Akab’uwawe.

Refrain

./.Ni wewe nsengera, ni wewe nsengera, mugenzi, ndakwifuriz’ Agakiza../.

Couplet 2

Mfise na Data wa twese mw’Ijuru, Yanshoboje kwizigir’ ibizoba. Azohav’ Andengukisha mw’Ijuru i We, icompa nawe tukazojanayo.

Refrain

./.Ni wewe nsengera, ni wewe nsengera, mugenzi, ndakwifuriz’ Agakiza./.

Couplet 3

Mfisey’ imvune nziz’ ikayangana nabikiwe mu Bgiza bgo mw’Ijuru, iyo mvune yer’ aho nzoyambarira, icompa naw’ ukazoyironkayo.

Refrain

./.Ni wewe nsengera, ni wewe nsengera, mugenzi, ndakwifuriz’ Agakiza./.

Couplet 4

Mfis’Amahoro ameze nk’ uruzi, n’amahor’ atazwi n’abantu b’isi, Umukiza wanje ni We Soko yayo, mugenz’ icomp’ ukayaronka nawe.

Refrain

./.Ni wewe nsengera, ni wewe nsengera, mugenzi, ndakwifuriz’ Agakiza./.

Couplet 5

Ubonye Yes’ Amaze kugukiza, ntiwigungan’ ivyo Yagukoreye, babgire k’Umukiza wanj’ ar’uwawe, basengere nk’uko nagusengeye.

Refrain

./.Ni wewe nsengera, ni wewe nsengera, mugenzi, ndakwifuriz’ Agakiza./.
