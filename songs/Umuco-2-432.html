Dufis’ishimwe ewe muvyeyi

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Dufis’ishimwe ewe Muvyeyi wacu, Ko waturinze mu bihe bigoye, Satani yar’afis’ imihigo mibi, N’ushimwe Yesu ko Wamunesheje!

Refrain

Warabiravye, Warabiravye, Uducira Inzira. Warabiravye, Warabiravye, Waduhay’ umwidegemvyo.

Couplet 2

Bamwe muri twe baramerewe nabi, Ariko Mana waradutabaye, Twagarukiye ku cobo c’urupfu, Ariko Yesu Waradukunze cane.

Refrain

Warabiravye, Warabiravye, Uducira Inzira. Warabiravye, Warabiravye, Waduhay’ umwidegemvyo.

Couplet 3

Abera bawe Mana bakwemangiye, bamw’ingo zabo ziracaboshwe, Kubw’ Urukundo warabaremesheje, N’ugir’ ubunt’ubah’ umunezero!

Refrain

Ko Wabarinze bakihangana, N’ubohore ingo zabo, Bazogushima bavuz’ impundu, Bat’ «Uhoraho n’Ashimwe»!

Couplet 4

Ewe Yesu n’udukiz’ivyago vyose, n’imitego yose y’Umugwanizi, Hab’iyo mu ngo canke n’iyindi yose, Dufashe Yesu tuzoshik’ Iwawe.

Refrain

Ah’Abizera, ah’Abizera, Tuzokwambikw’ Igitsibo, Tuzoririmba Haleluya, Tuzoruhuk’ imiruho.
