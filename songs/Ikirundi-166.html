Ngwino Soko y’umugisha

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Ngwino Soko y’umugisha mp’umutim’ ugushima, Migez’ idakama myiza, Tugutazire cane. Nyigisha kwama ngusenga Ngo mmeny’ ubgiza bgawe, Nizigir’ ubgami bgawe, Unyuzuz’ urukundo.

Couplet 2

Ibikuru wankoreye Uhor’ ubinyibutsa, Nizigiye k’uzonjana, Ukanshitsa mw ijuru. ; Wandondeye nazimiye, Nari kure y’Imana; Kandi ng’ uncungur’ urupfu, Wemeye kumbambirwa.

Couplet 3

Ndi mu mwenda wawe Mwami, W’imbabaz’ ungiriye; Unyigize hafi yawe. Mmeny’ urukundo rwawe. Ndafis’ ibinyosha nabi, Bigomba kukunyaka; Akir’ umutima wanje, Unzigam’ unkize pe.
