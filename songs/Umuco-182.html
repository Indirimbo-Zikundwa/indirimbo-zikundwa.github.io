Yesu n’umwami w’urukundo rwinshi

Umuco

Couplet 1

Yesu n’Umwami w’Urukundo rwinshi, kuko Yikoreye imibabaro yacu; Yajanjaguriwe ivyacu bigabitanyo, igihano cacu cabaye kuri we.

Refrain

N’ukuri, n’izacu ntimba Yishizeko, n’iyacu mibabaro Yikoreye, tweho twamwiyumviriye nk’uwakubiswe, nk’uwacumiswe n’Imana, akicurikirizwa n’akabonge, kubw’ ivyaha vyacu; ajanjaguriw’ ivyacu bigabitanyo, n’urukundo rw’imbura rugero ./.Kand’ imishishagu yiwe ni yo idukiza./.
