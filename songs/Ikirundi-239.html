#239

Uko ba banyabgenge baravye ya nyenyeri

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Uko ba banyabgenge baravye ya nyenyeri, Bakayikurikira N’ umunezero mwinshi, Natwe dukunde durtyo Kurab’ Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 2

Baciye batebuka Kuronder’uwo Mwana, Baramupfukamira, Ni we Mwami w’ ijuru: Natwe tumurondere, Tumubon’ Umukiza.

Couplet 3

Baramushikanira, Bamukurir’ ubgatsi, Baramurabukira, Asumb’ ab’ isi bose: Natwe tugire durtyo, Duhe Yes’ imitima.

Couplet 4

Yes’Uwera w’ Imana, Tuzigamtre rwose Mu nzira yawe nziza, Hanyum’ uz’ utujane Mu bgami bgo mw ijuru, Ahadacur’ umwiza.

Couplet 5

Abo mw’Ijuru bose nta matara bagomba, Ni wewe muco waho N’ izuba ritarenga: Yes’ uz’ utujaneyo, Tuguhimbaze rwose.
