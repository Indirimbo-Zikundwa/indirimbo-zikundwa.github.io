#415

Uburuhukiro bw’isabato

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Uburuhukiro bw’Isabato, Bubikiw’abana b’Imana, Abo kuruhuk’imitwaro yose-e-e, Abo kuruhuk’ubutame, Batamiye mur’iyi si, Bakiboner’ uwababambiwe: Yesu.

Refrain

Natwe ico twahamagariwe: Twahamagariwe kub’Abera. Ni naco gituma twumva, Rya Jambo ry’Urukundo, Ridutakisha ngo “Aba Dataaa” Ritwemeza ko Yes’ari We Rukundo.

Couplet 2

Intama zo mu mukuku, Zari zarasabagiye, Ubu zumvis’ijwi ry’Umwungere-e-e, Ziriko ziragaruka mu ruhongore rwazo, Zigomba gusangira Ijambo ryiwe.

Refrain

Couplet 3

Umwungere w’umukuku, N’Umwami Yesu Kristo, Yikwegeyekw’amahanga yose-e-e, Maze zumvis’ijwi ryiwe, zica ziraryitaba, Zigomba gusangira Ijambo ryiwe.

Refrain

Couplet 4
