Bakristo, ve hasi

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Bakristo, ve hasi yemwe twitegure neza, Abenshi bibaza yuko Yesu atebaganye.

Refrain

Uhorah’Imana yacu, Ntatebej’isezerano, Arikw’agoomba ko bose, Bihan’ivyaha vyabo.

Couplet 2

Abenshi ntibabon’ibimenyetso yavuze, Bibaza yukw ivyo yavuze bitarashika.

Refrain

Uhorah’Imana yacu, Ntatebej’isezerano, Arikw’agoomba ko bose, Bihan’ivyaha vyabo.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Uhorah’Imana yacu, Ntatebej’isezerano, Arikw’agoomba ko bose, Bihan’ivyaha vyabo.

Couplet 4

Refrain

Uhorah’Imana yacu, Ntatebej’isezerano, Arikw’agoomba ko bose, Bihan’ivyaha vyabo.

Couplet 5

Hahiriw’abamesur’ imvune zabo neza, Ngo babone kwegera ca giti c’Ubugingo.

Refrain

Uhorah’Imana yacu, Ntatebej’isezerano, Arikw’agoomba ko bose, Bihan’ivyaha vyabo.
