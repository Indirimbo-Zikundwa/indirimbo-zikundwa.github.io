Imana yamy’ar’imana

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Imana yamy’ar’ Imana, kuva ibihe vyose. Abahanuzi barahishurirwa, bakabwir’ abantu yuko har’Imana, arikw’ abantu bamye ar’abantu, kuko vyabagora kuyizera vy’ukuri.

Refrain

Imana Yaradukunze, isi n’ijuru bitarabaho, kuko mu gihe gikwiye Yaratugendeye, Iratwihishurira mw’Ijambo ryayo Rizima, Iduha kuryizera, Itubwira ko harih’ umusi, Izoza kudutora.
