#55

Yes’Araduhamagara kurek’ ivyaha vyose

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yes’Araduhamagara kurek’ ivyaha vyose, Atubgir’ imisi yose ngo tumukurikire.

Couplet 2

Tumere nka ba barovyi bumvis’ ivyo Yavuze, bagaca basig’ ivyabo, Bakamukurikira.

Couplet 3

Atubuza kwifuz’ ivyo Mw isi n’ icubahiro, Azoduh’ ibiduhaza, Ni twamukurikira.

Couplet 4

Naho twoba duhimbawe, canke turi mu vyago, Yes’ araduhamagara Ngo tumukurikire.

Couplet 5

Yes’ araduhamagara Ngo tumugendanire, Tumukunde tumusenge, Kugeza ku guhwera.
