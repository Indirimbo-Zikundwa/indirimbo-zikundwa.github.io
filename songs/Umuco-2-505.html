Umwami Yesu yaduhay’isezerano

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Umwami Yesu Yaduhay’ Isezerano Ry’ukw’Azoza kutujana mw’Ijuru i We Kugira ngw’ah’ari natw’ab’ ariho tuba Tumwizigire kuko ntahinduka.

Refrain

Couplet 2

Refrain

{Iyadusezeraniye Ntiyivuguruza, kuko ntabay’ umuntu, ngo Yivuguruze.} x2

Couplet 3

Refrain

{Iyadusezeraniye Ntiyivuguruza, kuko ntabay’ umuntu, ngo Yivuguruze.} x2

Couplet 4

Mu Rugendo hama har’ingorane nyinshi, Imana vyayihimbaye kuziducishamwo, na bene Data batanguy’uru Rugendo, bagiye bagwan’ Intambara nyinshi.

Refrain

{Iyadusezeraniye Ntiyivuguruza, kuko ntabay’ umuntu, ngo Yivuguruze.} x2

Couplet 5

Daniyeli yatawe mu rwobo rw’intambwe, Shaduraki, Meshaki na Abedi Nego, nabo batawe mw’itanure ry’umuriro, baravyemera kuber’ Ukwizera.

Refrain

{Iyadusezeraniye Ntiyivuguruza, kuko ntabay’ umuntu, ngo Yivuguruze.} x2

Couplet 6

Ni twashika mw’Ijuru mu Bwami bwa Data, Tuzonezererwa kubana n’Umwami, Dufise n’Ishimwe ryinshi mu mitima ry’ukw’ Adusohorej’ Isezerano.

Refrain

{Iyadusezeraniye Ntiyivuguruza, kuko ntabay’ umuntu, ngo Yivuguruze.} x2
