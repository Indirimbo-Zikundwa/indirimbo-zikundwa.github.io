#641

Ndashaka gukurikira

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Ndashaka gukurikira Yesu Krist’Umwami wanje, Ariko har’ibingora, Ndazi na vyo nzobitsinda.

Refrain

{Ni Wanshoboza Mana, Ndazi yuko nzobitsinda.} x2

Couplet 2

Ehe Mwami wanje Yesu, Ub’imitego ni myinshi, Ndiko mbona mu Rugendo, Nshoboza ndarubandanye.

Refrain

{N’ubwo mbona bitoroshe, N’unzigamire mw’Ijambo.} x2

Couplet 3

Warabanye na Dawidi: Igihe yagwa mu caha, Ntabwo Wamuhebeyeyo, Waciye umuharira.

Refrain

{Yongera gushing’intahe, Yemeza k’Ur’Uhoraho.} x2

Couplet 4

Petero yabanye nawe: Igihe yakwihakana, Ariko ku bw’Urukundo, Wamugiz’ Intumwa Yawe.

Refrain

{Yamamaz’Ijambo Ryawe, Nanje mpa kugukorera.} x2

Couplet 5

Waravuze ko Uwawe, N’aho yogw’Uzomuvyura, Kw’atazozimir’ ukundi, Kandi Uzomutabara.

Refrain

{N’unshoboze Mwami Yesu, Sinze nkor’icah’ ukundi.} x2

Couplet 6

Ubu Mana ndakwinginze, Umparir’ ivyaha vyanje, Kuko nd’umwe mu Bizera, Nze mbashe gushik’Iwawe.

Refrain

{N’unkoreshe ivy’Ushaka, Mbashe kugukurikira.} x2
