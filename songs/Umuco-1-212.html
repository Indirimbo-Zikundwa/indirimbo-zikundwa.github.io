#212

Nagize Yesu ubuhungiro

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Nagize Yesu ubuhungiro, Ni We nshuti idahinduka, Ni We yampaye ubugingo, Ancungurira ku musaraba.

Refrain

{Ndagushimiye Yesu, Ntuy’umutima wanje, Mubiri wanje korera Yesu.} x2

Couplet 2

Yesu ni We yandokoye, Muri benshi bafashwe mpiri, Umuyaga warahuhuse, We yampishe mu mababa yiwe.

Refrain

{Yesu ntahinduka, Asohoz’Ijambo Rye, Atsindanishiriz’uwo yamenye.} x2

Couplet 3

Umutima wanj’uvuz’inanga, Yo gucurarangira Yesu, Akanwa kanje kavuz’impundu, Reka mukomer’amashi n’Umwami.

Refrain

{Ni We yankuye kure, Anzura mu bapfuye, Haleluy’Izina Rye rirakiza.} x2

Couplet 4

Yesu n’Izuba mu mwijima, N’Umuco w’umutima wanje, N’umugaba mu Ntambara zose, N’igihome c’Urutare rwanje.

Refrain

{Ni We nihishamwo, N’isoko nziza idakama, Yes’amar’imibabaro yose.} x2

Couplet 5

Nshoboza name nguhimbara, Untsindir’ibigeragezo, Undememw’Ijambo mbe musha, N’inkota yanje n’inkinzo yanje.

Refrain

{Ni nanesha vyose, Yesu n’agaruka, Azomp’ubugingo buhoraho.} x2
