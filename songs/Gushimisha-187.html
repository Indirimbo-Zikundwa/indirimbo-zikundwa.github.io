#187

Ngwino Mwami wacu Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ngwino, Mwami wacu Yesu, ngwin’Ugume muri jye: Mpatwe n’Urukund’iteka kujya ngukurikira.

Couplet 2

Umutima wanjye wose, wuzur’Ijambo ryawe, mbone kunesha Satani n’imigambiye yose.

Couplet 3

Amahoro yawe, Mwami, ajy’aguma muri jye, anyifurishe gufasha abanyabyago bose.

Couplet 4

Urukundo rwawe rwinshi, rusandare muri jye. Naho natukwa, nkagawa, ruzabineshereza.

Couplet 5

No mu Ntambara njya ndwana, nkubitana n’Umwanzi, nzanesha, ntumbira Yesu Wamunesheje kera.

Couplet 6

Ubwiza bwawe, Mukiza, bungot’impande zose, bikureba jyew’ahubwo barebe Wowe musa.
