#647

Nkundira Mwami Yesu

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Nkundira Mwami Yesu name ngutazira, Ijambo ryawe Mwami Rimber’ Umuco, Uyu mutima wanje wam’ugutazira, Zaburi nsha y’ishimwe Wame Uyindonsa.

Refrain

{Nzokwama nkwizigira Mukiza Yesu, Ndizera k’Ur’Ijambo, Ukuri n’Umuco, Ntuziger’ Umaramaz’ Abakwizeye.} x2

Couplet 2

Ijambo ryawe Yesu n’Urih’ ikibanza mw’uyu mutima wanje, Satan’anje kure, Kukw’ah’ Ijambo riri hama har’Umuco, Satani ntahegera, yikundir’ Umwiza.

Refrain

{Nzokwama nkwizigira Mukiza Yesu, Ndizera k’Ur’Ijambo, Ukuri n’Umuco, Ntuziger’ Umaramaz’ Abakwizeye.} x2

Couplet 3

Duhe guheba vyose tugukurikire, Ntitwiziguriz’ ibibi tuvyit’ ivyiza, Kandi imbere Yawe ari ibisesema, Duhe kwumvir’ Ijambo Ryawe muri vyose.

Refrain

{Nzokwama nkwizigira Mukiza Yesu, Ndizera k’Ur’Ijambo, Ukuri n’Umuco, Ntuziger’ Umaramaz’ Abakwizeye.} x2

Couplet 4

Intambwe y’Igikorwa cawe Mwami Yesu, Ah’igeze bamwe basa n’abananirwa, Ukwumva no kwumvir’ Ijambo bikagora, Duhe guhishurirwa Igikorwa cawe.

Refrain

{Nzokwama nkwizigira Mukiza Yesu, Ndizera k’Ur’Ijambo, Ukuri n’Umuco, Ntuziger’ Umaramaz’ Abakwizeye.} x2

Couplet 5

Nogira gute jewe sinze mmaramare, Ndizera Mwami Yes’Ur’ Umutananirwa, Ubushobozi Bwawe ni bwo ndagusaba, Ibango ry’Igikorwa Cawe risozerwe.

Refrain

{Nzokwama nkwizigira Mukiza Yesu, Ndizera k’Ur’Ijambo, Ukuri n’Umuco, Ntuziger’ Umaramaz’ Abakwizeye.} x2
