#70

Nimuze mwese kumv’ inkuru nziza

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Nimuze mwese kumv’ inkuru nziza z’Agakiza kabwirizwa none. Yesu Yabasanze: Arabashaka; Namwe muh’Umwami imitima.

Couplet 2

Mwitinyishwa n’uko mufit’ibyaha, Ngw’azabang’ abater’umugongo; Ntabwo Yabigira: Yasezeranye kwakir’ uwihanny’ umwizera.

Couplet 3

Ni W’ubwe wizanye gukiz’ abantu; Yavuye ku Witek’aza mw’isi. Ngo tubon’Ijuru, Yaradukunze; Yaratubambiwe; Yaravumwe!

Couplet 4

Abwira bos’ati: Jye nd’Ubugingo, kand’unyizera wese ntazapfa. Nzamuha kubaho mu Bwami bwanjye; N’urupfu nzamuha kurunesha.
