Couplet 1

Refrain

Yesu Mwami n’utuzigame, turongore mu ngendo zacu.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Yesu Mwami n’utuzigame, turongore mu ngendo zacu.

Couplet 4

Refrain

Yesu Mwami n’utuzigame, turongore mu ngendo zacu.
