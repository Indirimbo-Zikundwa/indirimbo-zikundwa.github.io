Bene data nkunda

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Bene Data nkunda, mukoraniy’aha Nimushim’Imana. Kuko yaduhaye, kubon’uyu musi tugihumeka.

Refrain

Nari mugatengo, nari nihebuye Mfise n’ibibazo, Satani yanteje Arik’Umukiza, kuko we yankunze, Yaciy’Abinkurako.

Couplet 2

Refrain

Nari mugatengo, nari nihebuye Mfise n’ibibazo, Satani yanteje Arik’Umukiza, kuko we yankunze, Yaciy’Abinkurako.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Nari mugatengo, nari nihebuye Mfise n’ibibazo, Satani yanteje Arik’Umukiza, kuko we yankunze, Yaciy’Abinkurako.

Couplet 4

Refrain

Nari mugatengo, nari nihebuye Mfise n’ibibazo, Satani yanteje Arik’Umukiza, kuko we yankunze, Yaciy’Abinkurako.
