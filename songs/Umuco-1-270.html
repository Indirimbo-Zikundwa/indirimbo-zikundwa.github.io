#270

Har’umuryango wuguruye

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Har’umuryango wuguruye, Ntawushobora kuwugara, N’iyo wugaye ntawugurura, Nyenewo n’Umunyenkomezi.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango Mwiza, Ewe Mwungere Mwiza, Abaguciyeko bose, Bafis’Ubugingo budashira, Kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 2

Uwo ni w’intama zicamwo, Iyo zigiye mu canya, N’iyo zigiye mu buraro, Ego n’Umuryango w’intama.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango Mwiza, Ewe Mwungere Mwiza, Abaguciyeko bose, Bafis’Ubugingo budashira, Kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 3

Ayandi masho ntawucamwo, N’iy’ageragej’ arananirwa, Kuk’umuryango n’uw’abumva, Ijwi ry’Umwungere w’intama.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango Mwiza, Ewe Mwungere Mwiza, Abaguciyeko bose, Bafis’Ubugingo budashira, Kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 4

Uwo muryango ni Yesu Kristo, Kandi n’Umwungere w’intama, Ntawinjir’atari kuri We, Kukw’ari W’az’intama ziwe.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango Mwiza, Ewe Mwungere Mwiza, Abaguciyeko bose, Bafis’Ubugingo budashira, Kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 5

Ni W’aj’imbere y’umukuku, Akaziyobora mu canya, Kandi ni We azij’inyuma, Ngw’azirinde umwansi mubi.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango Mwiza, Ewe Mwungere Mwiza, Abaguciyeko bose, Bafis’Ubugingo budashira, Kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.

Couplet 6

Mwene Data emera ub’intama, N’iyiragirwa igacurwa, Mu muryango ayandi masho, Agejeje arananirwa.

Refrain

Ewe Muryango Mwiza, Ewe Mwungere Mwiza, Abaguciyeko bose, Bafis’Ubugingo budashira, Kuko bumvis’ijwi ryawe.
