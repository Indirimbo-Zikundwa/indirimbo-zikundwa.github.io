#292

Ku musaraba w’umwami

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Ku Musaraba w’Umwami Yesu, Ni ho naboneye intsinzi, Kuko ca cangera c’icaha, Ari co c’urupfu camvako, {Ni co gituma ndirimba.} x2

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, Isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, Uyo n’Umunyembabazi, Kuko umusi nzomubona, Nzoruhuka imiruho.

Couplet 2

Ku Musaraba w’Umukiza wanje, Ni ho kwa gutandukana kwari, Hagati yanje n’Imana, Kwavaho ubutagisubira, {Ni co gituma mfis’amahoro.} x2

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, Isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, Uyo n’Umunyembabazi, Kuko umusi nzomubona, Nzoruhuka imiruho.

Couplet 3

Ku Musaraba w’Umucunguzi, Ni ho n’Abera basinziriye, Baronkeye intsinzi vy’ukuri, Bakabohorwa bakanesha, {Ni co gituma tuzobabona.} x2

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, Isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, Uyo n’Umunyembabazi, Kuko umusi nzomubona, Nzoruhuka imiruho.

Couplet 4

Uwo Musaraba ndawukunda, Kuko wampaye kuzoragwa, Ubugingo budashira, Nkaba niteze no kuzobona, {Umwami imbona nkubone.} x2

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, Isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, Uyo n’Umunyembabazi, Kuko umusi nzomubona, Nzoruhuka imiruho.
