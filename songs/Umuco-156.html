#156

Uhereye muri egiputa

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uhereye muri Egiputa, duhagurukijwe n’Uhoraho, ntiyigeze kuduhemukira, inkingi y’umuriro yari imbere yacu.

Refrain

Uhoraho ntiyigeze guhemuka, ico Yakunze Yaragikunze; n’umuvyeyi adahemuka, yihanganira vyose.

Couplet 2

Twebw’ ubura bw’ Imana Ihoraho, abakowe n’Umwami Yesu, ntakizi tambika imbere, ndazi ko tuzoshika amahoro.

Refrain

Uhoraho ntiyigeze guhemuka, ico Yakunze Yaragikunze; n’umuvyeyi adahemuka, Yihanganira vyose.

Couplet 3

None ko twakowe n’Umugabo umwe, ariwe Kristo Yabambwe, tumwishikanire rwose, ntazigera Aduhemukira namba.

Refrain

Uhoraho ntiyigeze guhemuka, ico Yakunze Yaragikunze; n’umuvyeyi adahemuka, Yihanganira vyose.

Couplet 4

Ingorane ni nyinshi cane Kristo arabibona vyose, gir’ ivy’ izigiro n’ukwizera, Ubwami bw’ Imana buregereje.

Refrain

Uhoraho ntiyigeze guhemuka, ico Yakunze Yaragikunze; n’umuvyeyi adahemuka, Yihanganira vyose.

Couplet 5

N’irihe hanga na rimwe, Uhoraho atigeze kunesha; iy’imbere harih’ ivyiza, Iyasezeranye ntiyivuguruza.

Refrain

Uhoraho ntiyigeze guhemuka, ico Yakunze Yaragikunze; n’umuvyeyi adahemuka, Yihanganira vyose.

Couplet 6

Hanyuma y’ibigeragezo, k’uwutadendebukiwe, hama har’ umunezero, Yesu n’Umugabo adahemuka.

Refrain

Uhoraho ntiyigeze guhemuka, ico Yakunze Yaragikunze; n’umuvyeyi adahemuka, Yihanganira vyose.
