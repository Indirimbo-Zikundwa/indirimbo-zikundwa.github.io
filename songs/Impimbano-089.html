Reka nshimir’ umwami Yesu

Impimbano· Sr Jeannine

Couplet 1

Reka nshimir’Umwami Yesu, Yankunz’ Urukundo rutangaje. Yangiz’ Umwana wiwe nyakuri, non’ubu ndi murugo kwa Data.

Couplet 2

Mfis’umunezero mwinshi cane, kuko Yankuyeko imitwaro, yanje yose yar’indemereye, nimuze mumfashe tumushime.

Couplet 3

Yesu n’Umukunzi mwiza cane, Aba hafi y’abaremerewe, abarushe nimuze kuri We, Abaruhur’ imitwaro yose.

Couplet 4

None Yesu nyonger’ inkomezi, mpore nkor’ ibikunezereza. Uzompe no kurangiza neza, nzobeho ibihe bidashira.
