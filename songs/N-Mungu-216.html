#216

Ninasikia muchungaji

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Ninasikia muchungaji anayeita toka jangwa, Akiwaita wapotevu kama kondoo bila zizi.

Refrain

Uwalete, uwalete, uwalete toka zambi zao; Uwalete, uwalete, uwalete kwake Yesu.

Couplet 2

Nani ‘takwenda kusaidia kuwatafuta wapotevu, Kuwaletea Muchungaji, awaokoe toka zambi?

Refrain

Uwalete, uwalete, uwalete toka zambi zao; Uwalete, uwalete, uwalete kwake Yesu.

Couplet 3

Katika jangwa wanalia na kwa vilima na mabonde; Bwana anakuita wewe: “Kwenda kuwafikisha kwangu.”

Refrain

Uwalete, uwalete, uwalete toka zambi zao; Uwalete, uwalete, uwalete kwake Yesu.
