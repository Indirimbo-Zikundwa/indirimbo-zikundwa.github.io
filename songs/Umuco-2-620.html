#620

Mbega ncuti yanje wibaza iki?

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Mbega ncuti yanje wibaza iki ku vya Yesu? Ko mbona uyu musi waryohewe n’ivy’iyi si, uvug’uti nta wundi munezero nzobona; Nta mahor’ uzoronka mu vy’isi biguhenda.

Refrain

Nuze kwa Yesu Agukize ivyaha vyawe, Muri We ni ho Turonker’ Uburuhukiro, Ǔmwizera wese ntazotsindwa n’Urubanza, nshut’ uzob’ugowe Yes’Asanz’ utiteguye.

Couplet 2

Umwami Araba ko ku giti hari isukoni, Maz’Azibuze Arakivuma gica kiruma. Maz’ utamy’imbuto zikwiranye no kwihana, uzoc’ urungikwa mu gihano kidashira.

Refrain

Abizeye bose ni ryo Shengero, Umwami Yes’Akab’ Umwungere; Tuzobona Abera bose, Abari mu bihugu vyose, Abana b’Imana hose barahari.

Couplet 3

Ncuti ugikizwa war’ufise urukundo rwinshi, Noneho uyu musi ho rwaramaze gutimba. Uvuga uti nta wundi munezero uzobona; Nta mahor’ uzoronka mu vy’isi biguhenda.

Refrain

Abizeye bose ni ryo Shengero, Umwami Yes’Akab’ Umwungere; Tuzobona Abera bose, Abari mu bihugu vyose, Abana b’Imana hose barahari.

Couplet 4

Umwami Yesu ni We Mana Imwe twizeye, Ni We Yatwitangiye Aja ku Musaraba. Nta yindi Mana iriho, eka nta n’iyizoza, Ni Yesu Mwami; Ni W’Ahorah’ ibihe vyose.

Refrain

Abizeye bose ni ryo Shengero, Umwami Yes’Akab’ Umwungere; Tuzobona Abera bose, Abari mu bihugu vyose, Abana b’Imana hose barahari.

Couplet 5

Yesu ni We Ntango, Ubugingo n’Amahoro, Ni We Rutare n’Ibuye rigumy’ Imfuruka. Uwubatse kuri We ntasegenyeshwa namba; Azomera nk’igiti gitewe ku migezi.

Refrain

Abizeye bose ni ryo Shengero, Umwami Yes’Akab’ Umwungere; Tuzobona Abera bose, Abari mu bihugu vyose, Abana b’Imana hose barahari.

Couplet 6

Tandukane n’ibibi tumatane n’Ijambo, Tumenye neza yuko vyose bizokurwaho, Arikw’Ijambo ry’Umwami rizokwamaho; Hahirw’ uwumatana n’Ijambo, azohoraho.

Refrain

Abizeye bose ni ryo Shengero, Umwami Yes’Akab’ Umwungere; Tuzobona Abera bose, Abari mu bihugu vyose, Abana b’Imana hose barahari.
