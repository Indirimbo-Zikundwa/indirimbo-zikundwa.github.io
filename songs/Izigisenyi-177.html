#177

Bwana wewe

Iz'i Gisenyi

Couplet 1

Bwana wewe unajua kwamba mimi ni muzayifu nguvu zangu akili na anafsi havinipi kushinda, naogopa kukuacha wewe ili nisiangamiye, nimeona uzuri wako Yesu ninapenda uzima wako.

Refrain

Naomba:/ naomba Bwana unisikie ninapo kulilia Naomba:/ naomba Bwana nijaze roho ninapo jaribiwa.x2 Naomba Bwana Unisikie ninapo kulilia.

Couplet 2

Bwana wewe unajua kwamba naweza danganyika duniani kumejaa mapepo mengi na mafundisho ya Shetani macho yangu yanatazama na kuyatama aniya masikiyo yanaa sikiliza nataka kudanganyika.

Refrain

Naomba:/ naomba Bwana unisikie ninapo kulilia Naomba:/ naomba Bwana nijaze roho ninapo jaribiwa.x2 Naomba Bwana Unisikie ninapo kulilia.

Couplet 3

Bwana wewe unajua kwamba tupo majaribuni siku zote tupende tusipopenda twasumbuka vitani Shetani anapanga vita usiku na mchana ula nasi tuna mshinda kwanguvu zako wewe.

Refrain

Naomba:/ naomba Bwana unisikie ninapo kulilia Naomba:/ naomba Bwana nijaze roho ninapo jaribiwa.x2 Naomba Bwana Unisikie ninapo kulilia.

Couplet 4

Dunia zoruba napepo nyingi zinani piganisha, shida nyingi magonjwa na umaskini vinani piganisha, makusudi ya Shetani ni kwamba nikane Imani yangu nimeona uzuri wako Yesu na siwezi kukuacha.

Refrain

Naomba:/ naomba Bwana unisikie ninapo kulilia Naomba:/ naomba Bwana nijaze roho ninapo jaribiwa.x2 Naomba Bwana Unisikie ninapo kulilia.
