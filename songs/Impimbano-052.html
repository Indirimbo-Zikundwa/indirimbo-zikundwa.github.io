#52

Nitumara kwambuka

Impimbano· Fr NGARAMBE

Refrain

{Nimubona tumaze kwambuka mwitegure ibizakurikira.} x2

Couplet 1

Wanze gukizwa ngo umaramaze,witegure ibizakurikira, ntagutinya no kubaha ugira, witegure ibizakurikira,We munyabyaha wanze kwihana witegure ibizakurikira, urabwirwa ukavuna ijosi, witegure ibizakurikira.

Refrain

{Nimubona tumaze kwambuka mwitegure ibizakurikira.} x2

Couplet 2

Wowe usubira iyo waturutse, witegure ibizakurikira,hindukira usubire munzira ukurikire uwagucunguye,tuti "Yesu azagaruka vuba" muti "reka uko niko byahoze"; tuti "azaza gutwara itorero "; muti reka uko niko byahoze".

Refrain

{Nimubona tumaze kwambuka mwitegure ibizakurikira.} x2

Couplet 3

Ibyo amaso yacu atarabona nibyo twebwe abera tubikiwe,ibitazamutse mumutima nibyo twebwe abera tubikiwe, ibyo tutumvishije aya matwi nibyo twebwe abera tubikiwe.

Refrain

{Nimubona tumaze kwambuka mwitegure ibizakurikira.} x2

Couplet 4

Halleluya halleluya halleluya, mwitegure ibizakurikira, tuzazamurwa tujye mu Ijuru, mwitegure ibizakurikira, {ntagisibya hoya ntagihari mwitegure ibizakurikira.} x2

Refrain

{Nimubona tumaze kwambuka mwitegure ibizakurikira.} x2
