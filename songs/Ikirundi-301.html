#301

Nd’agatama ka Yesu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Nd’agatama ka Yesu, ndanezerewe cane; Mfis’ Umwungere Mwiza, Andagir’ Akankiza, Arankunda ndabizi, ndamwumv’ Ampamagaye.

Couplet 2

Uyo ni W’Anturutsa, ntanyica ntankubita, yam’Anjana buhoro, Akandagira heza, iyo ntewe n’inyota, Anshor’ amazi meza.

Couplet 3

None nta bgoba mfise, ndi mu Ntama za Yesu; Igihe ni cashika Akancura mu Rugo, nzogir’ Umunezero nta bukene kwa Yesu nzogir’ Umunezero: Nta bukene kwa Yesu.
