#373

Ijambo ry’imana n’inkinzo

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Ijambo ry’Imana n’inkinzo yacu, Mu gihe cose c’amagorwa y’aha mur’iyi si, Ijambo ry’Imana n’uburuhukiro, Bw’imitima yacu, duhore turyisunga.

Refrain

Kukw’iryo Jambo n’ubuzima bw’Abera Turisome kugira ngo tugendere mu, ntahe nzima, Dusoma mur’iryo Jambo nyene.

Couplet 2

Iyo dusom’Ijambo n’ubuzima, Hamwe n’intahe vy’Abera, dusabe Uhoraho, Kugira ngo natwe tugir’ubuzima, N’intah’Abera ba mbere bari bafise.

Refrain

Kukw’iryo Jambo n’ubuzima bw’Abera Turisome kugira ngo tugendere mu, ntahe nzima, Dusoma mur’iryo Jambo nyene.

Couplet 3

Ubuzima bw’abana b’Ihoraho, Ni ryo Jambo ry’Imana kuk’uhereye na kera, Ijambo ry’Imana ryar’ubuzima, Bw’abana b’Imana, nitubimenye neza.

Refrain

Kukw’iryo Jambo n’ubuzima bw’Abera Turisome kugira ngo tugendere mu, ntahe nzima, Dusoma mur’iryo Jambo nyene.

Couplet 4

Iy’umuvugabutumwa yigisha, Ashing’intahe y’ivyo yahishuriwe n’Imana, Natwe ku bwo kubon’intahe yiwe, Tukizera bikatuzanir’ubugingo.

Refrain

Kukw’iryo Jambo n’ubuzima bw’Abera Turisome kugira ngo tugendere mu, ntahe nzima, Dusoma mur’iryo Jambo nyene.
