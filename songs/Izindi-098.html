#98

Umusi k’uwundi

Izindi

Couplet 1

Nahisemwo gupfana na Kristo, mbaye mu vy’Ijuru nkiri aha kwisi. Mu kurindira kuza kwiwe kwiza... Ndindwa na Yesu umusi k’undi.

Refrain

Urukundo rwiwe rurandinda, buri munsi mu Mababa yiwe. Atanga Ubugingo Budashira... Nd’uwu MUKIZA iminsi yose.

Couplet 2

Nta kubabazwa atitwararika, nta mutwaro ataturemurura, nta marira adahanagura nta, n’indwara ananirwa gukiza.

Refrain

Urukundo rwiwe rurandinda, buri munsi mu mababa yiwe. Atanga Ubugingo Budashira... Nd’uwu MUKIZA iminsi yose.

Couplet 3

Iyo niha Yesu atega ugutwi, niyo nsinziriye ampora hafi, n’igihe nzosinzira ubutikoma, n’ukuboko kwe nzoja mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Urukundo rwiwe rurandinda, buri munsi mu mababa yiwe. Atanga Ubugingo Budashira... Nd’uwu MUKIZA iminsi yose.

Couplet 4

None kuki umwirengagiza, kandi ariwe vyizigiro biriho, n’Umucunguzi arahamagara, yewe urushe ngwino Akuruhure.

Refrain

Yewe munyavyaha n’umwitabe, buri munsi Arakurindira, n’igihe gito bikarangira, uzoba uhisemwo gupfa rubi.
