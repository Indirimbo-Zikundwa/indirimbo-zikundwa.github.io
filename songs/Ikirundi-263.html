#263

Mpwemu w’Uhoraho, umpumekeremwo

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mpwemu w’Uhoraho, umpumekeremwo ngo ndonk’ ubugingo busha, Butunganye rwose.

Couplet 2

Umpumekeremwo Unyez’ umutima, Ngo nkund’ ico wew’ ukunda, Nkore n’ic’ugomba.

Couplet 3

Umpumekeremwo Ngo mb’uwawe rwose, Umuriro wawe wake Mu mutima wanje.

Couplet 4

Umpumekeremwo Sinzokwigera mpfa, Nzoheza mporane nawe Ntunganye, nejejwe.
