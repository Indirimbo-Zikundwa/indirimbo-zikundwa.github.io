Inkuru nziza y’umunezero

Impimbano· Fr René

Couplet 1

Inkuru nziza y’Umunezero, Ndishimye cyane rwose, Narabohowe n’Umwami Yesu, nagizw’ umwana wayo.

Refrain

Nzahora nizer’Imana, Mbwir’ abanyumv’ ibya Yo. Mpora nezerewe cyane, Kubw’ Urukundo rwayo.

Couplet 2

Mbes’ubu wumv’ ubuz’ amahoro, Usang’ Umwami Yesu. Uhagaritsw’ umutima niki? Usang’ Umwami Yesu.

Refrain

Nzahora nizer’Imana, Mbwir’ abanyumv’ ibya Yo. Mpora nezerewe cyane, Kubw’ Urukundo rwayo.

Couplet 3

Mbes’ubabazwa n’indwara nyinshi, usang’ Umwami Yesu. Ntukiganyire, ntukitotombe, wishimir’ Inzira Ze.

Refrain

Nzahora nizer’Imana, Mbwir’ abanyumv’ ibya Yo. Mpora nezerewe cyane, Kubw’ Urukundo rwayo.

Couplet 4

Waba muzima, cyangwa mu rupfu, Araz’ abane nawe. Umwiringire n’uwo kwizerwa, Byose, nukw’ Adugukunda.

Refrain

Nzahora nizer’Imana, Mbwir’ abanyumv’ ibya Yo. Mpora nezerewe cyane, Kubw’ Urukundo rwayo.

Couplet 5

Wibuke iteka Ijambo ry’Imana, kw’Abana natw’iteka. Aradukunda ntukinangire, usang’ Umwami Yesu.

Refrain

Jyuhora wizer’ Imana, ubwir’ abumva bose. Inkuru y’Agakiza kayo, bizere babohorwe.
