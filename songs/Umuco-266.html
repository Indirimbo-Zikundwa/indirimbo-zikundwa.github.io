#266

Egereza ewe ncuti yanje

Umuco

Refrain

Egereza ncuti yanje, Umusozi w’abana b’Imfura ./.Umwe wubatsweko Yerusalemu, ni ryo Shengero ry’abana b’Umwami./.

Couplet 1

Uwo musozi wabaye ikoraniro rya Yesu Kristo, hamwe n’intumwa ziwe inkingi y’umuriro ica iramanuka bose buzura ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, Petero atangura kuvuga ati: Mwami ndakwubakire urusago, urundi narwo rub’urwa Mose, urundi narwo rub’urwa Eliya.

Refrain

Egereza ncuti yanje, Umusozi w’abana b’Imfura ./.Umwe wubatsweko Yerusalemu, ni ryo Shengero ry’abana b’Umwami./.

Couplet 2

Mana yanje Mana ya Yesu Kristo, biguhimbare ngo mbe mungoro yawe, name ngavye mu mutima wanje, name numva ijwi ry’inzamba yawe, kuko kumara isaha imwe mu nzu yawe, Binyibagiza imiruho yo mw’isi kandi kwumva ijwi ry’ijambo ryawe, bintera gushikama mu rugendo.

Refrain

Egereza ncuti yanje, Umusozi w’abana b’Imfura ./.Umwe wubatsweko Yerusalemu, ni ryo Shengero ry’abana b’Umwami./.

Couplet 3

Raba inyoni zose zo mu kirere, zironkeyeho uburaro bwiza, none weho ga mwene Data, ntuziruta se ko none ugira ubwoba, kandi Umwami yaguciriye inzira nziza, mum u biri wiwe maze akuzamura ahera cane, mu bwigobeko bw’iyisumba vyose.

Refrain

Egereza ncuti yanje, Umusozi w’abana b’Imfura ./.Umwe wubatsweko Yerusalemu, ni ryo Shengero ry’abana b’Umwami./.
