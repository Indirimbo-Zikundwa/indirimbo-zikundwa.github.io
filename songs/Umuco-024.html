#24

Ewe mwenzangu usikie siku

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ewe mwenzangu usikie, siku za Bwana zakaribia; dunia hii itakwisha, maandiko yanatwambia.

Refrain

Jameni tumuombeni Mungu apate kuturehemu ili tuepuke hukumu tusije tukapotea ./.Ee Mungu, tuokowe dunia yaangamia./.

Couplet 2

Roho wa Mungu yu’mwetu, roho aliye Muombezi wetu, anatusii tuyaache maovu yanatupoteza.

Refrain

Jameni tumuombeni Mungu apate kuturehemu ili tuepuke hukumu tusije tukapotea ./.Ee Mungu, tuokowe dunia yaangamia./.

Couplet 3

Jambo muhimu kwetu sote, tuwe hodari kwa neno lake, neno la Mungu la uzima liwezalo kutuokoa.

Refrain

Jameni tumuombeni Mungu apate kuturehemu ili tuepuke hukumu tusije tukapotea ./.Ee Mungu, tuokowe dunia yaangamia./.
