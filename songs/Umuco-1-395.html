#395

Imana ni yo buhungiro bwacu

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Imana niyo Buhungiro bwacu, N’inkomezi zacu ku rugamba, Ni co gituma rero tudatinya, Nah’iyi si yose yohinduka.

Refrain

Amarushwa y’umugororotsi, Ni menshi ntarondoreka, Yamar’Umukiza wacu Yesu, Ayamukiza yos’ukw’angana.

Couplet 2

Indimi zose zo mw’isi zature, Yuko Yesu Krist’ar’Uhoraho, Icubahiro ciwe nagihabwe, Arabikwiye n’Uw’imbabazi.

Refrain

Amarushwa y’umugororotsi, Ni menshi ntarondoreka, Yamar’Umukiza wacu Yesu, Ayamukiza yos’ukw’angana.

Couplet 3

Urugamba rwose ni We arugwana, Imana Rurem’Iri kumwe natwe, Abanguy’inkot’abansi barahunga, Nimwisigireho: n’Uhoraho.

Refrain

Amarushwa y’umugororotsi, Ni menshi ntarondoreka, Yamar’Umukiza wacu Yesu, Ayamukiza yos’ukw’angana.

Couplet 4

Agwany’urugamba rwos’ararutsinda, Mu biremwa ntawusa na Yesu, Mw’Ijuru n’ah’afis’intwari nyinshi Ntawuhwanye vy’ukuri na Yesu.

Refrain

Amarushwa y’umugororotsi, Ni menshi ntarondoreka, Yamar’Umukiza wacu Yesu, Ayamukiza yos’ukw’angana.

Couplet 5

Mbega muz’akarang’arushij’abandi, N’imbabazi ziwe n’urukundo, Anezezwa cane n’abiyoroheje, Akiyegerez’abana bato.

Refrain

Kuk’uwutabaz’ Umwami Yesu, Ntaziger’ amuhinda na gato, N’incuti Iseruka mu magorwa, Umutabazi n’ubuhungiro.
