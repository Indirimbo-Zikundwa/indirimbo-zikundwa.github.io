Numvise Ijwi ry’Umukiza

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Numvise ijwi ry’Umukiza, Anyihamagarira; None, mutim’ungomesha, Umvir’Umucunguzi.

Couplet 2

Akir’ubugingo bwanjye: Mwam’ Ubwitegekere; Ubwimemo, budakurwa mu mategeko yawe.

Couplet 3

Ujy’ukoresh’uk’Ushaka: Ubu bugingo bwanjye; byanjye ndabigutuye: Databuja, nd’uwawe.

Couplet 4

No mu bihe byanjye byose, komeza kunshorera n’imbaraga zawe zose; Ujy’untegek’iteka.
