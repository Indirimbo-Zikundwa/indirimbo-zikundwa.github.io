#267

Tuzonezerwa cane Aho Yes’ Azozira

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Turemeshw’ imitima no kwizigira cane ko Yes’ Azogaruka, Igihe kiri hafi rwose, Tuzonezerwa cane Aho Yes’ Azozira.

Couplet 2

Kand ‘ abakunzi bacu Iyo bavuye mw isi, Tukababara cane, Tugasigaran’ amagorwa, Dusigeho kurira, Tuzokira ni yaza.

Couplet 3

Naho twogir’ ivyago Vy’uburyo bubi bgoss, Cank’ amarushwa menshi, Dutinyuke, mw isi s’i wacu: N’ ivyo kwihanganirwa Kugez’ ah’ azozira.

Couplet 4

Tury’ Ingaburo yiwe, Umutsima na vino, N’ icibutso ça Yesu Kugez’ igih’ azogaruka; Ni h’ azogaburira Abo yatoranije.
