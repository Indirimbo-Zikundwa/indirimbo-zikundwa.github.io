#39

Mwami wacu Yesu turagusaba

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mwami wacu turagusaba twebwe abana bawe ngo utwumvirize; ico tugusaba n’udufashe Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Refrain

Mwami Yesu n’utwumvirize turagusavye ngo udufashe Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Couplet 2

Raba abana bawe aho turi mw’isi y’umwijima n’udufashe; n’udukangure twame maso Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Refrain

Mwami Yesu n’utwumvirize turagusavye ngo udufashe Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Couplet 3

Rab’isi yose yahagurutse kurwany’igikorwa cawe Mwami; none n’udufashe twikangure Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Refrain

Mwami Yesu n’utwumvirize turagusavye ngo udufashe Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Couplet 4

Raba abansi b’umusaraba badukikije ngo baturwanye none tugusaba ng’udufashe Mwami hagarikir’igikorwa cawe.

Refrain

Mwami Yesu n’utwumvirize turagusavye ngo udufashe Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Couplet 5

Turagusaba ng’udufashe kuko twebw’abantu ntaco dufise; ico tugusaba duhe kwitanga Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.

Refrain

Mwami Yesu n’utwumvirize turagusavye ngo udufashe Mwami hagarikira igikorwa cawe.
