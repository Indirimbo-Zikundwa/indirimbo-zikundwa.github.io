#200

Umusi wo kuza kw’umukiza

Umuco

Couplet 1

Umusi wo kuza kw’Umukiza uregereje, n’umunsi mwiza utey’ igomwe ku bizeye, ku bafise ikimenyetso ca Mpwemu Yera, bazokwumva inzamb’ ivuze iyo mu bicu.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’inzamba y’Umumalayika mukuru, rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, abari mu birere bine vy’isi bazoyumva, bati ni muze mu Munezero wa Data.

Couplet 2

Benshi mur’ico gihe ntibazomeny’ ivyabaye, kuko uzoba ar’umusi umeze nk’iyindi yose, abizeye gusa nibo bazomeny’ iryo banga, abuzuye Ubushobozi bw’Uhoraho.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’inzamba y’Umumalayika mukuru, rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, abari mu birere bine vy’isi bazoyumva, bati ni muze mu Munezero wa Data.

Couplet 3

Abitwa ko basenga bose uko bari, ntibazomenya ivyabaye uwo munsi, kuko bizomenya gusa abizeye, abazoba buzuye Mpwemu bazovyumva.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’inzamba y’Umumalayika mukuru, rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, abari mu birere bine vy’isi bazoyumva, bati ni muze mu Munezero wa Data.

Couplet 4

Ico gihe abizeye bose imitima yabo, izohuzagizwa kubw’ikigira kibe, niho bazocabumv’ inzamba ivuze hose, abafise ikimenyetso bazovyumva.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’inzamba y’Umumalayika mukuru, rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, abari mu birere bine vy’isi bazoyumva, bati ni muze mu Munezero wa Data.

Couplet 5

Uwo munsi n’umusi mwiza utey’ igomwe, tuzoba dusezey’ imibabaro yose, tuzogira umunezero utigeze kubaho, wewe, jewe, twese turi mu ruhande ruhe?

Refrain

Ijwi ry’inzamba y’Umumalayika mukuru, rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, abari mu birere bine vy’isi bazoyumva, bati ni muze mu Munezero wa Data.
