#277

Tshiena musue

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Tshiena musue kupingana panyima, Kupingana tô ne ku dibutuka; Ndi wa mu mulongo wa badi baya Kumpala tô ne ku dipeta muoyo.

Refrain

Tshieyemenu tshianyi, Yesu Kilisto, Unkuate ku tshianza ne undombole.

Couplet 2

Tshiena ntshina lufu anyi makenga, Ntatu yonso ya mu mubidi eu; Ndi muangata dipangadika dianyi Dia kukulonda tô ne tshiendelele.

Refrain

Tshieyemenu tshianyi, Yesu Kilisto, Unkuate ku tshianza ne undombole.

Couplet 3

Mu masanka ne ngamba bualu buebe, Mu makenga ne ngamba bualu buebe; Bampenda buebe ne bangamba buebe, Tshiena nteketa bua kukutumbisha.

Refrain

Tshieyemenu tshianyi, Yesu Kilisto, Unkuate ku tshianza ne undombole.

Couplet 4

Kuyi kule nanyi, Mufuki wanyi, Undame mu njila wa luendu elu; Bua tshiyi nebe tshiena mua kufika Kundekelu mene wa luendu luanyi.

Refrain

Tshieyemenu tshianyi, Yesu Kilisto, Unkuate ku tshianza ne undombole.
