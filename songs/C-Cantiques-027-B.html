#27B

Kukuna nkunwa

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Kukuna nkunwa ku lubanga, Nansha kukuna muno mu djuba, Kukuna nkunwa kyaku kyolwa, Nansha kukuna mubufuku.

Refrain

Le bikekala namani le? Pa kyaba kya kukangula. Kya bufuku, nansha kya mu dyuba, Kya bukoke, nansha kya bukomo, Muntanda yonso ne momwa mulu Bine bine byonso bisa kwangulwa.

Couplet 2

Kukuna nkunwa mudishinda, Nansha kukuna ko kudi mwala. Kukuna nkunwa mwadi miba, Nansha munshi mine miyampe.

Refrain

Couplet 3

Refrain

Couplet 4
