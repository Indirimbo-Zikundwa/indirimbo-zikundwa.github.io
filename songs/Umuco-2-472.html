#472

Nezerewe k’umukiza Yesu

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Nezerewe k’Umukiza Yesu, Yamenyeshej’ Urukundo rwiwe, Yes’ Arankunda, ndabizi neza, Ni We Mukiza wanje.

Refrain

Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Nararons’ Inzira y’Agakiza, Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Mw’Izina ry’Umukiza.

Couplet 2

Ndashimiy’Umukiza Yesu, Yaranyiguz’ aranyitangira, Ni nashik’ Iwe mu Bwami bwiwe, Nzogir’ Umunezero.

Refrain

Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Nararons’ Inzira y’Agakiza, Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Mw’Izina ry’Umukiza.

Couplet 3

Ndashimiye UmukizaYesu, Yamenyeshej’ Urukundo rwiwe, Yes’Arankunda ndabizi neza, Ni We Mukiza wanje.

Refrain

Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Nararons’ Inzira y’Agakiza, Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Mw’Izina ry’Umukiza.

Couplet 4

Aho ntari bwaronk’ Agakiza, Nari mfis’ umubabaro mwinshi, Maze kwizer’ Umukiza Yesu, Ndirimba nezerewe.

Refrain

Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Nararons’ Inzira y’Agakiza, Non’ubu ndaririmba Haleluya, Mw’Izina ry’Umukiza.
