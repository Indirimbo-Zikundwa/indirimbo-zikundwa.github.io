#167

Uhoraho yarampamagaye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uhoraho Yarampamagaye, Amboha n’imigozi y’Urukundo; Uhoraho Yarampamagaye, mvuka mu Bwami bwiwe bw’Urukundo.

Refrain

Mpagaze, k’uruhande rw’Uhoraho, nah’isi yose yonyanka, navutse mu bundi Bwami busha, nd’igikoresho c’Uhoraho, sinshobora guceceka ./.Kuko nd’umwana w’Uhoraho./.

Couplet 2

Mbeg’imigozi numva imboshe ingobera kumushingira intahe, ingobera ku mukorera mu rukundo, ariko inkomezi niz’Uhoraho.

Refrain

Mpagaze, k’uruhande rw’Uhoraho, nah’isi yose yonyanka, navutse mu bundi Bwami busha, nd’igikoresho c’Uhoraho, sinshobora guceceka ./.Kuko nd’umwana w’Uhoraho./.

Couplet 3

Yarampamagaye kand’ aramboha, nanje ndemera guca ah’ azoncisha, nditeze gukora ico ambwiye, niteze kuja aho Azonjana.

Refrain

Mpagaze, k’uruhande rw’Uhoraho, nah’isi yose yonyanka, navutse mu bundi Bwami busha, nd’igikoresho c’Uhoraho, sinshobora guceceka ./.Kuko nd’umwana w’Uhoraho./.

Couplet 4

Yankuye mu bakure bo mw’isi, anzana nk’intama yazimiye; ambohera mu rugo rwiwe rwera, Ankoresha ico We ashaka.

Refrain

Mpagaze, k’uruhande rw’Uhoraho, nah’isi yose yonyanka, navutse mu bundi Bwami busha, nd’igikoresho c’Uhoraho, sinshobora guceceka ./.Kuko nd’umwana w’Uhoraho./.

Couplet 5

Yemwe bana b’Imana Ihoraho, twaboshwe n’umugozi w’Urukundo; igikorwa ni c’Uhoraho Imana, twizere Amasezerano yiwe.

Refrain

Mpagaze, k’uruhande rw’Uhoraho, nah’isi yose yonyanka, navutse mu bundi Bwami busha, nd’igikoresho c’Uhoraho, sinshobora guceceka ./.Kuko nd’umwana w’Uhoraho./.
