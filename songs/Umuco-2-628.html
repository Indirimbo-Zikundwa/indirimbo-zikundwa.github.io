Uwo gutabaza mbega yoba nde?

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Uwo gutabaza mbega yoba nde? Atari Yes’Umwami, ni We twamenye, Ni We dutabaza kand’Akishura, Mu bihe bigoye biturengeye.

Refrain
