Couplet 1

Couplet 2

Couplet 3

Ni Data wa twese mwiza, Az’ intege nke zacu, Atubungabunga neza, Kand’ akatuzigama. Duhimbaz’ ubuntu bgiwe, Buzohoraho rwose.

Couplet 4
