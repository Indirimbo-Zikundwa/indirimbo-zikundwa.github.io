#201

Umunsi wo kuza kw’umwami

Umuco

Couplet 1

Umunsi wo kuza kw’Umwami, uregereje cane, nanje nama niteguriye uwo musi, ah’abapfiriye mu Mwami bazozuka ./.Bene Data bose tuzobonana, Paulo na Daniyeli tuzobonana./.

Refrain

Nezerwa himbarwa, mutima wanje kukw’ ivyo wizeye ari ivy’ukuri; kukw’ abatasi bose, abagiyeyo bose, ata numwe yagaye ico gihugu; n’igihugu gitemba amata n’ubuki, inzara n’urupfu ntibiriyo. N’igihugu c’Abera b’Iyera, nanje nziboner’ Umwami imbonankubone ./.Nzoririmba Urukundo rwiwe, turi kumwe n’Abera bose; turirimbe iyo mw’Ijuru duti: Haleluya!./.
