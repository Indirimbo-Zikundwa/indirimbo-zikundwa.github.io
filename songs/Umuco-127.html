#127

Twahunze babuloni

Umuco

Couplet 1

Twahunze i Babuloni habi, mu gisagara c’irimbukiro; twahunz’i Babuloni habi, mu gisagara c’amadayimoni. ./.Aharindirw’ impwemu mbi z’iy’ isi, z’iy’ isi y’umwiza w’umuzitanya./.

Refrain

Twaravuye imahonero, twaravuye i Sodomu; twaravuye i Edomu, igisagara c’amadayimoni; non’ubu turi i Yerusalemu, ubu turaririmba, ubu turacuraranga, ubu turavuza ingoma; turi mu Bwami butanyiganyizwa.

Couplet 2

Twahunz’i Babuloni habi, icicaro ca Antikristo; twahunz’i Babuloni habi, icicaro ca satani ./.Aho basenga imana zitazwi imana z’abanyamahanga zitatu./.

Refrain

Twaravuye imahonero, twaravuye i Sodomu; twaravuye i Edomu, igisagara c’amadayimoni; non’ubu turi i Yerusalemu, ubu turaririmba, ubu turacuraranga, ubu turavuza ingoma; turi mu Bwami butanyiganyizwa.

Couplet 3

Twahunz’i Babuloni habi, ah’ amayobera avugirwa; twahunz’i Babuloni habi, abitwa ko basenga nabo ./.Baseng’ imana z’amayobera, ./.Bakazisenga ntizibumve ntizibishure./.

Refrain

Twaravuye imahonero, twaravuye i Sodomu; twaravuye i Edomu, igisagara c’amadayimoni; non’ubu turi i Yerusalemu, ubu turaririmba, ubu turacuraranga, ubu turavuza ingoma; turi mu Bwami butanyiganyizwa.

Couplet 4

Twahunz’i Babuloni habi, ah’ amarogotan’ avugirwa; twahunz’i Babuloni habi, ahatigera hahanurwa ./.I Babuloni hagiye kurimbuka, hunga wiruk’ ibara ry’i Babuloni./.

Refrain

Twaravuye imahonero, twaravuye i Sodomu; twaravuye i Edomu, igisagara c’amadayimoni; non’ubu turi i Yerusalemu, ubu turaririmba, ubu turacuraranga, ubu turavuza ingoma; turi mu Bwami butanyiganyizwa.

Couplet 5

Mbes’ umunezero mwinshi, ku bahunze i Babuloni; n’amahoro kuri bose uko bari, bari mu Bwami bwera cane ./.Umwami wabwo ni nyen’ Amahoro, hunga wiruke ibara ry’i Babuloni./.

Refrain

Twaravuye imahonero, twaravuye i Sodomu; twaravuye i Edomu, igisagara c’amadayimoni; non’ubu turi i Yerusalemu, ubu turaririmba, ubu turacuraranga, ubu turavuza ingoma; turi mu Bwami butanyiganyizwa.
