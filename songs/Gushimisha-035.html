#35

Umpe kukwegera Mana yanjye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Umpe kukwegera Mana yanjye naho twafatanya Umusaraba nagumya kwishima, nkajya ngusenga nti:Umpe kukwegera Mana yanjye.

Couplet 2

Umpe kukwegera Mana yanjye Jy’Undenger’iteka ngumye nkwizere ntewe na Satani, Ujy’Undwanirira, Umpe kukwegera Mana yanjye.

Couplet 3

Naho Natungurwa n’umwijima nkisegur’ibuye mbaye Jyenyine nzabe nka Yakobo ndot’ibyo mw’Ijuru nkomeze nkwegere Mana yanjye.

Couplet 4

Wakinguy’Ijuru Mana yanjye urwego ruriho rutugezayo mugenzi komera, jy’usab’ itek’uti tujye tugumana Mana yanjye.
