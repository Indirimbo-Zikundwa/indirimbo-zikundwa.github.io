#306

Bufuku lo bu ka kya

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Bufuku lo bu ka kya ku a mapidi abuya, Ne lukumo lwa Kristu lwa munika bena Leza, E po tu ka languluka biya mo twalayilwa, Tu ka yukanika biya bufuku pakupita.

Refrain

Tuka mu yukidisha, Penape kusekela, Pakufika ‘lua lufuku, Lua nseko luine, luine; Bufuku pakupita, Tukayukanika biya.

Couplet 2

Dingi muno twa koka ne ku fitwa pa mutima, Ne dingi ku tukuta wa pandi wa pandi pala, Nkambo pakulaka amba, Iyai anwe ba dyese, Tu ka pwila bonso pamobyo bwaya dino bukye.

Refrain

Tuka mu yukidisha, Penape kusekela, Pakufika ‘lua lufuku, Lua nseko luine, luine; Bufuku pakupita, Tukayukanika biya.

Couplet 3

Tu ka pwila ku kipona kyandi ne lusekelo, Ne bantu bo twa temwa kwimona nabo pa menso, Ñimbo ya bukuji bwetu yo tukemba nyeke pe, Malwa onso pa ku pwa ne bufuku pa kupita.

Refrain

Tuka mu yukidisha, Penape kusekela, Pakufika ‘lua lufuku, Lua nseko luine, luine; Bufuku pakupita, Tukayukanika biya.
