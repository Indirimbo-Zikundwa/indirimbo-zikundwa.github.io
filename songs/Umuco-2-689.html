#689

N’ubwo tubabazwa

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Nubwo tubabazwa, dufis’ ivyizigiro ko tugiye kuruhuka vuba, Nuko rero twihanganire umusaraba, Kuko tugiye kubon’ Ivyasezeranywe.

Refrain

Mfis’inkumbu zo gushika iwacu, Maze nkiberay’ ubutakivayo, Iyi si numva yandambiye, Kuk’Umub’ ariw’ayiganza, Ariko mw’Isi Nsha n’Ijuru Risha, Hazoba haganzwa n’Umwami Yesu, Niho Tuzibera Amahoro, Haleluy’ izob’ibihe vyose.
