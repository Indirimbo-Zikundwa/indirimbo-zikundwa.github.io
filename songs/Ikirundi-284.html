Har’intam’ urwenda na cenda

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Har’intam’ urwenda na cenda zari mu ruhongore, Arikw imwe yar’ ihabiye kure cane y’irembo: Mu manga kure no mw’irinde, itay’ Umwungere yayikunda, itay’ Umwungere yayikunda.

Couplet 2

Mwam’ intama zawe kw ari nyinshi, mbega ntiziguhagije? Umwungere yishur’ at’ Oyaye, Iyo na yo yar’ iyanje; Naho yoba yambukiye kure ndagiye kuronder’ intama yanje, ndagiye kuronder’ intama yanje.

Couplet 3

Abacunguwe nta ho bazomenya inzuzi yajabutse, n’umuzimagiza yaciyemwo Aronder’ iyo ntama; Yumva yabiriy’ aho yari Asang’ivunits’ igomba gupfa, Asang’ ivunits’ igomba gupfa.

Couplet 4

Igihururu mbona kirekire c’amaraso n’ic’iki? Ati n’ayo naviriy’iyo ntama ndiko ndayirondera. Mwami, wishwe n’iki mu biganza? n’amahwa ndi kuyirondera, n’amahwa ndi kuyirondera.

Couplet 5

Umv’iryo jwi n’iry’ Umwungere Azanany’ akaneza, ati tunezeranwe, ko mbonye iyari yazimiye. No mw’Ijur’ Abamarayika bashim’Imana banezerewe, bashim’Imana banezerewe.
