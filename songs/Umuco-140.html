Har’ibanga rihambaye cane

Umuco

Couplet 1

Har’ ibanga rihambaye cane, n’ibanga rikomeye cane, n’ingene Imana Ikora, n’ingene Yiyerekana, n’ingene Yihishura.

Refrain

Kumeny’ iryo banga, s’ikintu coroshe, n’uguca bugufi rwose ./.n’ukurongorwa n’Ijambo, Haleluya, Haleluya, Amen!./.

Couplet 2

Iryo banga rikomeye cane, abana b’Imana bonyene, ni bo barihishurirwa, babona Igikorwa c’Imana, n’intambwe y’Ishengero.

Refrain

Kumeny’ iryo banga, s’ikintu coroshe, n’uguca bugufi rwose ./.n’ukurongorwa n’Ijambo, Haleluya, Haleluya, Amen!./.

Couplet 3

Abadahishurirwa bose, ntibigera banarimenya, bagendera mu vyashize, bakarya manu yaboze, ntibameny’ ibihe vy’Imana.

Refrain

Kumeny’ iryo banga, s’ikintu coroshe, n’uguca bugufi rwose ./.n’ukurongorwa n’Ijambo, Haleluya, Haleluya, Amen!./.

Couplet 4

Wihutire kumenya neza, ingene Imana ikora, utahur’ ibihe vy’Imana, ntuhav’usigara mu mwiza, ngo winjire m’urupfu

Refrain

Kumeny’ iryo banga, s’ikintu coroshe, n’uguca bugufi rwose ./.n’ukurongorwa n’Ijambo, Haleluya, Haleluya, Amen!./.

Couplet 5

Harahiriwe cane rwose, abahishurirwa intambwe, bameny’ ibihe vy’Imana, bumva ijwi ry’Umwami, bakagendera mu Muco.

Refrain

Kumeny’ iryo banga, s’ikintu coroshe, n’uguca bugufi rwose ./.n’ukurongorwa n’Ijambo, Haleluya, Haleluya, Amen!./.
