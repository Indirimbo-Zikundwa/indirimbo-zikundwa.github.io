#164

Yesu Yavukiy’i Betelehemu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yesu Yavukiy’i Betelehemu, bamuryamika mu bgato bg’inka; n’Umunyembabazi nyinshi cane n’Umukunzi wacu.

Refrain

Umv’uko Yadukunze, Umv’ uko Yadukunze, n’Umunyembabazi nyinshi cane, n’Umukunzi wacu.

Couplet 2

Yesu Yancunguriye ku giti, Kand’ Abohor’ umutima wanje, n’Umunyarukundo runtangaza, Ni je Yapfiriye.

Refrain

Ni jewe Yapfiriye, ni jewe Yapfiriye, n’Umunyarukundo runtangaza ni je Yapfiriye.

Couplet 3

Abazimiy’ Arabarondera, Abahamagar’ Adahumeka; Mwumv’ Ijwi ryiwe mwa banyavyaha, Arahamagara.

Refrain

Arabahamagara, Arabahamagara, mwumv’ Ijwi ryiwe mwa banyavyaha, Arahamagara.

Couplet 4

Abacunguwe n’urwo Rukundo, ntidusubire mu bicumuro, Uwamana na vyo ntiyokizwa, Aduha kunesha.

Refrain

Aduha kubinesha,Aduha kubinesha, uwamana n’ivyaha ntakizwa, Aduha kunesha.

Couplet 5

Yes’Azogaruk’ Atujan’i We, tugumane na W’imisi yose, kuko Yabisezeranye natwe, Agir’ Agaruke.

Refrain

Azogaruka vuba, Azogaruka vuba, kuko Yabisezeranye natwe, Agir’ Agaruke.
