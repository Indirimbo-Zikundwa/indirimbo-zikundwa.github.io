#181

Numv’ijwi ry’umwami rimpamagara.

Umuco

Couplet 1

Numv’ijwi ry’Umwami rimpamagara, rikampamagara mu gacerere; rimbwira ngo negere, negere dusangire ku meza y’Ubwami bwiwe, ni bwo butumire Umwami Adutumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe ariko agaburira abana biwe arobanura ingano mu muguruka; uwumva niyumwe kand’amenye uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.

Couplet 2

Numv’ijwi rya Yesu rihamagara, uteg’ugutwi wumve mwene Data; abari maso ijoro umutaga bose, ngo begere, begere ameza yiwe, ni bwo butumire Imana Idutumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe ariko agaburira abana biwe arobanura ingano mu muguruka; uwumva niyumwe kand’amenye uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.

Couplet 3

Yesu ari ku muryango arahamagara, ati; abinjira ni mwinjire vuba; kuk’umuco ugiye gutana n’umwiza, uwinjira ni yinjire mw’irembo rifunganye, ni bwo butumire Yesu Adutumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe ariko agaburira abana biwe arobanura ingano mu muguruka; uwumva niyumwe kand’amenye uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.

Couplet 4

Igihe c’ubutumire kiriko kirahita twinjire mu muryango ucuguruye; umuco ugiye gutana n’umwiza, injira, injira wew’usigaye, nibwo butumire Umwami Adutumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe ariko agaburira abana biwe arobanura ingano mu muguruka; uwumva niyumwe kand’amenye uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.
