#18

Kaze mukiza ndahevye vyose

Umuco

Couplet 1

Kaze Mukiza ndahevye vyose, n’ushike mu mutima wanje (Yesu) ah’Uzob’ Uhezagir’ abandi nanje nyene ntuze kunsiga.

Refrain

Ndagu…shima… Yesu ko we…meye kotu…bana.

Couplet 2

Ndushirizeho kwizera Yesu, simbe kure y’inyonga zawe (Mwami), ibinyanduza ubinyogeshe, ya maraso yo muru bavu.

Refrain

Ndagu…shima… Yesu ko we…meye kotu…bana.

Couplet 3

Mfukamy’imbere y’inyonga zawe, ngomba kwizera no gukora (Yesu), iri sengesho urihindure, ribir’imota imbere yawe.

Refrain

Ndagu…shima… Yesu ko we…meye kotu…bana.

Couplet 4

Nkukw’impongo zi-zihuzagizwa, zifuz’imigezi y’amazi (Mwami), ni ko umutima wanje wifuza, kubona mu nyonga h’Umwami.

Refrain

Ndagu…shima… Yesu ko we…meye kotu…bana.

Couplet 5

Mutima wanje ugir’inyota yo kukubona Mana yanje (Yesu), Agakiza nta handi nkaronka, atari mu nkomezi zawe.

Refrain

Ndagu…shima… Yesu ko we…meye kotu…bana.
