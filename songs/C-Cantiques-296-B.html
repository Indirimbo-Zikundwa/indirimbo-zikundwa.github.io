#296B

Ankinyi kyankulupila?

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Ankinyi kiankulupila? Anka mase a Kidishitu, Takwi mungi akunkwasha, Anka Yesu mululame.

Refrain

Nyi mweme kui Mfumu Yesu, Ebwe dishimate di poo, Ankunkusha nkosonkoso.

Couplet 2

Nsunga nkimumone mpala Natengela milayilo; Bu nanga mwelwe mumema Nakankamana anka mpo.

Refrain

Nyi mweme kui Mfumu Yesu, Ebwe dishimate di poo, Ankunkusha nkosonkoso.

Couplet 3

Mitshipo yamu mase-e, Na kilombeno kia buwa, Abinyish’eshimba diande Pantompibwe na Satana.

Refrain

Nyi mweme kui Mfumu Yesu, Ebwe dishimate di poo, Ankunkusha nkosonkoso.

Couplet 4

Pakwela mpungi mwiulu Nakapetshibwa mui Yesu, Mu mfuadikye kululame: Nki na kantukatopekwa.

Refrain

Nyi mweme kui Mfumu Yesu, Ebwe dishimate di poo, Ankunkusha nkosonkoso.
