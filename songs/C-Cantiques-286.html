Bulob’ebu ne bupite

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Bulob’ebu ne bupite Ne bintu bionso bidimu, Bionso bitudi tumona Kabienaku bua kashidi.

Refrain

Ndi njinga kupeta muaba Ku makasa ebe Yesu; Mu bukubi bua kashidi, Mu disanka dia kashidi.

Couplet 2

Ntshiena musue kusuika- Majinga anyi ku buloba, Mbuluile dinanga diebe Bua nkulamate kashidi.

Refrain

Ndi njinga kupeta muaba Ku makasa ebe Yesu; Mu bukubi bua kashidi, Mu disanka dia kashidi.

Couplet 3

Refrain
