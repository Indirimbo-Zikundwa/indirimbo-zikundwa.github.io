Mwanta wetu Yesu

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Mwanta wetu Yesu, Tukumoni, Tunadifukuli kunyendo Yeyi. Eyi wa lubanji, wabadika kulema Mukwa ŋovu jejima, Tukumoni.

Couplet 2

Spiritu Wajila tukutiyi Suluka hakachi, ka wantu Eyi. Inzesha nyichima, naluwi, na kukeŋa Namuzaŋalu Weyi, Tukutiyi.

Couplet 3

Nzambi Tata yetu tunalembi, Hoshaŋa kudetu ne Hizudeyi, Munyichima yetu muhizumuki lelu, Twili muchima Weyi, tunalembi.
