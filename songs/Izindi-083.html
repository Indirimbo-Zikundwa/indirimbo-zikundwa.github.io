#83

Iteka ntekereza kw’iwacu ari bugufi

Izindi

Couplet 1

Iteka ntekereza, kw’ Iwac’ari bugufi. Uko bukeye negereza, gusohora mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Hafi y’Iwacu, hafi y’Aho; Ubu ngeze hafi y’Iwacu, ngiye gusohorayo.

Couplet 2

Mu Rurembo rwa Data, hariy’ Amazu menshi; Yateguriy’ Abera bose, ngo babane na Yesu.

Refrain

Hafi y’Iwacu, hafi y’Aho; Ubu ngeze hafi y’Iwacu, ngiye gusohorayo.

Couplet 3

Ni njya kuger’Iwacu, Nzururuts’ umutwaro; Nzoroherwa n’umubabaro, nambar’ Ikamba ryiza.

Refrain

Hafi y’Iwacu, hafi y’Aho; Ubu ngeze hafi y’Iwacu, ngiye gusohorayo.
