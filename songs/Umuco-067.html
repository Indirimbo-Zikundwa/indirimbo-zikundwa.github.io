#67

Twizigire uhoraho

Umuco

Couplet 1

Twizigire Uhoraho n’ubwenge bwawe bwose; wizigire Uhoraho n’ubugingo bwawe.

Refrain

Twizigire Uhoraho n’imitima yacu, twizigire Uhoraho n’inkomezi zacu.

Couplet 2

Niwahura n’amarushwa izigir’ Imana; umubiri ukuremereye izigire Imana.

Refrain

Twizigire Uhoraho n’imitima yacu, twizigire Uhoraho n’inkomezi zacu.

Couplet 3

Umwami yaramaze kuvuza inzamba; ati vyuka mwikangure dutangur’urugamba.

Refrain

Twizigire Uhoraho n’imitima yacu, twizigire Uhoraho n’inkomezi zacu.

Couplet 4

Intambwe zivuze izigir’Imana; Apolowoni aguteye fata inkota yawe.

Refrain

Twizigire Uhoraho n’imitima yacu, twizigire Uhoraho n’inkomezi zacu.

Couplet 5

Goliyati aguteye izig’Imana; izere Uwanesheje Azogutsindira.

Refrain

Twizigire Uhoraho n’imitima yacu twizigire Uhoraho n’inkomezi zacu.

Couplet 6

Intsinzi twayironkeye k’Umusaraba; Umwami Yarasemereye ati birarangiye.

Refrain

Twizigire Uhoraho n’imitima yacu, twizigire Uhoraho n’inkomezi zacu.

Couplet 7

Abizeye Uhoraho bameze nk’umusozi; umusozi wa Siyoni utanyiganyizwa.

Refrain

Twizigire Uhoraho n’imitima yacu, twizigire Uhoraho n’inkomezi zacu.
