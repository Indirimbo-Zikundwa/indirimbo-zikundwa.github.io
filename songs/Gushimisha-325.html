#325

Ababatijwe none Ubarinde

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ababatijwe none, Ubarinde, Mwami, Ub’Umubyeyi wabo, kukw’ar’ abangizi. Bajya mw’Ijuru bate? Ntabwo bakwijyana, keretse bakwizeye, Wowe, Mucunguzi.

Couplet 2

Bashakish’ Urukundo, Ujy’Ubaramira. Bakwiringire, Mwami, bahunge Satani; Muvunyi Yesu, ngwino, Ubakiz’ibibi. Twese tuguhimbaze, ng’Udukiz’iteka.

Couplet 3

Mwam’Ubwawe Wenyine, Ubabatirishe Umwuka wawe wera, bajye bakwizera; Twese tuzafatanye, tuzab’umwe rwose, duhore dukundana nkuko Wadukunze.
