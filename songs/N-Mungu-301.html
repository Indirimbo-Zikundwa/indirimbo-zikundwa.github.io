#301

Zamani Mwokozi aliwakaribisha

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Zamani Mwokozi aliwakaribisha watoto wote na furaha, akawabariki; Alikemea wanafunzi, akasema kwa mapendo: “Acha watoto wadogo kuja kwangu.”

Couplet 2

“watoto wadogo ninawapenda sana, Nitawachunga kama Muchungaji wa kondoo; Na wakinipa roho zao, nitawaokoa wote, Acha watoto wadogo kuja kwangu.”

Couplet 3

Aliwapokea na pendo na furaha, Mikono yake aliweka Muchungaji wa kondoo; Na alitaka wafahamu anapenda kila mutu, “Acha watoto wadogo kuja kwangu.”

Couplet 4

Mwokozi aliwaonyesha pendo kubwa, Lakini hapa sasa wengi hawamutambui, Na hawajasikia bado kwamba Yesu ni Mwokozi, “Acha watoto wadogo kuja kwangu.”
