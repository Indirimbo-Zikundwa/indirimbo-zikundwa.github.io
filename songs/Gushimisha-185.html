#185

Genda mu Mucyo wa Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Genda mu Mucyo wa Yesu, Aguhishurire ubury’ Umwuk’afatanya Abana b’Umucyo.

Couplet 2

N’ugendera mu Mucyo We, uraba wuzuye n’Iyera yang’umwijima, Ituye mu Mucyo.

Couplet 3

N’ugendera mu Mucyo We, ibyaha byakwishe birozwa n’Amaraso Ye, nuk’uhumanuke.

Couplet 4

N’ugendera mu Mucyo We, Azagutinyura imva n’urupfu, wibuke ko Yabinesheje.

Couplet 5

Ugendere mu Mucyo We, umwijim’ushire, uvirw’iteka n’Umucyo w’Iman’Ihoraho.

Couplet 6

Kugendera mu Mucyo We, n’Inzir’iboneye, n’iy’Amahoro n’Ineza, Yes’Agushorera.

Couplet 7

Jy’ugendera mu Mucyo We, uhung’umwijima: Ni hw’Amaras’ akweza de, ng’uhor’utunganye.

Couplet 8

Kandi mu Mucyo wa Yesu, dusangamw’abandi, baz’Umurokozi na bo, tukamusangira.
