Yesu Krist’ Arazutse

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yesu Krist’ Arazutse, None ntidutiny’ urupfu; Kuko yarunesheje, Nta butware nigifise. Haleluyal

Couplet 2

Non’ urupfu n’ irembo Ry’ ubugingo budashira, Ivyo bidutinyure Ni twagenda kuricamwo. Haleluya!

Couplet 3

Yesu yadupfiriye: Ariho, natwe tubeho; None tumushimishe Imitima yac’ ikeye. Haleluya!

Couplet 4

None nta coshobora Kudutandukanya na we, Aratuzigamira Hose kand’ imisi yose. Haleluya!

Couplet 5

Aratugumbiriye, Nta n’umw’azomutunyaga, Naho yoba Satani Cank’ ivyago cank’ urupfu. Haleluya!

Couplet 6

Ariho, ni we Mwami W’ijuru n’isi wenyene; Tuzomusang’ Iy’ari, Twamane na we na ntaryo. Haleluya!
