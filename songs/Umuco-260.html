#260

Umpe ku kuyoboka

Umuco

Couplet 1

Umpe kukuyoboka, niwo munezero wanje, umpe kwama menetse, ngumiza ku musaraba.

Refrain

Niyo mahoro arut’ayandi, niwo munezero wanje ./.Niko kugukorera ni wampindur’ikimazi./.

Couplet 2

Umpe kuguhimbara, umpe kuba imbohe yawe, nuzur’ishaka ryawe, nuzure ukugomba kwawe.

Refrain

Niyo mahoro arut’ayandi, niwo munezero wanje ./.Niko kugukorera ni wampindur’ikimazi./.

Couplet 3

Umpe kwuzura Mpwemu yera, ngw’anyemeze ivyo ugomba, bimpe kuguhimbara, bimpe kuba intwari yawe.

Refrain

Niyo mahoro arut’ayandi, niwo munezero wanje ./.Niko kugukorera ni wampindur’ikimazi./.

Couplet 4

Fash’ishengero ryawe, twuzure Mpwemu Yera, tub’ibimazi bizima, tub’ishikanwa ryawe.

Refrain

Niyo mahoro arut’ayandi, niwo munezero wanje ./.Niko kugukorera ni wampindur’ikimazi./.

Couplet 5

Umpe kukuyoboka niwo munezero wanje umpe kwama menetse ngumiza ku musaraba.

Refrain

Niyo mahoro arut’ayandi, niwo munezero wanje ./.Niko kugukorera ni wampindur’ikimazi./.
