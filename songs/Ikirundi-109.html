#109

Nkund’ Ubucuti buva mw’ Ijuru

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Nkund’ Ubucuti buva mw’ Ijuru, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho, ngiz’ Amahirwe, ndons’ Amahoro, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho.

Refrain

Nkiza, nkiza nkikiz’ Amaboko yawe; Nkiza, nkiza, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho.

Couplet 2

Nzokwama ngend’ Urugendo rwanje, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho, nta mwiza ngira mu Nzira yanje, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho.

Refrain

Nkiza, nkiza nkikiz’ Amaboko yawe; Nkiza, nkiza, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho.

Couplet 3

Nta bgoba mfise, sinciganyira, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho, nguwe nez’ ubu, ndatunganiwe, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho.

Refrain

Nkiza, nkiza nkikiz’ Amaboko yawe; Nkiza, nkiza, nisunz’ Amabok’ Ahoraho.
