#269

Uhoraho turagushimiye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uhoraho turagushimiye kubw’igikorwa cawe kubw’imigambi yawe Uhoraho Mana, turagushimiye kubw’ijambo ryawe, Manu yanyegejwe.

Refrain

Uhoraho Ur’Imana, Imana yihisha uwo itihishuriye ntashobora kukumenya uwo wihishuriye asanga uko wari ejo ariko uri uyu munsi kandi ariko uzokwama ibihe bidashira.

Couplet 2

Turagushimiye ko watwihishuriye ukaturongoza inkingi y’umuriro ukaduha manu nsha y’igihe cacu niryo jambo ryawe, Jambo rihishuwe.

Refrain

Uhoraho Ur’Imana, Imana yihisha uwo itihishuriye ntashobora kukumenya uwo wihishuriye asanga uko wari ejo ariko uri uyu munsi kandi ariko uzokwama ibihe bidashira.

Couplet 3

Wakuye Isirayeli, muri Egiputa ni wewe warirongoye mu bugaragwa kandi ni wewe warishikanye I Kanani kuko wewe ur’Imana, Imana y’bushobozi.

Refrain

Couplet 4

Couplet 5
