Yesu nd’uwawe

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yesu, nd’uwawe: Nyakira n’ibyanjye mfite byose. Njye ngukunda, nkwizigire, nkomeze nkuyoboke.

Refrain

Nguhay’ibyanjye, uko bingana. Mwami wanjye Mwiz’Unkunda, Unyiharire!

Couplet 2

Yesu nd’uwawe: Nyakira, niyoroshye, nkuramye. Mpaz’ Ibyishimo byo mw’isi. Ubu, Yes’Unyakire.

Refrain

Nguhay’ibyanjye, uko bingana. Mwami wanjye Mwiz’Unkunda, Unyiharire!

Couplet 3

Refrain

Nguhay’ibyanjye, uko bingana. Mwami wanjye Mwiz’Unkunda, Unyiharire!

Couplet 4

Refrain

Nguhay’ibyanjye, uko bingana. Mwami wanjye Mwiz’Unkunda, Unyiharire!
