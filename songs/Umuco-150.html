#150

Mw’ijoro mu gicugu hagati

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mw’ijoro mu gicugu hagati, humvikany’ ijwi rikomeye, risemerera cane rwose, rivuga riti: Umukw’Araje.

Refrain

Inkumi z’ubwenge n’iz’ Ibijuju zose zarikanguye; zose zitez’ Umukwe Araje Araje.

Couplet 2

Ibijuju mu gutegereza ntibifis’ amavut’ akwiriye, nguy’ Umukwe agiye kuza, amatara azobazimirako.

Refrain

Inkumi z’ubwenge n’iz’ Ibijuju zose zarikanguye; zose zitez’ Umukwe Araje Araje.

Couplet 3

Wirinde itara ntirizime, amavuta ntagukamane, uzotererwa iyo hanze ahavugira insya z’amenyo.

Refrain

Inkumi z’ubwenge n’iz’ Ibijuju zose zarikanguye; zose zitez’ Umukwe Araje Araje.

Couplet 4

Shakashak’ Ubushobozi bwa Mpwemu, gend’ ugure hakiri kare, reka gusinzirira mw’isi, ayo mahwa ntakuzitire.

Refrain

Inkumi z’ubwenge n’iz’ Ibijuju zose zarikanguye; zose zitez’ Umukwe Araje Araje.

Couplet 5

Uwifatanya n’ab’ iki gihe, ntashobora kuzomubona, n’uwundi wese yambaye nkabo ntashobora kuzomwegera.

Refrain

Inkumi z’ubwenge n’iz’ Ibijuju zose zarikanguye; zose zitez’ Umukwe Araje Araje.

Couplet 6

Ubwo Bwami burabandanirwa, babunyakurish’ ubuhizi, ubu turi mu gihe kibi, nesha nesha uzobubamwo.

Refrain

Inkumi z’ubwenge n’iz’ Ibijuju zose zarikanguye; zose zitez’ Umukwe Araje Araje.
