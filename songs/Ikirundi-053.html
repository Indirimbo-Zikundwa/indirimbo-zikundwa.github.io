#53

Muze mwes’ aho Yes’ ari, Arabaruhura

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Muze mwes’ aho Yes’ ari, Arabaruhura; ni muvyumve, abarushe, Yasezerany’ artyo. Afis’ Ubushobozi, Atang’ umugisha. Ni w’ atang’ ubugingo Butazoshira.

Couplet 2

Muze mwes’ aho Yes’ ari, Arabarongora; Ni muvyumve, abahavye, Yasezerany’ artyo. Kand’ ubabara wese, Agahush’ inzira. Yumvire Yesu vuba Aranezerwa.

Couplet 3

Muze mwes’ aho Yes’ ari, Arabakomeza; Mumwumv’ abanyantege nke, Yes’ abaremeshe. Satan’ arwana cane, Yam’ abosha nabi; Ntimumutinye, kuko Yes’ amuruta.

Couplet 4

Kand’ uwoz’ aho Yes’ ari Ntazomwirukana; Nuko ntimukekeranye. Mwumve, munezerwe. Akund’ abanyavyaha Urukundo rwinshi, Arabahamagara Ngo baz’ ah’ ari.
