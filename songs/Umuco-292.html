#292

Ku musaraba w’umwami

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ku musaraba w’Umwami Yesu, niho naboneye intsinzi, kuko ca cangera c’icaha, arico c’urupfu camvako ./.Nico gituma ndirimba./.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, uyo n’Umunyembabazi kuko umunsi, nzomubona nzoruhuka imiruho.

Couplet 2

Ku musaraba w’Umukiza wanje, niho kwa gutandukana kwari hagati yanje, n’Imana kwavaho ubutagisubira ./.Nico gituma mfise amahoro./.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, uyo n’Umunyembabazi kuko umunsi, nzomubona nzoruhuka imiruho.

Couplet 3

Ku musaraba w’Umucunguzi, niho n’Abera basinziriye baronkeye intsinzi vy’ukuri, bakabohorwa bakanesha ./.Ni co gituma tuzobonana./.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, uyo n’Umunyembabazi kuko umunsi, nzomubona nzoruhuka imiruho.

Couplet 4

Uwo musaraba ndawukunda, kuko wampaye kuzoragwa ubugingo budashira, nkaba niteze no kuzobona ./.Umwami imbonankubone./.

Refrain

Umwami Yesu Yarankunze, isi n’ijuru bitararemwa, uyo n’Umunyembabazi kuko umunsi, nzomubona nzoruhuka imiruho.
