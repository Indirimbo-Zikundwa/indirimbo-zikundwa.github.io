#197

Imana yatoranije isirayeli.

Umuco

Couplet 1

Imana Yatoranije Isirayeli, Iyikura mu gihugu c’amahanga, iyishira mu gihugu cayo bwite, n’ihanga ry’Uhoraho Umwam’Imana.

Refrain

Mw’irinde Ndi kumwe nawe; mu magorwa Ndi kumwe nawe; mu muriro Ndi kumwe nawe, no mu rupfu nzobana nawe ./.Ni wewe uwo mfash’ ukuboko; ni wewe uwo nakunze kera; ni wewe nameny’ ubwa mbere na mbere, ah’uzoja hose ndi kumwe nawe./.

Couplet 2

Bose bar’abana ba Aburahamu, nk’ukw’ Imana yari Yasezeranye nawe; yukw’ Izomuhindur’ ihanga rikomeye, n’ihanga ry’Uhoraho Umwami Imana.

Refrain

Mw’irinde Ndi kumwe nawe; mu magorwa Ndi kumwe nawe; mu muriro Ndi kumwe nawe, no mu rupfu nzobana nawe ./.Ni wewe uwo mfash’ ukuboko; ni wewe uwo nakunze kera; ni wewe nameny’ ubwa mbere na mbere, ah’uzoja hose ndi kumwe nawe./.

Couplet 3

Abazokuvuma bose nzobavuma, abazoguhezagira mbahezagire; kandi muri wewe nih’ imiryango yose yo mw’isi izoherwa umugisha.

Refrain

Mw’irinde Ndi kumwe nawe; mu magorwa Ndi kumwe nawe; mu muriro Ndi kumwe nawe, no mu rupfu nzobana nawe ./.Ni wewe uwo mfash’ ukuboko; ni wewe uwo nakunze kera; ni wewe nameny’ ubwa mbere na mbere, ah’uzoja hose ndi kumwe nawe./.

Couplet 4

Abazokurwanya bose nzobarwanya, abazogufasha bose nzobafasha, imisozi izokwikubita hasi it’Icubahiro n’ic’Umwami Imana.

Refrain

Mw’irinde Ndi kumwe nawe; mu magorwa Ndi kumwe nawe; mu muriro Ndi kumwe nawe, no mu rupfu nzobana nawe ./.Ni wewe uwo mfash’ ukuboko; ni wewe uwo nakunze kera; ni wewe nameny’ ubwa mbere na mbere, ah’uzoja hose ndi kumwe nawe./.

Couplet 5

Nezerwa himbarwa bana b’Imana, agakiza nak’ Imana ya Aburahamu; Iyaremye vyose iri kumwe natwe, Iyanesheje vyose iri kumwe natwe.

Refrain

Mw’irinde Ndi kumwe nawe; mu magorwa Ndi kumwe nawe; mu muriro Ndi kumwe nawe, no mu rupfu nzobana nawe ./.Ni wewe uwo mfash’ ukuboko; ni wewe uwo nakunze kera; ni wewe nameny’ ubwa mbere na mbere, ah’uzoja hose ndi kumwe nawe./.
