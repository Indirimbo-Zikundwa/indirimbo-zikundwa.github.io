#177

Sinari nzi

Umuco

Couplet 1

Sinari nzi, Sinarinzi k’umuntu buntu, ashora kwizer’ Imana; kuk’ ubwenge, kuk’ ubwenge bw’umwana w’umuntu, butandukanye n’ubw’ Imana ./.Ivyifuzo vy’umwana w’umuntu bitandukanye n’ivy’Imana./.

Refrain

None vyvuye kuki? ni kubwa Mpwemu w’Uhoraho, ni kubw’ Ubushobozi bw’Uhoraho, Ubushobozi bwo mw’Ijuru, butanga guhishurirwa, bukinjira mu mwana w’umuntu, bukamuhindura icaremwe gisha, ahagadutse hagatotahara, ahar’ ikibi hakaba iciza, Ubwami bw’Imana bukamwuzura, urukundo, umunezero, amahoro, ukwihangana ./.Umuntu akabeshwaho na Mpwemu, akabaho kubwa Kristo./.

Couplet 2

Sinari nzi! sinarinzi k’umuntu buntu, ashobora kumenya ivya Mpwemu, kukw’ivyo Mpwemu, kukw’ivyo Mpwemu ashaka vyose, bitandukanye n’ivyakamere././.Iy’umuntu yumvise ivyo asanga bidahuje n’ivyiyumviro vyiwe./.

Couplet 3

Sinari nzi, sinarinzi k’umuntu buntu, ashobora kwumva ururimi rw’Imana; kukw’ivy’Ubwami, kukw’ivy’Ubwami bw’Imana nzima bitandukanye nivy’Ubwami bw’iyisi n’Ubwami bw’Imana nzima n’Ubwami bw’Abera B’Imana Yera./.
