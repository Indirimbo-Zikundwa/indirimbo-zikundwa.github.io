Mbes’uzoba witeguye?

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Mbes’uzoba witeguye Yes’aje, Ng’utahane na We mw’Ijuru? Nta Munezero uruta uwo tuzaba, Dufite mur’iyi si nsha.

Refrain

Nta bwoba no kwihisha bizarangwayo, Kuko hazaba Abanesheje gusa, Ubw’impanda izavuga bizarangira, Byose bizaba bishize.
