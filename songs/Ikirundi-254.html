#254

Yemwe, rab’ igitangaza

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yemwe, rab’ igitangaza: Wa Munyamibabaro Yaneshej’ Umurwanizi, Tumukomer’ amashi: Haleluya, Haleluya, Tumwimike n’ Umwami!

Couplet 2

Muh’ igitsibo c’ ubgiza, Ngo tumushengerere; Mumuh’ intebe y’ inganji, Ririmba vuz’ impundu: Haleluya, Haleluya, Ari n’inganji nyinshi.

Couplet 3

Kur’igitsibo c’amahwa bamwambitse batwenga: Mwambik’ ic’ icubahiro, Mwemere kw ar’ Umwami: Haleluya, Haleluya, Ganza, ganza Mukama!

Couplet 4

Vuz’ impundu, semerera, Yanesheje Satani! Yashitse mu bgiza bgiwe, Tumukurir’ ubgatsi! Haleluya, Haleluya, N’ Umwam’ aganz’abami!
