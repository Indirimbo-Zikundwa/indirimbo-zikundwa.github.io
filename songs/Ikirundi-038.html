Mukiza nsesa ng’Umenye

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mukiza, nsesa ng’Umenye ivyo nkora vyose, Werekan’ uko ndi kwose, ntifat’uko ntari.

Couplet 2

Ses’uyu mutima wanje, ni Wew’ Ushobora kugaragaz’ ivyikinze i bgina muri wo.

Couplet 3

Umurik’ ahatabona, ah’ inzigo yimye, Ump’ umutim’ ukangutse, uzinukw’ icaha.

Couplet 4

Menya n’ ivyo niyumvira n’imigabo yanje, n’ibimbamwo binyanduza ivyo njakariye.

Couplet 5

Mvugutira kand’ Uncure, inkamba zimvemwo, Umpindur’ icuma cawe Uhor’ Ukoresha.

Couplet 6

None nkwikubis’ imbere, Mpishurira rwose yukw’ Iman’ ar’ Urukundo rubuz’ akagero.
