#432

Abo mur’iyi si izashira

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Abo mur’iyi si—izashira nta cyo mwibajije—ku by’imperuka?

Couplet 2

Ntimwitegereje—icyiza twabonye? None, mwisigara—mu butamenya!

Couplet 3

Dor’icyo twahawe—cy’igiciro: Twahaw’ Ubugingo—butazashira.

Couplet 4

Yesu ni We zuba—ritwakira; Rituma tureba:—tumusingize!

Couplet 5

Isi yo mw’Ijuru—N’Ihebuza. Turek’ iby’iyi si,—tuhagabane!

Couplet 6

Ushak’ Ubugingo—apfirw’ibye, nk’ushak’ izahabu,—ukw’ apfirw’ibye.

Couplet 7

Yemw’ abameze nka —muka Loti, mwikund’ iby’iyi si—izarimbuka!

Couplet 8

Twebw’ Abakijijwe,—dutambuke; Ntitugakebuke,—ngo duhomberwe!
