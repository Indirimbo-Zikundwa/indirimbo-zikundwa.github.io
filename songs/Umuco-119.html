#119

Har’urugo rw’imana data

Umuco

Couplet 1

Har’Urugo n’Urugo rw’Imana Data, ruzitijwe amabuye y’izahabu; irembo ryarwo n’Umwami Yesu, ahamagara nk’Umwungere kw’irembo; ati irembo riruguruye ni mwinjire.

Refrain

Abari mur’urwo Rugo, n’abana b’Imana Ihoraho; buzuy’ Urukundo muri bo, buzuye Ubumwe muri bo ./.Buzuy’ Amahoro n’Ubumwe baciye kw’Irembo ry’Amahoro./.

Couplet 2

Har’Urugo n’Urugo rw’Imana Data, Umwami Yesu ni we rembo ryarwo; utamuciyeko ntiyinjira, afis’ inkovu mu biganza vyiwe. Ahamagarish’ Urukundo rwinshi.

Refrain

Abari mur’urwo Rugo, n’abana b’Imana Ihoraho; buzuy’ Urukundo muri bo, buzuye Ubumwe muri bo ./.Buzuy’ Amahoro n’Ubumwe baciye kw’Irembo ry’Amahoro./.

Couplet 3

Har’Urugo n’Urugo rw’Imana Data abari muri rwo n’abana b’Ihoraho; ab’Umwami Yesu yiguz’ Amaraso, bari bafashwe mpiri mu mahanga yose, Umwami Yesu Arabakiz’ Urupfu.

Refrain

Abari mur’urwo Rugo, n’abana b’Imana Ihoraho; buzuy’ Urukundo muri bo, buzuye Ubumwe muri bo ./.Buzuy’ Amahoro n’Ubumwe baciye kw’Irembo ry’Amahoro./.

Couplet 4

Bari mu rugo rwa Dat’Ahoraho, banezerejwe n’Ubuntu bagiriwe; umunezero ubazwa muri bo, havugw’ Amahoro Ubumwe n’Urukundo, baciye kw’ irembo ry’ Agakiza kabo.

Refrain

Abari mur’urwo Rugo, n’abana b’Imana Ihoraho; buzuy’ Urukundo muri bo, buzuye Ubumwe muri bo ./.Buzuy’ Amahoro n’Ubumwe baciye kw’Irembo ry’Amahoro./.

Couplet 5

Imana Data Ivugana na bo bose, Ikavugana na bo bakumva bose; Umwe wese mu kibanza ciwe, nta mu ryan’ uvugwa hagati yabo, n’Amahoro iminsi yose Haleluya.
