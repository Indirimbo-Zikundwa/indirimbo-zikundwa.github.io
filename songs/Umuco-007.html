#7

Mwene data uri ngaha

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mwene Data uri ngaha uyu munsi, wumva witeguye vy’ukuri. Ewe ncuti ambar’impuzu zera, vuba ubukwe burashitse.

Refrain

Eh’umukwe arashitse, aje gutora wa Mugeni afis’itara n’amavuta, ni we Mugeni yiteguye;

Couplet 2

Umugabo n’umugore bab’umwe, Nawe ub’umwe n’Umukiza; Umugore yubah’umugabo we, nawe wumvir’Umwami Yesu.

Refrain

Eh’umukwe arashitse, aje gutora wa Mugeni afis’itara n’amavuta, ni we Mugeni yiteguye;

Couplet 3

Ewe ncuti ushobora kuvuga uti nanje nditeguye pe; ntiwihende abatuzuye Mpwemu, ntibazokwinjira mu Bukwe.

Refrain

Eh’umukwe arashitse, aje gutora wa Mugeni afis’itara n’amavuta, ni we Mugeni yiteguye;

Couplet 4

Amajambo yavuzwe yarashitse Umuhanuzi yarashitse, yaj’azanye Ijambo ry’Ubugingo, Rihuz’abana naba se.

Refrain

Eh’umukwe arashitse, aje gutora wa Mugeni afis’itara n’amavuta, ni we Mugeni yiteguye;
