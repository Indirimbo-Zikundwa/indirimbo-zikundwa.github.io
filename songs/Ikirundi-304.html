#304

Har’Umukunzi w’abana

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Har’Umukunzi w’abana mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho, Abakund’ Urukundo rutagir’ iherezo, Abadukunda mw isi Boduharuruka: Yesu we n’ Umukunzi Atoduhemukira.

Couplet 2

Harih’ uburuhukiro mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho, bg’abana bamukunda bambaz’ Izina ryiwe, Ah’ abagenzi bato Bahora bataha, Ni ho baruhukira Mu mahor’ adashira.

Couplet 3

Mw ijuru harih’urugo, Nyene rwo n’ Umukiza Abana bamukunze bazoshika muri rwo: N’ urugo rwiza cane, Rw’ amahoro masa, Umunezero wabo Urut’ uw’ abo mw isi.

Couplet 4

Har’ ibitsibo mw ijuru Vy’ abana bakijijwe, Babikiwe na Yesu Ku musi bamwizeye; Abamukunda bose, Bakamushimisha, Bazogererwa na we Inger’ itazoshira.

Couplet 5

Harih’ indirimbo nziza Y’ abana bakijijwe; Bazama baririmba Ni bashika mw’Ijuru, ishim’ Umwami Yesu Ko yabacunguye: Kand’ abayiririmba Ntibazocobogora.

Couplet 6

Har’impuzu zera kandi z’abana bakijijwe, N’ indirimbo z’ ishimwe, N’ inanga z’ izahabu; Abakunz’ Umukiza Ni bo babihabga: Ni muze bana mwese, muvyakiran’ ishimwe.
