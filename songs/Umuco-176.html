#176

Yewe yerusalemu wo mw’ijuru

Umuco

Couplet 1

Yewe Yerusalemu, wo mw’Ijuru wuzuye Ubwiza bw’Uhoraho; wubatswe n’Umwubatsi nyakuri, ndatazir’Imana ku bwawe.

Refrain

Iyo nzamuriy’ amaso kuri wewe, nuzura umunezero mwinshi, iyo numvise inkuru z’iyo, nca numva nohagurukira, nkaj’i Yerusalemu Iwacu, har’ Amahoro menshi, har’ Umunezero mwinshi, hari Itunga ryinshi har’ Amazu menshi ./.Haleluya, Haleluya, Haleluya./. Har’ Amazu menshi.

Couplet 2

Ewe Yerusalemu, wo mw’Ijuru, uburuhukiro bw’abahizi; nta munyakibi avurwa muri wewe, ur’Umurwa w’Abera b’Iyera.

Refrain

Iyo nzamuriy’ amaso kuri wewe, nuzura umunezero mwinshi, iyo numvise inkuru z’iyo, nca numva nohagurukira, nkaj’i Yerusalemu Iwacu, har’ Amahoro menshi, har’ Umunezero mwinshi, hari Itunga ryinshi har’ Amazu menshi ./.Haleluya, Haleluya, Haleluya./. Har’ Amazu menshi.

Couplet 3

Ewe Yerusalemu, wo mw’Ijuru uwakubonye wese araririmba, kuko ari igitangaza kukubona, ur’Umurwa w’Abera b’Iyera.

Refrain

Iyo nzamuriy’ amaso kuri wewe, nuzura umunezero mwinshi, iyo numvise inkuru z’iyo, nca numva nohagurukira, nkaj’i Yerusalemu Iwacu, har’ Amahoro menshi, har’ Umunezero mwinshi, hari Itunga ryinshi har’ Amazu menshi ./.Haleluya, Haleluya, Haleluya./. Har’ Amazu menshi.

Couplet 4

Ewe Yerusalemu wo mw’Ijuru, Umwami wawe n’uwagahebuza, Yanesheje abami bose, Arabatsinda Yatubereye Umuhizi mu Rugendo.

Refrain

Iyo nzamuriy’ amaso kuri wewe, nuzura umunezero mwinshi, iyo numvise inkuru z’iyo, nca numva nohagurukira, nkaj’i Yerusalemu Iwacu, har’ Amahoro menshi, har’ Umunezero mwinshi, hari Itunga ryinshi har’ Amazu menshi ./.Haleluya, Haleluya, Haleluya./. Har’ Amazu menshi.

Refrain
