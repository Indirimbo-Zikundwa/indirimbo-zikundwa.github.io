#60

We mutim’urira

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

We, mutim’urira,—uze kwa Yesu; Amakuba yawe,—uyamubwire. Mubwire buhoro—ibikuriza; We kugir’ubwoba,—kandi wirira.

Couplet 2

Bwira Yesu byose,—n’Inshuti yawe, ibikubabaza—n’ibigutera. Arakund’ Afasha—abashavuye; Wizer’Ijambo rye,—kandi wirira.

Couplet 3

Abaremerewe—n’ibiruhanya, babwire kw’Imana—Yabaruhura. Ubahumurize,—ut’Umukiza Amar’agahinda:—None, mwirira!
