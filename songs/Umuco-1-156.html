Uhereye muri egiputa

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Uhereye muri Egiputa, Duhagurukijwe n’Uhoraho, Ntiyigeze kuduhemukira, Inkingi y’umuriro yar’imbere yacu.

Refrain

Uhoraho ntiyigeze guhemuka, Ico yakunze yaragikunze, N’umuvyeyi adahemuka, Yihanganira vyose.
