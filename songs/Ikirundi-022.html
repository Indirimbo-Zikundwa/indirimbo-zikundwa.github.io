#22

Ngwino Mana nkuru

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Ngwino Mana nkuru, Udufashe none kugushima: Data n’uhimbazwe, Uri n’ Ubuganji ngwin’ utuganz’ ubu, Mana nkuru.

Couplet 2

Ngwino Jambo wayo, We nkot’ isogota, Yesu Kristo: Mu gusenga kwacu Uduh’ umugisha, Duhe kukwumvira, Jambo ryera.

Couplet 3

Ngwino Mpwemu Yera, We Mufasha wacu, Manuk’ ubu; Ganzish’ inkomezi Imitima yacu, Ntunsige nk’impfuvyi, Mpwemu Yera.

Couplet 4

Butat’ Iman’ imwe, Turaguhimbaza, Tudasiba: Duhe kuzoshika I wawe mw ijuru, Tukubone Mana Y’Ubutatu.
