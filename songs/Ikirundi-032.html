#32

Mwam’ Utuvubir’ Imvura

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mwam’ Utuvubir’ Imvura, nk’uko Wasezeranye, mu mitima twumva twumye, Utubogoz’ Imvura.

Refrain

Utuvubir’ Imvura, Imvura y’Umugisha, Turarons’ urunyanyaga, Uduh’ iy’ Umuvuvu.

Couplet 2

Mwam’ Utuvubir’ Imvura, yoduhembura rwose, mu matw’ irikw’ irasuma, non’ Uyidushitseko.

Refrain

Utuvubir’ Imvura, Imvura y’Umugisha, Turarons’ urunyanyaga, Uduh’ iy’ Umuvuvu.

Couplet 3

Mwam’ Utuvubir’ Imvura, iducuncubukeko; Tuje kuraba rya rindi Wadusezeranira.

Refrain

Utuvubir’ Imvura, Imvura y’Umugisha, Turarons’ urunyanyaga, Uduh’ iy’ Umuvuvu.

Couplet 4

Mwam’ Utuvubir’ Imvura: Icomp’ igashirako, ubu tugisenga Mana, tukigutakambira.

Refrain

Utuvubir’ Imvura, Imvura y’Umugisha, Turarons’ urunyanyaga, Uduh’ iy’ Umuvuvu.
