#132

Yemwe hanga ryera

Umuco

Couplet 1

Yemwe hanga ryera, yemwe hanga ryiharije, abahamagawe n’Umwami Yesu, abavyawe n’ubutumwa bwiza ni muze dufatan’amaboko mu kwemangira Umwami wacu.

Refrain

Mu gusangira Mpwemu, mu gusangir’Umwami, mu gusangir’ urufatiro; no mu kuba ab’umwe, dufatane amaboko turwanir’ ishaka ry’Ubutumwa Bwiza twakiriye muri twe.

Couplet 2

Yemwe Hanga ryera, abasangiye Umwami; n’icishimikizo c’Ijambo ryiwe, twaronse ubutumwa bwiza ntitubwigungane natwe tubumurikire abatabuzi.

Refrain

Mu gusangira Mpwemu, mu gusangir’Umwami, mu gusangir’ urufatiro; no mu kuba ab’umwe, dufatane amaboko turwanir’ ishaka ry’Ubutumwa Bwiza twakiriye muri twe.

Couplet 3

Yemwe Hanga ryera, abasangiy’urugamba, n’imibabaro yo kuramukwa, dushire imitima hamwe mu gusengera ubutumwa bwiza no mu kubwitangira twese.

Refrain

Mu gusangira Mpwemu, mu gusangir’Umwami, mu gusangir’ urufatiro; no mu kuba ab’umwe, dufatane amaboko turwanir’ ishaka ry’Ubutumwa Bwiza twakiriye muri twe.

Couplet 4

Umuco twamurikiwe, tuwumurikir’ abandi, ijambo twaronse, Ijambo rizima turibwire abandi bose, mu kurwan’irishaka ubutumwa bwiza no mukwitangira ubutumwa bwiza.

Refrain

Mu gusangira Mpwemu, mu gusangir’Umwami, mu gusangir’ urufatiro; no mu kuba ab’umwe, dufatane amaboko turwanir’ ishaka ry’Ubutumwa Bwiza twakiriye muri twe.

Couplet 5

Nk’uk’umwe yitanze, kubwa twebwe twese ababi, natwe twitangire bene Data, kubwa bene Data bose mu gushingir’intahe ubutumwa bwiza Ijambo rizima ry’iki gihe cacu.

Refrain
