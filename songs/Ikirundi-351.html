#351

Ngo, mwes’ abanyotewe mu mitima

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Ngo, mwes’ abanyotewe mu mitima, Abananiwe n’abiganyira, Ngwino kw isoko muhazwe na Yesu, Ivyo mwifuza ni muvyakire.

Refrain

Unyotewe nzomusukakw’ amazi, N’imigez’ ize kw isi yumye; Nyugururira ndabah’ umugisha; Undondera wese, arambona.

Couplet 2

Munyesi we nturambiwe n’ingoyi? Nturuhijwe n’ivy’ isi bihenda? Ntunyoterew’ umugisha w’Imana? Umve, isezerano ririho.

Refrain

Unyotewe nzomusukakw’ amazi, N’imigez’ ize kw isi yumye; Nyugururira ndabah’ umugisha; Undondera wese, arambona.

Couplet 3

Muntu w’Imana ni wuzure Mpwêmu, Nta coguhaz’ atar’ ivya Yesu: Bikuzur’ ubuging’ umwete wiwe; Yesu ni we yabisezeranye.

Refrain

Unyotewe nzomusukakw’ amazi, N’imigez’ ize kw isi yumye; Nyugururira ndabah’ umugisha; Undondera wese, arambona.
