#623

Ni nasenga ngateba kwishurwa

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Ni nasenga ngateba kwishurwa, Nzosaba Yesu kwihangana, Nzomenya neza kw’igihe canje, co kwishurwa kitarashika.

Refrain

(“Jewe”) {Nzihangana gushika kw’iherezo, Nzihangana gushika nishurwe, Nzihangana gushik’ Agarutse, Nzihangana, Nzihangana.} x2

Couplet 2

Ni nabon’ibintu bihindutse, Nzosaba Yesu Ukwihangana, Nzomenya kw’ar’ ivy’igihe gito, Kandi kw’isi igiye gushira.

Refrain

(“Jewe”) {Nzihangana gushika kw’iherezo, Nzihangana gushika nishurwe, Nzihangana gushik’ Agarutse, Nzihangana, Nzihangana.} x2

Couplet 3

N’aho Satani uzogerageza, Kunyerek’ ivyiza vy’iyi si, Nzokubwira nti: «Je ko namenye, Ivyiza birut’ivyo mw’isi».

Refrain

(“Jewe”) {Nzihangana gushika kw’iherezo, Nzihangana gushika nishurwe, Nzihangana gushik’ Agarutse, Nzihangana, Nzihangana.} x2

Couplet 4

Ewe Satani waratsinzw’ubu, N’aho wonteza intege nke, Ndazi kw’integuro y’Umukiza, Yama yiteguye gushika.

Refrain

(“Jewe”) {Nzihangana gushika kw’iherezo, Nzihangana gushika nishurwe, Nzihangana gushik’ Agarutse, Nzihangana, Nzihangana.} x2
