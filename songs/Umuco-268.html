#268

Umwami Imana y’aburahamu

Umuco

Couplet 1

Umwami Imana y’Aburahamu ni we rutare rwatumenewe yihishuriye intama ziwe noneho zigendera mu muco.

Refrain

Abahambaye bayishakira mu bikubita amaso bihinda ./.Ariko abato b’Ubwami bwayo banezerwa no guhishurirwa./.

Couplet 2

Afise inyenyeri ndwi mu minwe ni we ntsinzi y’abizeye bose iyo yugaye ntawugurura kandi yuguruye ntawugara.

Refrain

Abahambaye bayishakira mu bikubita amaso bihinda ./.Ariko abato b’Ubwami bwayo banezerwa no guhishurirwa./.

Couplet 3

Mu bari mw’isi no mw’ijuru nta wuhwanye n’Uhoraho Imana afise Ubwiza bukayangana aca n’imanza zigororotse.

Refrain

Abahambaye bayishakira mu bikubita amaso bihinda ./.Ariko abato b’Ubwami bwayo banezerwa no guhishurirwa./.
