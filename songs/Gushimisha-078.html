#78

Kera har’Umubibyi wasohoy’ imbuto

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Kera har’Umubibyi wasohoy’ imbuto; Zimwe zigwa ku nzira, zimarwa n’inyoni, izindi ku rutare, zumishwa n’izuba, kuko zari zibuze ahantu horoshye.

Couplet 2

Mu mahwa hagw’ izindi, zikurana na yo; Nukw’ aziniga zose, ntizera na gato. Izindi mbuto zigwa mu butaka bwiza; Zimeze zirakura, Zer’imbuto nyinshi.

Couplet 3

Har’umubibyi wundi, ni Yesu Krisito. Ijambo ry’Agakiza ni iyo mbut’abiba, kand’ imitima yacu ni na yo mbibe ye, ari mib’ari myiza, amishamw’ imbuto.

Couplet 4

Ku mutwe n’ibiganza no ku birenge bye, hatonyang’ Amaraso akibib’ imbuto; Imvura ni y’imeza imbuto z’iyi si: Amaras’akameza izo mu mitima.

Couplet 5

Mugenzi, har’ibiki mu mutima wawe? N’inzira nyinshi, mbese? Cyangwa n’urutare? Cyangwa n’imbibe nziza? Mbe harafumbiwe? Weramw’ imbuto nziza, Cyangw’ umez’amahwa?

Couplet 6

Umwami Yes’Azaza Gusarur’ imbuto; Azaz’ Afit’intara, maz’azigosore; Ingano nziza zose Azazihunika, maz’umuram’ Awute na wo mu Muriro.

Couplet 7

Uzajya hehe wowe? Nturabimenya se? Uzajya mu kigega, cyangwa mu Muriro? Urorere gutinda: Wihane nonaha! Uyu n’umunsi mwiza wo kwemererwamo.
