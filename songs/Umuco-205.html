#205

Umwami yasigiye ishengero ryiwe

Umuco

Couplet 1

Umwami Yasigiye Ishengero ryiwe umuragi w’Amahoro, mukundane mwese nk’uko nabakunze ni yo ndagi ndabaraze.

Refrain

Urukundo n’Ukwizera, Amahoro n’Ivyizigiro; Ubugwaneza n’ukwirinda, kugira neza kwicisha bugufi ./.Ako niko karanga(x3) k’Ishengero./.

Couplet 2

Umwami Yabwiye Ishengero ryiwe, ni mube umwe mwese, nk’uko nanje Nd’umwe na Dat’ Imana niyo ndagi ndabaraze.

Refrain

Urukundo n’Ukwizera, Amahoro n’Ivyizigiro; Ubugwaneza n’ukwirinda, kugira neza kwicisha bugufi ./.Ako niko karanga(x3) k’Ishengero./.

Couplet 3

Ikirang’ Umwami mw’Ishengero ryiwe n’Urukundo n’Amahoro natwe twer’ imbuto za Mpwemu Yera Umwami Azoshemegizwa.

Refrain

Urukundo n’Ukwizera, Amahoro n’Ivyizigiro; Ubugwaneza n’ukwirinda, kugira neza kwicisha bugufi ./.Ako niko karanga(x3) k’Ishengero./.

Couplet 4

Uwuri mu Mubiri, wa Yesu Kristo, arangwa nizo mbuto; natwe twer’ imbuto z’uburyo bwa Mpwemu Umwam’ Azoshemagizwa.

Refrain

Urukundo n’Ukwizera, Amahoro n’Ivyizigiro; Ubugwaneza n’ukwirinda, kugira neza kwicisha bugufi ./.Ako niko karanga(x3) k’Ishengero./.
