Imbabazi z’Umukiza zamuzanye kuncungura

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Imbabazi z’Umukiza zamuzanye kuncungura, nari nzitswe n’ivyaha vyanje, Ankuray’ Arankiza.

Refrain

Nari mw’ishayo y’ivyaha, Aramfat’ Aranshayura, kera nama mu mwiza mwinshi, non’ Aramurikira.

Couplet 2

Natevye cane kwitaba Uwampamagaz’ ineza. Nyuma nizey’ Ijambo ryiwe, Yes’ ankurakw’ ivyaha.

Refrain

Nari mw’ishayo y’ivyaha, Aramfat’ Aranshayura, kera nama mu mwiza mwinshi, non’ Aramurikira.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Nari mw’ishayo y’ivyaha, Aramfat’ Aranshayura, kera nama mu mwiza mwinshi, non’ Aramurikira.

Couplet 4

Refrain
