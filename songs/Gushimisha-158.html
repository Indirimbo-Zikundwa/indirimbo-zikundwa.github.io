#158

Umvir’Ijwi rya Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Umvir’Ijwi rya Yesu, ni We Mukiza wawe. Umv’Arakubaz’ati: Munyabyah’urankunda?

Couplet 2

Umugore yabasha kwibagirw’ umwana we? Nahw’ibyo byashoboka, Jyeho nzajya nkwibuka.

Couplet 3

Ni jye Wakubohoye, Nkuvur’ ibikomere; Ni jye Waguhabuye; Ni jye Mucy’ukwakira.

Couplet 4

Kand’Urukundo rwanjye ntirubasha gushira; Rurut’urundi rwose, kuko rutarangira.

Couplet 5

Mu gihe gikwiriye, nzakujyana mw’Ijuru; Uzabon’ibyishimo: Mbes’urankunda cyane?

Couplet 6

Mwami, birambabaje k’Urukundo ngukunda ari ruke hanyuma y’urwo Rukundo rwawe.

Couplet 7

Ndagukunda buhoro, nubwo bidahebuje. Nuk’umpe kurushaho kugukunda, Mukiza.
