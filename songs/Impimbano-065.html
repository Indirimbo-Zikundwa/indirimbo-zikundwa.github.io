#65

Twambare ibigwanishwa Imana itanga

Impimbano· Sr Christella

Couplet 1

Twambar’ Ibigwanishw’ Iman’ itanga, kugira ngo dushobore guhagarara tudatsinzwe, n’ubugunge bwa wa Mugwanizi Satani.

Refrain

S: Kukw’abo tunigana, TS: s’abinyama n’amaraso, S: ariko tunigana, TS: n’abafis’ ububasha, S: bakaganza iy’isi, TS: yuzuye umwiza mwinshi, {S: n’impwemu mbi TS: z’ahantu ho mw’Ijuru.} x2

Couplet 2

Nuko rero dushikame dukenyej’ Ukuri, Twambay’ ukugororoka, nk’icuma gikingira igikiriza. Twambaye n’inkweto zivy’ Ubutumwa bubwiriza.

Refrain

S: Kukw’abo tunigana, TS: s’abinyama n’amaraso, S: ariko tunigana, TS: n’abafis’ ububasha, S: bakaganza iy’isi, TS: yuzuye umwiza mwinshi, {S: n’impwemu mbi TS: z’ahantu ho mw’Ijuru.} x2

Couplet 3

Mwabire inkinzo y’ ukwizera bene Data, niyo mushobora kuzimisha imyampi yose ya wa mubi. Kuko iyo myampi yose yak’ umuriro bene Data.

Refrain

S: Kukw’abo tunigana, TS: s’abinyama n’amaraso, S: ariko tunigana, TS: n’abafis’ ububasha, S: bakaganza iy’isi, TS: yuzuye umwiza mwinshi, {S: n’impwemu mbi z’ahantu TS: ho mw’Ijuru.} x2

Couplet 4

Mwabire inkofero y’Agakiza ka Yesu, be n’Inkota nziza ya Mpwemu Yera bene Data tugwanisha. Niyo Jambo ry’Imana twahawe tukiri mw’isi.

Refrain

S: Kukw’abo tunigana, TS: s’abinyama n’amaraso, S: ariko tunigana, TS: n’abafis’ ububasha, S: bakaganza iy’isi, TS: yuzuye umwiza mwinshi, {S: n’impwemu mbi z’ahantu TS: ho mw’Ijuru.} x2

Couplet 5

Musengere muri Mpwemu bene Data, ibihe vyose mu buryo bwose bwo gusenga no kwinginga. Kubw’ ivyo tuzoshobora guhagarara tudatsinzwe.

Refrain

{S: Mubibere maso, TS: mukobeje cane gose, S: kandi musengere, TS: n’Abera kw’isi yose, S: kugeza igihe TS: Yesu Azozira.} x2
