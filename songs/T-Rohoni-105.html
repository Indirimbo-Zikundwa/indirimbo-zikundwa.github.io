Kuwatafuta

Tenzi za Rohoni

Couplet 1

Kuwatafuta wasioweza, Kuomba wamrejee Yesu, Kuwaambia maneno yake, “Njooni kwangu, nawapenda”.

Refrain

Nitakwenda, (kwenda), niwatafute Waongofu (wa Bwana) wageuke, Waingie (wote) katika zizi La Mkombozi (wetu) Yesu Kristo.

Couplet 2

Kuwatafuta wasioweza, Waonyeshwe Mwokozi wetu, Kuwaongoza, wapate wote Uzima wa milele.

Refrain

Nitakwenda, (kwenda), niwatafute Waongofu (wa Bwana) wageuke, Waingie (wote) katika zizi La Mkombozi (wetu) Yesu Kristo.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Nitakwenda, (kwenda), niwatafute Waongofu (wa Bwana) wageuke, Waingie (wote) katika zizi La Mkombozi (wetu) Yesu Kristo.
