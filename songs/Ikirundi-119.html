#119

Yes’ andutir’ isi yose, Yampay’ Ubugingo

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yes’ andutir’ isi yose, Yampay’ Ubugingo; Ni we yam’amp’ inkomezi Ngo nshikame sintembe: Iyo ngowe nja kuri we Nta wund’ anduhura nka we, Nduzuy’ umunezero kukw’ankunda.

Couplet 2

Yes’ andutir’isi yose, Amfash’ iyo ngowe, Ni w’ahor’ amp’ imigisha Irut’ uko novuga; Ni w’atum’ izuba ryaka, Akamvubira n’imvura, Akampa kwam’ imbuto Kukw’ankunda.

Couplet 3

Yes’ andutir’ isi yose, Nzama mmukorera, Kw’ar’uwo kwizigirwa pe Nomuhemukira nte? Nzokwama mmukurikira, Ko nd’uwiwe nta co nzoba, Kand’ahor’ anzigama Kukw’ankunda.

Couplet 4

Yes’ andutir’ isi yose, N’ Umukunzi wanje, Nta wundi nobona nka we, Nzohora mmwizigira. Yampay’ ingabire nziza Y’ ubugingo budashira, Nduzuy’ umunezero kukw ankunda.
