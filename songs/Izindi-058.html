Nshima ndirimba ndanezerewe

Izindi

Couplet 1

Nshima ndirimba ndanezerewe kubwo guhimbaz’ Umwami wanjye,We wasiz’ Ubwiza bwe mu Ijuru, kubwo kunkiz’ uburetwa bwa Satani.

Refrain

Mwami wanjye Yesu, Ushimwe cyane Izina ryawe rihimbazwe cyane. /: Nguhay’ ubugingo bwanjye, Rinda Ugenzure intambwe zanjye Oh! Yesu ndakwihaye wese:/.
