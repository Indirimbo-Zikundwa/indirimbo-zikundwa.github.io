#305

Tshiena ne mukuabo

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Tshiena ne mukuabo Musungidi, Yesu wanyi ndi ndifila kuudi; Ntatu ne makenga pa buloba Kabiena mua kumpandulula nende.

Refrain

Musungidi iyaku undombole mu njila, Njila webe wa muoyo: Yesu kundekedi to. Yesu nkuata ku tshianza Tuya nebe mu diulu, Musoko wa disanka: Yesu kundekedi to.

Couplet 2

Bena lukuna ba pa buloba Basua kumpingaja anu mu midima; Musungidi wanyi iya pabuipi, Umpatula mu lufu lua kashidi.

Refrain

Musungidi iyaku undombole mu njila, Njila webe wa muoyo: Yesu kundekedi to. Yesu nkuata ku tshianza Tuya nebe mu diulu, Musoko wa disanka: Yesu kundekedi to.
