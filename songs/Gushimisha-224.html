#224

Wasiz’Ubwiza war’ufite mw’Ijuru

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Wasiz’Ubwiza war’ufite mw’Ijuru, Ubwo Wazaga mw’isi, Yesu; No mw’Ivuka ryawe nta hantu hariho hagutunganiye, Mwami.

Refrain

Ngwin’uze mu mutima wanjye, Umbemo, ndabikwemereye. Ngwin’utahe mu mutima wanjye, Wiboneremw’aho kuba.

Couplet 2

Mbes’Abamarayika ntibagushingije, ubwo Wimikwaga, Krisito? Ariko, Mwami, Wicishije bugufi, Utuvukir’utyo mw’isi!

Refrain

Ngwin’uze mu mutima wanjye, Umbemo, ndabikwemereye. Ngwin’utahe mu mutima wanjye, Wiboneremw’aho kuba.

Couplet 3

Dor’inyoni yos’igir’aho yarika, na y’ingunzu ntibur’intaho. Ariko Wowe, Mwami Yesu, Wabuze ah’urambik’ umusaya.

Refrain

Ngwin’uze mu mutima wanjye, Umbemo, ndabikwemereye. Ngwin’utahe mu mutima wanjye, Wiboneremw’aho kuba.

Couplet 4

Waj’Uzany’ Ijambo rizima, Krisito, ryo kubatur’ abantu bawe. Bakwitura kwang’ Urukundo rwaw’ubwo, bakumanika ku Giti.

Refrain

Ngwin’Ube mu mutima wanjye: Nizigiy’urwo rupfu rwawe ngwin’utahe mu mutima wanjye: Nizigiy’Umusaraba.

Couplet 5

Abari mw’Ijuru bazagusingiza, n’Uza kwima ya Ngoma yawe. Nzumv’ umbwir’uti: Mfit’aho nkugeneye, ngwino, wimane nanjy’ubu!

Refrain

Bizanezeza cyane, Yesu, ubwo nzumv’Ijwi ryawe ryiza! Bizanezeza cyane, Mukiza; Nzagusingiza, ntahwema!
