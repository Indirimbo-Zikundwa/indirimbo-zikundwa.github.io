#157

Har’uruzi runini cane

Umuco

Couplet 1

Har’uruzi runini cane, isoko yarwo ni Gologota; rutemba ruja muri Paradizo, ruzengurutswe n’ibiti ./.Ibitivy’ igikundiro./.

Refrain

Urwo ruzi ni Yesu Kristo, kandi niwe soko y’Agakiza akaba n’uruzi rw’Ubugingo, Atotesha ahagadutse ./.Akamenagura ahakomeye, amar’ inyota abanyotewe./.

Couplet 2

Har’uruzi runini cane, rudasa n’izindi nzuzi har’ ibiti iruhande yarwo, bidasa n’ibindi biti ./.Kandi bikisangiz’ imbuto./.

Refrain

Urwo ruzi ni Yesu Kristo, kandi niwe soko y’Agakiza akaba n’uruzi rw’Ubugingo, Atotesha ahagadutse ./.Akamenagura ahakomeye, amar’ inyota abanyotewe./.

Couplet 3

Har’uruzi runini cane, abatewe kuri rwo bose, bama batotahaye cane, barateye igomwe cane ./.Kubw’ ubuzima bw’ urwo ruzi./.

Refrain

Urwo ruzi ni Yesu Kristo, kandi niwe soko y’Agakiza akaba n’uruzi rw’Ubugingo, Atotesha ahagadutse ./.Akamenagura ahakomeye, amar’ inyota abanyotewe./.

Couplet 4

Ivyo biti n’abana b’Imana, urwo ruzi ni Yesu Kristo, abatewe kuri rwo bose, bama imbuto zitar’ iz’iy’isi ./.Barateye igomwe cane./.

Refrain

Urwo ruzi ni Yesu Kristo, kandi niwe soko y’Agakiza akaba n’uruzi rw’Ubugingo, Atotesha ahagadutse ./.Akamenagura ahakomeye, amar’ inyota abanyotewe./.

Couplet 5

Abadatewe muri rwo bose, barumaganye cane rwose, ntibigera ber’ imbuto, kuko badatewe kur’urwo ruzi ./.Nawe niwisuzume uruhande urimwo./.

Refrain

Urwo ruzi ni Yesu Kristo, kandi niwe soko y’Agakiza akaba n’uruzi rw’Ubugingo, Atotesha ahagadutse ./.Akamenagura ahakomeye, amar’ inyota abanyotewe./.
