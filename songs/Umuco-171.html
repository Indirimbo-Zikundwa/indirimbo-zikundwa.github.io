#171

Yemwe shengero ry’abazima

Umuco

Couplet 1

Yemwe Shengero ry’abazima, abashitse k’Umusaraba wera, dukovye mu gusenga cane, duhumuke amaso tubone, ./.Ibikorerwa mu Rugo rw’Imana./.

Refrain

Ntaho ntaho vyoshoboka, k’umukozi akora ata mpembo, kukw’ Imana Itagabitanya, ngo yibagir’ imiruho n’ubutame, twatamye kubw’ Izina ryayo ./.Non’impera yacu iri mw’Ijuru./.

Couplet 2

Yemwe Shengero ry’Abera, imbuto zose zitar’izu Mwami, n’ibikorwa bidakorerwa muri We vyose bizoshirana n’iy’isi, ./.Harahiriw’ abatamira mu Mwami./.

Refrain

Ntaho ntaho vyoshoboka, k’umukozi akora ata mpembo, kukw’ Imana Itagabitanya, ngo yibagir’ imiruho n’ubutame, twatamye kubw’ Izina ryayo ./.Non’impera yacu iri mw’Ijuru./.

Couplet 3

Wibuke ko inkomezi n’ubuzima vyose wabironse kubw’Ubuntu, ivy’ufise wabironse kubw’Ubuntu, wicishe bugufi imbere y’Imana ./.Uyishikanir’ uhinda agashitsi./.

Refrain

Ntaho ntaho vyoshoboka, k’umukozi akora ata mpembo, kukw’ Imana Itagabitanya, ngo yibagir’ imiruho n’ubutame, twatamye kubw’ Izina ryayo ./.Non’impera yacu iri mw’Ijuru./.

Couplet 4

Gir’umutima uhumuye cane, witonde mu gukorer’Imana, uyikorere n’umutima wawe wose wizigire k’uzohembwa mw’ijuru ./.Ubuntu n’ukuri ntibikuve hejuru./.

Refrain

Ntaho ntaho vyoshoboka, k’umukozi akora ata mpembo, kukw’ Imana Itagabitanya, ngo yibagir’ imiruho n’ubutame, twatamye kubw’ Izina ryayo ./.Non’impera yacu iri mw’Ijuru./.

Couplet 5

Ibihe biriko birihuta cane, ibimenyetso birashika vyihuta, witwararik’ ivy’Ubwami bw’Imana, ukorere Imana hakibona ./.Wirundaniriz’ itunga mw’Ijuru./.

Refrain

Ntaho ntaho vyoshoboka, k’umukozi akora ata mpembo, kukw’ Imana Itagabitanya, ngo yibagir’ imiruho n’ubutame, twatamye kubw’ Izina ryayo ./.Non’impera yacu iri mw’Ijuru./.
