#221

Bana mw’Ijuru tuzohabg’ ibintu vyinshi

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Bana, mw’Ijuru tuzohabg’ ibintu vyinshi ni twizer’ Umukiza wacu, Tuzambikw’ ibitsibo vy’intete nziza, Tuzambara n’ impuzu zera.

Refrain

Bana, tuzohabga, tuzohabga vyinshi, Tuzogabirwa vyinshi mw’Ijuru. Tuzama tunezerewe twese hamwe. Ni twizera Yesu We Mukiza.

Couplet 2

Tuzogendagenda mu miharur’irimwo Ifeza n’ izahabu nyinshi, Kandi Yesu ni we zuba ryaho ryiza, Ntituzobon’ ijoro mw’ijuru.

Refrain

Bana, tuzohabga, tuzohabga vyinshi, Tuzogabirwa vyinshi mw’Ijuru. Tuzama tunezerewe twese hamwe. Ni twizera Yesu We Mukiza.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Bana, tuzohabga, tuzohabga vyinshi, Tuzogabirwa vyinshi mw’Ijuru. Tuzama tunezerewe twese hamwe. Ni twizera Yesu We Mukiza.
