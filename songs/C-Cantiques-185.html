#185

Kulala tulu mu Yesu

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Kulala tulu mu Yesu Kudi disanka dinene, Kakuena makenga nansha, Kakuena mateta onso.

Couplet 2

Kulala tulu mu Yesu Kudi dikisha dinene, Ne ditekemena dimpe, Dia kuikalaku kashidi.

Couplet 3

Kulala tulu mu Yesu Ne kubika mudi Yesu, Kakuena bowa kashidi, Anu bukole bwa Yesu.

Couplet 4

Kulala tulu mu Yesu, Kubika kunshikidilu. Nkita ne yunzuludibwe, Ne babike ne disanka.
