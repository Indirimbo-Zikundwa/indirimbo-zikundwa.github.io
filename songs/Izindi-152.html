#152

Niwowe buhungiro bwanjye

Izindi

Couplet 1

Ni wowe Buhungiro bwanjye Mwami, ni wowe Mahoro yanjye. Igihe ngeze aho nanirirwa, ni wowe usubizamwo imbaraga.

Refrain

Mwami wanjye ndakwiringiye, kuko nziko udakoza isoni. Unshoboze ngukiranukire, kugezaho Uzagarukira.

Couplet 2

Ni wowe ubwawe wivugiye, ut’abarushe n’abaremerewe nimusange ndabaruhura, none nduhura imitwaro yanjye.

Refrain

Mwami wanjye ndakwiringiye, kuko nziko udakoza isoni. Unshoboze ngukiranukire, kugezaho Uzagarukira.

Couplet 3

Ariko uwakwizeye Mukiza, akagushiraho ivyiringiro, Ijambo ryawe uhora uryibuka, kandi Usohoza ivyo wavuze Yesu.

Refrain

Mwami wanjye ndakwiringiye, kuko nziko udakoza isoni. Unshoboze ngukiranukire, kugezaho Uzagarukira.
