#174

Uwirata yirate Kristo

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uwirata yirate Kristo, uwirata yirate Ubuntu; ni co cirato nyakuri, kukw’ inyama n’amaraso, bitazoragwa Ubwami bw’Imana.

Refrain

Uko nik’Uhoraho agize ng’ umunyabwenge yoye, kwirata ubwenge bwiwe; umunyenkomezi yoye, kwirat’ inkomezi ziwe; cank’ umutunzi yoye, kwirat’ ubutunzi bwiwe; ./.Arik’ uwirata yirate ko yamenye, Uhoraho n’ubushobozi bwiwe./.

Couplet 2

Uhoraho niwe umutima wawe ukwiriye kwirata; abaciye bugufi bazovyumva, banezerezwe n’ico, ico nico Uhoraho Ashaka.

Refrain

Uko nik’Uhoraho agize ng’ umunyabwenge yoye, kwirata ubwenge bwiwe; umunyenkomezi yoye, kwirat’ inkomezi ziwe; cank’ umutunzi yoye, kwirat’ ubutunzi bwiwe; ./.Arik’ uwirata yirate ko yamenye, Uhoraho n’ubushobozi bwiwe./.

Couplet 3

Niwe yakweretse iciza ic’ari co, akwerek’ivy’ umuhezagiro; akwereka ivy’umuvumo, niwe vyirato vyawe hose ugendane nawe wicishije bugufi.

Refrain

Uko nik’Uhoraho agize ng’umunyabwenge yoye, kwirata ubwenge bwiwe; umunyenkomezi yoye, kwirat’ inkomezi ziwe; cank’ umutunzi yoye, kwirat’ ubutunzi bwiwe; ./.Arik’ uwirata yirate ko yamenye, Uhoraho n’ubushobozi bwiwe./.

Couplet 4

Niw’aduha kuba muri Kristo, yaduhindukiy’ ubwenge nyakuri; yaduhindukiy’ ukugororoka kugira ngo tubone gucungurwa ng’uwirata yirate Kristo.

Refrain

Uko nik’Uhoraho agize ng’ umunyabwenge yoye, kwirata ubwenge bwiwe; umunyenkomezi yoye, kwirat’ inkomezi ziwe; cank’ umutunzi yoye, kwirat’ ubutunzi bwiwe; ./.Arik’ uwirata yirate ko yamenye, Uhoraho n’ubushobozi bwiwe./.

Couplet 5

Uwirata yirate Uhoraho, kuk’ umuntu yishimagiza, atari we yemerwa namba, ariko hemerwa uw’ Umwami, ni we vyirato vyawe hose.

Refrain

Uko nik’Uhoraho agize ng’ umunyabwenge yoye, kwirata ubwenge bwiwe; umunyenkomezi yoye, kwirat’ inkomezi ziwe; cank’ umutunzi yoye, kwirat’ ubutunzi bwiwe; ./.Arik’ uwirata yirate ko yamenye, Uhoraho n’ubushobozi bwiwe./.
