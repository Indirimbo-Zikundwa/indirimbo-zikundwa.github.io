#238

Abera baciye mu nzira ifunganye

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Abera baciye mu nzira ifunganye, Abera baciye mu vyo badatahura, Abera baciye mu bisig’ubwenge, Kuk’Uhoraho ni ko yabishimye

Refrain

Nshimir’Uhoraho kw’ar’ivyo yashimye, Nshimir’Uhoraho kw’ar’ivyo bimuhimbara, Nshimir’Uhoraho kw’ar’ivyo yanyuzemwo, Nshimir’Uhoraho kw’aduha kubinyuramwo.

Couplet 2

Nowa yaburiwe kwubak’ ubwato, Mu bisig’ubwenge yubak’ubwato, Kukw’Ijambo ry’igihe ciwe niko, ryamusaba, Abikora naho atabitahura.

Refrain

Umpe kuba Mwami mu batahura ivyawe, Sinze maramare ku musi uzogaruka, Nk’uko abo mu gihe ca Nowa vyagenze, Barimbuwe n’umwuzure bitwengera.

Couplet 3

Mu gihe cawe Mwami ni ko vyagenze, Wariko uravuga Ijambo bakwitegereza, Abafarisayo n’Abasadukayo, Ntibashobora kwizer’Ijambo ry’igihe cabo.

Refrain

Umpe Mwami ntegurwe n’Ijambo ryawe, Nshobore gutambuka uyo murongo, Kuko ataco nobarusha, Kiretse ngiriwe ubuntu nawe Mwami.

Couplet 4

Abera bene Data batubere akarorero, Ntitwishinge kwama tuguma muvyo, twamenye, Ahubwo twame duhinda agashitsi, Nk’abazi ko tuzobibazwa n’Imana..

Refrain

Twizigir’Imana mu bugingo bwacu, Ntiducobogore mu vyo twizera, Birtyo dushobore kwiteg’Umwami, N’agaruka kutuzamar’iwe mw’Ijuru.
