Abasenga ni benshi

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Abasenga ni benshi, bamwebaseng’ Imana, abandi nabo bagasenga Satani; Ikizobakwereka, uzobamenyera kuvyo bama n’ibikorwa bakora.

Refrain

Hahirwa abamesura, Imvune mu maraso Kugirango begere, igiti c’ubugingo Bazokwama bahimbaw’ibihe vyose.

Couplet 2

Refrain

Hahirwa abamesura, Imvune mu maraso Kugirango begere, igiti c’ubugingo Bazokwama bahimbaw’ibihe vyose.

Refrain

Hahirwa abamesura, Imvune mu maraso Kugirango begere, igiti c’ubugingo Bazokwama bahimbaw’ibihe vyose.

Couplet 4

Refrain

Hahirwa abamesura, Imvune mu maraso Kugirango begere, igiti c’ubugingo Bazokwama bahimbaw’ibihe vyose.

Couplet 5

Refrain
