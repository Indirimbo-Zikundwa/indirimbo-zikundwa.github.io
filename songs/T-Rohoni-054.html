#54

Yesu nataka kutakaswa sana

Tenzi za Rohoni

Couplet 1

Yesu nataka kutakaswa sana, Nataka moyo uwe enzi yako. Ukiangushe kilichoinuka Unioshe sasa niwe mweupe.

Refrain

Mweupe tu, ndiyo mweupe, Ukiniosha nitakuwa safi.

Couplet 2

Bwaba Yesu, unitazame sasa, Unifanye niwe dhabihu hai; Najitoa kwako, na moyo wote; Unioshe sasa niwe mweupe.

Refrain

Mweupe tu, ndiyo mweupe, Ukiniosha nitakuwa safi.

Couplet 3

Bwana kwa hiyo nakuomba sana, Nakungojea miguuni pako, Naomba unioshe damuni tu, Unioshe sasa niwe mweupe.

Refrain

Mweupe tu, ndiyo mweupe, Ukiniosha nitakuwa safi.

Couplet 4

Bwana nidumu kukungojea, Niumbie moyo safi, Ee Mungu, Wanaokujia hutupi kamwe, Unioshe sasa niwe mweupe.

Refrain

Mweupe tu, ndiyo mweupe, Ukiniosha nitakuwa safi.
