Rukundo rurut’ urundi

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Rukundo rurut’ urundi Ngwin’ utwuzure none, C’usohor’ urwanko rwacu. Ngwin’ utugereremwo.

Couplet 2

Yesu we, Munyakigongwe, Zan’ agakiza kawe, Shika mu mitima yacu Utwuzuz’ urukundo.

Couplet 3

Tuma Mpwemu wawe Yera Atubarir’ ivyawe, Tubone kuragw’ivyiza Wadusezeranije.

Couplet 4

Udukuremw’ agatima Kama gakund’ ivyaha, Ntidutwarwe na k’ukundi: B’ari wew’ utuganza.

Couplet 5

N’ uze se Soko y’ ubuntu, Kuko tugukumbuye, Ducik ivyaremwe bisha Bitagir’ agasembga.

Couplet 6

Tuzogutazira Mwami, Ni twashika mw ijuru, Dushiz’ibitsib’ imbere Y’ intebe yawe yera.
