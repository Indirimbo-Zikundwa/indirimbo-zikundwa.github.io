Ndaririmb’intsinzi

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Ndaririmb’Intsinzi, ndanezerewe cane, Narabohotse nkurwa mu nyigisho z’ibinyoma, Nd’uw’Umwami Yesu nawe nyene n’Uwanje, Agend’ Anyobora mur’ iyi Nzir’ifunganye.
