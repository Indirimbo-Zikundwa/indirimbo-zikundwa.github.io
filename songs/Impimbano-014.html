#14

Nzikomeza ku rutare niho har’ ubuhungiro

Impimbano· Fr Josué & Sr Joy MURUTA

Couplet 1

Nzikomeza ku Rutare niho har’Ubuhungiro, niho ntazanyiganyizwa mpaboner’ Uburuhukiro.

Refrain

Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, Umpishe mu Mababa yawe. Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, nanjye nzitwe intwari yawe by’ukuri.

Couplet 2

Umwijima n’uba mwinshi, nzikomeza ku Rutare. Azamurikira hose, ngendere mu bwiza bwe bwinshi.

Refrain

Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, Umpishe mu Mababa yawe. Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, nanjye nzitwe intwari yawe by’ukuri.

Couplet 3

Ninagira intege nke cyane, nzikomeza ku Rutare, Azanyongerer’ imbaraga ngir’umwete kubw’Ubuntu bwe.

Refrain

Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, Umpishe mu Mababa yawe. Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, nanjye nzitwe intwari yawe by’ukuri.

Couplet 4

Naho haz’Intambara nyinshi, nzikomeza ku Rutare. Azandwanirira ndabizi, ngende nesha kubw’Izina rye.

Refrain

Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, Umpishe mu Mababa yawe. Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, nanjye nzitwe intwari yawe by’ukuri.

Couplet 5

Ndashimira cyan’Urutare, kuko rwamenetse ku bwanjye. Kandi rwamberey’ Ubwihisho, ndahimbaz’ Imbaraga zarwo.

Refrain

Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, Umpishe mu Mababa yawe. Nkomeza Mwami wanjye, nanjye nzitwe intwari yawe by’ukuri.
