#40

Dushime cane izina ryawe

Umuco

Couplet 1

Dushime cane Izina ryawe, wewe Mukiza wacu wewe Bugingo bwacu; turagushengereye ng’Udufashe,

Refrain

Eh’imitima ikwifuza ng’Uyifashe, none Yifashe; ngwino, ngwino, ngwino Yesu dufashe, ngwino ngo.

Couplet 2

Namwe bana b’amasezerano, turirimbire Yesu turirimbe n’ishimwe; duh’ Icubahiro Uhoraho duti:

Refrain

Eh’imitima ikwifuza ng’Uyifashe, none Yifashe; ngwino, ngwino, ngwino Yesu dufashe, ngwino ngo.

Couplet 3

Namwe mwes’ abakiwe nuy’umuco, tukaronka ubwo buntu, enda tumwunamire; ni we Mukiza wacu aganza vyose.

Refrain

Eh’imitima ikwifuza ng’Uyifashe, none Yifashe; ngwino, ngwino, ngwino Yesu dufashe, ngwino ngo.

Couplet 4

Dushim’Uhorah’imisi yose, twikine amatavu kubw’ Umuco twakiwe; Umuco wo kukagoroba waratse.

Refrain

Eh’imitima ikwifuza ng’Uyifashe, none Yifashe; ngwino, ngwino, ngwino Yesu dufashe, ngwino ngo.

Couplet 5

Umunsi tuzoshik’ iy’iwacu mw’Ijuru, aho Yesu ari tuzomushimagiza; duh’ Icubahiro Uhoraho duti:

Refrain

Haleluya, Haleluya, Haleluya Nushimwe Yesu; Shimwa, Shimwa, Shimwa Yesu n’Ushimwe ibihe vyose.
