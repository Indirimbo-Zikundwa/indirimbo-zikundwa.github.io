#181

Mwa nshuti za Yesu mwe

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mwa nshuti za Yesu mwe, nimuze, twamamaze hos’umunezero wacu, nukw’ Indirimbo z’ishimwe zizamuke ziger’i Bwami mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Turajy’i Siyoni, ku Murwa mwiza w’Imana, tunyur’Inzir’ijya mw’Ijuru: Ku Murwa w’Imana yacu.

Couplet 2

Utaz’Imana yacu yaziririmb’ate se? N’abana b’Umwami basa, bashobora gusingiza Uwabacunguye, nkuko bikwiriye.

Refrain

Turajy’i Siyoni, ku Murwa mwiza w’Imana, tunyur’Inzir’ijya mw’Ijuru: Ku Murwa w’Imana yacu.

Couplet 3

Umurwa w’i Siyoni wuzuy’ibyiza byinshi, kandi mu nzir’iganayo. Tutaragera mw’Ijuru, twahaw’ingwate yabyo ngo tunezerwe.

Refrain

Turajy’i Siyoni, ku Murwa mwiza w’Imana, tunyur’Inzir’ijya mw’Ijuru: Ku Murwa w’Imana yacu.

Couplet 4

Muhumure noneho! Nimuze, twishimane. Tugende turirimbana, duca mu bwatsi bwa Yesu; Tugiye mw’is’irut’iyi, ni mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Turajy’i Siyoni, ku Murwa mwiza w’Imana, tunyur’Inzir’ijya mw’Ijuru: Ku Murwa w’Imana yacu.
