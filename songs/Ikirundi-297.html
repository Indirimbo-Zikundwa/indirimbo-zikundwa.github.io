#297

Twebge tur’ abana gusa

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Twebge tur’ abana gusa ariko tur’ aba Yesu: Mbega har’ ico twokora Comugirir’ ikimazi?

Couplet 2

Kiriho, kuko ni twumva Uburake mu mitima, Tukabunesha kubgiwe, Tuba tumushimishije.

Couplet 3

Ni twagarur’ukuboko Ngo twoye gukubitana, Tugatekereza gusa, Tuba tumushimishije.

Couplet 4

Ni twaguman’ amahoro Tuzomuber’ udutara Two kumurik’ i munira; Na vyo biramushimisha.

Couplet 5

Naho tur’ abana gusa, Ntihobur’ ico twokora Coshimish’ umwami Yesu Yapfiriye kudukiza.
