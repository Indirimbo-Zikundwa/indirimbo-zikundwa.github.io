#34

Ndusheho kukwegera Mukiza

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ndusheho kukwegera, Mukiza, ne kukuba kure: Nkwishingikirizeho, Mukiza, Ungendeshe neza.

Couplet 2

Nerekan’Icyubahiro cyawe mu byo nkora byose; Akanwa kanjye kajye kavuga ibyiz’Umbwiriza.

Couplet 3

Umpindure nk’isoko y’ibyiza, itemb’idakama; He kugir’undi nkorer’iteka, Databuja Mwiza.

Couplet 4

Kand’imigambi yanjye n’inama Ubyigandurire; Nyemeza k’ur’Umwami Wenyine Utegeka byose.

Couplet 5

Ni Wowe mutsima w’Ubugingo n’amazi mazima; Iyo nishwe n’inzara n’inyota, ni Wow’ujy’ Umpaza.

Couplet 6

Ni Wow’uhaza kwifuza kwiza, kose niyumvamo. Ni tujy’i wawe, nta cyo, Mukiza, tuzifuz’ukundi.
