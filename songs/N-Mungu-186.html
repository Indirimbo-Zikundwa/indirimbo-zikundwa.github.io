Nisadiki Yesu Kristo

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Nisadiki Yesu Kristo sikuzote duniani, Hata na imani ndogo, nisadiki Yesu pekee.

Refrain

Nisadiki Yesu pekee, nisadiki sikuzote, Kati ya bahari gani, nisadiki Yesu pekee.

Refrain

Nisadiki Yesu pekee, nisadiki sikuzote, Kati ya bahari gani, nisadiki Yesu pekee.

Refrain

Nisadiki Yesu pekee, nisadiki sikuzote, Kati ya bahari gani, nisadiki Yesu pekee.

Refrain

Nisadiki Yesu pekee, nisadiki sikuzote, Kati ya bahari gani, nisadiki Yesu pekee.
