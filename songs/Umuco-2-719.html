#719

Imiruho n’ubutame

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Imiruho n’ubutame vyo mur’iyi si, Hazogera yemw’ ibihe Tubiruhuke; Kandi yemwe ivyo Bihe biregereje Ko bishika.

Refrain

Ntegereje ibihe vyiza vyo kubamwo, Maze nkinjira nkabon’Ahera; Ah’Umwami Yesu Avyagiye.

Couplet 2

Nimumenye kw’Abazoshika mw’ivyo bihe, N’Abazoba bemangiy’ Ukuri Kuzima; Bakemera kwikorera umusaraba w’Umukiza.

Refrain

Ntegereje ibihe vyiza vyo kubamwo, Maze nkinjira nkabon’Ahera; Ah’Umwami Yesu Avyagiye.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Ntegereje ibihe vyiza vyo kubamwo, Maze nkinjira nkabon’Ahera; Ah’Umwami Yesu Avyagiye.

Couplet 4

Bibereye ko twuzura Ivyizigiro, Vy’iri Jambo ritwerek’ Inzira y’Ukuri; Bizotume Dusanganir’ Umwami Yesu, Twiteguye.

Refrain

Ntegereje ibihe vyiza vyo kubamwo, Maze nkinjira nkabon’Ahera; Ah’Umwami Yesu Avyagiye.

Couplet 5

Refrain

Ntegereje ibihe vyiza vyo kubamwo, Maze nkinjira nkabon’Ahera; Ah’Umwami Yesu Avyagiye.

Couplet 6

Tegerez’Ibihe Vyiza twiyeze cane, Tubandane Ubwo Bwami burabandanirwa; Ababunyakura bose babunyakurisha Ubuhizi.

Refrain

Ntegereje ibihe vyiza vyo kubamwo, Maze nkinjira nkabon’Ahera; Ah’Umwami Yesu Avyagiye.

Couplet 7

Ntegereje ibihe vyiza vyo kubamwo, Maze nkinjira nkabon’Ahera; Ah’Umwami Yesu Avyagiye.
