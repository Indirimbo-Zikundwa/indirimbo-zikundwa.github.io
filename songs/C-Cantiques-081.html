#81

Tutumbishayi Yesu

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Tutumbishayi Yesu, Mfumu wa mfumu yonso, Mukalenge wa Butumbi, Nzambi wa tshiendelele; Bikondo ne bikondo, Kena ushintuluka Ne kena ukulakaja, Yeye udi momumue.

Refrain

{Yesu Kilisto atumbe} x2 Mu diulu ne pa buloba, Atumbe tshiendelele, Mfumu wa ba mfumu, atumbe!

Couplet 2

Diulu ne buloba ne Mayi manene onso, Mitoto, diba ne ngondo, Ne bidi momu bionso; Bifukibua bia Yesu, Tutumbishayi Yesu, Tuzanjikayi Kilisto Mu butumbi kashidi.

Refrain

{Yesu Kilisto atumbe} x2 Mu diulu ne pa buloba, Atumbe tshiendelele, Mfumu wa ba mfumu, atumbe!

Couplet 3

Banunu ne bansonga, Balume ne bakaji, Banene nansha bakese, Babanji ne bapele; Bamfumu ne bapika, Ne badishikamine, Bonso tutumbishayi Yesu, Mukalenge munene.

Refrain

{Yesu Kilisto atumbe} x2 Mu diulu ne pa buloba, Atumbe tshiendelele, Mfumu wa ba mfumu, atumbe!

Couplet 4

Bumfumu bua Kilisto Budi bua tshiendelela, Ne bukalenga bua Yesu Kabuena ne ndekelu; Makokeshi a bantu Adi mu Tshianza tshiandi, Ne Yeye udi ku mutu Kua makokeshi onso.

Refrain

{Yesu Kilisto atumbe} x2 Mu diulu ne pa buloba, Atumbe tshiendelele, Mfumu wa ba mfumu, atumbe!
