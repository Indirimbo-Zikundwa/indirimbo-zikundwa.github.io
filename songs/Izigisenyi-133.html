Sinabona uko ngushima

Iz'i Gisenyi

Couplet 1

Sinabona uko ngushima Mwami kuba waramvanye mw’isayo y’ibyaha ntanubwo nibona nk’intumbi y’imbaragasa Mwami ukaba warangize umwana wawe.

Refrain

S: Waranyiyeretse umara ubwoba ndahamya yuko utazandimbura. TS: Abo uzarimbura ntiwabihishurira nubwo naba mubi imbere y’abantu. Wowe wambyaye ntuzanyihakana kuko n’ubundi Wankunze ntacyo unshiye.x2
