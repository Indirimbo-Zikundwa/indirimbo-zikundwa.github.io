#30

I wanna go to heaven

Impimbano· Fr René

Couplet 1

I wanna go to Heaven brighter than the sun, I wanna meet my Savior up above, when i’ll meet my king. What a joy shall fill in my heart, what a joy shall fill in my heart, when i meet my King above. When i’ll see his Face in Glory, I’ll be singing forever more.
