Uhoraho Mana turagushimiye

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Uhoraho Mana turagushimiye, Ku bw’igikorwa cawe, ku bw’imigambi yawe, Uhoraho Mana, turagushimiye, Ku bw’Ijambo ryawe: manu yanyegejwe.

Refrain

Uhoraho ur’Imana... Imana yihisha, Uwo utihishuriye ntashobora kukumenya, Uwo wihishuriye, Asanga uko wari ejo, Ariko uri uyu musi, Kandi ariko uzokwama, Ibihe bidashira.

Couplet 2

Turagushimiye ko watwihishuriye, Ukaturongoza inkingi y’umuriro, Ukaduha manu nsha y’igihe cacu, Ni ryo Jambo ryawe: jambo rihishuwe.
