#170

Igihe kiriko kirihuta cane

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Igihe kiriko kirihuta cane, Mwami tegura Ishengero ryawe, Ubwami bwawe buregereje, None ambika Ishengero ryawe.

Refrain

Ehe Uraje Urihuta cane, Guha impera abakozi bawe, Gwisha imvura mu mbuto zawe, Ababagara babagare, Abimbuzi bimbure, Kukw’igihe gishize.
