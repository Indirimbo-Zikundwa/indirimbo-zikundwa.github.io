Nariboney’ikiganza c’umwami

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Nariboney’ikiganza c’Umwami wanje Yesu Kristo, igihe nari mu Mwiza, nd’ikuzimu kwa Satani, Yankujey’ Ubushobozi Bwiwe bwinshi butovugwa, Yangiriy’ Imbabazi nyinshi.

Refrain

(Reka, reka) Reeeka ngushimire Mwami Yesu, Kuba Warampaye Ukubaho nizey’ Ijambo Rizima ry’Igihe c’Akagoroba, Ntaco mfise noguha gikwiye.
