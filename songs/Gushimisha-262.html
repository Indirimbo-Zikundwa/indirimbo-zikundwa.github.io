#262

Umva gusaba kwanjye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Umva gusaba kwanjye, Mwuka w’imbaraga shyir’ Ububasha bwawe mu mutima wanjye. Mwuka Wera, hishura ibyaha bindimo; Mpesh’ Ubugingo bushya, ibyaha bimvemo.

Couplet 2

Za mu mutima wanjye mbese, nabaho nte, ntagufite ng’umbemo? Najya nizera nte? Mwuka, nyobora neza: Nzajya nkwemerera; Ump’ Ubugingo bushya n’umutima mwiza.

Couplet 3

Ne kongera gutinya iby’Umwanzi mubi, kuk’Ukunda kumfasha, Ukampa kunesha. Mwuka, nyemez’ ibyawe mu mutima wanjye; Uwuzuz’ Ubugingo no kunesha byose.
