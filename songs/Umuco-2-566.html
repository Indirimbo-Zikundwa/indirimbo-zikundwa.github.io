#566

N’aho ngendera hagati y’amarushwa

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Naho ngendera hagati y’amarushwa, Uhorah’ Imana yanj’ Izonzigama, Kukw’ari Yo Yantoranije kera, Ikamp’ Agakiza kayo ku Buntu.

Couplet 2

Ngendera mw’isi yiganziwe n’Umubi, kuyibamwo wizera birarusha.

Couplet 3

Ariko nzi yuk’Uhorah’ Ahambaye, Ari ku ruhande rw’abamwubaha.

Couplet 4

Iyi si ni ko yahoze kuva kera, kuv’icaha kimaze kuyinjiramwo, Abana b’Imana vyamye bibagora, kuko bagwanir’ Ukwizera kwabo.

Couplet 5

Is’iraremerewe n’ivyaha vyinshi, nko mu gihe c’i Sodomu n’i Gomora; Harageze kw’Iman’ Itabar’Abayo, Ibakiz’ Uburake bugiye kuza.

Couplet 6

Mur’iki gihe tugezemwo ca nyuma, Twebw’ Ab’Imana Yagiriye Ubuntu, Dukwiye kugir’ ikanguro mu mitima, kandi tukuzura na Mpwemu w’Imana.

Couplet 7

Ndatakambish’ Uhoraho ijwi ryanje, ijwi ryanje ndaryingingish’ Uhoraho, Nipfuza kugenda nesh’uyu mubiri, Kugira mbeho mpimbar’ Umwami wanje.
