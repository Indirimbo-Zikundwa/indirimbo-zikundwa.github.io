#78

Kera har’ umubivyi yasohoy’ imbuto

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Kera har’ umubivyi yasohoy’ imbuto zimwe zigwa ku nzira, zinobga n’inyoni, Izindi ku rutare, zumishwa n’izuba, Kuko zari zibuze ahantu habomvye.

Couplet 2

Mu mahwa hagw’ izindi, zikurana na yo, Ac’ azinyoha zose, nia co zamye na mba. Arikw izindi zigwa mw ivu ryiza cane, Zimeze zirakura zam’ imbuto nyinshi.

Couplet 3

Har’ uwundi Mubivyi, n’Unnikiza Yesu, Jjambo ry’agakiza ni ryo mbut abiba, Kand’ imitima yacu ni yo mbibe yiwe, Ari mib’ari myiza, amija mw’ imbuto.

Couplet 4

Kand’ibiganza vyiwe n’ibirenge vyiwe Bitonyang’amaraso arikw arabiba, Imvura ni y’ imeza imbuto z’i>i si, Amaras’ akameza izo mu mitima.

Couplet 5

Mugenzi, har’ibiki mu mutima wawe? Harimw’ inzira nyinshi? harimw’urutare? Canke n’imbibe nziza, mbeea harinjiye? Wamamw’imbuto nziza, cank’ umez’ amahwa?

Couplet 6

Hazob’ umusi Yesu azoz^ kwinibura, Azozan’urutaro mu kuboko kwiwe. Azimburir’ ingano mu kigega ciwe, Umuguruka na wo azowuturira.

Couplet 7

Mugenz’ uzoja hehe? Ni wiyumvire se Uzoja mu kigega, canke mu muriro? Ni wihan’ uyu musi, ejo ntihahera Uyu n’umusi mvviza wo gukirizwako.
