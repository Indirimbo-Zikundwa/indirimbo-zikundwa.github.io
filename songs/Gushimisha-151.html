#151

Yes’Arush’ abandi bose

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yes’Arush’ abandi bose—Kudukunda; Arush’ukw’ abavandimwe—Bakundana. Nahw’izindi nshuti zacu zaduhararuka zose, Yesu ntabw’Ahemukira—Ab’akunda.

Couplet 2

N’iki gihesh’ubugingo—Budashira? S’ukumenya Yesu wacu—Udukunda? Twarazimiye nk’intama. Yes’Aradushakashaka, Atubony’ Atwinjiz’ubwo—mu Rugo rwe.

Couplet 3

Tumumenye kw’ar’ Inshuti—Yishimira kuduh’ imigisha yose—N’imbabazi. Imitima yac’ikunda kumvir’iyi Nshuti yacu no kumwizigira cyane—tudatinya.

Couplet 4

Kand’ibyaha byacu byari—bikabije: Yes’Aterwa n’Urukundo—Kudupfira: Ni byo bituneshereza; Ni byo biturind’ ibibi, tukayoboka yanzira—Iger’i We.
