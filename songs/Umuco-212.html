#212

Mburanya kandi untsinde

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mburanya, kandi untsinde; kuk’ urubanza rwatanguriye, mu b’inzu yawe Mana, nkubita kandi unsubire ./.Kukw’ inkoni yawe Mana, ariy’ inyobora Mwami (vy’ukuri)./.

Refrain

Muri Wewe, niho har’ inyishu, muri Wewe, niho hari vyose; muri Wewe nta oya canke ego, muri wewe hari “Amen” ./.Unkubite kand’ unsubireee, kukw’ indagizo, yawe Mwami, ariy’ inyobora./.

Couplet 2

Ndemeye gutsindwa n’Ijambo, kuk’urubanza rwawe rukomeye, mu b’inzu yawe Mana, nkubita kandi unsubire ./.Kukw’inkoni yawe Mana, ariy’inyobora Mwami (vy’ukuri)./.

Refrain

Muri Wewe, niho har’ inyishu, muri Wewe, niho hari vyose; muri Wewe nta oya canke ego, muri wewe hari “Amen” ./.Unkubite kand’ unsubireee, kukw’ indagizo, yawe Mwami, ariy’ inyobora./.

Couplet 3

Mpa kwubah’ Ijambo ryawe, mpa kwubaha mwene Data, mpa kuba mu kibanza canje, unyobore wame unyobora ./.Kukw’ Ijambo ryawe Mana, ari ryo rimurikira./.

Refrain

Muri Wewe, niho har’ inyishu, muri Wewe, niho hari vyose; muri Wewe nta oya canke ego, muri wewe hari “Amen” ./.Unkubite kand’ unsubireee, kukw’ indagizo, yawe Mwami, ariy’ inyobora./.

Couplet 4

Umwe wese, azobazw’ ivyiwe, umwe wese azoburanywa, twemere gutsindwa n’Ijambo, umwe wese yubahe mwene Se ./.Kukw’Ijambo ry’Imana Yera, ari ryo rimurikira./.

Refrain

Muri Wewe, niho har’ inyishu, muri Wewe, niho hari vyose; muri Wewe nta oya canke ego, muri wewe hari “Amen” ./.Unkubite kand’ unsubireee, kukw’ indagizo, yawe Mwami, ariy’ inyobora./.
