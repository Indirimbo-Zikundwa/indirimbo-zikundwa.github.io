#160

Ntituz’umusi umwami azogarukirako

Izindi

Couplet 1

Ntituz’umusi Umwami Azogarukirako, Umusi w’ubukwe w’Ishengero ryiwe. Nitwitegurire kurindira ico gihe, Tube twiteguye kumusanganira.

Refrain

Ubukwe-Ubukwe bw’Umwami nibushika, Tuzonezererwa twese muri ubwo Bugeni mw’Ijuru tuzoruhuka.

Couplet 2

Twibukire kuri za nkumi zari icumi, batanu muri bo bari ibijuju. Batagira amavuta mu matara yabo, nyuma bananirwa kumusanganira.

Refrain

Ubukwe-Ubukwe bw’Umwami nibushika, Tuzonezererwa twese muri ubwo Bugeni mw’Ijuru tuzoruhuka.

Couplet 3

Natwe twibaze ku vyamwa vy’ibikorwa vyacu, niba bikwiriye Ubukwe bw’Umwami. Tube twiteguye kuja kumusanganira, canke gusigara umuryango wugaye.

Refrain

Ubukwe-Ubukwe bw’Umwami nibushika, Tuzonezererwa twese muri ubwo Bugeni mw’Ijuru tuzoruhuka.
