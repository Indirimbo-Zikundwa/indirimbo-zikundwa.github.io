#324

Nzoririmb’ ivy’Umukiza wanje

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Nzoririmb’ ivy’Umukiza wanje n’Urukundo rwiwe yankunze Yambabarijwe ku Musaraba Ngo nkir’ igihano c’Urupfu.

Refrain

Nzama ndirimb’ Umukiza wanje Yanguz’ amaraso ku giti, Nari n’ umwend’ukomeye cane Maz’ arawunyishurira.

Couplet 2

Nzokwama mvug’ ivy’ igitangaza Yes’ Umukiza yankoreye, Umutima wiwe w’ ikigongwe Watumy’ ampfir’ amber’ incungu.

Refrain

Nzama ndirimb’ Umukiza wanje Yanguz’ amaraso ku giti, Nari n’ umwend’ukomeye cane Maz’ arawunyishurira.

Couplet 3

Nzohora ntazii’ uwo Mukiza, Nzoririmb’ ububasha bgiwe, Kukw anneshereza muri vyose, Sinkiganzwa n’ icaha na mba.

Refrain

Nzama ndirimb’ Umukiza wanje Yanguz’ amaraso ku giti, Nari n’ umwend’ukomeye cane Maz’ arawunyishurira.

Couplet 4

Nzoririmb’ Umucunguzi wanje, N’urukundo rw’igitangaza: Yankijij’ urubanza rwo gupfa, Maz’ akangir’ umwana wiwe.

Refrain

Nzama ndirimb’ Umukiza wanje Yanguz’ amaraso ku giti, Nari n’ umwend’ukomeye cane Maz’ arawunyishurira.
