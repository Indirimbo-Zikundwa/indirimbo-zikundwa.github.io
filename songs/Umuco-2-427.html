Yesu Kristo n’igitangaza

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Yesu Kristo n’igitangaza, Umujanama w’abizera, Ni We Mana y’Abera bose, Data wa twes’ari mw’Ijuru, {Ni na We soko y’Ubugingo, Idudubiz’ ibihe vyose, Yashizeh’ amatanguriro, Ni We Mwami w’Ijuru n’isi.} x2

Couplet 2

Ni We yamyeho kand’ariho, Ariho kand’azokwamaho, Ni We Alufa na Omega, Kandi ni We agiye kuza, {Afise n’inkot’ ikarishe, N’Ijambo ryiza Rihishuwe, Uwo ni We Mwami dusenga, Ni Data Mwiz’ Asumba vyose.} x2

Couplet 3

Ewe Mwami w’icubahiro, Waduhaye izina ryiza, Ivyoba Mwami biduhagije, N’uko twokwuzur’ Ubugingo, {Waducishije mur’iyi si, Iyi si yuzuy’ umwijima, Nta ciza kiyirimwo, Mwami, Dufashe tugende tunesha.} x2

Couplet 4

Couplet 5

Couplet 6

Couplet 7
