#76

Yesu wiya mu makumbi

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Yesu wiya mu makumbi, Wiyanga kubikala. Bonso bakamumonanga, Wiya mu ntumbo yandi. {Aleluya! Yesu ukabila.} x2

Couplet 2

Pungi Yandi ukavuma Ne diwi dikatampe, Bonso bakumutengela Bakatekwinwa mulu. {Aleluya! Yesu ukabikala.} x2

Couplet 3

Bakumushikwa badila Pakaya Mwana Leza, Bakatyibilwanga nsambu Ne kukaponejibwa. {Aleluya! Yesu ukabikala.} x2

Couplet 4

Bonso bakumutengela, Teja matwi kwishinda, Tukevwana Diwi Dyandi, Shi twi bomi shi bafwe. Tukaselwa kwenda mulu mwa Leza, Kubikala na Mfumwetu nyeke-ke.
