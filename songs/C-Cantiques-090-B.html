#90B

Elili ƞahi aƞanakeni

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Elili ŋahi aŋanakeni, Antambuli na mashi a Chamwinu? Akanfwili ami naluwa, Yami namukojejeli Mwanta. O luwi ho-o! Eyi Nzambi wantiyilili, Anfwili Yesu Mwaneyi, ami nahayama.

Refrain

O luwi ho-o, Nzambi yami, Wuhani Mwaneyi hadami!

Couplet 2

Kuhayama! Sawumi hakufwa! Muntwinyi atweshi kulumbulula? Hel’aŋelu anakaŋanyi. Kushinshika mwakeŋayi Nzambi. O luwi ho-o! Mpata yonsu yilemeshaŋa, Ejim’aleki kweseka cheŋi kwilukamu.

Refrain

O luwi ho-o, Nzambi yami, Wuhani Mwaneyi hadami!

Couplet 3

Washil’ itanda, ŋovu ni Mpuhu, Nswana wadibondeliyi yejima; Welili muntu wadizoza, Wafwilili muchidi wa Adama. O luwi ho-o! O chilwatoha, lwabadika, Lwakunkeŋa, lwakunlonda, nilwakuŋamwina!

Refrain

O luwi ho-o, Nzambi yami, Wuhani Mwaneyi hadami!

Couplet 4

Nadiŋi nduŋu hanalehi ho, Natanyini nanshidi yachisemwa; Wantoŋeluhu,wankuulili, Hinukwila mwana wakuhanda. O luwi ho-o! Wuduŋu wanshidi wamana, Nendaŋa nakudikunda naChamwinu Yesu.

Refrain

O luwi ho-o, Nzambi yami, Wuhani Mwaneyi hadami!

Couplet 5

Nayiŋi dehi! Nikweti womaku, Aŋitiyila mudi Kristu Yesu. Mudi Yena nahandilaŋa, Naloŋa namudimu wa Mwanta. Natweshi kukundama na Nzambi Wajila, Wuswana wami mudi Kristu nakatambula.

Refrain

O luwi ho-o, Nzambi yami, Wuhani Mwaneyi hadami!
