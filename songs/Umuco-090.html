#90

Ewe ncuti wahamagawe

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ewe ncuti wahamagawe, uhamagarirw’ ivyiza vyo mw’Ijuru; wamaze gutorany’ ivy’ Ubugingo, hamagara abandi babibone.

Refrain

Hamagarish’ Urukundo rwawe, hamagarisha intahe yawe; hamagarish’ ibikorwa vyawe, hamagarish’ Ubutumwa Bwiza.

Couplet 2

Imana Yaguhaye Ubugingo bwerekane abandi babubone. Imana Yaguhaye Umuco, werekane abandi bawubone.

Refrain

Hamagarish’ Urukundo rwawe, hamagarisha intahe yawe; hamagarish’ ibikorwa vyawe, hamagarish’ Ubutumwa Bwiza.

Couplet 3

N’uririmbe Umuco wakiriye, kandi shingir’ intahe Ukuzuka; semerera ut’ Umwami Yarazutse, bizer’ Umwami Yabapfiriye.

Refrain

Hamagarish’ Urukundo rwawe, hamagarisha intahe yawe; hamagarish’ ibikorwa vyawe, hamagarish’ Ubutumwa Bwiza.

Couplet 4

None ko bugira bwire ncuti, umaze gukorer’ Imana iki? Mbega ncuti yanje ko bugira bwire, uzomwerek’ iminyag’ iyihe?

Refrain

Hamagarish’ Urukundo rwawe, hamagarisha intahe yawe; hamagarish’ ibikorwa vyawe, hamagarish’ Ubutumwa Bwiza.

Couplet 5

Garagaza igikorwa cawe, raba nta gihe gisigaye; Mwerekane bigishoboka uwukora akora ku murango.

Refrain

Hamagarish’ Urukundo rwawe, hamagarisha intahe yawe; hamagarish’ ibikorwa vyawe, hamagarish’ Ubutumwa Bwiza.
