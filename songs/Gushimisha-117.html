#117

Numvise Yes’Ambwir’ati

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Numvise Yes’Ambwir’ Ati: Ngwino nkuruhure. Zana ibyo bikuruhije, mbikwikorerere! musang’uko nari nd’uko; Ndushye mfite ishavu; Ambera Uburuhukiro, Ampa umunezero.

Couplet 2

Numvise Yesu Ambwir’ Ati: Nguherey’ Ubuntu amaz’azan’ Ubugingo, unyw’uram’ iteka. Njy’aho ari, nywa nezerewe kuri ayo mazi ye umutima ushira inyota, mpabwa Ubugingo bwe.

Couplet 3

Numvise Yesu Ambwir’ Ati: Ni jye Mucyo w’isi. Wavukiye mu isi, wavukiye mu mwijima, ngwin’ umurikirwe. Ndebye kuri Yesu mbona ko ari Izuba ryanjye rizamvira iminsi yose y’Ubugingo bwanjye.
