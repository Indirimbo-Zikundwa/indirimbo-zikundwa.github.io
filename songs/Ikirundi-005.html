#5

Twese dushim’ Umwam’ Imana

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Twese dushim’ Umwam’ Imana ni tuyisenge, Kukw ari yo yaremy’ ivyo mw isi n’ ivy’ ijuru; Yesu Mwami, wez’ imitima yacu Ngo turirimbir’ Imana.

Couplet 2

Ni tumushim’ Umwam’ Imana ni tuyisenge, Kw ari y’ iganza nez’ ivyo mu bihugu vyose; Yam’ iduha ibidukwiye vyose, Twizer’ iryo jambo ryiza.

Couplet 3

Ni tumushim’ Umwam’ Imana, ni tuyisenge; Yaraturemye, ni yo yaduhay’ ubugingo; Iduh’ amahoro mu vyago vyacu, N’ Iman’ igir’ imbabazi.

Couplet 4

Ni tumushim’ Umwam’ Imana, ni tuyisenge; Ibihumeka vyose bishim’ izina ryayo; Tuyikunde kukw ar’ umuco wacu; Tuyiseng’ imisi yose.
