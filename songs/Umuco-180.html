Duga nawe mwene data

Umuco

Couplet 1

Duga nawe mwene Data usigaye, k’umusozi w’abera Siyoni, urabe kandi witegereze, (Ubone kandi utahure neza) Ivyo wizeye ivy’ari vyo, (Ubone kandi utahure neza) Umwami wawe uw’ari We, (Ubone kandi utahure neza) Amasezerano ay’ari yo, (Ubone kandi utahure neza.)

Refrain

Eh’Abera bashagaje, k’umusozi w’Abera Siyoni, amaso yabo ntagoheka, amatwi yabo yama yiteze kwumva; ijwi ry’inzamba y’iherezo, ryerekana ko vyose bishize, umusi w’agakiza kacu, tuzoba turangije intambara ./.Nguy’ uraje nguy’ uraje, nguy’ uraje Haleluya!./.
