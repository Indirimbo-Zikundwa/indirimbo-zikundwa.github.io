Inzira y’imiruho

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Inzira y’imiruho ku bizera, Ibazanira guhimbar’Imana, Kukw’inzira yoroshe ku bizera, ituma baba abahwahutse.

Couplet 2

Abagiye baba abahizi, Na bo iyo niyo yabay’Inzira, Natwe bidukwiye kuba nka bo, Kugira tuzoshobore kubana.

Couplet 3

Ihanga ry’Uhoraho kuva kera, Imiruho ni yo yariranga, Natwe biradukwiye kuba nka bo, Tukiri mur’ubu bugingo.

Couplet 4

Ishengero ry’Isirayeli mw’Egiputa ryabayeho birigoye, Natwe turyigane guseng’ Imana, Ngw’ Idutsindir’iki gihe kigoye.

Couplet 5

Imiruho ni myiza ku bizera, Yamara ku batizer’ irabishe, Twemere turushwe ku bwa Yesu, Tuzoshobore kubana na We.

Couplet 6

Shengero ry’Imana tunezerwe, Kuko igihe gishitse vuba, Twihangane rwose Yes’araje, ibiturusha Abituruhure.
