#85

Isezerano ry’imana ntirijya rihera

Impimbano· Sr Henriette

Couplet 1

Isezerano ry’Imana ntirijya rihera, naho ryaba ritinze rigomba gusohora. Ritegereze nshuti uzabona rishyitse, naho ryaba ritinze rigomba gusohora.

Couplet 2

Sogokur’Aburahamu yararitegereje, igihe kirekire kugeza rishyitse. Ritegereze nshuti uzabona rishyitse, naho ryaba ritinze rigomba gusohora.

Couplet 3

Mbere y’uko rishyika nawe yarageragejwe, igihe gisohoye yaribonye rishyitse. Ritegereze nshuti uzabona rishyitse, naho ryaba ritinze rigomba gusohora.

Couplet 4

Wowe mwana w’Imana har’icyo yakuvuzeho, gitegereze nshuti uzabona gishyitse. Ritegereze nshuti uzabona rishyitse, naho ryaba ritinze rigomba gusohora. Uzaca mu biruhanya have ntushidikanye, wizer’ Uwasezeranye niw’Uzarisohoza.

Couplet 5

Nawe Mugeni wa Yesu uti ko mbona bitinze, Oya ntabwo bitinze bigiye gusohora. {Ubur’amaso hakurya, nguriy’ Umukwe Araje. Ambar’ Umwambaro wera, Asange witeguye.} x2 {Ambar’ Umwambaro wera, Asange witeguye.} x2
