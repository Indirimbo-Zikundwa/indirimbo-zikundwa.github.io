Yesu Niw’ Ufit’Izina ryiza

Agakiza

Couplet 1

Yesu ni w’ufit’ izina ryiza, Mu mazina yose mur’ iyi si. Iryo zina Yesu, Yesu, N’umubavu mwiza cyane rwose.

Refrain

Iryo zina rirakomeye, Rirashobora gukurahw ibyaha. Iryo zina Yesu, Yesu, Ni ryo rinezeza mu mutima.

Couplet 2

Nta n’irindi zina mur’ iyi si Rifit’ imbaraga n’ubugingo. Iryo zina Yesu, Yesu, Ryaririmbwe n’abamarayika.

Refrain

Iryo zina rirakomeye, Rirashobora gukurahw ibyaha. Iryo zina Yesu, Yesu, Ni ryo rinezeza mu mutima.

Couplet 3

Ni ryo zina rihebuj’ayandi, Nde tse ni ryo ryahanits’ ijuru. Iryo zina Yesu, Yesu, Ririmbwa mur’ iyi si yose.

Refrain

Iryo zina rirakomeye, Rirashobora gukurahw ibyaha. Iryo zina Yesu, Yesu, Ni ryo rinezeza mu mutima.

Couplet 4

Sinshobora kwibagirwa Yesu, Iryo zina n’agakiza kanjye. Yesu, Yesu nzamubona Turi mw ijuru tunezerewe.

Refrain

Iryo zina rirakomeye, Rirashobora gukurahw ibyaha. Iryo zina Yesu, Yesu, Ni ryo rinezeza mu mutima.
