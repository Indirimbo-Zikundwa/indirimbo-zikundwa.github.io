We shengero ba maso

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

We Shengero ba maso utegereze, Isezerano wahawe rirashitse, Ba maso mu mutima, niwihangane, I Siyoni hav’ivyiza biguhagije.

Couplet 2

Inkomezi ziva kwa Yesu, wihangane, Umucunguz’ari hafi, utegereze, Daniyeli yabereye mas’ubugingo bwe, Uhoraho yabanye nawe mur’iyi si.

Couplet 3

Guma ku Rugamba, nesha byose byo mw’isi, Turi hafi gutaha, niwihangane, Yerusalemu yacu tuzayibamo, Ni wo Munani w’Abera banesheje.

Couplet 4

Tuzabana n’Uhorah’ ibihe bidashira, Tuzaririmban’ ishimwe tunezerewe Urugamba turi hafi kururangiza. Mbeg’ Ubuntu, mbeg’ Ubuntu twagiriwe!

Couplet 5

Ba maso we Shengero wakunzwe na Kristo, Niduhang’ amas’ Umwami nka Elisa, Ntituze tubur’ico twasezeranijwe, Kuko Wa musi uregereje cane.
