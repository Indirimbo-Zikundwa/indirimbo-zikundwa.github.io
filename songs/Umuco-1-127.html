Twarahunze i babuloni

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Twarahunze i Babuloni habi, Mu gisagara c’irimbukiro, Twahunze i Babuloni habi, Mu gisagara c’amadayimoni, {Aharindirwa impwemu mbi z’iyi si.... Z’iyi si y’umwiza w’umuzitanya.} x2

Refrain

Twaravuye imahonero, Twaravuye i Sodomu, Twaravuye i Edomu, Igisagara c’amadayimorii, Non’ubu turi i Yerusalemu, Ubu tararirimba, ubu turacuraranga, Ubu turavuza ingoma, Turi mu Bwami butanyiganyizwa.
