Unatelemkia hukumuni

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Unatelemkia Hukumuni kabisa, Unasonga mbali Naye Mwokozi, Wewe washawishika kuenda adhabuni, Pole pole wewe wadidimia.

Refrain

Ndugu, mwamini Mwokozi, Usije kupotea, rudi kwa Mungu, (Ndugu yangu, leo umwamini Yesu Mwokozi, Usije kupotea, Leo rudi kwake Mungu) Hukumuni ondoka, ingia Uzimani,Yesu Kristo mwamini, uokoke.
