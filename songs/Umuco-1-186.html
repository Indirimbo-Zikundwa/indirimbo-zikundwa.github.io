#186

Mwami Yesu nkunda cane

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Mwami Yesu nkunda cane, Ijwi ryawe riranezera, Iyo ngenda mur’iyi si, Mpumurizwa n’uk’unkunda Yesu, {Ndakwihaye jewe nd’uwawe, Unyigishe ngendane nawe.} x2

Couplet 2

Mwami Yesu wanyiguze, Mpura n’ibingora mu nzira, Iyo ngenda mur’iyi si, Mpumurizwa n’ijwi ryawe Yesu, {Rinyemeza yuko nd’uwawe, Bikamaramaza Satani.} x2

Couplet 3

Mwami Yes’uko ndi kwose, Jewe nd’uwawe Bwirukiro, Mu mibabaro nd’uwawe, Mu munezero nd’uwawe Yesu, {Mu magorwa jewe nd’uwawe, Mu ntambara jewe nd’uwawe.} x2

Couplet 4

Mwami Yesu jewe nyakira, Kuko wankunze ubwa mbere, Aho wampamagara Mwami, Ukanyigisha kukwubaha Yesu, {Mu ntege nke none nd’uwawe, Mu nkomezi jewe nd’uwawe.} x2

Couplet 5

Ubu singikekeranya, Wanyigishij’Ijambo ryawe, Kuko ndiho ku bwawe Mwami, No ku rupfu nzob’uwawe Yesu, {Uruhure umutima wanje, Nce mur’iyi si ngororoka.} x2
