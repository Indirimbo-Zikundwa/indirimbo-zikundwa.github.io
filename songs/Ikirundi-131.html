#131

Yesu, ndagomba guhabuka

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yesu, ndagomba guhabuka nta handi nasanz’ ikinezero, narahevy’ inzira y’ivyaha ngomba guhabuka.

Refrain

Yesu ndagomba guhabuka Singipfa nsubiye kwangara, Mwami Yesu ngirir’ ikigongwe, Ngira mpabuke.

N’ igihe kirekire cane Nabaye mu nzira ya Satani, Noneho ndayihevye rwose, Ngomba guhabuka.

Refrain

Refrain
