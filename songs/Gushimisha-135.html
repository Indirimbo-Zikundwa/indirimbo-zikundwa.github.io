#135

Mukiz’Utubonez’ iminsi yose

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mukiz’Utubonez’ iminsi yose; Mu kubaho kwacu, tugusingize. Utubonekere, tur’abangizi; Nukw’Amahoro yaw’Atwinjiremo.

Couplet 2

Tugendan’iteka mu byiringiro; Tubabaye nabwo, tukwizigire. Hahirw’uruhurwa n’inama zawe, Ukamutabar’ Ukamwikiriza.
