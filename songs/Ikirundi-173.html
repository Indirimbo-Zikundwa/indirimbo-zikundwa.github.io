#173

Imana ni yo Mwungere Andagiz’ Urukundo

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Imana ni yo Mwungere Andagiz’ Urukundo. Nizigiye yuko ntazokena, Kuko nd’ intama y’Imana.

Couplet 2

Niy’indyamika mu canya C ubgatsi bgiza cane, Injan’ i ruhande y’ imigezi Itemba nez’ idasuma.

Couplet 3

Insubizamw’ intege nsha. Zindemesh’ umutima, Inyobora mu nzir’ itunganye, Kubg’ izina ryayo ryera.

Couplet 4

Naho noca no mu mwonga w’ igitutu c’ urupfu, Sinogir’ ikibi ntinya na mba, Kuko tuba turi kumwe.

Couplet 5

Undemeshesh’ ikibando cawe Mukiza wanje. Ni wew’uzompumuriza Mana, Ni nashika no ku mpfiro.

Couplet 6

Untunganiriz’ ameza mu maso y’ abanyanka, Ukansig’ amavuta mu mutwe, Uriyujurij’ igikombe.

Couplet 7

N’ukur’ imbabazi zawe Zizohorana nanje, Na ntaryo nzoba mu ngoro yawe, Bimpimbaye bihebuje.
