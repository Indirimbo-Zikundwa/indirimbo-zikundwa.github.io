Iyo ndebesheje amaso ibyo mu isi

Izindi

Couplet 1

Iyo ndebesheje amaso ibyo mu isi njya mbibonamo ubusa bene Data bashiki banjye uwashaka yakorera Umwami Yesu.

Eh eh eh eh eh! Abakiriye Ubugingo tunezerwe, turi hafi yo gutaha mu Ijuru, aho tutazongera kubarara, uwashaka yakorera Umwami Yesu.
