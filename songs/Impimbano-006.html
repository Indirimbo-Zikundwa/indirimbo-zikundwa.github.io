#6

Yesu mukiza wanjye nje nkuzaniye amashimwe

Impimbano· Fr Siméon

Couplet 1

Yesu Mukiza wanjye nje nkuzaniye amashimwe. Indirimbo zigushima nukuri uzemere, nonese iyataba wowe Mwami, nari kuba jye ndihehe? Ndaririmban’ ibyishimo kuko Wanesheje.

Refrain

Hahiriwe umuntu utoranya, agahora imbere yawe, azahazwa ibyiza byinshi bivuye mu Ijuru.

Couplet 2

Ibyaha byanjye byinshi Yabimbariye kera, None ubu mba mumudendezo wabana b’Imana, Tuyobowe mu nzira ducamo, ntamwijima uhaba rwose, twe turagana i Sioni mu Murwa w’Imana

Refrain

Hahiriwe umuntu utoranya, agahora imbere yawe, azahazwa ibyiza byinshi bivuye mu Ijuru.

Couplet 3

Tugendane Mukunzi, mwiyinzira igana Iwacu, ngo njye mbona uko nkubwira byose binaniza. Fata Ingabo zawe nini ni nto, maze undwanirire rwose, iherezo ry’urugamba rishaka intwarane

Refrain

Hahiriwe umuntu utoranya, agahora imbere yawe, azahazwa ibyiza byinshi bivuye mu Ijuru.

Couplet 4

Ninsoza uru rugamba, Mwami uzanyakira iwawe, Ndikumwe n’abera bose uko banesheje, Hahiriwe umuntu wemeye ubutumire bw’Umwami harageze muze atwinjize mubirori byiza

Refrain

Dore Ameza manini ateguwe yuzuyeho ibyiza byinshi, reba niyo Yerusalemu ukuntu irimbishijwe.
