#261

Mpwemu we, Mufasha wacu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mpwemu we, Mufasha wacu, uhor’ uduhumuriza, uturongor’ utwengenga mu mahanga tuj’i wacu. Abarush’ ubakomeze, ubumvish’ ijwi ryawe, rihamagara ngo ngwino kuri jew’unkurikire.

Couplet 2

Mpwemu we, Mukunzi wacu, duhumurize, dufashe ntidukekeranye na mba, ntitugendere mu mwiza, ni twageragezwa nabi, twankiwe n’ukwizera, duhamagar’uti ngwino kuri jew’unkurikire.

Couplet 3

Mpwemu we, Muvunnyi wacu, ndamutse ntsinzwe n’urupfu, nshitse ku nkombe y’uruzi, ngatakishwa n’ubgoba, nti mbega ndarujabuka nte? Mfasha kubg’ Amaraso: Umpamagar’uti ngwino aho naguteguriye.
