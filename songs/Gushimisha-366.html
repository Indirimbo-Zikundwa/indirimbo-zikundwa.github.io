#366

Ngutuy’ubugingo bwanjye Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ngutuy’ubugingo bwanjye, Yesu, Mwana w’Imana; Ubwakire n’Urukundo, ngo bub’ubwaw’iteka. Nkuzaniy’ ibyaha byanjye n’umutim’ unangiwe: Mu Maraso Wamviriye, Unyoze, Mucunguzi.

Couplet 2

Uz’uko mpora nirwanya ngo nkomez’ umutima; Uz’uko nari merewe n’ishavu n’agahinda. Ububasha bwawe. Mwami, bundokore mu byaha Kand’Umwuka w’Ubugingo ujy’unkura mu bibi.

Couplet 3

Nemeye kwitanga rwose, nta cyo nishigarije; Mpisemo kujyana nawe no kukumvira, Yesu. Njye nkuyobokan’ Ishimwe kukw’ari Wow’Ubimpa; Ujy’Unyobor’ Inzir’ijya mu Bwami bwawe bwiza.

Couplet 4

Reb’uyu muhigo mpize nibwo nzivuguruza. Wukomeresh’ Amaraso n’Urukundo Wankunze. Itek’iyo nirebyeho, ndiheba, nkazimira; Ariko kuk’ujy’ Unkunda, Uzamfasha, Mukiza.
