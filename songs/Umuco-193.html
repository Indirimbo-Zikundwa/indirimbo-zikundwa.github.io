#193

Har’ibuye abubatsi bagaye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Har’Ibuye abubatsi bagaye, hari ibuye abubatsi bataye; ryahinduts’ irigumya imfuruka, ryashizweho n’Imana Nzima.

Refrain

Iryo Shengero rya Kristo Yesu, ryubatswe n’amabuye mazima; har’ urufatiro rw’ amabuye, aribo Ntumwa n’Abavugishwa; ./.Yesu niwe Buye rigumy’ imfuruka./. ./.Nanje nta handi nzishimira kuba atari mu Ngoro y’Uhoraho./.

Iryo Shengero ry’Imana Nzima, n’Ishengero ry’abazima, ryubatswe mu Rufatiro, ryubatswe n’amabuye mazima.
