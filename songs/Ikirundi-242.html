#242

Nzama nsaba Mpwemu Yera

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Nzama nsaba Mpwemu Yera ngw’ agendez’ imbere yanje, Ivyo Yesu Yangorewe, birarengereje cane; ./.Bingarura nkiri hafi, ngatinya kumuhemukira./.

Refrain

./.Mwami Yesu wanje, Nzokugendanira, Mmeny’ Urukundo rw’Umukiza./.

Couplet 2

Ni mwumv’ Amagorwa yiwe Yaboneye ngaha mw’isi Ahorw’ abagarariji, twar’abo kuzogesezwa: ./.Amaraso y’Umukiza, ni yo gusa yahadukuye./.

Refrain

./.Mwami Yesu wanje, Nzokugendanira, Mmeny’ Urukundo rw’Umukiza./.

Couplet 3

Abansi b’Umwami Yesu bamusanz’i Getsemane, bamufashe, bamujana mw’ikombe ry’abacamanza ./.bamubohey’ amaboko, i mugong’ari twebg’Azira./.

Refrain

./.Mwami Yesu wanje, Nzokugendanira, Mmeny’ Urukundo rw’Umukiza./.

Couplet 4

Bamushikanye mw’ ikombe, bamureg’ ivy’Atakoze, baramusananikira, ata ho Yigez’ ikibi: ./.Ivyo twagabitanije nivy’ Umukiza Yishizeko./.

Refrain

./.Mwami Yesu wanje, Nzokugendanira, Mmeny’ Urukundo rw’Umukiza./.

Couplet 5

Bamaze kumutsindisha baca baramucurira, batonagur’ Umukiza, Asigar’ ar’ishishagu. ./.Iyo mimyore ya Yesu Niyo gusa Yadukijije./.

Refrain

./.Mwami Yesu wanje, Nzokugendanira, Mmeny’ Urukundo rw’Umukiza./.

Couplet 6

Nta hantu yar’ Aciyumva, Aremekw’ Umusaraba, bamujana kumubamba mu gahing’i Gologota, ./.Yasabiy’ abamubamvye, Ati Mana, n’ubaharire./.

Refrain

./.Mwami Yesu wanje, Nzokugendanira, Mmeny’ Urukundo rw’Umukiza./.
