Nimushim’Izina ryiza

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Nimushim’Izina ryiza ry’Uwiteka Rurema; N’Icyubahiro cye cyinshi nimucyamamaze hose hose! Nimuvug’ Urukundo rwe, bose barwumve, abo ku mpera y’isi bamenye kw’Akiza.

Refrain

Urukundo rw’Uwiteka ruratemba nk’uruzi, kand’Ubuntu bwe n’ibambe ntabwo bizashir’iteka ryose.

Refrain

Refrain

Couplet 4
