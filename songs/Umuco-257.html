#257

Mana we waturemye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mana we waturemye turagusavye umugisha duhezagire, duhe ijambo ridushikirize agakiza.

Refrain

Nico gituma uyu munsi twaje mu ngoro yawe Yera, turagusavye utweze Mwami…twambare imvune z’ubukwe.

Couplet 2

Mana rurema, we wadukunze twe turicishije bugufi duhe ukwizera, duhe urukundo twuzure amahoro muri twe.

Refrain

Ni co gituma uyu munsi twahevye vyose vyo gukora, tukaza hano mu ngoro yawe…kugusenga wewe w’ukuri.

Couplet 3

Mana rurema, Mana y’Abera twuzuze ijambo rihishuwe, mu nguvu zacu ntidushobora kwitez’intambwe mu gakiza.

Refrain

Nico gituma abana bawe duhanze amaso umusaraba, turakwinginze Mwami utuvure tube abakristo bashikamye.

Couplet 4

Mana rurema, we watwiguze suka Mpwemu wawe muri twe tujanjagure, uduhindure ibikoresho vyawe Mwami.

Refrain

Kaze Mukiza ndakwizeye wampishuriye ijambo ryera n’ubu Indirimbo z’Umuco wo kukagoroba komeza kumurikira…nze nshike mw’ijuru amahoro.
