#32

Mw’izuka rya mbere

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mw’izuka rya mbere, tuzomwibonera: Yesu Mwami wacu abazoba biteguye pe.

Refrain

Inzamb’izovuga abasinziriye bazovyuka bose, gusanganir’ Umwami.

Couplet 2

Abasinziriye, ubushobozi bwiwe; buzobavyura bose, buzuye umunezero mwinshi.

Refrain

Inzamb’izovuga abasinziriye bazovyuka bose, gusanganir’ Umwami.

Couplet 3

Natw’abakiriho, abuzuye Mpwemu, tuzomwibonera, Yesu ari mu bwiza bwiwe.

Refrain

Inzamb’izovuga abasinziriye bazovyuka bose, gusanganir’ Umwami.

Couplet 4

Tuzomwibonera, Yesu mu Bwiza bwiwe; tumushime cane dusa nawe mu Bwiza bwiwe.

Refrain

Inzamb’izovuga abasinziriye bazovyuka bose, gusanganir’ Umwami.

Couplet 5

Ewe ncuti yanje, ntumwitege nabi; wibaz’uti nanje, ndizeye ko nzomwibonera.

Refrain

Inzamb’izovuga abasinziriye bazovyuka bose, gusanganir’ Umwami.
