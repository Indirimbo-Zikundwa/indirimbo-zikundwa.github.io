#347

None Yes’uramugir’ute? Hitamwo bigishoboka

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Igihe baregera Yesu, Mw ikomb’ imbere ya Pilato Yabajij’ abantu bos’ ati Yesu ndamugire nte?

Refrain

None Yes’uramugir’ute? Hitamwo bigishoboka: Hazoz’ umus’ uzibaz’uti: Jeh’ Arangirir’ate?

Couplet 2

Ivyo na non’ubihitemwo, ubigomvye womwahakana canke womwakiran’ ineza, non’ Uramugir’ute?

Refrain

None Yes’uramugir’ute? Hitamwo bigishoboka: Hazoz’ umus’ uzibaz’uti: Jeh’ Arangirir’ate?

Couplet 3

Couplet 4

Refrain

None Yes’uramugir’ute? Hitamwo bigishoboka: Hazoz’ umus’ uzibaz’uti: Jeh’ Arangirir’ate?
