#382

Yes’Aduh’ Amahoro by’ukuri

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yes’Aduh’ Amahoro by’ukuri, Ahor’ Ayaduhesh’ Amaraso Ye.

Couplet 2

Nta mirimo se yayatwambura? Yes’Ahor’ Aruhur’ abamwumvira.

Couplet 3

Twayagira, tutaz’ ibizaba? Tuzi Yesu kw’ari W’Utwara byose!

Couplet 4

Nta byago byadukur’ imitima; Nta bwoba bagir’ abamwihishemo.

Couplet 5

Inshuti nubwo tutari kumwe, nkukw’ Aturind’ Arazirinda na zo.

Couplet 6

Mu rupfu Azaduh’ Amahoro Ye: Rwaneshejwe n’Umutabazi wacu.

Couplet 7

Ibyago byo mw’isi bizashira, Umwam’ Atwinjize mu Mahoro Ye.
