#22

Mfise umuntu ankunda

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Mfise umuntu ankunda, N’umwami Yesu, urukundo rwiwe, N’agahebuza mu bugingo bwanje.

Refrain

Mbega ncuti wubatse he? Kuri Kristo wumva ufite, Urukundo rwa Yesu Kristo?

Couplet 2

Yesu ni We Jambo, Jambo ni Yesu, Uryizeye vy’ukuri afite ubugingo, Hamwe n’urukundo.

Refrain

Mbega ncuti wubatse he? Kuri Kristo wumva ufite, Urukundo rwa Yesu Kristo?

Couplet 3

Vyose bizoshira kiretse kimwe: Urukundo gusa ruzokwamaho, Ibihe bidashira.

Refrain

Mbega ncuti wubatse he? Kuri Kristo wumva ufite, Urukundo rwa Yesu Kristo?

Couplet 4

Ahari urukundo rwa Yesu ntihaba, ubwoba, Nta gushidikanya nta n’amaganya, Nta n’umwijima.

Refrain

Mbega ncuti wubatse he? Kuri Kristo wumva ufite, Urukundo rwa Yesu Kristo?

Couplet 5

Yesu n’urukundo, n’ umwakire ubu, Wizere Ijambo rye, ubone ubugingo, Uronke urukundo.

Refrain

Mbega ncuti wubatse he? Kuri Kristo wumva ufite, Urukundo rwa Yesu Kristo?
