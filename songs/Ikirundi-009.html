#9

Yes’Ashimwe

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yes’Ashimwe, turamushim’ Umukiza, Bose bashim’ Urukundo rwiwe, Mumushime mwa Bamarayika bera. Muhimbaz’ Icubahiro ciwe. Umukiza aratubungabunga, N’Umwungere mwiz’ Atuzigama. Mumushime mwa Bamarayika bera, Mutazir’ Icubahiro ciwe.

Couplet 2

Yes’Ashimwe, turamushim’ Umukiza, Kubg’ ivyaha vyacu Yapfiriye, Kubgiwe twizigiye ko tuzokizwa Tugahabg’ Ubugingo bg’Imana. Yarabambge, yemejwe n’Urukundo abansi bamushinyagurira, Yambitsw’ igitsibo c’amahwa mu mutwe, Yavyemejwe n’Urukundo rwiwe.

Couplet 3

Yes’Ashimwe, turamushim’ Umukiza, Yarazuts’ Avyagira mw’Ijuru. Non’ Ashimwe n’Abamarayika bera. Bamuhimbaza n’ijwi rirenga. Non’abantu boye gutiny’ urupfu: Mwami Yesu, Wararunesheje; Ariko rwahinduw’ irembo ry’Ijuru, n’Ukuzuka kwawe Mutabazi.
