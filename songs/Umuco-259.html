#259

Uhoraho n’ingabo idukunda

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uhoraho n’ingab’idukingira, niwe Mukiza ni we Mwungere, adukiza mu magorwa akomeye, turi mu biganza vyiwe ntituzopfa.

Refrain

Ni we Mugabisha w’intambara, ari imbere y’ishengero ryiwe, arineshereza imbere n’inyuma, mu kuboko kwiwe harimwo intsinzi.

Couplet 2

Uhoraho niwe nyen’igikorwa, Uhoraho niwe azogikomeza, amaraso yuvuye ku musaraba, arimw’intsinzi y’ibihe bidashira.

Refrain

Ni we Mugabisha w’intambara, ari imbere y’ishengero ryiwe, arineshereza imbere n’inyuma, mu kuboko kwiwe harimwo intsinzi.

Couplet 3

Nta nkomezi z’umuntu zivugwa, nta ntege nke z’umuntu zivugwa, nta bikorwa ntakugororoka, amaraso ya Yesu araduhagije.

Refrain

Ni we Mugabisha w’intambara, ari imbere y’ishengero ryiwe, arineshereza imbere n’inyuma, mu kuboko kwiwe harimwo intsinzi.
