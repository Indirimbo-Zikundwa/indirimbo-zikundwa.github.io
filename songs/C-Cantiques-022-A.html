#22A

Ifwe natumfwe

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Ifwe natumfwe landwe, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Kabebenikwa muti, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Kabiyeni ne Landwe, Muye mu fyalo fyonse, E funde ilya Mfumu, “Yesu Mupusushi.”

Couplet 2

Bilikisha kwi shilya, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Ku bantu shimikila, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Mwimbile mwe filila, Mwasuke mwe fyambeshi, Nobe calo sumina “Yesu Mupusushi.”

Couplet 3

Sanshikisha mashiwi, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Pantu alyanshishe mfwa, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Tepelesho mutima Waikala mu mfifi, Ku nshishi sanshikisha “Yesu Mupusushi.”

Couplet 4

Cishibikwe fye konse, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Ndupwa shonse nashange, “Yesu Mupusushi.” Bilikisha ku bantu Ba fyalo fya kutali, Natuletelo bumi, “Yesu Mupusushi.”
