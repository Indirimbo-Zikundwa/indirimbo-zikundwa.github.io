#139

Moyo wangu una wimbo leo

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Moyo wangu una wimbo leo, Yesu ananiambia: “Siku zote nitakuongoza, Ee usiogope tena.”

Refrain

Yesu, Yesu, Yesu, Jina tamu sana; Linajaza roho na Furaha na Salama.

Couplet 2

Mbele nilitaka kuharibu roho yangu na makosa; Yesu aliingia kuokoa, sasa nimesamehewa.

Refrain

Yesu, Yesu, Yesu, Jina tamu sana; Linajaza roho na Furaha na Salama.

Couplet 3

Hata ana’ngoza kwa mateso, taabu ya kuzidi sana, Yesu ni Mushinda sikuzote, naye anajua njia.

Refrain

Yesu, Yesu, Yesu, Jina tamu sana; Linajaza roho na Furaha na Salama.

Couplet 4

Yesu ni karibu kurudia kutukaribisha kwake, Kutawala naye kwa milele Kwa Mungini wake Mbingu.

Refrain

Yesu, Yesu, Yesu, Jina tamu sana; Linajaza roho na Furaha na Salama.
