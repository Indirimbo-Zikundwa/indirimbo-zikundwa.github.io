#230

Umv’Intumwa zo mw’Ijuru

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Umv’Intumwa zo mw’Ijuru Ziririmbira Yesu, Zit’ Iman ishimwe cane, Amahor’ abe mw isi, Kuk’ Umwana way’ avutse, Azokiz’ abanyavyaha; Namwe ni munezerwe Tunezeranwe cane:

Refrain

Intumwa zo mw ijuru Ziririmbira Yesu.

Couplet 2

Uvukiye mu kiraro C’ink’ avyawe n’umworo, Uwo Mwana n’Uhoraho, Bose bamuhimbaze: Tugushime cane Yesu, Ur’ Iman’ uri n’umuntu; Nk’ uko witw’ Imanweli, Iman’ ibane natwe:

Refrain

Intumwa zo mw ijuru Ziririmbira Yesu.

Couplet 3

Ur’ izuba ritwakira, Ur’ amahoro yacu, Wemeye kuva mw ijuru. Ub’ umuntu wo gupfa, Watuzaniy’ ubugingo, Ngo tukwizere tubeho, Tunezerw’ uyu musi Ko duhaw’ Umukiza.

Refrain

Intumwa zo mw ijuru Ziririmbira Yesu.

Couplet 4

Nukw’amoko yos’ ashime Krist’ Uwera w’ Imana. Hose hose humvikane Indirimbo z’ ishimwe Amahoro n’ abe mw’isi. Kuk’ Umwami Yes’ avutse Ni we Mukunzi wacu, N’Umuvunnyi duhawe:

Refrain

Intumwa zo mw ijuru Ziririmbira Yesu.
