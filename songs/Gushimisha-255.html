#255

Yesu Kristo Yarazutse

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yesu Kristo Yarazutse: Twese tumushime: Haleluya uyu munsi, tunezerwe, twese tumushime: Haleluya kera ku Musaraba, ni ho Yesu Yatubabarijwe, ngw’ab’ Impongano y’ibyaha byacu: Haleluya, reka tumuhimbaze.

Couplet 2

Yes’Umwami wo mw’Ijuru, twese tumushime: Haleluya kukw’ar’ Umukiza w’abo mw’isi, Yarabambw’ Arahambwa, Ajya n’i kuzimu mu bapfuye. Maz’Arabinesh’ava mu rupfu Haleluya, reka tumuhimbaze.

Couplet 3

Ibyo Yababajwe byose, Umukiza wac’Abishimirwe ni byo byonyine byatumye tugir’Ubugingo budashira. None, yimye mw’Ijuru, ahw’Abamarayika baririmba, tumusingizanye na bo, tuti: Haleluya, reka tumuhimbaze.
