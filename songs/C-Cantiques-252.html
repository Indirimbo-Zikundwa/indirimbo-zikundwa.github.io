Panakadi mu dikenga

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Panakadi mu dikenga Yesu wakansungila, Nenye kuambila bakuabo Ne baye nanyi kulu.

Refrain

Tshiena musue kuya kulu Ku musoko wa Nzambi Ne bianza bianyi bitupu, Bianza bianyi bitupu.

Couplet 2

Meme tshiena ntshina lufu, Yesu wakansungila; Kadi tshiena musue kuya Meme mene nkayanyi.

Refrain

Tshiena musue kuya kulu Ku musoko wa Nzambi Ne bianza bianyi bitupu, Bianza bianyi bitupu.

Couplet 3

Tshiakuambila bantu bangi Bualu bua Musungidi; Mutshima udi usama, Nakuenza bibi bushuwa.

Refrain

Tshiena musue kuya kulu Ku musoko wa Nzambi Ne bianza bianyi bitupu, Bianza bianyi bitupu.

Couplet 4

Tuyayi kuambila bakuabo Bualu bua muoyo wabo; Tudi basue kuya kulu Ne bakuabo ba bungi.

Refrain

Tshiena musue kuya kulu Ku musoko wa Nzambi Ne bianza bianyi bitupu, Bianza bianyi bitupu.
