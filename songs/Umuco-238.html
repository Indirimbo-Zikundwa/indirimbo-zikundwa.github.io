#238

Abera baciye mu nzira ifunganye

Umuco

Couplet 1

Abera baciye mu nzira ifunganye, Abera baciye muvyo badatahura Abera baciye mu bisig’ubwenge kuk’Uhoraho niko yabishimye.

Refrain

Nshimir’Uhoraho, kwar’ivyo yashimye, nshimir’Uhoraho kwar’ivyo bimuhimbara, nshimir’Uhoraho kwar’ivyo yanyuzemwo, nshimir’ Uhoraho kw’aduha kubinyuramwo.

Couplet 2

Nowa yaburiwe kubak’ubwato mu bisig’ubwenge, yubak’ubwato kukw’ijambo ry’igihe ciwe niko ryamusaba abikora naho atabitahura.

Refrain

Umpe kuba Mwami mu batahura ivyawe, sinze maramare ku musi uzogaruka, nkuko abo mu gihe ca Nowa vyagenze, barimbuwe n’umwuzure bitwengera.

Couplet 3

Mu gihe cawe Mwami niko vyagenze, wariko uravuga ijambo bakwitegereza, abafarisayo n’abasadukayo ntibashobora kwizer’ijambo ry’igihe cabo.

Refrain

Umpe Mwami ntegurwe n’ijambo ryawe nshobore gutambuka uko uyo murongo, kuko ataco nobarusha kiretse ngiriwe ubuntu nawe Mwami.

Couplet 4

Abera bene Data batubere akarorero, ntitwishinge kwama tuguma mu vyo twamenye ahubwo twame duhinda agashitsi nk’abazi ko tuzobibazwa n’Imana.

Refrain

Twizigir’Imana mu bugingo bwacu, ntiducobogore mu vyo twizera, gurtyo dushobore kwiteg’Umwami n’agaruka kutuzamur’i we mw’ijuru.
