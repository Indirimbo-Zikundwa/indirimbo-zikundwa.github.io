#330

Nshita shisa no kupita

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Nshita shisa no kupita, Fitupele fishibilo, Leelo kuliubulolelo No’bucetekelo, mu mitima, Pa cinshi Mfumu ya kokola Bufumu bukesa lilali, I caloo no mulu fyaimba Auleni Mfumu ya isa.

Refrain

Mwe bushe cikaba shani Ilyo Imfumu ikesa Bushe cikaba shani (lya kesa) Ilyo Imfumu ikesa?

Couplet 2

Imimana ya sansulwa: Mfumu yaisa ku bakwe. Mpili, malungu fya anga: Imfumu ipelo bucindamo; Mwe bali mutulo buceni no kuya, Kukumanya Shibwinga, Makaa ya mfimfi ya butuka, Cikaba shani ilyo akesa?

Refrain

Mwe bushe cikaba shani Ilyo Imfumu ikesa Bushe cikaba shani (lya kesa) Ilyo Imfumu ikesa?

Couplet 3

Icalo conse ca samwa: Ububi bonse na bupwa, Kuloleela kwapwa nomba: Kwasangula kwaba ubuseko Bangeli babili babila: Mutende nomba wa kosa, Mitii ne mpanga fyasekela AuleniMfumu yaisa.

Refrain

Mwe bushe cikaba shani Ilyo Imfumu ikesa Bushe cikaba shani (lya kesa) Ilyo Imfumu ikesa?

Couplet 4

Mwe bamunina laleni: Kuca kulimupepifye Ubuubi bulipo nomba: Bwa sangula nomba ubuseko, Luse ne cine fikesapoYesu; Alumbaniwe wafwile Nelyo tukacula katwishiba! Cikabashani ilyakesa?

Refrain
