#83

Mwami tuma non’aha

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mwami tuma non’aha, Mpwemu w’ubushobozi. Mwami duhe Ijambo ryo kuturemesha.

Refrain

N’urabe abana bawe wabire imitima yacu ./.Utwuzuz’ Inkomezi n’Ubushobozi bwawe./.

Couplet 2

Mwami tuma non’aha Ijambo ry’Ubugingo; riduh’ubushobozi bwo kunesha Satani.

Refrain

N’urabe abana bawe wabire imitima yacu ./.Utwuzuz’ Inkomezi n’Ubushobozi bwawe./.

Couplet 3

Duh’Ijambo ry’Umuco, ryo kutumurikira; twuzur’ijambo ryawe twuzur’umuco wawe.

Refrain

N’urabe abana bawe wabire imitima yacu ./.Utwuzuz’ Inkomezi n’Ubushobozi bwawe./.

Couplet 4

Mwami twibutse non’aha amasezerano yawe; tuve aha twese twuzuye ubushobozi bwawe.

Refrain

N’urabe abana bawe wabire imitima yacu ./.Utwuzuz’ Inkomezi n’Ubushobozi bwawe./.

Couplet 5

Mwami shimwa n’ushimwe kuko utivuguruza; kukw’ivyo wasezeranye vyama bishika vyose.

Refrain

N’urabe abana bawe wabire imitima yacu ./.Utwuzuz’ Inkomezi n’Ubushobozi bwawe./.
