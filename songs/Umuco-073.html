Tuvuga ko twahumutse

Umuco

Couplet 1

Tuvuga ko twahumutse amaso, s’imigani twarahumutse ./.Mbeg’ ubwo Buntu, twagiriwe kumeny’ ivyari vyanyegejwe./.

Couplet 2

Imana Yarungitse Umuco, w’akagoroba ng’uduhumure ./.Ngo tumeny’ ingene Satan’ akora kuk’ ubu iminsi ari mibi.

Couplet 3

Couplet 4

Couplet 5

Couplet 6

Couplet 7
