#161

Mp’Amakuru y’ivya Yesu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mp’Amakuru y’ivya Yesu, ubindumishe vyose, ririmb’ indirimbo nziza irut’ izindi zose, imw’ abungere bumvise ya ba Bamarayika, Ng’ Uhoraho ni yubahwe. No mw’ isi ni hab’ amahoro.

Refrain

Mp’Amakuru y’ivya Yesu, ubindumishe vyose, ririmb’ indirimbo nziza irut’ izindi zose.

Couplet 2

Mbgir’uko Yesu Yahwanye n’Umurwanizi mubi, uko Yamutsindishije Ijambo ry’Ubugingo. Unyibuts’ imibabaro Yababajwe kubganje, kwar’ ukugira ngw’ Aduhe Agakiza katazoshira.

Refrain

Mp’Amakuru y’ivya Yesu, ubindumishe vyose, ririmb’ indirimbo nziza irut’ izindi zose.

Couplet 3

Mbgir’ivy’ urupfu rwa Yesu Yampfiriye ku giti, ririmb’ Ukuzuka kwiwe, ni kwo nganji ya twese. Kukw icatumye ncungurwa ar’ Urukundo rwiwe, Yampfiriy’ Akanzukira: Muhimbaze, birarangiye.

Refrain

Mp’Amakuru y’ivya Yesu, ubindumishe vyose, ririmb’ indirimbo nziza irut’ izindi zose.
