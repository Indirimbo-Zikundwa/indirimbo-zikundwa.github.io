#40

Ishimwe ryawe ndigendana hose

Impimbano· Fr Siméon

Couplet 1

Rukundo rwanjye, We Mahoro yanjye, Ineza yawe njya nyirata iteka. Ingabo yanjye inkingira mu byago, Ur’Urutare nihishamo Iteka. Igihe cyiza gihebuje rwose, N’igihe Wampamagaye Mukunzi, Muntama zawe mpabonamo umwanya, Kuva uwo munsi mba mbonye Indirimbo.

Refrain

Ishimwe ryawe ndigendana hose, namenye yuko Wankunze ntabizi. Simbasha nanjye guhish’ urukundo, {Wacanye Umuriro mumutima wanjye.} x2

Couplet 2

Iteraniro ry’Abakunzi bawe, mukanwa kabo hahoramo ishimwe. Baba bashima bahimbaza Imana, bati Uwiteka Mana yacu Ushimwe. Noneho Shimwa, Shimwa Mwami wanjye, Waduhishuriye Imigambi yawe. Ijambo ryawe rituguye neza, niryo rituma tunezerwa iteka.

Refrain

Ishimwe ryawe ndigendana hose, namenye yuko Wankunze ntabizi. Simbasha nanjye guhish’ urukundo, {Wacanye Umuriro mumutima wanjye.} x2

Couplet 3

Nimuze twese abubaha Imana, Iteraniro ry’Abakunzi bawe. Duhimbaza Urukundo rw’Umukiza, dukore byose tuyobowe nawe. Igihe nigisohora dutashye, Tuzazamurwa mubyishimo byinshi. N’ubundi byose ntacyo byahindura jye nzagushima, e Mukunzi wanjye.

Refrain

Ishimwe ryawe ndigendana hose, namenye yuko Wankunze ntabizi. Simbasha nanjye guhish’ urukundo, {Wacanye Umuriro mumutima wanjye.} x2
