#122

Imana yaciye inzira

Umuco

Couplet 1

Imana yaciye inzira, inzira y’imigambi yayo; Satani agerageza kuyisivya ./.Ayiteramw’ amahwa menshi arik’ Umwami Yesu Yarayisibuye./.

Refrain

Es’Imigambi, es’Imigambi, y’Imana Ihoraho; n’Imigambi ikomeye cane iyo Migambi n’iyerekeye ivyiza vyacu twese abasiburiwe iyo Nzira.

Couplet 2

Imana Yaremy’isi yose, ijuru n’ibirimwo vyose; iyo yar’ Imigabo y’Uhoraho ./.Satani agerageza kuyisivya arik’ Umwami Yesu Yarayisibuye./.

Refrain

Es’Imigambi, es’Imigambi, y’Imana Ihoraho; n’Imigambi ikomeye cane iyo Migambi n’iyerekeye ivyiza vyacu twese abasiburiwe iyo Nzira.

Couplet 3

Imana Yaremye Adamu na Eva ibashira mw’ itongo ryayo; aho har’ Imigabo y’Uhoraho ./.Satani agerageza kuyisivya arik’ Umwami Yesu Yarayisibuye./.

Refrain

Es’Imigambi, es’Imigambi, y’Imana Ihoraho; n’Imigambi ikomeye cane iyo Migambi n’iyerekeye ivyiza vyacu twese abasiburiwe iyo Nzira.

Couplet 4

Imigambi y’Uhorah’Imana, nta co yihindura namba; ni nde yigeze kwig’ ubwenge bwayo ./.Iyo migambi n’iy’ ivyiza vyacu twese abasiburiwe inzira n’Umwami Yesu./.

Couplet 5
