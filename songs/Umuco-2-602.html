#602

Uhoraho twarakubonye

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Uhoraho twarakubonye, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Mu Ntambara Utugwanirira, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. Ubu rero ntagisigaye, Atari kugukurikira tugaca bugufi mu Nyonga zawe, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 2

Ur’imbere y’Ishengero ryawe, - Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Ni we Mugabisha w’Intambara, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. Ukarineshereza hose, Ukarishikiriz’ Intsinz’ itagabanije, Mukiza Mwiza, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 3

Uhoraho ni We Mwungere, Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Uremesha umutima wanje, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. Uhoraho ni We gihome gikingira ubungingo bwanje. Ni nd’azonc’ ivutu mubimbarire, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 4

Ico nsab’Uhoraho ni kimwe, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Ni co nzohora nitwararika, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. N’ukuba mu Ngoro y’Uhoraho, Imisi yose y’ubugingo bwanje, Nkarab’ Ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 5

Mwami Yesu ndaguhimbaza, - Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Wadukuye habi Mukiza, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. N’ukur’ ivyiza n’imbabazi, bizonyamak’ imisi yose yo, kubaho kwanje Mukiza Mwiza, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 6

Uhoraho twarakwizeye, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Mu mahirwe no mu magorwa, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. Nzemangir’ Izina rya Yesu, N’aho vyoba binsaba guheb’ ubugingo bwanje je sinotinya, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 7

Uhoraho ur’Umutabazi, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Utunesherez’ imisi yose, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. Ivyo tuzi n’ivyo tutazi, Kuk’Ur’ Imana y’Imbabazi, Wama Witwararik’ Abana bawe, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 8

Uzaza ryari Mucunguzi? Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Wime bwa Bwami budashira, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Ngw’ivyaremwe vyose vyishime. Icamp’ Ukaza Udatinze nkaruhuk’iyi mibabaro y’isi, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.

Couplet 9

Uz’ubu canke hirya y’ejo, - Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo, Mu gicugu canke ku murango, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo. Izina ryawe Mwami Yesu, Jewe nzokomeza nizere gushika no mu rupfu sinotinya, Ur’Uwera Krist’Ur’ Ijambo.
