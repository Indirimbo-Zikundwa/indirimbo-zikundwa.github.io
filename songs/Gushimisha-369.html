#369

Ngukunde Yesu Mwami wanjye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ngukunde Yesu, Mwami wanjye, Mukiza wanjye, ngukunde; Ngukunde, nkor’ibyo gushimwa n’ibikunezeza byose, ngeze ku munsi wo gupfa, Yesu, nkigukunda!

Couplet 2

Nta wundi ngir’ uhwanye nawe kuk’ungwiriz’ Imbabazi; Ngukunde, Yesu, ngushimishe akanwa, no mu mutima. Ni Wowe Yes’Ukuraho ibyaha by’abantu.

Couplet 3

Ngukunde. Yesu Databuja, Mwana w’Imana Rurema; Ngukundish’ imbaraga zose, nahw’ amakub’ari menshi, ngeze ku munsi wo gupfa, Yesu, nkigukunda!
