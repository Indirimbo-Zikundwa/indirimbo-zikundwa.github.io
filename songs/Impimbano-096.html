#96

Umutim’udendebuwe wifuza agakiza kawe

Impimbano· Fr Salem

Couplet 1

Uhoraho, Undangire ivyo washinze: ndavyitondere gushitsa kw’iherezo. Ushitse umusavyi wawe mw’isezerano, iryo wasezeraniye abakwubaha.

Refrain

{Umutim’udendebuwe, wifuza Agakiza kawe: (Ariko nzizigira Wewe. x2)} x2

Couplet 2

Kugororoka kwacu sikw’udusaba, ukuva mu kukwizera nikwo wemera. Mbonekeye muri Wewe Mwami wanje, ngir’Ukwizera nyakuri kuva ku Mana.

Refrain

{Nkuzur’Urukundo rwawe, na kamere kawe Yesu: (Nkabaho mu bushake bwawe. x2)} x2

Couplet 3

None ibihe bidashira biregereza, nyemerera Mpwemu wawe amvugutire. Mu bugingo bwanje bwose abwiganzire, kugira mpagarare mu Ntsinzi yawe.

Refrain

Amaso yanje akobowe, yifuz’Igikorwa cawe: Nkibaza nti: Mbese ni ryari? Nzisanga ngutunganiye, Urukundo ruhinguwe; {nkamenya ko vyose ar’Ubuntu.} x2

Couplet 4

{Bambazwa mu mutima no kwirabako, ariko nzi kw’ataco nokwiyungurako.} x2 Mfasha mpang’amaso Yesu Yambambiwe; Ubuntu bwiwe bwuzuye bumperekeze.

Refrain

Umvi’ijwi ryanje Mukiza, mp’Akamere kawe Yesu, Uhimbar’ imisi isigaye. Urukundo rw’agahore, kugororoka kw’Imana, {bimbemwo bivuye i Wawe.} x2
