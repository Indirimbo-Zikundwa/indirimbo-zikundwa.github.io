#323

Uyu mugenzi wacu aravuye mu marushwa

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Uyu mugenzi wacu aravuye mu marushwa, Arajabuts’ uruzi, Non’ arafashe hakurja: Aratash’ Iman’ ishimwe, Aradutanze mw ijuru.

Couplet 2

Mu rugo rwo kwa Data Nta marira, nta magorwa: Satani ntashobora Kumwononer’ amahoro; Nta buryo bund’ agifise Kukw atakimushikira.

Couplet 3

Uwizer’ Umukiza, Akarab’ urnusaraba, Akozwa n’amaraso, Akavuk’ubga kabiri, Akihan’ ivyaha vyiwe Ni we yemerwa kujayo.

Couplet 4

Uyu mubiri wiwe Si we, ni nk’ivyo yambuye; Wubgiw’ aracariho, Tuzongera kubonana; Tuzohurira mw ijuru, Duhamagaweyo natwe.
