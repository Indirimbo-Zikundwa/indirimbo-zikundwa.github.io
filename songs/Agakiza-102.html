#102

Ump’akanya Yesu nze nkwegere

Agakiza

Couplet 1

Ump’akanya, Yesu, nze nkwegere, kuko naniriwe mur’iyi si. Ndetse no mu gihe cy’Intambara, na bwo Mwami, ujy’ump’Amahoro.

Refrain

Ump’akanya, Yesu, nze nkwegere, kuk’umwijim’ ukabije mw’isi. Icyo nkeneye n’ukukwegera kugira ngo numv’Ijambo ryawe.

Couplet 2

Ump’akanya, Yesu, nze nkwegere mu bingerageza n’amakuba. Ni wowe buhungiro nizeye mu gihe cy’akaga mur’iyi si.

Refrain

Ump’akanya, Yesu, nze nkwegere, kuk’umwijim’ ukabije mw’isi. Icyo nkeneye n’ukukwegera kugira ngo numv’Ijambo ryawe.

Couplet 3

Ump’akanya, Yesu, nze nkwegere kuko Wanyikorerey’ibyaha. Ndumva nshaka kukubwira byose kuko, Mwami, nizeye k’Unyumva.

Refrain

Ump’akanya, Yesu, nze nkwegere, kuk’umwijim’ ukabije mw’isi. Icyo nkeneye n’ukukwegera kugira ngo numv’Ijambo ryawe.
