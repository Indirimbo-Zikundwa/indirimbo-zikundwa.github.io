#255

Jesus is tenderly calling you home

Izindi

Couplet 1

Jesus is tenderly calling you home- calling today, calling today; Why from the sunshine of love wilt thou roam farther and farther away?

Refrain

{Calling today,} x2 Jesus is calling, is tenderly calling today.

Couplet 2

Jesus is calling the weary to rest- calling today, calling today; Bring Him thy burden and thou shalt be blest- He will not turn you away.

Refrain

{Calling today,} x2 Jesus is calling, is tenderly calling today.

Couplet 3

Jesus is waiting; O come to Him now- waiting today, waiting today; Come with thy sins, at His feet lowly bow- Come, and no longer delay.

Refrain

{Calling today,} x2 Jesus is calling, is tenderly calling today.

Couplet 4

Jesus is pleading; O list to His voice- hear Him today, hear Him today; They who believe on His name shall rejoice- quickly arise and away.

Refrain

{Calling today,} x2 Jesus is calling, is tenderly calling today.
