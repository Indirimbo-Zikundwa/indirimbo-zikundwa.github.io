Umuntu wese atang’ic’afise

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Umuntu wes’atang’ic’afise, Kuk’uwurongorwa n’umubiri, Atang’ivy uyo mubiri nyene, Uwurongorwa na Mpwemu Yera, Agatang’ivy’uyo Mpwemu nyene.

Refrain

Ivy’uyu mubiri Bene Data, Nta kindi bizan’ atar’urupfu, Arikw’ivyo Mpwemu Yer’atanga, Bikatuzanira kuzoragwa, Ubwami bw’Imana buhoraho.

Couplet 2

Kwitwararik’ivy’uyu mubiri, Gufis’ikimazi kuri bike, Kukw’ar’ivy’igihe gito cane, Kwitwararik’ivya Mpwemu Yera, Kuzan’ubugingo n’amahoro.

Refrain

Ivy’uyu mubiri Bene Data, Nta kindi bizan’ atar’urupfu, Arikw’ivyo Mpwemu Yer’atanga, Bikatuzanira kuzoragwa, Ubwami bw’Imana buhoraho.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Abagize ubwenge nyakuri, Bama barind’imibiri yabo, Bakagendera mu bibereye, Ni bo bazokwaka nk’inyenyeri, Kuri wa musi w’Umwam’Imana.

Couplet 4
