#384

Ben’Imana Yizerwa

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ben’Imana Yizerwa, twiyonger’ umwete; Maz’imbaraga zacu tuzih’Umukiza kuko Yatwihaye. Dusingize dushima “Ubwiza n’Urukundo by’Umwam’ Udukiza.”x2

Couplet 2

Abashavuye mwese, muze mudatinya: Abasenga bizeye barahumurizwa. Umuvunyi Yesu ni W’Urengera bose, “kukw’Ar’ Umunyaneza; Ntabw’ Azabahana.”x2

Couplet 3

Yesu n’Inshuti yacu; Tujye tumwinginga; Maze, mu byago byacu Ajy’aturengera. Ni W’Uturerera Ubugingo twahawe, “Mu Butunzi bwe, twese Akadukenura.”x2
