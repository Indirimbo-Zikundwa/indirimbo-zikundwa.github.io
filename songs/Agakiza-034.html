#34

Nyoborwa mu Nzira yose

Agakiza

Couplet 1

Nyoborwa mu Nzira yose N’ukuboko k’Umukiza. Iyo mbony’ inez’Agira, nta bwo mba ngishidikanya. Ndetse ngir’umunezero, N’amahor’ asendereye. Angirir’ ubuntu bwinshi, Butagir’uko bungana.

Couplet 2

Nyoborwa mu nzira yose, niringiy’ Umwami Yesu. Antsindiran’ ibishuko, Anyongeramw’ imbaraga. Mu gihe nishwe n’inyota naniriwe mu Rugendo. Rwa rutare rwasadutse, ruradudubiz’ Amazi.

Couplet 3

Nyoborwa mu Nzira yose, kubw’ Urukundo rwe rwinshi. No mw’Ijur’ imbere ya Se, nzanezerwa bihebuje. Yesu ku birenge byawe, ni ho mpfukamye nkuramya. Kuko wa nyoboye neza mur’iyi si ngituyemo.
