#201

Bayoboke mubyuke

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Bayoboke, mubyuke. Mwe, Ntore z’Umwami, dufate mu maboko Intwaro za Yesu. Mu nzira yo kunesha tuyoborwa na Yesu, tujyane na W’iteka, Aturwanirire.

Couplet 2

Impanda ziravuze uti nimuze, mwitabe Ikamba ry’Ubugingo n’ingabire yanyu. Ni mwangwa n’ababisha mukomere, murwane, mugir’umwete mwinshi, mutsinde Satani.

Couplet 3

Mubyuke, mutabare, mudashidikanya ntihagir’usigara; Mutumbire Yesu. Kandi mujye mutwara Intwaro zo Kwizera zizatuma munesha, mugir’ibyishimo.

Couplet 4

Mubyuke vuba vuba, murwane mwihwema. Mwizer’Izina ryera; Ni iryo muneshesha. Ni muva ku Rugamba mutahanye na Yesu, muzabon’ Amahoro y’iteka mw’Ijuru.
