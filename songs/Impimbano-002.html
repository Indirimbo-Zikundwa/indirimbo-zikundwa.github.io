#2

Umusi wo gucungurwa kwacu uri hafi

Impimbano· Fr Siméon

Couplet 1

Ubwo Amahanga ari, mumidugararo, Isiraheli ibaye maso; Ibimenyetso byose uko byahanuwe, ngibi birasohoye, iminsi yabanyamahanga irabaze, nibiteye ubwoba byinshi, twegereje cyane, kandi turi hafi, yibihe bidashira.

Refrain

Umunsi wo Gucungurwaaa, kwacu dore uri hafi, ndets’ imitima iwutegereje ubu, irihw’ iragurumana, Yemwâ bu- zuy’Umwuka Wera, mwiboneze mumitima, Amata-baza yanyu nahore yaka, gucunguuurwa kuri hafi.
