#218

Shengero ry’imana ihoraho

Umuco

Couplet 1

Shengero ry’Imana Ihoraho, ntimuri ishengero nk’ayandi, ivyo ni mubimenye bagenzi, kuko muri ihanga riri ukwaryo; ihanga ry’Imana isumba vyose.

Refrain

Umve rero ncuti zanje, dusangiy’ukwizera kuzima, nimwihutire kugendera mwese, mw’ijambo ry’Imana ryose, kuko niho hari ubugingo budashira.

Couplet 2

Ijambo ngiryo riravuga, n’uwufise amatwi niyumve, uwufise amaso n’abone, kuko Uhoraho ubwiwe ni we yakenyereye ishengero ryiwe.

Couplet 3

N’Ubuntu butangaje bene Data, kubona Imana ariy’ikora, mbega n’impamvu yihe haba ivyo, nuko ihanga ar’iry’Imana nzima, yarikunze ritaribwabeho na gato.

Couplet 4
