#138

Iy’isi yashaje rwose

Umuco

Couplet 1

Iy’isi yashaje rwose, vyose vyashaje cane, ntaco mbona gisigaye, vyose vyashaje cane ./.Ico nshaka jewe nuko noba aho ntegerezwa kuba./.

Refrain

Imana Yancishije mur’ iy’ isi, kugira ndayishingire intahe; kugira ndayibere icabona, Yigaragaze muri jewe ./.None Mwami ntaco nokwishoboza, nunyeze rwose uze twibanire, mbone gukora ivy’ Ugomba./.

Couplet 2

Iy’isi yashaje rwose, s’ivyatsi s’ibiti vyose, vyose vyashaje cane, none nicare ndye nywe ./.Vyose jewe vyandambiye Ivyo si vy’ Imana Inshakako./.

Refrain

Imana Yancishije mur’ iy’ isi, kugira ndayishingire intahe; kugira ndayibere icabona, Yigaragaze muri jewe ./.None Mwami ntaco nokwishoboza, nunyeze rwose uze twibanire, mbone gukora ivy’ Ugomba./.

Couplet 3

Iy’isi yashaje rwose, kandi yandambiye, non’ ubu har’ ico nifuza, kiruta ibindi vyose ./.N’ugukora ukugomba kw’Imana Yera Ihoraho./.

Refrain

Imana Yancishije mur’ iy’ isi, kugira ndayishingire intahe; kugira ndayibere icabona, Yigaragaze muri jewe ./.None Mwami ntaco nokwishoboza, nunyeze rwose uze twibanire, mbone gukora ivy’ Ugomba./.

Couplet 4

Iy’isi yashaje rwose, isigaye ir’ urunuka, ivyayo narabihinyuye, nifuza gukora ./.Ic’Imana Yancishirije mur’ iy’ isi yuzuy’ umwiza./.

Refrain

Imana Yancishije mur’ iy’ isi, kugira ndayishingire intahe; kugira ndayibere icabona, Yigaragaze muri jewe ./.None Mwami ntaco nokwishoboza, nunyeze rwose uze twibanire, mbone gukora ivy’ Ugomba./.

Couplet 5

Refrain
