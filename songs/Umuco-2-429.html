Umwami Yesu yarankunze

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

{Yesu n’Umwungere w’intama, Kukw’ az’abo Yatoranije, Mu biwe nta wuzozimira, Bose bari mu biganza vyiwe.} x2

Refrain

{Yesu n’Umwungere w’intama, Kukw’ az’abo Yatoranije, Mu biwe nta wuzozimira, Bose bari mu biganza vyiwe.} x2

{Yesu n’Umwungere w’intama, Kukw’ az’abo Yatoranije, Mu biwe nta wuzozimira, Bose bari mu biganza vyiwe.} x2

Refrain

{Yesu n’Umwungere w’intama, Kukw’ az’abo Yatoranije, Mu biwe nta wuzozimira, Bose bari mu biganza vyiwe.} x2
