#273

Twikangure, dukor’ ibikorwa

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Twikangure, twikangure, Dukor’ ibikorwa cane, Twitange tub’ishikanwa ryo guhimbar’ Uhoraho.

Couplet 2

Kera twamye tur’ubute Noneho muze twihane Dukorer’ Imana yacu Dushimitse vy’ukuri.

Couplet 3

Kumbur’umus’urupfu Rwoshika rukatwubira, Canke Yesu yogaruka: Bisange tugororotse.

Couplet 4

Tureke kwigira beza, Twibuke yuk’ Uboraho Abon’ ivyo mu mitima. Kw ata co twomunyegeza.

Couplet 5

Nk’ ukw Imana yaduhaye Izuba ryo kutwakira, Tumurikir’ imitima Y’ abari mu mwiza mwinshi.

Couplet 6

Twikangure, tuyishime, Turirimban’ indirimbo N’abayishimira bose Mw ijuru no mw isi hose.

Couplet 7

Mukiza, turikanguye, Utwuzuze Mpwemu wawe, Duhimbarish’ Iman’ ivyo Tuvuga n’ ivyo dukora.
