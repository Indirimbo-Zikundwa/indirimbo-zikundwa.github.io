#65

Musange Umwami ugira Ubuntu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Musange Umwami ugira Ubuntu, abatakimwizigira. Ntiyanga kwakira abatindi; Ahabur’ abazimiye kandi yemeye kubapfira: Yabaviriy’ Amaraso. Nta kindi abasha gukirisha abanze kumvir’Imana.

Couplet 2

Musang’Umwami ujy’ahembura nimuze, mwes’abaguye, batacyizigir’ Agakiza: Abategey’ amaboko! Yakirishij’ Ubuntu bwinshi wa munyabyahakaz’ arira; Na wa mwambuzi babambanywe ntiyamwisungiy’ ubusa.

Couplet 3

Musang’ubasha kuruhura; Az’ibibarushya byose. Kubw’Urukundo rusaga, Ahez’ ibibashavuza. Cya gihe, muri Getsemani,Yariz’amarira menshi: Kubw’Ubuntu bwe buhoraho, Azahanagur’ayanyu.
