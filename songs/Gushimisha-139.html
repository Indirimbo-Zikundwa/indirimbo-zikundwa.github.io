#139

Mukiza Yesu ntabara

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mukiza Yesu, ntabara, kukw’abantu babiri bari mu mutima wanjye, Umw’akund’ und’agoma.

Refrain

Ndengera ndengera Mwami wanjye, ndengera.

Couplet 2

Umwe ni we Mwuka wawe Unyiringiz’Ijuru; Und’ahor’ anyoshya nabi, ashaka kunshumuza.

Refrain

Ndengera ndengera Mwami wanjye, ndengera.

Couplet 3

Mwami, sinkor’ibyo nkunda, nubwo mpora mbyifuza. Ibibi byo ndabikora, Nubw’ iteka njya mbyanga.

Refrain

Ndengera ndengera Mwami wanjye, ndengera.

Couplet 4

Murokoz’Umucyo wawe umurikire neza, ngo wa muntu mubi nanga atamperana rwose.

Refrain

Ndengera ndengera Mwami wanjye, ndengera.

Couplet 5

Iyo nirwanya ntyo, Mwami, nta Mahoro mba mfite. Ndagukunda, ndagushaka mfasha, nesh’uwo Mubi.

Refrain

Ndengera ndengera Mwami wanjye, ndengera.

Couplet 6

Kiz’imbata yaw’irushywa, ihor’ikuganyira; Umpe n’ububasha bwawe ngo njye nsinda Satani.

Refrain

Ndengera ndengera Mwami wanjye, ndengera.

Couplet 7

Ndashak’Uburuhukiro mu Bwami bwawe bwiza; Mfasha ngo wa wundi nanga atambuza kujyayo.

Refrain

Ndengera ndengera Mwami wanjye, ndengera.
