#34

Biri vuba tugataha

Impimbano· Fr Siméon

Refrain

{Biri vuba tugataha, bivuge mumahema yose, Kubagenzi bajya mu Bwami iyo n’inkuru nziza cyane.} x2

Couplet 1

Ndumva amajwi menshi cyane, ndumva Abera bo mwisi yose, Bahamagara bati ngwino, nukuri dushaka gutaha. Mwiri cumbi siho Iwacu, nukuri dushaka gutaha.

Refrain

{Biri vuba tugataha, bivuge mumahema yose, Kubagenzi bajya mu Bwami iyo n’inkuru nziza cyane.} x2

Couplet 2

Bibwire za Ntore z’Umwami, zirira ku manywa n’ijoro, uti nimukomere cyane, ntimucogore Kurugamba. Nagahe gato mwihangane, nirusoza turataha.

Refrain

{Biri vuba tugataha, bivuge mumahema yose, Kubagenzi bajya mu Bwami iyo n’inkuru nziza cyane.} x2

Couplet 3

Nimurwane mushiz’ ubwoba, mukomeze abarushye cyane. Ntimutinye dufite Intwari, niyo Iyoboye uru Rugamba. Kuba turi kumwe na Yesu, ibi nukuri tuzataha.

Refrain

{Biri vuba tugataha, bivuge mumahema yose, Kubagenzi bajya mu Bwami iyo n’inkuru nziza cyane.} x2

Couplet 4

Mbese ubundi ubu, hari umuntu utabona ko turi hafi, ntiwabeshya ko ujya mu Bwami kandi ukikundiye iri cumbi. Kuko ujya kunanirwa wese, avuga ko dukabya cyane.

Refrain

{Biri vuba tugataha, bivuge mumahema yose, Kubagenzi bajya mu Bwami iyo n’inkuru nziza cyane.} x2

Couplet 5

Sinzatindana n’utizera, naba ntakaza umwanya wanjye. Niba amaso ye atakireba, biragoye ko abona iyo ajya. Nimuhura asebya iby’iwacu ntumutindeho s’umugenzi.

Refrain

{Biri vuba tugataha, bivuge mumahema yose, Kubagenzi bajya mu Bwami iyo n’inkuru nziza cyane.} x2

Couplet 6

Iherezo ngiri riraje, Igihugu turakireba, birarangiye turatashye, nimuhumurizanye mwese. Mwe Marembo y’Ijuru mwumve, nimwuguruke turatashye.

Refrain

{Biri vuba tugataha, bivuge mumahema yose, Kubagenzi bajya mu Bwami iyo n’inkuru nziza cyane.} x2
