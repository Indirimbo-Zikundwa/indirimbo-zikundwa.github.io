#181

Numv’ijwi ry’umwami

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Numv’ijwi ry’Umwami rimpamagara, Rikampamagara mu gacerere, Rimbwira ngo negere, negere, Dusangire ka meza y’Ubwami bwiwe, Nibwo butumire Umwami atumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe, Ariho agaburira abana biwe, Arobanura ingano mu muguruka, Uwumva niyumve kandi amenye, Uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, Ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.

Couplet 2

Numv’ijwi rya Yesu rihamagara, Uteg’ugutwi wumve mwene Data, Abari maso ijoro umutaga bose, Ngo begere, begere ameza yiwe, Nibwo butumire Imana idutumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe, Ariho agaburira abana biwe, Arobanura ingano mu muguruka, Uwumva niyumve kandi amenye, Uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, Ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.

Couplet 3

Yesu ari ku muryango arahamagara, Ati: «Abinjira nimwinjire vuba, Kuko Umuco ugiye gutana n’Umwiza, Uwinjira niyinjire mw’irembo rifunganye.» Nibwo butumire Yesu adutumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe, Ariho agaburira abana biwe, Arobanura ingano mu muguruka, Uwumva niyumve kandi amenye, Uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, Ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.

Couplet 4

Igihe c’ubutumire kiriko kirahita, Twinjire mu muryango ucuguruye, Umuco ugiye gutana n’umwiza, Injira, injira wew’usigaye, Nibwo butumire Umwami atumira.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami ari ku meza yiwe, Ariho agaburira abana biwe, Arobanura ingano mu muguruka, Uwumva niyumve kandi amenye, Uwiteguye wese aronk’umugabane wiwe, Ni vyo vyamwa vyo mu Bwami bw’Imana.
