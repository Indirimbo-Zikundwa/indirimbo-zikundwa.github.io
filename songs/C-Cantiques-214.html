#214

Dituku adi dinene

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Dituku adi dinene, Luse lwa Nzambi luakulua kundi. Dinakitabuja Yesu, Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi.

Refrain

Ndi mumanye, mumanye, Mumanye, mumanye; Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi.

Couplet 2

Diambedi kuakadi miidima, Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi, Katataka Munya wakulua, Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi.

Refrain

Ndi mumanye, mumanye, Mumanye, mumanye; Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi.

Couplet 3

Diambedi nakadi ndila, Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi, Disanka diakulua kundi, Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi.

Refrain

Ndi mumanye, mumanye, Mumanye, mumanye; Luse lua Nzambi luakulua kundi.
