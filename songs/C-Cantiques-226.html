#226

Wa kucula! e shina

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Wa kucula! E shina Lya Mwana wa kwa Lesa; Alubwilya babipa, Aleluya, Mupusushi!

Couplet 2

‘Bantu bamusoweke, Amfwilile ne mubi, ‘Mulopa wansumine, Aleluya, Mupusushi!

Couplet 3

Twabipilwa, twabipa, Wene e wa kaele Cine, apo kutula, Aleluya, Mupusushi!

Couplet 4

Asamikwe ku kufwa, Akutile: “Naipwa.” Mu mulu nomba ‘fika, Aleluya, Mupusushi!

Couplet 5

Ikese Mfumu Yesu, ‘Kututwalaku mwesu, Tukembila mu mulu, Aleluya, Mupusushi!
