#148

Imana yadukuye muri egiputa

Umuco

Couplet 1

Imana Yadukuye muri Egiputa, ngo tuyishikanire ibimazi, ngo tuyishikanire amashikanwa; tugaragaze Ubwiza bw’Uhoraho, tugaragaze Ubwiza bw’Iyera.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, ubundi nabwo bukishura, iyo hariho ukubohwa haba hariho n’agakiza ./.Ubu twaramaze kubohoka dushikanir’ Imana./.

Couplet 2

Imana Yadukuye i Babuloni, aho twari twaboshwe nabi, tutagikorera Imana yacu; tutagishikanira Uhoraho, twari twuzuye umubabaro.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, ubundi nabwo bukishura, iyo hariho ukubohwa haba hariho n’agakiza ./.Ubu twaramaze kubohoka dushikanir’ Imana./.

Couplet 3

Agakiza karatanzwe rimwe risa, ntakuganzwa n’umuntu w’Inyuma ngo atubuze kwidegemvya na gato; ngo dushikanire Uhoraho, tumushikanire ibimazi.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, ubundi nabwo bukishura, iyo hariho ukubohwa haba hariho n’agakiza ./.Ubu twaramaze kubohoka dushikanir’ Imana./.

Couplet 4

Ubu turi mu rugo rwa Dat’Imana, tuduz’ amababa tuguruke tuje gukora mu murima wa Data, nta kikituboheye hasi, tumushikanire ibimazi.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, ubundi nabwo bukishura, iyo hariho ukubohwa haba hariho n’agakiza ./.Ubu twaramaze kubohoka dushikanir’ Imana./.

Couplet 5

Umurima wa Data ureze, twiyambure uyo muntu mubi, tujakarire gusa Yesu Kristo; tumubere abaherezi nyakuri, ni we Mwami w’i Siyoni mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Ubwina burahamagara, ubundi nabwo bukishura, iyo hariho ukubohwa haba hariho n’agakiza ./.Ubu twaramaze kubohoka dushikanir’ Imana./.
