#409

Kushinda sasa atashinda

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Kushinda sasa, atashinda, Mufalme wa nguvu Anaongoza waminifu Katikati ya vita; Ona wanakwenda mbele, Na mavazi meupe, Wakisifu Bwana yao, Mwenye kuwaongoza:

Refrain

“Si wa nguvu watashinda, Wala wa mbio sana; Lakini wenye imani Ni wao watashinda.”

Couplet 2

Kushinda sasa, atashinda, Nani ni Mufalme? Kuongoza majeshi yote, Yanamwimbia sifa? Bwana Mukombozi wetu, Mufalme Mwokozi, Wingi wao kama nyota Kuangaza milele.

Refrain

“Si wa nguvu watashinda, Wala wa mbio sana; Lakini wenye imani Ni wao watashinda.”

Couplet 3

Kushinda sasa, atashinda, Yesu ni Mutawala, Viti vya enzi vitaisha, Taji zitaanguka, Lakini majeshi Yake Yatatii kabisa, Wataishi kwa milele, Kisha watapumua.

Refrain

“Si wa nguvu watashinda, Wala wa mbio sana; Lakini wenye imani Ni wao watashinda.”
