#185

Abagendera mu Muco

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Abagendera mu Muco bizobazanira Umubano w’urukundo Utangwa na Mpwemu.

Couplet 2

Kand’ umutima w’umuntu Agenda mu muco Ub’ ufatanye n’Imana kukw iba mu Muco.

Couplet 3

Ni mugendere mu muco, Maz’ ivyaha mwanka Muzovyozwa n’amaraso Y’Uwadupfiriye.

Couplet 4

Kand’ ugendera mu muco Ntatinya n’urupfu, Kukw ab’ azi k’Umukiza Yarutsinze rwose.

Couplet 5

Imitima y’ab’ umuco Nta mwiz’ uyibamwo. Kuko bakirwa n’umuco W’Iman’ ihoraho.

Couplet 6

Abagendera mu muco Bagir’ amahoro, Kukw Imana Nyen’ ubuntu Ar’ umuco wabo.

Couplet 7

Ni mugendere mu muco, Muvuye mu mwiza Ni h’amaras’ azoboza, Mukab’ intungane.

Couplet 8

Kandi mu muco wa Yesu, Tubonamw’ abandi, Baz’ Umurokozi na bo, Tukabasengera.
