#75

Yesu ni We Nshuti yacu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yesu ni We Nshuti yacu; Ni W’Utwikorerera imibabaro n’ibyaha, Akatwuzuza na Se. Dore, tubur’amahoro, tuyabuzwa n’ubusa, kuko tudasaba neza ibyo dukena byose.

Couplet 2

Ni tugir’ibyago byinshi. Tugashukwa n’Umubi, tumuganyir’ amaganya, kukw’ Ayadukuraho. Harihw’indi Nshuti nk’iyi Yababarana natwe? Yes’Az’ intege nke zacu, tumwisunge dusaba.

Couplet 3

Niba ufit’ishavu ryinshi ukababara rwose, Ubuhungiro ni Yesu: ngwino tumwihishemo. Inshuti zidutaye, zikaduhemukira, Yes’Adukingir’ ingabo, Akaduhumuriza.
