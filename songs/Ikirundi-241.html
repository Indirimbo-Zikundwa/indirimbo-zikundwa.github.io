#241

Mbeg’uriya yambaye

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mbeg’ uriya Yambaye impuzu zitabutse, zihomets’ Amaraso, mbarira, n’uwa hehe? Eh’ ingen’ Ananiwe n’igiti Yikoreye, urya baboshe kurya ni nde, kand’ azir’iki?

Couplet 2

Uwo Munt’abay’ artyo ni We Nyen’ Ubugingo, ni We Mwana w’Imana Aziz’ ivyaha vyawe. Sigaho gucurumba, musang’i Gologota, upfukame wihane icamubambishije.

Couplet 3

Ibisonga vy’amahwa ni vyo gitsibo ciwe, kand’ Intebe y’Ubgami yabay’ Umusaraba; Mbeg’ Umwam’ agizw’artyo yob’Umukiza wanje? Ego, som’ikirego: "Yesu w’i Nazareti".

Couplet 4

Umve Yes’ avug’ati: Mwana wanje nakunze, icankuye mw’Ijuru N’ukuguh’ Ubugingo; Ni wewe mboney’ aya, ni wewe mbabarizwa, emera ndagukize, nkwuzuze n’Iman’ubu.

Couplet 5

Mukiza ndakwihaye, Mparir’ ivyaha vyanje, Uz’Uhor’ ummenyesha Imibabaro yawe: Ah’ utung’ urutoke ni yo nzira nzocamwo, nshits’ah’ izoherera, Yesu Mukunzi wanje.
