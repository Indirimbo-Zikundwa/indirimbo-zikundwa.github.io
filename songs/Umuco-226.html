#226

Uhoraho n’umuco wawe

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uhoraho n’umuco wanje, amurikira ibirenge vyanje, n’ahimbarizw’ Izina ryiwe, ni ryo ryampaye kubana nawe.

Refrain

./.Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa./.

Couplet 2

Ntegereje izuka ry’Abera, no kubona Umukiza wanje, twibonanire hirya mw’ijuru, ibi nivyo vyizigiro mfise.

Refrain

./.Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa./.

Couplet 3

Uhoraho n’umwungere wanje, ntaco nzokena mur’iyi nzira, naho umwijima wonsugereza, inkingi y’umuriro n’itara ryanje

Refrain

./.Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa./.

Couplet 4

Nyenyeri yo ku kagoroba, suk’umuco mu bana bawe, bahumuke babon’inzira, ubahe n’amavuta asagutse.

Refrain

./.Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa./.

Couplet 5

Yesu abwira abamwizeye ati : ni je Muco w’ukuri, n’umwungere w’igura intama, utandiye wese ntaronk’ubugingo.

Refrain

./.Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye kuzambikw’umubiri udapfa./.

Couplet 6

Nyenyeri yo mu gaturuturu, ah’ushitse nta mwiza uhaba, tugushimiy’ ijambo uduha, ni ryo gari rizodushitsa mw’ijuru.

Refrain

./.Ubu ntegereje kandi nihanganye kuzambikw’ umubiri udapfa./.
