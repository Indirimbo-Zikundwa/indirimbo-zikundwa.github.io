#110

Nzikorer’ umusaraba aho nzajya hose

Izindi

Refrain

Nzikorer’ umusaraba aho nzajya hose, aho nzaba hose nzagumana nawo, mubuzima bwanjye bwose kubwa Yesu Kristo, nzagumana nawo kubitugu byanjye.
