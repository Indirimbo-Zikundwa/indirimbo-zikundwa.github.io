#205

Mushakashak’ ubwami bw’ijuru

Izindi

Couplet 1

Mushakashak’ Ubwami bw’Ijuru, no gukirinuka kwayo; Nibw’ ibindi byose muzabyogerwa, Hallelu, Halleluya.

Couplet 2

Nta muntu utungwa n’umutsima gusa, ahubwo n’Ijambo ryose, riva mu kanwa k’Imana gusa, Hallelu, Halleluya.

Couplet 3

Ni musaba nukuri muzahabwa, mushake muzabona, mukomange kandi mukigurirwe, Hallelu, Halleluya.

Couplet 4

Nimuteranira mw’Izina ryanjye, nzaba ndikumwe namwe. Icyo munsaba cyose nzagikora, Hallelu, Halleluya.
