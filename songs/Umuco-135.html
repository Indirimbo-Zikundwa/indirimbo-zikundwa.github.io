#135

Umuco uraka cane rwose

Umuco

Couplet 1

Umuco uraka cane rwose, ababona bakawubona abazimira bakazimira abakira bagakira ./.Abasinzira bagasinzira n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana./.

Refrain

Abana b’Imana barawukurikira, waduga umusozi bakawukurikira, ah’uciye niho baca uhagaze bagahagarara; iy’ugiye bakagenda, n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana.

Couplet 2

Uwo muco uraka cane rwose, hari abawegereye cane, hari abari kure yawo benshi, bawubona agace cane ./.Hakaba n’abawubona rwose n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana./.

Refrain

Abana b’Imana barawukurikira, waduga umusozi bakawukurikira, ah’uciye niho baca uhagaze bagahagarara; iy’ugiye bakagenda, n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana.

Couplet 3

Uwo muco nanje ndi muri wo, abari muri wo bose ndabazi ab’umwiza bari mu mwiza, ab’umuco bari mu muco ./.Nta ruvange ruri muri wo n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana./.

Refrain

Abana b’Imana barawukurikira, waduga umusozi bakawukurikira, ah’uciye niho baca uhagaze bagahagarara; iy’ugiye bakagenda, n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana.

Couplet 4

Ab’uwo muco usize bose, basigara mu mwiza bose, satani nawe akabakubita bakuzura amarira bose ./.Uwo ubuntu bugendereye niw’asubira kuwubona./.

Refrain

Abana b’Imana barawukurikira, waduga umusozi bakawukurikira, ah’uciye niho baca uhagaze bagahagarara; iy’ugiye bakagenda, n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana.

Couplet 5

Uwo muco ntur’uruvange, n’umuco utamanzuye cane, abari muri wo bose uko bari, bihutira kubona wa mubi ./.Ntawigera atsitara muri bo n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana./.

Refrain

Abana b’Imana barawukurikira, waduga umusozi bakawukurikira, ah’uciye niho baca uhagaze bagahagarara; iy’ugiye bakagenda, n’umuco w’Uhoraho Imana.
