#341

Fat’ukuboko kwanjye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Fat’ukuboko kwanjye,—Ungendeshe; Tujyan’ Ungez’ Aheza—Ku Witeka. Sinagir’aho ngera,—Ntagufite. Mwam’ ah’Ushaka ko njya,—Uhanjyane.

Couplet 2

Ibyago ni bintera—Mpumuriza; Unturiz’ umutima,—Ni mbabara. Naje nguhungiyeho,—Ngo nduhuke: Sintinya turi kumwe,— Mwami wanjye.

Couplet 3

Ubu naho nahishwa—K’Untabara, Nizeye k’Unzanjyana.—Ukankiza. End’ukuboko kwanjye,—Unyobore, Tujyan’ Ungez’ Aheza—Ku Witeka.
