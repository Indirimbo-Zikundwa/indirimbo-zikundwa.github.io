#67

Uhagaze Mukiza, ku rugi rwugaye

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Uhagaze Mukiza, ku rugi rwugaye, Urorereye yuko Bakwugururira. Ariko bene Data, S’ivy’ isoni nyinshi Ko twugarana hanze Umukiza wacu?

Couplet 2

Akomanga ku rugi, Kand’ afis’ inkovu z’imambo n’ iz’ amahwa Yatsibg’ igitsibo. Urukundo si rwinshi Rw’ urorerey’ artyo? N’iki corut’ icaha Co kutamwinjiza?

Couplet 3

Yes’ aratwinging’ ati Ko nabapfiriye, None n’iki gituma Mungirira murtyo? Mwami duteterejwe N’ ico caha kibi; Kaza, kaza, nyakuza Ntituvan’ukundi.
