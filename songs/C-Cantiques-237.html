Ndi musue kumanya

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Ungambile bua Yesu ne Luse Luandi kudi bantu; Nakumvua lumu luimpe ne: Yesu wakafuila bonso.

Refrain

{Ndi musue kumanya,} x2 Malu makuabo a Yesu Ne bu muakatufuileye.

Couplet 2

Nyuma wa Nzambi, Muyishi, Buela mu mutshima wanyi. Unyishe malu makuabo Makane a Musungidi.

Refrain

{Ndi musue kumanya,} x2 Malu makuabo a Yesu Ne bu muakatufuileye.

Couplet 3

Unjinguluile Meyi andi Adi mu Mukanda wandi Adi atuleja Muoyo, Ngitabuje ne mutshima.

Refrain

{Ndi musue kumanya,} x2 Malu makuabo a Yesu Ne bu muakatufuileye.

Couplet 4

Undeje Njila wa Muoyo Wakatulengejileye; Wewe mene udianjile, Tshiena musue kupambuka.

Refrain

{Ndi musue kumanya,} x2 Malu makuabo a Yesu Ne bu muakatufuileye.
