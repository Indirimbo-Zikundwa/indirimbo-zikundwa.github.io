#246

I Gologota ku Giti

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

I Gologota, ku Giti, ni ho Yesu Yabambwe; Yabambiwe kugira ngo tubone kudapfa.

Couplet 2

Yes’Amaze Kunesha, Avug’Ijwi rirenga, Abivuga yeruye, Ati: Birarangiye!

Couplet 3

Amaze kuvug’atyo, Aherakw’ arunama, Yunamishwa n’ibyaha byanjye nanze kwihana.

Couplet 4

None nsaby’imbabazi, nihane, mukureyo, mumanure ku Giti, Aruhuke umutima.

Couplet 5

Abantu batihana bongeye kumubamba, n’umuruho munini, umubabaza iteka.

Couplet 6

Twari twararuhijwe, twiganyira, twihebye, none turaruhutse: Yesu ni Uburuhukiro

Couplet 7

Nuk’abishwe n’ibyaha, ni muhunge, mukire. Muze, mubikurwemo: Yesu n’Uburuhukiro

Couplet 8

Mukiza, ndakwihaye. ni Wowe Wantaruye. Uko nzaba ndi kose, Yesu, nzagusingiza.
