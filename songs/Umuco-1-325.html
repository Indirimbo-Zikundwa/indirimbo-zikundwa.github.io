#325

Mbe bizokumarir’iki?

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Mbe bizokumarir’iki mwene Data, Kuri wa musi w’Umwami Yesu, Ni wasangwa waratamiye ubusa, Kandi waraboneka ko wari warizeye?

Refrain

Uwo musi ni washika (ncuti), Umwami Yesu Azogaruka, Azohagarara iyo mu bicu, Azosemerera n’ijwi rirenga, Abizeye gusa vy’ukuri, Ni bo bonyene Bazozamurwa.

Couplet 2
