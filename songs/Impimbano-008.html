#8

Muze dusange umwungeri

Impimbano· Fr Siméon

Couplet 1

Muze dusange Umwungeri, Ahagaz’ahw’ Aragira, Mubwatsi bwiza bubisi hahora isoko idakama.

Refrain

Muze dusange Umwungeri yemwe Abakunda by’ukuri, Aruta abandi bungeri ni Yesu nshaka Wenyine.

Couplet 2

Ni Yesu nshaka Wenyine ntubwire abandi bungeri, Ampamagara mwizina nkamwitaba nezerewe.

Refrain

Nejejwe nuko Andagira Yantaruye nazimiye, ninde mukunzi umuruta Wapfira intama aragira?

Couplet 3

Iyo bukeye kumucyo n’igihe bwije n’ijoro, mbanshakako twagumana nuwo Mukunzi Ubaruta.

Refrain

Mukunzi wanjye Ivugire kuko Ur’ Isoko idakama ,Nzaguma nywere kuriyo nzahazwa n’ibyiza byawe.

Couplet 4

Unyuzure mumutima sinshakako Wandekura, gumana nanjye Mwungeri ndagira Unjyane Murugo.

Refrain

Nd’Uwumukunzi wanjye ubu nawe nuwanjye by’ukuri vuba aha jye nzitahira mbane na Yesu mu Ijuru.
