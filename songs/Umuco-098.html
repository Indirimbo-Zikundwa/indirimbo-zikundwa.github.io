#98

Mwami Yesu Mwami w’ubuntu

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mwami Yesu Mwami w’Ubuntu, mbeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwawe; musi y’ijuru mbona ibingora vyinshi, je mbeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe.

Refrain

Munsi y’ijuru harih’ ingorane nyinshi zishikira umwana w’umuntu./. Ntankomezi eka nta bushobozi, tubeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe./.

Couplet 2

Je narahuye n’ingorane nyinshi, nkarondera inzira nocamwo; nkaca nsanga naciye ahatari ho je mbeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe.

Refrain

Munsi y’ijuru harih’ ingorane nyinshi zishikira umwana w’umuntu./. Ntankomezi eka nta bushobozi, tubeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe./.

Couplet 3

Ngerageza kwikubit’ impu zompi, ndondera Umwami wanje; ariko nabonye ko atari kubw’ inkomezi, je mbeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe.

Refrain

Munsi y’ijuru harih’ ingorane nyinshi zishikira umwana w’umuntu./. Ntankomezi eka nta bushobozi, tubeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe./.

Couplet 4

Nter’ intambwe kubw’ Ubuntu bwayo kubind’ imbere niteze Ubuntu; nd’umukristo kubw’ Ubuntu bwayo, je mbeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe.

Refrain

Munsi y’ijuru harih’ ingorane nyinshi zishikira umwana w’umuntu./. Ntankomezi eka nta bushobozi, tubeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe./.

Couplet 5

Ntac’ umwana w’umuntu yokwirata kubw’ inkomezi canke kubw’ ubushobozi arik’ uwirata yirate Ubuntu, tubeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe.

Refrain

Munsi y’ijuru harih’ ingorane nyinshi zishikira umwana w’umuntu./. Ntankomezi eka nta bushobozi, tubeshwaho n’Ubuntu bwiwe./.
