#307

Igihe ni kibi, shengero ry’imana.

Umuco

Couplet 1

Igihe ni kibi, Shengero ry’Imana, Guma mw’Ijambo rya Yesu tugira dutahe. Teg’ugutwi bwenge, wiyamirize gipfu, Niwirind’ umwambiro w’abafarisayo.

Refrain

Umuvugishwa w’igihe cacu yarashiz’ ijwi hejuru, ngo mwongera kumubamba nko ku Musaraba. Inyigisho z’amadini muzirutish’ivya Yesu, mwongera kumubamb’ ubugira kabiri.

Couplet 2

Ibuk’aho bamubamba, ico bamuziza, ngo yigira Imana kand’azirur’isabato. Bari bahegekey’ibitabo vyabo, mugabo ntibahishuriwe Umwami uw’ari we.

Refrain
