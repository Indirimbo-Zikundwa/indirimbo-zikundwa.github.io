Uhor’ urwan’ Intambara nziza

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Couplet 2

Couplet 3

Umukorer’ amaganya yawe. Wishimikize kuri we; Umv’ ubuntu bgiwe buraguhagije, N’urukundo n’imbabazi.

Couplet 4

Ntutinye, reka gucobogora, Ntahinduk’ aragukunda, Izere gusa ntuzogir’ ic’ ubura: Ufise Yes’ aken’ iki?
