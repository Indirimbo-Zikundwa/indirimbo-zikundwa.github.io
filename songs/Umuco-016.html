Nzohora ndagushima

Umuco

Couplet 1

Nzohora ndagushima, ko We Yesu Wankunze ntabizi nari kure yawe Mwami, maze umbonye uca Urampamagara,

Refrain

Ewe Yesu Urankunda, nzosenga mw’Izina ryawe undongore mu vy’Ugomba, mb’uwawe imisi yose name ngendana nawe Mwami, ibihe vyose mb’uwawe.

Couplet 2

Nzohora ngushimira, kuko atarindi shikanwa mfise, tuma Mpwemu az’amfashe mpore ngushimira imisi yose.

Refrain

Ewe Yesu Urankunda, nzosenga mw’Izina ryawe undongore mu vy’Ugomba, mb’uwawe imisi yose name ngendana nawe Mwami, ibihe vyose mb’uwawe.

Couplet 3

Refrain
