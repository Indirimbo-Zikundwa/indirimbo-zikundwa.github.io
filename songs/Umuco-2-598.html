#598

Ubu turi mu rugamba ruhambaye

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Ubu turi mu Rugamba ruhambaye cane, Tugwanir’ Inzira y’Agakiza kacu. Satani yahagurukany’ ishavu ryinshi, ateg’ imiteg’ Abana bawe Mwami; {None Mwami duteguz’ Ijambo ryawe.} x2

Refrain

Emera tuyoborwe n’Ijambo, Ubu turi mur’iyi si tuyoborwe n’Ijambo. Kuko ni Ryo Rizodufasha mu Rugendo, Nk’uko vyamye kuri bene Data bose; {Abagiye batwitangir’ imbere.} x2
