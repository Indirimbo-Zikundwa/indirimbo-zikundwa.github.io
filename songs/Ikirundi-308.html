Mwami Yesu, tuzanywe

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mwami Yesu, tuzanywe no kugaburirw’ ivyawe, Duhage mu mitima Ijambo ryawe riryoshe; Ivyo mw isi tubireke, Twiyumvir’ ivyo mw ijuru.

Couplet 2

Nta co twebge tworonka, imitim’ icirabuye, Itarezwa n’umuco Uva kuri Mpwemu wawe Utwigish’ ingeso nziza, N’ukugira neza kwose.

Couplet 3

Raba Mwana w’Imana, Twebge twese tugusavye Ng’utweze mu mitima, Kand’ utuzibur’ amatwi, Ngo twumve, ngo turirimbe, Tuguhimbare Mukiza.
