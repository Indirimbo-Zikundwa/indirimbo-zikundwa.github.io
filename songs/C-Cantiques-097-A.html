This is the day

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

{This is the day,} x2 {That the Lord has made,} x2 {We will rejoice} x2 {And be glad in it.} x2

Refrain

This is the day that the Lord has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it. {This is the day,} x2 That the Lord has made.

Couplet 2

{We are the children,} x2 {Of the loving God,} x2 {We will rejoice} x2 {And be glad in Him.} x2

Refrain

We are the children of the loving God. We will rejoice and be glad in Him. {We are the children,} x2 Of the loving God.
