Mungu ni pahali pote

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Mungu ni pahali pote, hutamukimbia yeye; Anakutazama sana, naye hatalala kamwe.

Refrain

Giza, nuru, sikuzote Mungu anakutazama; Hata unafanya nini, Mungu anaona yote.

Couplet 2

Neno gani unasema, Mungu analisikia; Hata mafikili yote anayatambua wazi.

Refrain

Giza, nuru, sikuzote Mungu anakutazama; Hata unafanya nini, Mungu anaona yote.

Couplet 3

Refrain
