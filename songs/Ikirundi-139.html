Nd’uwawe Mwami, kuko numvise

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Nd’uwawe Mwami, kuko numvise Ijwi ryawe ry’Urukundo, Ariko nifuza kukwizera no kukwegera rwose.

Refrain

Nyegereza hafi Yawe, Mukiza, hafi y’Umusaraba, Nyegerez’ Urubavu rwawe, Mwami, rumwe rwancumitirwa.

Couplet 2

Unsabishe Mwami, nkuyoboke, nshobozwa n’Ubuntu Bgawe, ivyo ngomba bicik’ ivy’ Ugomba, ngutumbere nkwizeye.

Refrain

Nyegereza hafi Yawe, Mukiza, hafi y’Umusaraba, Nyegerez’ Urubavu rwawe, Mwami, rumwe rwancumitirwa.

Couplet 3

Eka ndanezerewe ko ndonse uko twobonana Nawe, nd’imbere y’Inyundo zawe Mwami, tukayaga nk’incuti.

Refrain

Nyegereza hafi Yawe, Mukiza, hafi y’Umusaraba, Nyegerez’ Urubavu rwawe, Mwami, rumwe rwancumitirwa.

Couplet 4

Refrain

Nyegereza hafi Yawe, Mukiza, hafi y’Umusaraba, Nyegerez’ Urubavu rwawe, Mwami, rumwe rwancumitirwa.
