#367

Yesu ndakwitabye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yesu ndakwitabye,—ntakwiriye: Imbabazi zawe—zirantinyuye. Mfit’ ibicumuro—byuzuye muri jye; Niringiye gusa—ko Wampfiriye.

Couplet 2

Ibyo nacumuye—ndabyihannye. Kand’uko ndi kose—simbiguhishe. Nuk’Unyuhagize—Amaraso yawe, nyogemo, ntungane,—ico rimvemo.

Couplet 3

Mwami, mbabarira;—ndakwizeye. Nkwikubis’ imbere;—ndakwiringiye. Amaraso yawe—Y’Umwana w’Intama, Anyoz’ antembeho,—anyeze dede!

Couplet 4

Nuko, ndanezerwa;—mb’Amahoro; Njyana n’Uwo nkunda,—Ntaramubona; Itek’uko ntewe—N’iby’isi n’ibyaha. Negamira Yesu,— bikanesheka.
