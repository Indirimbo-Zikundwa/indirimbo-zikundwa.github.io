Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Ni muze turirimbane n’Abera bo mw’ Ijuru, Nah’ ar’ indim’ ibihumbi, banezerwa kumwe.

Couplet 2

Bat’ Umukiz’ Abereye guhabg’ Icubahiro, natwe tut’ Arakubahwa, Yaradupfiriye!

Couplet 3

Yes’ abereye guhabga Ubushobozi bginshi n’Ubutunzi n’Umugisha n’Ubgenge n’ Ishimwe.

Couplet 4

Ivyo mw’ Ijuru no mw’ isi mwe n’ ibir’ ikuzimu, ni mukoranire hamwe gutazira Yesu.

Couplet 5

Yemw’ ivyaremwe, ja hamwe mushim’ Izina Ryera ry’ Iyivyagiye ku Ntebe, n’irya wa Mwagazi.
