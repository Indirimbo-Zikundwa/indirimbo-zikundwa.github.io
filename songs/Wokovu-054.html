Usiogope mateso yako

Wokovu

Couplet 1

Usiogope mateso yako, Mungu anakulinda, bali tazama mapenzi yake, Mungu anakulinda.

Refrain

Mungu anakulinda siku kwa siku njiani mwake. Anakulinda salama. Mungu anakulinda.

Refrain

Refrain

Refrain
