#390

Ngabo z’umwete mubyuke

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ngabo z’umwete, mubyuke, nimurwanir’ Umwami; Mwatoranijwe n’Imana: Kubwayo muranesha. Mwitegure mu mitima, mwirind’ ibibashaka; Isi muyigandurire Yes’Umwami w’abami.

Couplet 2

Mubwiriz’ab’isi bose Urukundo rw’Imana; Abatuye mu mwijima mubabwir’ iby’Umucyo. Abayobye batizera, muti: Mwemere Yesu, kukw’ Akunda guhabura imitim’izimiye.

Couplet 3

Mujye mu bihugu byose, mubwiriz’ iby’Umwami; Mujyan’ Umwuka We Wera; Muzavug’ ibye neza, Itorero rye rigwire mu bari mw’isi yose: Izina ry’Umwami Yesu ripfukamirwe hose.

Couplet 4

Hahirw’abizey’ Umwami, bakamubabarizwa; Bazabon’ Ingororano Umuns’ Azagaruka: Baziman’ Iteka ryose hamwe n’Umwami Yesu, mu Bwami bwe budashira, kuko bamukoreye.
