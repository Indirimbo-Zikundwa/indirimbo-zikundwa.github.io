#47

Iyo mw’ijuru iwacu

Umuco

Couplet 1

Iyo mw’ijuru iwacu hariy’ivyiza vyinshi bizotunezereza, ntaho bizoshira.

Refrain

Tuzoshika iy’iwacu dushim’ Uhoraho tuti; Haleluya, Haleluya iminsi yose.

Couplet 2

Iyo mw’ijuru iwacu hariy’amazu menshi, Yesu yaduteguriye niyo tuzobamwo.

Refrain

Tuzoshika iy’iwacu dushim’ Uhoraho tuti; Haleluya, Haleluya iminsi yose.

Couplet 3

Iyo mw’ijuru iwacu hariy’itunga ryacu, ahatagerwa n’inyenzi canke ibisuma.

Refrain

Tuzoshika iy’iwacu dushim’ Uhoraho tuti; Haleluya, Haleluya iminsi yose.

Couplet 4

Iyo kwa Data Imana tuzonezerwa cane, umunezero mwinshi utigeze kubaho.

Refrain

Tuzoshika iy’iwacu dushim’ Uhoraho tuti; Haleluya, Haleluya iminsi yose.

Couplet 5

Yemwe mwa busho buto mwitwararike cane ivy’Ubwami bw’Imana duhung’ivy’isi.

Refrain

Tuzoshika iy’iwacu dushim’ Uhoraho tuti; Haleluya, Haleluya iminsi yose.
