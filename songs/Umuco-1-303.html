#303

Bana b’imana mwacunguwe

Umuco 1

Refrain

[Tad. en Fr., voir Cant n° 454]

Couplet 1

Bana b’Imana mwacunguwe, n’Amaraso y’Imana, Emera kuyoborwa na Mpwemu mu, nzir’ifunganye, Noneho rero fat’ibigwanishwa vya, Mpwem atanga, Kuko tutagwana n’ab’inyama, canke n’amaraso.

Refrain

{Mukenyez’ukuri, ari kwo Jambo rihishuwe, Mugirir’umwete agakiza twahawe na Yesu.} x2

Couplet 2

Ambar’ibirato vy’agakiza mu-, gir’umwete mw’Ijambo, Maze muduge na ya misozi yaruhije, benshi, Mu gikiriza naho mwambar’icuma, gikomeye, Kibakingir’imyampi wa mubi abarasa, yose.

Refrain

{Mwitwaze n’inkinzo, ni yo kwizera kuzima, Kumwe kwahawe Abera bose bakakugwanira.} x2

Couplet 3

Itwaz’inkota ya Mpwemu Yera, kand’ifis’ubugi, Imwe Umwami Yesu yaneshesh’-, umwansi Satani, Maze twirinde na Satani ntaduce mu, ryahumye, Ngw atwambur’ibirrovanishwa vyacu, ngo tube dutsinzwe.

Refrain

{Duseng’urudaca, bene Data muri Mpwemu, Yera, Twicisha bugufi muri vyose dusabir’Abera.} x2

Couplet 4

Ibisigaye muremesherezwe mu, Mwami Yesu, Mugende mwerekan’akaranga, k’Uwaducunguye, Maz’ukwizera, ivyizigiro, hamwe, n’urukundo, Vyame vyuzuy’imitima yany’ibihe, bidashira.

Refrain

Twirukane Satani, bene Data maz’azoduhunga, Twiyegurire Umwami Yesu ni ho tuzonesha.} x2
