#173

Atukenga kudi mwanta

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Atukeŋa kudi Mwanta, Diyi wekala nanetu, Diyi watwilukishaŋa, Twakeŋewa.

Couplet 2

Atukeŋa haya nyaka, Kudona wafuma mwiulu, Anenti wumi niluwi, Twakeŋewa.

Couplet 3

A tukeŋa kudi Mwanta, Hela mafuku akala, Etu chochitwamwitiya, Twakeŋewa.

Couplet 4

Atukeŋa kudi Mwanta, Hakashiya antwinduku, Wenana afwililiyi, Twakeŋewa.

Couplet 5

Atukeŋa nihaloshi, Nilelu, nikumadiki; Mwejima twaya tweluka, Twakeŋewa.

Couplet 6

Atukeŋa: twadi kwimba, Mataŋwa ejima netu, Mwanta watela, walala, Watukeŋa.
