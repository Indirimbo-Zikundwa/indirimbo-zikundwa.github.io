#210

Akaruru k’Intambara

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Akaruru k’Intambara numva karerema, Satan’ atabaz’ abiwe bose bo mw’isi, ariko singir’ ubgoba, nanse gutabara, nta c’angira ko mb’ahera cane.

Refrain

Ninjiy’ahera cane Yesu Yanteguriye, mpora nyw’ Amazi meza y’isok’ idakama, Manu ni yo ndy’imisi yose, wa Mutsima w’Imana, nagererey’ ahera cane.

Couplet 2

Satani wa Mubi yam’ azan’ impagarara, ngw’ aturas’ imyampi myinshi yak’ umuriro, ariko ntashobora gushingur’ akarimbi k’Umusaraba Yesu Yashinze.

Refrain

Couplet 3

Non’isegenya ni yagw’ inkukur’ igatwaza, umuyag’ uhuhuse bikansurirako, nzi kw’ata co nzoba kuko nshinzwe ku rutare nzirits’ umutim’ ahera cane.

Refrain

Couplet 4
