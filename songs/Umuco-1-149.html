#149

Ewe ncuti yanje uri ngaha

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Ewe ncuti yannje uri ngaha, Wiyemeje gukurikira, Umwami Yesu ah’uzoba hose, Mu muriro canke mu rupfu, Shir’umutima kw’isezerano.

Refrain

Uwusezerana ntashitse, Ameze nk’umuguruka, Utwarwa n’umuyaga, None ko wasezeraniye Uhoraho, K’uzomushingira intahe hase, K’uzogendana nawe hose, Shir’umutima kw’isezerano.

Couplet 2

Washinz’intahe umunsi wabatizwa, Imbere y’Imana n’imbere y’abantu, K’ugiye kwemangir’Umwami Yesu, Mu mibabaro canke mu marushwa, Shir’umutima kw’isezerano.

Refrain

Uwusezerana ntashitse, Ameze nk’umuguruka, Utwarwa n’umuyaga, None ko wasezeraniye Uhoraho, K’uzomushingira intahe hase, K’uzogendana nawe hose, Shir’umutima kw’isezerano.

Couplet 3

Ntuz’igihe uzomara kw’isi, Ntuz’umusi Yesu azozira, Ntuz’umunsi wawe wo kwimurwa, None bite ni wadendebukirwa? Shir’umutima kw’isezerano.

Refrain

Uwusezerana ntashitse, Ameze nk’umuguruka, Utwarwa n’umuyaga, None ko wasezeraniye Uhoraho, K’uzomushingira intahe hase, K’uzogendana nawe hose, Shir’umutima kw’isezerano.

Couplet 4

Iyambure ubunebwe mu rugendo, Ugir’umwete mu vya Mpwemu, Iyambure umugenzo mu vy’Ijambo, Wuzure uguhishurirwa, Ubone gushika amahoro.

Refrain

Couplet 5

Ntuhengamire iburyo cank’ibumoso, Ntuhengamire mu vy’umubiri, Ntuhengamire muvya Mpwemu, N’ukorere agakiza kawe, Uhind’agashits’imbere y’Uhoraho.
