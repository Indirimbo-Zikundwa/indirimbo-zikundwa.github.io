Yesu udi dina dimpa

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Yesu udi dina dimpe Didi disankisha, Didi ditalaja muoyo, Didi dikolesha.

Couplet 2

Muoyo wa kanyinganyinga Wakusankishibwa, Yeye udi bidia bietu, Udi tshikishilu.

Couplet 3

Dina edi didi Ngabu, Didi Tshieyemenu; Yeye udi Tshibutshilu, Udi muule luse.

Couplet 4

Yesu Mulami, Mulunda, Muishi, Muakuidi, Mbangidilu, Nshikidilu, M’biuma bianyi bionso.

Couplet 5

Meme tshiena ne bukole, Ndi nkutekemena; Panamona buimpe buebe, Nentambe kusanka.
