#266

Man’Ubwami bwawe

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Man’Ubwami bwawe—Buze mur’iyi si! tsind’ Umurimbuzi,—Wic’ubutware bwe.

Couplet 2

Mwami, dukumbuye —iyo Ngoma yawe. Usimbuz’ ineza—inzangano z’isi.

Couplet 3

Turagukumbuye!—Ngwin’uc’ intambara. Uce n’ingeso mbi,— Zos’uzimareho.

Couplet 4

Zanan’imbaraga; —Uz’Uduhembure. Turakurindira; —Amas’ahezeyo.

Couplet 5

Dor’ukw’amasega—Amarahw’intama, kandi hagwiriye—Abatakwitaho.

Couplet 6

Amahanga yose—Ari mu mwijima; Yesu, wa zuba We.—Non’uz’Uyavire.
