#356

Batoboy’ibiganza bye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Batoboy’ibiganza bye, byabumby’ imisozi, byaremye byose, byarambikwaga ku bana bato, byakizag’ indembe n’impumyi nyinshi.

Couplet 2

Batoboy’ibirenge bye, byatambagiye ku nyenyeri zose, byagendagenze no mw’isi yacu. Bisur’abakene n’abanyabyaha.

Couplet 3

Bacumis’umutima we, Wakundag’ indushyi n’aboroheje; Uvamw’ Amaraso yoz’ibyaha, Atemba nk’uruzi kubwac’ ababi.

Couplet 4

Uwo mutima wa Yesu n’Ingingo ze nziza byaziz’iki se? Ni jye byazize n’ibyaha byanjye, i Gologota, ku Musaraba We.

Couplet 5

Uko ndi, Yes’Unyakire, ingingo zanjye n’umutima wanjye, ko Wanshunguye, nakwiyima nte? Njye nkor’iby’ Ukunda, mb’imbata yawe!
