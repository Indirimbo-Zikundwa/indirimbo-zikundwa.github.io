#172

Guh 125 NKUKW’IZUBA RITUVIRA

Izindi

Couplet 1

Nkukw’ izuba rituvira, rikarabagirana, ni k’Umucyo wo mw’ijuru wansanze mu mutima. Wawukuyemw iby’amanjwe, utangaz’ ibihishwe; Kuko mu mutima wanjye, nahatuj’ Umukiza.

Refrain

Mbeg’Umucyo w’agatangaza, wansanze mu mutima! Ineza y’Umwami Yesu yambereye nk’Umucyo.

Couplet 2

Natay’agahinda kose; nta bwo nkikitayeho; Kuk’ unyambik’ amahoro, ameze nk’umwitero. Nkwerekejeh’ umutima, ndakwishimiye cyane. Nzagezw’i wawe mw’Ijuru na rwa Rukundo rwawe.

Refrain

Mbeg’Umucyo w’agatangaza, wansanze mu mutima! Ineza y’Umwami Yesu yambereye nk’Umucyo.

Couplet 3

Mukiza waranshunguye; nd’uwaw’ uko ndi kose. Nyemerera nguhimbaze, nkuber’ umucyo waka. Nkwerekejeh’ umutima, ndakwishimiye cyane. Nzagezw’i wawe mw’Ijuru na rwa Rukundo rwawe.

Refrain

Mbeg’Umucyo w’agatangaza, wansanze mu mutima! Ineza y’Umwami Yesu yambereye nk’Umucyo.
