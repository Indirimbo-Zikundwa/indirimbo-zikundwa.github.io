#5

Umusi w’ibirori mu ijuru uri bugufi

Impimbano· Fr Siméon

Couplet 1

Umunsi w’ibirori mu Ijuru, uri bugufi uregereje Mugeni wa Yesu uhumure {Umukwe ntakabuze araje}.x2

Refrain

Hazabaho Indirimbo mu ljuru, Numunezero utarondoreka, Abera b’Imana muhumure, Araje Araje mushikame mutegure umwambaro w’Ubukwe Nukuri tugiye kugerayo.

Couplet 2

Ubu nitegurwa ry’Umugeni, Ejo byashoboka ko ari Ubukwe. Umukwe Yamaze kuvuga ko {Adatinda nuko tube maso.}x2

Refrain

Nukuri tuzigira mu ijuru tumaze no kwambikwa kudapfa hamwe Nabera bo mubihe byambere. Numunezero uzaba mu Ijuru, Nibyishimo bitarondoreka Hahiriwe abatorewe Ubukwe.

Couplet 3

Intambara yigihe cyanyuma, iriho iragana kwiherezo Mwami Undwaninire nyirangize, {Nkuremo uyumwambaro wurugamba.} x2

Refrain

Sinzi ibi ntegereje mu Ijuru, uretse kumva ko bikomeye, ibyo ijisho ritari ryabona ugutwi ntikwigeze kubyumva. Nibitigeze mumutima wanjye, nibyo Abera twebwe tubikiwe.x2

Refrain

{Hallellula Hallellula mu ljuru} x3 {Numunezero utarondoreka} x3
