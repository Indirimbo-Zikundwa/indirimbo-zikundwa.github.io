#293

Tshiena nshala to

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Ndi njinga ne muoyo wanyi onso Kumona Yesu dituku diadia; Diambuibua tshisamba tshia mulayi, Tshiena nshala to!

Refrain

Ndi musue kuya kulu kua Nzambi, Ndi musue kusabuka Yadena; Ndi musue kubuela mu disanka, Tshiena nshala to!

Couplet 2

Ndi muakupangila bintu bionso: Bubanji, lumu bia pa buloba, Bua kuya ku didia dia dibanji, Tshiena nshala to!

Refrain

Ndi musue kuya kulu kua Nzambi, Ndi musue kusabuka Yadena; Ndi musue kubuela mu disanka, Tshiena nshala to!

Couplet 3

Muamua kamuakuikala masama, Kamuena lufu ne binsonji to; Mudianu misambu ya banjelo, Tshiena nshala to!

Refrain

Ndi musue kuya kulu kua Nzambi, Ndi musue kusabuka Yadena; Ndi musue kubuela mu disanka, Tshiena nshala to!

Couplet 4

Ngitabi kufua ku bukuapanu, Nzambi angata bumuntu buanyi; Ndombola mu diyi diebe dionso, Bua ntshishadi to!

Refrain

Ndi musue kuya kulu kua Nzambi, Ndi musue kusabuka Yadena; Ndi musue kubuela mu disanka, Tshiena nshala to!
