#294

Har’ubw’abagore bazany’abana babo

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Har’ubw’abagore bazany’abana babo, Abigishwa ntibemera ko barushya Yesu. Nyamara Yes’abony’ibyo, Arabahamagar’ati: Mwemerer’ abana ngo baz’aho ndi!

Couplet 2

Ati: Ndabakira, mbashyire ku bibero; Ntimuzi ko nd’Umwungeri w’abana bato, se? Ni banyisunga bitanze, nzabaha kujya mw’Ijuru: Mwemerer’ abana ngo baz’aho ndi!

Couplet 3

Icyaduh’abana bo mumahanga yose! kumv’inkuru za Yesu, bakaza kuriwe abayeruriy’ umwijima, abakiran’ imbabazi twemerer’ abana ngo baz’ahwari!
