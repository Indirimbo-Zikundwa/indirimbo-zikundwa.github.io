#86

Yemwe ngabo z’umwete ni mubyuke

Impimbano· Sr Henriette

Couplet 1

Yemwe Ngabo z’umwete mwe ni mubyuke, tujye ku Rugamba rw’iherezo. Ntimutiny’ ikombe ry’ibigeragezo, murwanane n’Umwami Krisito. Mbese mufit’ Intwaro zanyu zose, Urugamba rugez’iherezo.

Couplet 2

Nshuti yanjye har’iby’ usiz’ inyuma se, wasiz’ udasezeye ku nshuti? Banz’ ugend’ uzagaruke witeguye, nta wakund’isi ngo akund’ Ijuru. Ntwari mwe mbese muriteguye kurwanana n’Umwami Krisito?

Couplet 3

Nimutware kwizera nk’Intwaro yanyu, muzimish’ imyambi y’umuriro. Mwambare n’Agakiza kab’ ingofero, inkweto zo n’Ubutumwa bwiza. Benedata museng’ ibihe byose, uzanesha n’Intwarane gusa.

Couplet 4

Mwami Yesu uturangaj’ imbere pe, n’Intsinz’ iri mu ruhande rwacu. Kw’iherezo twizeye ko tuzanesha, Data wera twuzuz’ imbaraga. {Tuguhaye n’ubu bugingo bwacu bub’ubwawe iteka n’iteka.} x3
