#257

Mana rurema we waturemye

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Mana rurema, We waturemye, Turagusavye umugisha, Duhezagire, duhe ijambo, Ridushikirize agakiza.

Refrain

Nico gitumn uyu musi, Twaje mu Ngoro yawe yera, Turagusavye utweze Mwami... Twambare imvune z’Ubukwe.

Couplet 2

Mana Rurema, We wadukunze, Twe turicishije bugufi, Duhe ukwizera, duhe urukundo, Twuzure amahoro muri twe.

Refrain

Nico gituma uyu musi, Twahevye vyose vyo gukora, Tukaza hano mu’Ngoro yawe... Kugusenga Wewe w’Ukuri.

Couplet 3

Mana rurema, Mana y’Abera, Twuzuze Ijambo rihishuwe, Mu nguvu zacu, ntidushobora, Kwitez’intambwe mu gakiza.

Refrain

Nico gituma abana bawe, Duhanze amaso umusaraba, Turakwinginze Mwami utuvure, Tube abakristo bashikamye.

Couplet 4

Mana rurema, We watwiguze, Suka Mpwemu wawe muri twe, Tujanjagure uduhindure, Ibikoresho vyawe Mwami.

Refrain

Kaze Mukiza ndakwizeye, Wampishuriye Ijambo Ryera, N’ubu komeza kūmurikira... Nze nshike mw’ijturu amahoro.
