#626

Igihe duhwaniye n’umwami Yesu

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Igihe duhwaniye n’Umwami Yesu, Akanya kose ndirimba Haleluya, Ubu mushingir’ intahe nezerewe, Akanya kose ndirimba Haleluya.

Refrain

Nzomuhimbaza ndamutazire, Ndangij’ Urugendo rwanje mur’iyi si, Nzoririmbana n’Abera, Mu Nyonga z’Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 2

Ndagushima Mana yuko Wambohoye, Akanya kose ndirimba Haleluya, Wankijije abansi b’Umusaraba, Ubu Mwami je ndirimba Haleluya.

Refrain

Nzomuhimbaza ndamutazire, Ndangij’ Urugendo rwanje mur’iyi si, Nzoririmbana n’Abera, Mu Nyonga z’Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 3

Ndashima ko Wabohoye bene Data, Ubu Mwami je ndirimba Haleluya, Nzovug’Izina ryawe ndaryemangire, Kuko rifis’Ububasha bukomeye.

Refrain

Nzomuhimbaza ndamutazire, Ndangij’ Urugendo rwanje mur’iyi si, Nzoririmbana n’Abera, Mu Nyonga z’Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 4

Reka ntazire Izina ryawe Mana, Kuko ari ryo ryampay’ Intsinzi yawe, Haba mu bikomeye nzoryemangira, Kuk’Utigeze Unta Mana y’Abera.

Refrain

Ubu ni jewe niyemereje gukurikir’ Umukiza wanje Yesu, Mu mahirwe no mu magorwa, Nzemangir’ Uwambambiwe.
