#159

Yesu Kristo aho yari kw’isi

Umuco

Couplet 1

Yesu Kristo aho Yari kw’isi, Yageragaje Kuducirinzira, ivyo yakora vyose uko biri, kw’ar’ ukugira ngo Aducire inzira.

Refrain

We Yakunda gusenga cane, Asenger’ Igikorwa c’Imana, kubw’ Ubushobozi bw’ Amasengesho, Yaneshej’ intege nke z’umubiri, yari yuzuye ukwihangana, yaseng’ ijoro ryose, kumutaga akavuga Ubutumwa.

Couplet 2

Aho yaseng’ar’ Igetsemane, kw’ar’ ukugira ngo aducir’ inzira, kubw’ Ubushobozi bw’ Amasengesho, Yabony’ inkomezi zivuye mw’ijuru.

Refrain

We Yakunda gusenga cane, Asenger’ Igikorwa c’Imana, kubw’ Ubushobozi bw’ Amasengesho, Yaneshej’ intege nke z’umubiri, yari yuzuye ukwihangana, yaseng’ ijoro ryose, kumutaga akavuga Ubutumwa.

Couplet 3

Yabwiy’ abigishwa biwe ati : nimupfukame musenge cane, kugira ntimurwe mumosha mabi, natwe dusenge ijoro n’umutaga.

Refrain

Couplet 4

Yakijij’ ugutwi umwansi wiwe, kwar’ ukugira ngo Aducir’ inzira, Yasemerey’ ati : baharire, kw’ar’ ukugira ngo Aducir’ inzira.

Refrain

Couplet 5
