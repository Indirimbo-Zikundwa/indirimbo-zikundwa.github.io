#510

Eh’amazi si ya yandi

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Eh’amazi si ya yandi bene Data, Niduhumur’ amaso maze tubone, Ic’Umwami wacu Yes’Atugombako, Niduteg’ amatwi maze duhanurwe, Tube maso duhore dusenga. Ntidusinzirire mur’iyi si, Twishire mu Maboko y’Umwami, maze turongorwe n’Uhoraho.

Refrain

Mbeg’abashonje kw’ari benshi, wasanze bagaburirwa na nde? Kw’abambay’ ubusa ari benshi, mbe wasanze bambikwa na nde? Eh’Umukiza Yesu ni W’abambika, kandi ni na We nyen’ Abagaburira, Ishimwe n’iry’Uhorah’ Imana yacu, kuv’ubu kugez’ Ibihe bidashira.

Couplet 2
