Shimwa Mwami!

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Shimwa Mwami ko wazutse, Natwe twizeye iyo ntsinzi nyakuri, Gushik’ igihe uzozira Mwami, Kutujana Iwawe mw’Ijuru.

Couplet 2

Ibisinzikaza vyose, Tuzobiruhuka ni twager’i wawe, Uzaduh’ Amazu yacu Mwami, Tuzoruhuka umubabaro wose.

Couplet 3

Tunezerwe mu mitima, Kuk’Umwami Yesu Yatuvukiye, Dufis’ umunezero mwinshi cane, Ko twagizwe Ihanga ry’Imana.

Couplet 4

Couplet 5

Couplet 6

Couplet 7

Couplet 8

Shengero tube maso pe, Dukovye kugira ngo ntidutsindwe, Dusab’ Imana ngo Iduhe, Kugendera mw’Ijambo tubwirwa.
