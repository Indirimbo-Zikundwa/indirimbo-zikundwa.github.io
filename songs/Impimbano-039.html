#39

Mukunzi wanjye ndagutegereje

Impimbano· Fr Siméon

Couplet 1

Mukunzi wanjye ndagutegereje, Tebuka umvane muriyisi. Ntereye amaso ndabona igihugu, Nifuza cyane kugerayo. (x2)

Refrain

{Nuzuy’impundu mumutima wanjye, Ndahumurijwe ndanizera, niringiye ko vuba nzakubona, Mukunzi Wanjye nitahiye.} x2

Couplet 2

Mukunzi wanjye nkunda gusubira, Muri za nzandiko Wanditse. Nkahita numva mbuze uko nifata, Ntekereje ubwo nzakubona. (x2)

Refrain

{Nuzuy’impundu mumutima wanjye, Ndahumurijwe ndanizera, niringiye ko vuba nzakubona, Mukunzi Wanjye nitahiye.} x2

Couplet 3

Nubwo nkifashe igihe Muntambara, hakaba nubwo ndemererwa. Nkomezwa cyane no gutegereza, Umugabane nabikiwe. (x2)

Refrain

{Nuzuy’impundu mumutima wanjye, Ndahumurijwe ndanizera, niringiye ko vuba nzakubona, Mukunzi Wanjye nitahiye.} x2

Couplet 4

Banguka untware ndagutegereje, mve mwiri cumbi nitahire. Mugihe cyose nkigutegereje, Ngumye gusenga ngo Ubanguke. (x2)

Refrain

{Nuzuy’impundu mumutima wanjye, Ndahumurijwe ndanizera, niringiye ko vuba nzakubona, Mukunzi Wanjye nitahiye.} x2
