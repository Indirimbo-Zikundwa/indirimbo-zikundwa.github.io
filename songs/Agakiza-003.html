#3

Nari narazimiriye kure

Agakiza

Couplet 1

Nari narazimiriye kure, nibagiwe Yesu, Nyuma Yes’Ambwiz’ Ijwi rye, rinzanir’ Umunezero.

Refrain

Non’ubu ndanezerewe, kuko Yesu Yantaruye. Aherakw’ Anyoz’ ibyaha. Niyemeje kumukunda.

Couplet 2

Sinajyaga niyibutsa ko nzajy’ imbere y’Imana. Ubw’ izaducir’ imanza, zihwanye n’ibyo twakoze.

Refrain

Non’ubu ndanezerewe, kuko Yesu Yantaruye. Aherakw’ Anyoz’ ibyaha. Niyemeje kumukunda.

Couplet 3

Nari narushye mu byaha, nza kugarukira Yesu. Nukw ambwir’ ijambo ryiza, mperako mbon’ amahoro.

Refrain

Non’ubu ndanezerewe, kuko Yesu Yantaruye. Aherakw’ Anyoz’ ibyaha. Niyemeje kumukunda.
