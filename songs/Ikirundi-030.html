#30

Yesu Mukunzi wanje, Mpa kuguhungirako

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yesu, Mukunzi wanje, Mpa kuguhungirako, iyo Satan’ anteye N’ ivy’ isi bikanyosha, Umpishe Mukiza we, Ba bansi ntibamfate, Umber’ ubuhungiro, Ni napf’uz’ Unyakire.

Couplet 2

Nta bundi buhungiro, Mwam’ unyirokorere, Ntunsige na gatoya, Arik’ umpumurize: Nkwizigiye wenyene, Ndemeshwa nawe gusa. Undindishe Satani Ubushobozi bgawe.

Couplet 3

Ur’ umunyembabazi Zitarangira Yesu; Usindagiz’ urushe, N’ urway’ uramukiza; Urera bihebuje, Nta ho ngutunganiye, ndi mubi bishimitse, Weh’ Uragororotse.

Couplet 4

Yesu, ni wewe soko Y’amazi y’ubugingo, Reka nkunyweko cane, Undudubiz’ imbere: Nuk’umutima wanje Wam’ ukwuzuye rwose. Amazi y’ iyo soko Abe mensh’ adakama.
