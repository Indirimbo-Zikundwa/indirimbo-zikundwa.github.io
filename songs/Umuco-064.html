#64

Ntaco nigeze menya

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ntaco nigeze menya narajuragirika; nari nk’impumyi itabona ntazi iyo nja n’iyo mva.

Refrain

Yarandondeye, Umwami wanje; kandi Yaranyiyeretse, ku Musaraba.

Couplet 2

Ngize ngo ndamumenya Uwancunguye impwemu mbi inzibir’amaso, mbon’idini.

Refrain

Yarandondeye, Umwami wanje; kandi Yaranyiyeretse, ku Musaraba.

Couplet 3

Ngize ngo ndab’imbere nsubij’inyuma; nsanga hose ni imisozi nzitijwe n’umwiza.

Refrain

Yarandondeye, Umwami wanje; kandi Yaranyiyeretse, ku Musaraba.

Couplet 4

Ngize ntya ndazamurwa ndavy’unzamuye; nsaga n’Umwami w’ubuntu niwe yancunguye.

Refrain

Yarandondeye, Umwami wanje; kandi Yaranyiyeretse, ku Musaraba.

Couplet 5

Non’ubu naramenye uwancunguye; nahinduts’umwana wiwe niwe Mwami wanje.

Refrain

Yarandondeye, Umwami wanje; kandi Yaranyiyeretse, ku Musaraba.

Couplet 6

Muri we har’itunga ry’uburyo bwose; ryateguriw’ abaragwa ni bo bahizi.

Refrain

Yarandondeye, Umwami wanje; kandi Yaranyiyeretse, ku Musaraba.
