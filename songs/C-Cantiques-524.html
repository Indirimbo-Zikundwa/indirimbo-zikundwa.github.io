#524

A Yesu, tu songuile

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

A Yesu, tu songuile; Tua yeva ondaka yove; Tua likuminya uvangi Wusoma wove u kola. Tu ku panduila otembo Yeliangiliyo lietu. Cilo, a soma yetu. Tu votokela upange.

Couplet 2

A Yesu, tu songuile; Ohele ka tu kuete, Momo ocisola cove Ca linga onyima yetu; Ocinyi cekulusu Ca tuila itima vietu. Anjuli yowelema, Yulisa omanu vove.

Couplet 3

A Yesu, tu songuile Toke ovitangi vi pua Kuenda olofeka viosi Vi mola ulamba wove. Halovimalehoko, Pamue lutoi wetimba, Te le Espiritu liove Usoma wove wiya.
