#1B

Yesu, Yesu udiku

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Yesu, Yesu udiku, Nenye kuudi lubilu,

Refrain

Meme tshiena ne muoyo, Mibi yakunkengesha. Mutshima wanyi mubi, Umpe luse lubilu.

Couplet 2

Satana wakunteya, Nakushipa mikenji Tshiena mua kupanduka, Mibi yanyi idiku. Tshiena mwa ku’ijimija, Anu Wewe nkayebe.

Refrain

Meme tshiena ne muoyo, Mibi yakunkengesha. Mutshima wanyi mubi, Umpe luse lubilu.

Couplet 3

Tshiena ne bintu bianyi, Bianza bidi bitupu; Ndi ne mutshima mubi, Tshiena mua kumvua bimpe; Unguvuile mutshima, Tshiena musue kukenga.

Refrain

Meme tshiena ne muoyo, Mibi yakunkengesha. Mutshima wanyi mubi, Umpe luse lubilu.

Couplet 4

Pandi bianyi ne muoyo, Kundekedi patupu; Palua bualu bukole, Walua biebe pabuipi; Palua lufu pabuipi, Umpe muoyo kashidi.

Refrain

Meme tshiena ne muoyo, Mibi yakunkengesha. Mutshima wanyi mubi, Umpe luse lubilu.
