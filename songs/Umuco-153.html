#153

Ni nde yompa kuva mur’iy’isi

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ninde yompa kuva mur’ iy’isi, isi yuzuye umubabaro; ni nde yompa kuva mur’ uyu mubiri ./.Nkigir’ iy’ Iwacu./.

Refrain

Iyo mbona ivy’iy’isi n’ububi bw’iki gihe kibi mbona n’ingorane nyinshi, ziterwa n’uyu mubiri, nca nibuka iy’Iwacu ./.Mw’Ijuru ku Mana./.

Couplet 2

Ni nde yompa kuva mur’ uyu muniho, meze nk’umugore afise ibise, ndemerewe n’umutwaro munini ./.Nkumbuy’ iy’ Iwacu./.

Refrain

Iyo mbona ivy’ iy’ isi n’ububi bw’iki gihe kibi mbona n’ingorane nyinshi, ziterwa n’uyu mubiri, nca nibuka iy’Iwacu ./.Mw’Ijuru ku Mana./.

Couplet 3

Ni nde yonkiza abansi banje, bambwira inkuru mbi z’iy’ Iwacu, bakikij’ amatwi yanje hose ./.Nifuza Umwami./.

Refrain

Iyo mbona ivy’ iy’ isi n’ububi bw’iki gihe kibi mbona n’ingorane nyinshi, ziterwa n’uyu mubiri, nca nibuka iy’Iwacu ./.Mw’Ijuru ku Mana./.

Couplet 4

Ni nde yompa kuva mur’ uyu mwiza, nkava no mur’ urugendo rubi, nkigurukir’ iy’ Iwacu Kwa Data ./.Muri Yerusalemu./.

Refrain

Couplet 5

Refrain
