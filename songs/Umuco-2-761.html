Yesu mukiza mwiza

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Yesu mukiza mwiza, Mana y’ubushobozi Rwanir’abana bawe, tuneshe tuze dushike iwawe. Twatanguy’ Inzira, twahishuriwe nawe, N’inzira nziza y’ukuri, n’ijambo ry’ubugingo.
