#360

Rukundo rwinshi

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Rukundo rwinshi,—Buntu butarutwa, Umwami Yesu—Kw’Agiriwe nabi, Akubitirwa—Ibibi nakoze ngw’Anshungure rwose:—Abishimirwe.

Refrain

Ubuntu bwawe—Bumfasha, Mwami. Busumba cyane—Impano zose. Njya ntekereza,—Nkir’aha mw’isi, ubwo Buntu bwinshi—bwanshunguye.

Couplet 2

Kand’iyo ngoswe—N’umwijima mwinshi inshuti yanjye—Imfat’ukuboko; Singitiny’ubwo,—Ari Y’Imfashije: Ntumbira mw’Ijuru,—Yes’Antinyuye.

Refrain

Ubuntu bwawe—Bumfasha, Mwami. Busumba cyane—Impano zose. Njya ntekereza,—Nkir’aha mw’isi, ubwo Buntu bwinshi—bwanshunguye.

Couplet 3

Umusaraba—Wawe ni w’unkiza; Ni w’unduhura—rwos’imibabaro. N’ubuhungiro—bwanjye buhoraho; Umbera n’Icyiru—Ku Mucamanza.

Refrain

Ubuntu bwawe—Bumfasha, Mwami. Busumba cyane—Impano zose. Njya ntekereza,—Nkir’aha mw’isi, ubwo Buntu bwinshi—bwanshunguye.
