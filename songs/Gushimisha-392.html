#392

Basirikare ba Kristo mubyuke

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Basirikare ba Kristo, mubyuke, murasane n’isi n’umubiri n’Umubi; Muze murwan’iyo Ntambara nziza: Dor’Ibendera mwahawe n’Umusaraba. Nimumuyoboke! Arabarengera! Tumufite, tuzanesh’ Umwanzi Satani.

Couplet 2

Ntumwa za Yesu, ngo, mwese mugende, mujye mu bihugu byose byo mur’iyi si. Mwumvish’ abaremwe bos’Ubutumwa, muti: Yesu Yabacunguj’ Amaraso Ye. Muti: Mumwemere, nukw’Ababature! Ni We Wadutumye kubwa rwa Rukundo rwe.

Couplet 3

Yemwe Bakristo, mubyuke n’ingoga! Nimucan’ Amatabaza, mubo mas’ubu. Wa Mukw’Araje: Mumusanganire! Azajyan’ Abiteguy’i Bwami mw’Ijuru. Mwitange, noneho ntimugapfapfane! Dor’Araje niko Yadusezeranije!
