#166

Ngwino Soko y’Umugisha

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ngwino, soko y’Umugisha; Mp’umutim’ ugushima, ntabw’ukam’iteka ryose; Njye ngusingiza cyane. Nyigisha guhora nsenga; Menye n’Ubwiza bwawe, Nizigir’Ubwami bwawe; Nyuzuz’ urukund’ubu!

Couplet 2

Nyibutsa bya byiza byawe byose Wakankoreye. Nizeye yukw’Uzanjyana, Ukangeza mw’Ijuru Yesu, Waranshatse, mpabye; Nari kure yaw’ubgo, Unyigur’urupfu rubi, Wemera kumbambirwa.

Couplet 3

Ndi mu mwenda wawe, Mwami, w’Ubuntu Wangiriye; Unyigize hafi yawe, meny’Imbabazi zawe. Satan’ajy’ angerageza! Ngo nindek’ unkund’atyo. Akir’umutima wanjye, nuk’Undinde, mb’uwawe.
