#214

Mbe Mwami je nzonesha?

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mbe Mwami je nzonesha ryar’ intambara zose, ngo nger’ Iwawe Mwami nanje nduhuke? Nzogeza ryari Mwami kubabarizwa mw’isi, ngo nger’ Iwawe Mwami nanje nduhuke?

Refrain

Iyo nzira ncamwo iduga cane, nca mu mivyimba no mu magufa, kubwo kubona inzir’ ah’ica, sinumviriz’ abo ku ruhande, kubwo kumenya nez’iyo ngiye, sinterw’ ubwoba n’iyo mivyimba, izuba ni ryinshi Mwami nje ngo nduhuke, imvura ni nyinshi Mwami nje ngo nduhuke, nduhuke.

Couplet 2

Nzogera ryar’iwawe ngo nduhuk’ imiruho, ubwo ntazongera kuririra mw’isi ukundi, umutima wanje udendebuwe no kurira, nzoger’ iwawe ryari mbon’ Uwo nkunda.

Couplet 3
