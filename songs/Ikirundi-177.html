#177

Waman’ imigabo yo gutungana

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Waman’ imigabo yo gutungana, ube muri Yesu, wam’ umwumvira, uz’ukund’ abiwe, fash’ abarwaye, rondera na ntary’ Umugisha wiwe.

Couplet 2

Waman’ imigabo yo gutungana, kukw’ abo mw’ isi bavyirengagiza, wihererane na Yes’Umukiza; Umwitegereje uzoba nka We.

Couplet 3

Waman’ imigabo yo gutungana, Azokurongora, ntumuj’ imbere, mu mubabaro no mu munezero wome kuri Yesu, umwizigire.

Couplet 4

Waman’ imigabo yo gutungana, mu mutima wawe gir’Amahoro, Maze Mpwemu Yer’ Azokuyobora, kand’ Azoguha gutungana rwose.
