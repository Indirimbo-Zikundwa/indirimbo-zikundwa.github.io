Umwami Yesu arikw’arasozera

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Umwami Yesu arikw’ Arasozera, Igikorwa Yitangurij’ Ubwiwe, kukw’arikw’ arinjiz’ ihanga ryiwe, mu ntambwe ya nyuma yo gutunganywa, intambwe yo gutunganywa rwose.

Refrain

Nimuze twihanganire gutozw’ indero nziza, kuko nimb’ Ijambo ridukangira, Ni ku bw’ineza yacu... Kuko hanyuma bizotwamir’ Imbuto, zizan’ amahoro zo Kugororoka.
