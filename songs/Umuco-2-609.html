Iyi si ndimwo Mwami iranduhije

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Iyi si ndimwo Mwami iranduhije cane, Mbaye nkivuka mw’isi nca mbon’ ibindusha, umubiri wanje nawo ukamber’ ikigwisha, kurya no kwambara kwanje kwambujije Ijuru.

Refrain

{Ewe Mana (yanje)..., Ewe Mwami wanje, Mfasha nger’Iwawe.} x2 Ubu meze Mwami wanje nka wa mwambuzi mubi, yar’abambw’i buryo bwawe, atak’ati:”Mana mbambwe ku bw’ivyaha vyanje, Wewe nta cah’Ufise, Uz’Unyibuke Mwami mu Bwami bwawe!
