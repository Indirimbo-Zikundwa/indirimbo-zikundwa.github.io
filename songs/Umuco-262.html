#262

Uhoraho niwe mukiza

Umuco

Couplet 1

Uhoraho niwe Mukiza, Uhoraho niwe mwungere, anvyagiza mu canya c’ubwatsi butotahaye, antegurira imeza imbere y’abansi banje.

Refrain

Niwe Mwungere Aruta abungere, niwe Mukunzi Aruta abakunzi, uwageze mu Biganza vyiwe, nta Mwansi yomukorako.

Couplet 2

Nta mwana w’Imana ashobora kumaramara, kukw’Uhoraho nyen’inkomezi, ashobora kumuzigama, naho yoba mu magorwa akomeye, afise intsinzi.

Refrain

Niwe Mwungere Aruta abungere, niwe Mukunzi Aruta abakunzi, uwageze mu Biganza vyiwe, nta Mwansi yomukorako.

Couplet 3

Imana ishimwe, ku ntambwe zose twaciyemwo, ibikomeye twarabinesheje ibigatanya tubirya butsinda, ibitur’imbere, tuzobitsinda mw’izina ry’Umwami wacu.

Refrain

Niwe Mwungere Aruta abungere, niwe Mukunzi Aruta abakunzi, uwageze mu Biganza vyiwe, nta Mwansi yomukorako.
