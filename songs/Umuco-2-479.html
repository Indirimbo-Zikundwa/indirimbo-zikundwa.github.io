#479

Bene data muri ngaha

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Bene Data muri ngaha, mwakiriye Agakiza, Iki gihe tugezemwo, n’ico kwikangura.

Refrain

Hahirw’uwihangana agashitsa Igihe Yes’Azogaruka, Azoronk’ Ubugingo n’Amahoro, Azobana na Yesu.

Couplet 2

Bazobahama, bazobatuka, mbere bazonabica. Ariko gikuru: twihangane, dukovye mu gusenga.

Refrain

Hahirw’uwihangana agashitsa Igihe Yes’Azogaruka, Azoronk’ Ubugingo n’Amahoro, Azobana na Yesu.

Couplet 3

Indyarya zarashitse, n’ibinyoma vyarashitse, Ijambo ry’Uhoraho, barivuga ukundi.

Refrain

Hahirw’uwihangana agashitsa Igihe Yes’Azogaruka, Azoronk’ Ubugingo n’Amahoro, Azobana na Yesu.

Couplet 4

Nimusome mu gitabo c’Ivyahishuriwe Yohana, Igihimba ca mirongwibiri na kabiri, duhere ku murongo wa cumi n’umunani; Muzahasang’ ingero y’abavug’ ibinyoma.

Refrain

Dukovye mu gusenga cane, dusab’Imana yacu Idufashe, mu Kunesha Satani n’ivyiwe vyose, Twambare Urukundo.
