#166

Yesu namutemwa shikulu

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Yesu namutemwa, Shikulu wandi, ‘Cibwe-camba candi, Umupusushi, Katula wa luse, nkamwimbila pe, E imwe mwalingo kulumbaniwa.

Couplet 2

Namutemwa pantu mwantemwa ine, Mwalinculile cibi ku Kalbale Impumi yenu yalashilwe myunga, E imwe mwalingo kulumbaniwa.

Couplet 3

Kuti namutemwa mu mweo, mu mfwa, Kuti namutotele nshiku shonse, No kwimba ningemba ne mfwa pa menso: E imwe mwalingo kulumbaniwa.

Couplet 4

Ilyo tukamono lulumbi lwenu, Na ‘benu banino kuba ne mwe pe, Nkapundisha mu bumpashanya bwenu: E imwe mwalingo kulumbaniwa.
