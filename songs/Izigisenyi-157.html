Umunsi umwe mu bikari

Iz'i Gisenyi

Couplet 1

Umunsi umwe mu Bikari by’Uwiteka Umuremyi wanjye bindutira iminsi igihumbi mu kubaho mubinezeza.

Refrain

Ibyago n’amakubaa intimba ni mibibaro tuby’iko reze Umwami Yesu Kukw’ariwe ukwitaho.

Couplet 2

Amahoro n’umunezero bibonekera imbere y’Umwami uhageze acyura urubyaro Anna yahakuye Samweli.

Refrain

Ibyago n’amakubaa intimba ni mibibaro tuby’iko reze Umwami Yesu Kukw’ariwe ukwitaho.

Couplet 3

Kubirenge by’Umwami Yesu niho tubabarirwa ibyaha maraya uwo yarahageze atah’asinyiw’ijuru.

Refrain

Ibyago n’amakubaa intimba ni mibibaro tuby’iko reze Umwami Yesu Kukw’ariwe ukwitaho.

Couplet 4

Uwar’urwaye igicuri yageze imbere ya Yesu dayimoni ihita imuvamo intumwa ziti twe twananiwe nawe niko kuzibwira ko mu gusenga no kwiyiriza harimo imbaraga nyinshi yo ni intwaro ikomeye.

Refrain

Imbaraga z’Umukristo azibonera mugusenga dusenge ubudasiba Laodikiya irakurura.x2 Intwaro yo gusenga Imana niyo tuzaneshesha Umubii.x2
