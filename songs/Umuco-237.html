#237

Igihe ni cashika

Umuco

Couplet 1

Igihe nicashika, turangij’ Urugendo, tuzoca tuzamurwa, dusanganir’ Umwami mu bicu.

Refrain

Bene Data basinziriye bazoca bazuka; natw’ abazoba bakiriho, tuzohindurwa mu kanya gato ./.Twambikw’ Umubiri musha dusanganir’ Umwami mu bicu./.

Couplet 2

Ico gihe ndakiraba nkabona cegereje; kuko ibimenyetso, vyinshi vyamaze guseruka.

Refrain

Bene Data basinziriye bazoca bazuka; natw’ abazoba bakiriho, tuzohindurwa mu kanya gato ./.Twambikw’ Umubiri musha dusanganir’ Umwami mu bicu./.

Couplet 3

Ico gihe nicashika, intambara n’amarira; indwara hamwe n’urupfu, vyose bizoba birangiye.

Refrain

Bene Data basinziriye bazoca bazuka; natw’ abazoba bakiriho, tuzohindurwa mu kanya gato ./.Twambikw’ Umubiri musha dusanganir’ Umwami mu bicu./.

Couplet 4

Ni twashika mw’Ijuru, tuzobonana na Yesu; amaso mu yandi, tuzosa nawe dutash’ Ubukwe.

Refrain

Tuzosangira na Yesu, ku Meza yiwe mw’Ijuru, amosozi yo mur’ iy’isi, Azoyaduhanagura ./.Umwami wacu Yesu, Azotubera nk’izuba ryaka./.

Couplet 5

Abazotah’ubwo Bukwe, mbega bobo ni bande? N’ab’ Imana Yamenye, isi n’ijuru bitarabaho.

Refrain

Eh’Umwami Yesu Araje, Aje gutwar’Ishengero, hahiriwe abari maso, bamesuye imvune zabo ./.Abo nibo Mugeni, w’Umwagazi w’Intama w’Imana./.
