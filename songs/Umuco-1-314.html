Umv’inzamba y’uhoraho

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Umv’inzamba y’Uhoraho irasemerera, Yemwe Hanga ry’Umwami, Ubukwe bwa wa Mwagazi w’Intama buraje, {Nimwambar’imvune zera de.} x2

Couplet 2

Hahiriw’uwatumiwe mur’ubwo bugeni, Ibitigeze biduga mu mutima, w’umuntu ni vyo twabikiwe, {Vyabikiw’ab’Iman’ikunda.} x2

Couplet 3

Ibisinzikaza mw’isi vyo ntibizobura, Tunezerwe mu mitima ni vyo, bitwambika ca gitsibo c’ubwiza, {Tuzohabwa n’Umwami Yesu.} x2

Couplet 4

Hahiriw’uwihanganir’ ibigeragezo, Kukw’ivyiza tubikiwe ntitwobi-, gereranya n’amahwa yo mw’isi, {Tuzonesh’ivyo biturusha.} x2

Couplet 5

Twes’abatsinz’ Urugamba rwo mur’iyi si mbi, Tuzoshoka kuri rwa ruzi rw’ubugingo, Dutazir’Umucunguzi, {Amahor’ azabah’iteka.} x2

Couplet 6

Mu mihanda y’izahabu tuzosemerera, Birakwiye Mana yacu, We wironkey’ Ihanga wiguz’ Amaraso, {Ur’Umwami wacu vy’ukuri.} x2
