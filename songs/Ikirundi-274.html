#274

Wa zub’ utumwe kutunezereza

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Wa zub’ utumwe kutunezereza, non’ehe buratamanzuye rwose; Mutima wanje, ririmbir’ Imana, Roresha nonaha ivyiyumviro ku Mwami mw ijuru.

Couplet 2

Imana yatuginy imbabazi Zingana n’ myenyeri zitovugwa, Zingana n’ udutonyanga tw’ ikime; Kand’ uko bukeye wam’ ump’imbabazi Zisesekaye.

Couplet 3

Imana yaray’ inzigamye neza, N’ aba bose bacu n’ urugo rwacu, Kandi nta rupfu rwadushikiriye; Rwaraturenganye: ndamutse nkiriho: Mana n’ ushimwe.

Couplet 4

Icaha ntikimpumish’ umutima; Mbony’ ivy’ Iman’ igomba ndabikore. Mana mw ijur’ ungirir’ imbabazi: Kand’ umpezagirr mu vyo nkora vyose: Ni wew unganza.
