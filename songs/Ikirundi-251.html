#251

Musi y’Umusaraba w’Umukiza Yesu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Musi y’Umusaraba w’Umukiza Yesu, niho nifuza rwose kwama mpagaraye; N’ibuye ry’Igitare rimber’ igitutu, rinkiza n’indugumba mu rugendo rwanje.

Couplet 2

Mu Musaraba wiwe ni ho mw’ ihuriro ry’Ukuri n’Urukundo vy’Iman’Ihoraho: Nk’uko muri za nzozi Yakobo yarose, wabay’ icuririzo gishika mw’ ijuru.

Couplet 3

Hirya y’Umusaraba mbona nyabarega, n’umunwa w’i kuzimu wakamw’ umuriro: Ariko hino yaho har’ Umusaraba, uteze nk’amaboko ng’unkize singweyo.

Couplet 4

Kur’uwo Musaraba umenga nshobora kubona Yes’Abambge, ari jew’Azira: Ncumitwa mu mutima, mbony’ igitangaza, ko yemeye kunkunda naho namubamvye.

Couplet 5

Musi y’Umusaraba ni ho nzogerera, Naho nohomb’ ivyanje bizomber’ inyungu. Nta kizonter’ isoni, shit’ ivyaha vyanje, nzama nirata gusa uwo Musaraba.
