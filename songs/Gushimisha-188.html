#188

Ndashaka gusa nawe Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ndashaka gusa nawe, Yesu, Mucunguzi: Nta wigeze kukumva, Uvug’urakaye. Ndashaka gusa nawe: Wasengaga kenshi, Wihereranye na So kuri wa musozi.

Couplet 2

Ndashaka gusa nawe: Nubwo bakwangaga, Ntiwiturag’ inabi Wagiriwe yose. Ndashaka gusa nawe: Wafashag’ abantu; Ump’ Urukundo rwawe, ruzabankundisha.

Couplet 3

Ndashaka gusa nawe: Wahamagaraga abana bato bose, maz’Ukabigisha. Nyamara, sinsa nawe: Bose barabizi; Mukiza mwiz’Umfashe, nse nawe byukuri.
