#132

Nsiz’ububata n’umwijima

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Nsiz’ububata n’umwijima, ndaje, Yesu; Ndaje, Yesu. Mbony’umudendezo n’umucyo: Yesu, ndakwitabye! Nsiz’ubutindi n’uburema, mbony’Ubukire n’intege nyinshi. Mvuye mu byah’Urankijije: Yesu, ndakwitabye!

Couplet 2

Mvuye mu byago n’ibihendo, ndaje, Yesu; Ndaje, Yesu, Mbony’indamu mu rupfu rwawe, Yesu ndakwitabye! Mvuyemw’ intimba n’amarira, ngeze mu byishimo. bidashira. Nshizemw’ induru, mpaw’ impundu Yesu, ndakwitabye!

Couplet 3

Nsiz’amahane n’ubwigenge, ndaje, Yesu; Ndaje, Yesu. Ngeze ku kwemer’ iby’Ukunda, Yesu, ndakwitabye! Ntay’ibyanjye, nguhungiyeho; Mvuye mu bwihebe, nd’Amahoro: Ntaye Satani, ngo mb’uwawe: Yesu, ndakwitabye!

Couplet 4

Nsize gutiny’urupfu n’imva, ndaje, Yesu; Ndaje, Yesu. Mbony’ umutim’ ushyitse mu nda, Yesu, ndakwitabye! Nkuwe mu kanwa k’inyamaswa, ntashye mu Rugo rw’Intama zawe! Mpere none, nkugandukire: Yesu, ndakwitabye!
