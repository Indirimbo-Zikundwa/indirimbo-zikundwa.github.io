#261

Buleni ine wine

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Buleni ine wine, Naipela kuli’mwe; ‘Nshinku shandi shonse fye, Leka mulumbanye pe.

Couplet 2

Buleni minwe yandi, ‘Kuti imubombele; ‘Makasa yandi nayo Yamukonkyo lubilo.

Couplet 3

Buleni shiwi lyandi, ‘Kuti nyimbile imwe; Pamo na kanwa kandi, Kesulemo Ilandwe.

Couplet 4

Buleni fyuma fyandi Awete ‘ti mutane ‘Maka yandi na mano, Kamubombele nayo.

Couplet 5

Buleni mifwaile, Ndepela Kuli Imwe; Mu mutima wandyu yu Mube cipuna cenu.

Couplet 6

Buleni ukutemwa, ‘Kuti mutemwe mweka; Mumbule ine wine, ‘Kuti mbe wenu onse.
