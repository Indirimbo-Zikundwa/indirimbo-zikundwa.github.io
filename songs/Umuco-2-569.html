Mana y’urukundo, Mana y’imbabazi

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Mana y’Urukundo, Mana y’Imbabazi, Ni Wewe twizeye, ni Wew’Udufasha, Urugendo rwacu ruraturuhije, Intambara ni nyinshi zo ku mpera yarwo.

Refrain

Mwami fash’umwe wese mu bizera, Ashobore kwubaha no kwumvir’ Ijambo; {Ni ho tuzambikw’ Impuzu Zera z’Ubukwe, Na bwa Bushobozi buzotuzamura.} x2
