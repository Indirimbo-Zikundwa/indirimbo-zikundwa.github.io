#257

Shimwa Mukiza—Utuzukiye

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Shimwa, Mukiza—Utuzukiye tuguhimbarize—Ko Wanesheje! Imana yashatse,—Mu gihe cyayo, Gutsindish’urupfu—Umucunguzi.

Refrain

Shimwa, Mukiza—Utuzukiye tuguhimbarize—Ko Wanesheje.

Couplet 2

Nimumurebe:—Ni We, ni Yesu Mwe gushidikanya:—Ni We Shobuja! Munezerwe mwese,—Bantu b’Umwami, Nimuvuge, muti:—Yatuzukiye!

Refrain

Shimwa, Mukiza—Utuzukiye tuguhimbarize—Ko Wanesheje.

Couplet 3

Nta cyo ngitinya:—Yesu ntagipfa Ni We mpora nshima:—N’uw’Amahoro. Aramfash’ iteka;—Arankomeza. Mu kubaho kwanjye,—Nta cyo ngitinya.

Refrain

Shimwa, Mukiza—Utuzukiye tuguhimbarize—Ko Wanesheje.
