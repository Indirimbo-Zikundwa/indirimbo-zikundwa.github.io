#197

Imana yatoranije isirayeli

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Imana yatoranije Isirayeli, Iyikura mu gihugu c’amahanga, Iyishira mu gihugu cayo bwite, N’Ihanga ry’Uhoraho Umwami Imana.

Refrain

“Mw’irinde ndi kumwe nawe, Mu magorwa ndi kumwe nawe, Mu muriro ndi kumwe nawe, No mu rupfu nzobana nawe, {Ni wewe uwo mfash’ukuboko, Ni wewe uwo nakunze kera, Ni wewe nameny’ubwa mbere na mbere, Ah’uzoja hose ndi kumwe nawe”.

Couplet 2

Bose bar’abana b’Aburahamu, Nk’ukw’Imana yari yasezeranye nawe, Yukw’izomuhindur’’Ihanga rikomeye, N’ihanga ry’Uhorah’ Umwam’Imana.

Refrain

“Mw’irinde ndi kumwe nawe, Mu magorwa ndi kumwe nawe, Mu muriro ndi kumwe nawe, No mu rupfu nzobana nawe, {Ni wewe uwo mfash’ukuboko, Ni wewe uwo nakunze kera, Ni wewe nameny’ubwa mbere na mbere, Ah’uzoja hose ndi kumwe nawe”.

Couplet 3

Refrain

Couplet 4

Refrain
