#199

Turi mu ntambara y’iherezo

Umuco

Couplet 1

Turi mu Ntambara y’iherezo, turi ku Rugamba rukomeye, mbega ni nde azoneshereza, n’Umwami Yesu Yanesheje, ./.Yatsinz’ urupfu ku Musaraba./.

Refrain

Ehe ng’Uyo Azananye n’ibicu, Abizera bose bazomubona; Harahiriwe uwunesha, azorya ku giti c’Ubugingo, ntazobona urupfu rwa kabiri, azicarana n’Umwami Yesu ./.Mur’ ubwo Bwami Buhoraho./.

Couplet 2

Turi ku Rugamba rukomeye, turwana n’inkomzi z’umwiza; s’intambara y’inyama n’amaraso, n’abakuru baganz’ ikirere ./.Harahiriwe uwunesha./.

Refrain

Ehe ng’Uyo Azananye n’ibicu, Abizera bose bazomubona; Harahiriwe uwunesha, azorya ku giti c’Ubugingo, ntazobona urupfu rwa kabiri, azicarana n’Umwami Yesu ./.Mur’ ubwo Bwami Buhoraho./.

Couplet 3

Satani n’abamukorera bose, bashiz’ intambamyi mu nzira, urugamba rurakomeye cane, harahiriwe uwunesha ./.Ngw’ azinjire mu buruhukiro./.

Refrain

Ehe ng’Uyo Azananye n’ibicu, Abizera bose bazomubona; Harahiriwe uwunesha, azorya ku giti c’Ubugingo, ntazobona urupfu rwa kabiri, azicarana n’Umwami Yesu ./.Mur’ ubwo Bwami Buhoraho./.

Couplet 4

Mu nzira yose har’ intambamyi, Satani yanteze imitego; ivyo vyose birwany’ ubugingo, ngo simbon’ Ubwiza bw’Uhoraho ./.Uneshereze Mukiza wanje./.

Refrain

Ehe ng’Uyo Azananye n’ibicu, Abizera bose bazomubona; Harahiriwe uwunesha, azorya ku giti c’Ubugingo, ntazobona urupfu rwa kabiri, azicarana n’Umwami Yesu ./.Mur’ ubwo Bwami Buhoraho./.

Couplet 5

Umpe kurwan’ Intambara nziza, nimpfa mpfire muri wewe Yesu; Umbumbatish’ amaboko yawe, wam’ Unyigisha kugororoka ./.Ngo nze ndaganwe n’Abera bose./.

Refrain

Ehe ng’Uyo Azananye n’ibicu, Abizera bose bazomubona; Harahiriwe uwunesha, azorya ku giti c’Ubugingo, ntazobona urupfu rwa kabiri, azicarana n’Umwami Yesu ./.Mur’ ubwo Bwami Buhoraho./.
