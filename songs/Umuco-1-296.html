#296

Raba turi ku rugamba

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Raba turi ku rugamba rwa nyuma, N’Ijambo ry’Imana riduhanura, Yuko twokwizera uwanesheje, {Ni ho natwe tuzoshobora kunesha.} x2

Refrain

Aho niho har’Agakiza k’Imana, N’ukwambar’ ibigwanishwa vyose, Mu gusengera muri Mpwemu, No mu kwitwaza Inkota ya Mpwemu, Ari Ryo Jambo Ry’Uhoraho.

Couplet 2

Kw’Ijambo ry’Imana rivuga riti: “Ic’umuntu abiba nico azokwimbura”, None natwe nitubibe muri Mpwemu, {Tuze twimbure Ubugingo budashira.} x2

Refrain

Aho niho har’Agakiza k’Imana, N’ukwambar’ ibigwanishwa vyose, Mu gusengera muri Mpwemu, No mu kwitwaza Inkota ya Mpwemu, Ari Ryo Jambo Ry’Uhoraho.

Couplet 3

Ntituzoshobozwa n’inkomezi zacu, Kiretse ni twizera Umwami Yesu, Ni ho tuzorongorwa na Mpwemu Yera, {Tukagendera no mu muco w’Ijambo.} x2

Refrain

Aho niho har’Agakiza k’Imana, N’ukwambar’ ibigwanishwa vyose, Mu gusengera muri Mpwemu, No mu kwitwaza Inkota ya Mpwemu, Ari Ryo Jambo Ry’Uhoraho.

Couplet 4

Ijambo ry’Imana ni ryo rizoduha, Kugira ingeso nziza hamwe n’ubwenge, Ukwirinda n’ukwihangana na vyo, {Bituj’imbere mu rugendo rwacu.} x2

Refrain

Aho niho har’Agakiza k’Imana, N’ukwambar’ ibigwanishwa vyose, Mu gusengera muri Mpwemu, No mu kwitwaza Inkota ya Mpwemu, Ari Ryo Jambo Ry’Uhoraho.

Couplet 5

Ni twubaha Ijambo ryImana vy’ukuri, Kandi tugakunda na Bene Data, Ni ho tuzogira intsinzi muri vyose, {N’Imana izoshimagizwa muri twe.} x2

Refrain

Aho niho har’Agakiza k’Imana, N’ukwambar’ ibigwanishwa vyose, Mu gusengera muri Mpwemu, No mu kwitwaza Inkota ya Mpwemu, Ari Ryo Jambo Ry’Uhoraho.
