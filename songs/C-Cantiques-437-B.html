#437B

Mwanta Yesu, tukukuhamek’eyi

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Mwanta Yesu, tukukuhamek’Eyi, Tunenzi lelu namuzaŋalu; Tunashakami kumesu Eyeyi, Eyi watela, Mwanta Eyi ho-o.

Refrain

{Mwanta watela,} x2 Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho, Tunakooleki, tukukuhameka, Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho.

Couplet 2

Yemweni Nzambi walekeli mpuhu, Welili muntu nakutufwila; Welili mukwakwimika mushindu, Wadatili ŋovu ya Satana.

Refrain

{Mwanta watela,} x2 Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho, Tunakooleki, tukukuhameka, Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho.

Couplet 3

Eyi Mwanta, wadizozeli ŋahi? Akupopeleli kumutondu; Akuumbili, Nzambi wakushiyili, Wafwili nayihuŋu had’etu.

Refrain

{Mwanta watela,} x2 Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho, Tunakooleki, tukukuhameka, Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho.

Couplet 4

Mwanta Yesu wabadikili ŋovu Wanoŋesheli wanta wakufwa; Nochu tushimuni nsaŋwiyi, netu, Mwanta wetu nemiki mushindu.

Refrain

{Mwanta watela,} x2 Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho, Tunakooleki, tukukuhameka, Eyi watela, Mwanta Yesu ho.
