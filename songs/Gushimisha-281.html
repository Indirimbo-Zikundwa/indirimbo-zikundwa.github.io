#281

Ngiy’inkuru twumvise

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Ngiy’inkuru twumvise; N’uko Yes’Akiza! Vuga hos’iyo nkuru yuko Yes’Akiza! Menyesh’ abarimbuka, abo mu birorero n’abo mu midugudu, yuko Yes’Akiza!

Couplet 2

Menyesh’abananiwe yuko Yes’Akiza menyesh’ abashobewe yuko Yes’Akiza! Yavuy’ Amaraso ye kera ku Musaraba, ngw’abakize, be gupfa: Ni ko Yes’Akiza!

Couplet 3

I Rukig’ubabwire yuko Yes’Akiza! Umvish’i Bugesera yuko Yes’Akiza! Vug’i Bunyambiriri n’i Ndorwa n’i Bumbogo, bwira n’i Bufumbira yuko Yes’Akiza!

Couplet 4

Banyarwanda, mumenye yuko Yes’Akiza! Nimwihane, mwemere yuko Yes’Akiza! Umvish’ abacuruzi, no mu nkik’ubivuge, n’i buzungu n’i bwami, yuko Yes’Akiza!
