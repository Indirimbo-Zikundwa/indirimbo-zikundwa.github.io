#110

Igituma nkunda Yesu

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Igituma nkunda Yesu, nkikumenyeshe none. N’ukw’uwo Mukiza Mwiza Yanguz’ Amaraso ye.

Refrain

Igituma nkunda Yesu Akandutira byose, N’uko Yankuyehw’ ibyaha, Akanyoza, nkera de.

Couplet 2

Igituma nkunda Yesu n’Urukundo Yankunze, Akankiz’ibyaha byanjye byajyaga bintegeka.

Refrain

Igituma nkunda Yesu Akandutira byose, N’uko Yankuyehw’ ibyaha, Akanyoza, nkera de.

Couplet 3

Igituma nkunda Yesu n’ukw’ar’ Umurengezi. Ahor’Antabar’iteka, uk’Umwanz’ Anyoheje.

Refrain

Igituma nkunda Yesu Akandutira byose, N’uko Yankuyehw’ ibyaha, Akanyoza, nkera de.

Couplet 4

Igituma nkunda Yesu n’ukw’ Atazarorera kumber’ Umukunzi Mwiza, Akamber’ Umwungeri.

Refrain

Igituma nkunda Yesu Akandutira byose, N’uko Yankuyehw’ ibyaha, Akanyoza, nkera de.
