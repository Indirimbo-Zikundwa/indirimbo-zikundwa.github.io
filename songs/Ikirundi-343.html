#343

Kuri wa mus’ aho tuzozuka

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Kuri wa mus’ aho tuzozuka twese tugiz’ imibiri misha, Amarira n’ uburibge Ntibizoba bikiboneka.

Couplet 2

Ubu bugingo n’ uyu mubiri Vyompi bikwiye gutandukana, Bikarorer’ urya musi Biryamye vyisinziririye.

Couplet 3

Non’uyu mubiri w’ikiruhe Turawuryamitse ngaha mw ivu, Arik’ ubugingo bgo bgo, Buririmbir’ Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 4

Hanyum’ ubugingo n’ umubiri Ni vyamara gusubira hamwe, Nta kizobitandukanya, Bizohag’ ishusho ya Yesu.

Couplet 5

Ewe munezero mwinshi cane Tuzobona ku musi w’izuka, Nih’ imva zose zo mw isi Zizogarukan’ abapfuye.

Couplet 6

Uwo mus’ abavyeyi n’ abana N’ abagabo n’ abagore babo, Bo n’ abavukana bose Bazosubira kubonana.

Couplet 7

Mwami Yesu, mur’ iryo huriro emera kuduha gushikayo. Uturenz’ urupfu natwe, twizey’ Umusaraba wawe.
