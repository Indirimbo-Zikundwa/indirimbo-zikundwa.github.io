#131

Mujane n’umwami hose

Umuco

Couplet 1

Mujane n’Umwami hose; aho muja hose, mujane n’Umwami hose; ni We Rufatiro, ni We Bendera ryacu.

Refrain

Shira ku muzirikanyi, ivy’Umwami Yababwiye, Umwami ari kumwe namwe, Mugendere mu Muco wi we, (Mugendere muri We) mu Gusenga, (Mugendere muri We) mu Mahoro, (Mugendere muri We) mu ngorane, (Mugendere muri We) no mw’ishaka, (Mugendere muri We.)

Couplet 2

Mujane n’Umwami hose, ababere Inkinzo, mujane n’Umwami hose, ni we cishimikizo ni We Bendera ryacu.

Refrain

Shira ku muzirikanyi, ivy’Umwami Yababwiye, Umwami ari kumwe namwe, Mugendere mu Muco wi we, (Mugendere muri We) mu Gusenga, (Mugendere muri We) mu Mahoro, (Mugendere muri We) mu ngorane, (Mugendere muri We) no mw’ishaka, (Mugendere muri We.)

Couplet 3

Mujane n’Umwami hose, mujane n’amahoro, mujane n’umunezero, muhoberane mu rukundo nk’uko Yesu Yadukunze.

Refrain

Shira ku muzirikanyi, ivy’Umwami Yababwiye, Umwami ari kumwe namwe, Mugendere mu Muco wi we, (Mugendere muri We) mu Gusenga, (Mugendere muri We) mu Mahoro, (Mugendere muri We) mu ngorane, (Mugendere muri We) no mw’ishaka, (Mugendere muri We.)
