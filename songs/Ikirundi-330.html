#330

Habay’umus’ Ijuru ryanezerwa

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Habay’umus’ Ijuru ryanezerwa, ariko mw’isi huzuy’ umwiza, uyo musi Yes’Amanuka mw’isi ngw’avyarwe n’umukobga atunganye.

Refrain

Rab’Urukundo rwatumy’ Ampfira, Agahamb’ ivyaha vyanje mu mva, mu Kuzuk’ Akantsindanishiriza: Hazoz’ umus’ Azogarukako.
