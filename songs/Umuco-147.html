Hahirwa uwahariwe ivyaha

Umuco

Couplet 1

Hahirwa uwahariwe ivyaha, icaha ciwe kigaharirwa hahirwa umuntu Uhoraho adaharurako ibigabitanyo ./.Umutima wiwe ntubemwo ubugunge./.

Refrain

Kuk’uwuteg’ugutwi, uguhanura kw’Uhoraho, kumushitsa kubugingo, kuko kwubaha Uhoraho, kuzana ubwenge, kandi ukwicisha bugufi, kwitangirimber’ imbere y’ukwubahwa.

Couplet 2

Uhoraho ahimbarwa nabantu biwe, agashariza abicisha bugufi, ubuntu n’ukuri ntibizobavako, ntibahengamira iburyo canke ibumoso ./.Nab’intumbero imwe gusa./.

Refrain
