#352

Twobon’ ibizob’ imbere

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Twobon’ ibizob’ imbere, Nk’Uhoraho, namb’ ibicu vyeyutse pe, Hakabona; Ntitwobabazwa n’ivyago, Twokwibagir’ ivya none, Har’ akaneza twiteze, Wewe nanje.

Couplet 2

Twomeny’ ibizoh’ imbere, Nk’Uhoraho, N’igitum’ bipfa, Tukarira: Twoba dushitse mu muco, Tuvuye mu mwiza mwinshi, Ivyago bicits’ivyiza. Mu kwizera.

Couplet 3

Kubona n’ugutahura, Twogomvy’ ivyo, Arik’ Uhoraho na we, Adukunda, Aradukingir’ akabi, Ntitubon’ ibir’ imbere, Ni ho tumugumya rwose, Izera we.
