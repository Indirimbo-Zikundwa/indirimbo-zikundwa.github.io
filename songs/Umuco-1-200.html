#200

Umusi wo kuza kw’umukiza

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Umusi wo kuza kw’Umukiza uregereje, N’umusi mwiza utey’igomwe ku bizeye, Ku bafise ikimenyetso ca Mpwemu Yera, Bazokwumva inzamba ivuze iyo mu bicu.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’Inzamba ry’Umumarayika mukuru, Rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, Abari mu birere bine bazoyumva, Bati: «Nimuze mu munezero wa Data».

Couplet 2

Benshi mur’ico gihe ntibazomenya ivyabaye, Kuko uzoba ar’umusi umeze nk’iyindi yose, Abizeye gusa nibo bazomeny’iryo Banga, Abuzuye ubushobozi bw Uhoraho.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’Inzamba ry’Umumarayika mukuru, Rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, Abari mu birere bine bazoyumva, Bati: «Nimuze mu munezero wa Data».

Couplet 3

Abitwa ko basenga bose uko bari, Ntibazomenya ivyabaye uwo musi, Kuko bizomenya gusa Abizeye, Abazoba buzuye Mpwemu bazovyumva.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’Inzamba ry’Umumarayika mukuru, Rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, Abari mu birere bine bazoyumva, Bati: «Nimuze mu munezero wa Data».

Couplet 4

Ico gihe abizeye bose imitima yabo, Izohuzagizwa kubw’ikigira kibe, Niho bazoca bumv’Inzamba ivuze hose, Abafise Ikimenyetso bazovyumva.

Refrain

Ijwi ry’Inzamba ry’Umumarayika mukuru, Rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, Abari mu birere bine bazoyumva, Bati: «Nimuze mu munezero wa Data».

Couplet 5

Uwo musi n’Umusi mwiza utey’igomwe, Tuzoba dusezey’ imibabaro yose; Tuzogir’Umunezer’ utigeze kubaho, wewe jewe, twese turi mu ruhande ruhe?

Refrain

Ijwi ry’Inzamba ry’Umumarayika mukuru, Rizosemerera cane mu mahanga yose, Abari mu birere bine bazoyumva, Bati: «Nimuze mu munezero wa Data».
