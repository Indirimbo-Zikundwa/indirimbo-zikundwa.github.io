#190

Tujanye na Yesu, tumwumviye cane

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Tujanye na Yesu, tumwumviye cane, twogendera mu Muco wiwe, Akatuyobora, Akabana natwe n’abamwizer’ uko bangana.

Refrain

Mwizigire, umwumvire cane, ni ho womuronkamwo Akaneza kenshi.

Couplet 2

Ntihakingirizwa, nta gicu gikuba, Yes’ Amwenyuye kireyuka, nta bgoba buriho, cank’ umubabaro, bimarwa n’uko tumwizeye.

Refrain

Mwizigire, umwumvire cane, ni ho womuronkamwo Akaneza kenshi.

Couplet 3

Amarushwa yose ntiyotubabaza, ni W’aduhembur’ imbu zose; Ivyo twita bibi bitubera vyiza, tumwizigiye, tumwumviye.

Refrain

Mwizigire, umwumvire cane, ni ho womuronkamwo Akaneza kenshi.

Couplet 4

Ntitwokwiyungura kugir’ Akaneza tudakijijw’ ivy’isi vyose, kuk’ Ubuntu bgiwe n’imbabazi ziwe ar’impera y’abamwizeye.

Refrain

Mwizigire, umwumvire cane, ni ho womuronkamwo Akaneza kenshi.

Couplet 5

Tumugiye hafi tukamugendako, twoba tugirirany’ ubumwe, maze yodutuma tugaca tugenda, tukamwumvira tumwizeye.

Refrain

Mwizigire, umwumvire cane, ni ho womuronkamwo Akaneza kenshi.
