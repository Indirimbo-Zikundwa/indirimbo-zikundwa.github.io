#221

Bana mw’Ijur’ Abiringiy’ Umucunguzi

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Bana, mw’Ijur’ Abiringiy’ Umucunguzi tuzaherway’ ibintu byinshi. Azaduh’ Amakamba y’Ubwiza bwinshi, kand’ Atwambik’ imyenda yera.

Refrain

Bana, tuzahabwa, tuzahabwa byinshi, tuzagabirwa byinshi mw’Ijuru tuzahoran’ ishimwe, tunezerewe, ni twizer’ Umukiza Yesu.

Couplet 2

Refrain
