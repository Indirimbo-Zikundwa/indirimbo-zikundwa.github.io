#310

N’irihe hanga rizoduga?

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

N’irihe hanga rizoduga ku, musozi w’Uhoraho? N’irihe hanga rizohagarara, ahera cane h’Imana, Rigashikama mu gihe gikwiye, n’ikidakwiye; none n’irihe, Kandi rimeze gute?

Refrain

N’ihanga rifise amaboko akeye, n’umutima utanduye, N’iryavukiye muri Yesu Kristo, Ubwoko bwaryo n’iryera, {Kuko rirongorwa na Mpwemu, Yera w’Imana Ihoraho.} x2
