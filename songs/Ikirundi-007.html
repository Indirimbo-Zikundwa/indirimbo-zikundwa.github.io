#7

Umv’Abera bo mw’Ijuru

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Umv’ Abera bo mw’ Ijuru baranezerewe, Baririmbira Yes’iyi ndirimbo nsha.

Refrain

Bat’ Icubahiro cose kib’ ica Yesu Yadukunz’ akatwuhagiz’ amaraso.

Couplet 2

Abo bose kera bari bandujwe n’ivvaha, None bambay’impuzu z’abakijijwe. Bat’ Icubahiio cose.

Refrain

Bat’ Icubahiro cose kib’ ica Yesu Yadukunz’ akatwuhagiz’ amaraso.

Couplet 3

Uwagarariza cane Yes’ amuhindura Umuherezi w’ Iman’ akamwimika. Nukw icubahiro cose.

Refrain

Bat’ Icubahiro cose kib’ ica Yesu Yadukunz’ akatwuhagiz’ amaraso.

Couplet 4

Iyo Yes’ atadukunda tutarozw’ ivyaha, Tuba twihebuye, twokwogejwe na nde? Non’ icubahiro cose.

Refrain

Bat’ Icubahiro cose kib’ ica Yesu Yadukunz’ akatwuhagiz’ amaraso.

Couplet 5

Turirimbire n’ aband’ uko yadukijije, Bibareherez’ ah’ ari, baririmbe. Bat’ Icuhahiro cose.

Refrain

Bat’ Icubahiro cose kib’ ica Yesu Yadukunz’ akatwuhagiz’ amaraso.
