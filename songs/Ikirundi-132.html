#132

Nsiz’ubugurano bg’umwiza, ndaje Yesu

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Nsiz’ubugurano bg’umwiza, ndaje Yesu, ndaje Yesu; Nshitse mu masabo y’Umuco, ndaje Bgirukiro. Nsiz’ indwara, ndons’ amagara, nsiz’ ubgoro, ndons’ Ubutunzi, nsiz’ ivyaha, ndons’ agakiza: Ndaje Bgirukiro.

Couplet 2

Nsiz’ubuhombe bukomeye, ndaje Yesu, ndaje Yesu; Ndons’ inyungu z’ Umusaraba, ndaje Bgirukiro. Nsiz’ umubabaro w’iyi si, nshitse mu Munezero wawe, nsiz’ induru, nshitse mu mpundu: Ndaje Bgirukiro.

Couplet 3

Nsiz’ubgibone bganje bginshi, ndaje Yesu, ndaje Yesu; Nshitse mu vy’ Ugomba Mukiza, ndaje Bgirukiro. Ntay’ ivyanje, nguhungiyeko, sincihebura, nd’amahoro. Ntaye Satani, nkwumiyeko: Ndaje Bgirukiro.

Couplet 4

Nsiz’ ubgoba bg’urupfu n’imva, ndaje Yesu, ndaje Yesu; Ndashitse mu Mahoro yawe, ndaje Bgirukiro. Mvuye mu mahonero y’isi, nshitse mu Makiriro yawe, mpore mbona mu Nyonga hawe: Ndaje Bgirukiro.
