#79

Uhor’undinganirije Amajamb’ atunganye

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Uhor’undinganirije Amajamb’ atunganye, Umpubure mmeny’ Ukuri kw’ Amajambo yawe. Nkund’ Amajambo yawe kw’ ampa kukwizigira.

Refrain

Amajambo meza cane yo kunter’ Umunezero, ni yo meza, ndayakunda, ntah’atanezera.

Couplet 2

Umwami Yes’ Arineze, Niw’ Atang’ Amajambo, munyavyaha, ngo renguka, wumv’ ivy’ Ubugingo: Atugirir’ Ubuntu, Akatwiyegereza.

Refrain

Amajambo meza cane yo kunter’ Umunezero, ni yo meza, ndayakunda, ntah’atanezera.

Couplet 3

Menyesha bos’ Ubutumwa n’Amajamb’ atunganye. Ni muz’ Arabaharira, mwakir’ Ubugingo. Yesu ni We Mukiza, Niw’ Atang’ Ubugingo.

Refrain

Amajambo meza cane yo kunter’ Umunezero, ni yo meza, ndayakunda, ntah’atanezera.
