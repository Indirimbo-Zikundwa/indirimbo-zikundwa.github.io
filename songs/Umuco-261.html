#261

Ico nshaka ni kimwe gusa

Umuco

Couplet 1

Ico nshaka ni kimwe gusa, n’ugushika mw’Ijuru, ivyo mw’isi ndabihinyuye, ndonder’ivyo mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Mbega jewe nzoshikayo (jewe), mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho Imana, nzorushaho kunezerwa ninashika mw’Ijuru ku Mana Yera.

Couplet 2

Ngaha kw’isi turabashitsi, iwacu ni mw’Ijuru ku Mana ni ho tuzohora, imyaka bo n’imyaka.

Refrain

Mbega jewe nzoshikayo (jewe), mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho Imana, nzorushaho kunezerwa ninashika mw’Ijuru ku Mana Yera.

Couplet 3

Ewe ncuti ndakuburire, niwizigire vuba uzoronka umunezero niwashika mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Mbega jewe nzoshikayo (jewe), mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho Imana, nzorushaho kunezerwa ninashika mw’Ijuru ku Mana Yera.

Couplet 4

Mwene Data reka ivyo vyose n’ukurikire Imana, mbega uwo munsi niwashika, uzogenda mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

Mbega jewe nzoshikayo (jewe), mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho Imana, nzorushaho kunezerwa ninashika mw’Ijuru ku Mana Yera.

Couplet 5

Iy’iwacu ku Mana Yera, ivyiza biruzuye, n’unezerwe kuj’iy’iwacu, gusanganir’ Umwami.

Refrain

Mbega jewe nzoshikayo (jewe), mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho Imana, nzorushaho kunezerwa ninashika mw’Ijuru ku Mana Yera.

Couplet 6

Mbega woba wizeye Yesu, kugir’uje mw’Ijuru, kuko niwe inzira n’ukuri, Buging’imyaka yose.

Refrain

Mbega jewe nzoshikayo (jewe), mw’Ijuru k’Uhoraho Imana, nzorushaho kunezerwa ninashika mw’Ijuru ku Mana Yera.
