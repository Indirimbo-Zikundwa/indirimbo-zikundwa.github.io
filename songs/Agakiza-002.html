#2

Nari naramenyerey’ibyaha

Agakiza

Couplet 1

Nari naramenyerey’ ibyaha. Sinari nz’ ijambo ry’Umukiza. Sinari nz’inkoni Yakubiswe kubera jyewe.

Refrain

Yes’ Umwami Yarambabariye. Yankuyehw’ ibyaha byanjye byose. Non’ubu ndamushimira cyane Umusaraba.

Couplet 2

Ubgo numvag’ Ijambo rye ryiza, Nararize mu mutima wanjye. Mperako meny’imibabaro ye, Yatewe nanjye.

Refrain

Yes’ Umwami Yarambabariye. Yankuyehw’ ibyaha byanjye byose. Non’ubu ndamushimira cyane Umusaraba.

Couplet 3

Yes’Umwami n’Umukiza wanjye, Ni we zuba ndetse n’ubugingo. Ndu- shaho kujya muhimbariza wa Musaraba.

Refrain

Yes’ Umwami Yarambabariye. Yankuyehw’ ibyaha byanjye byose. Non’ubu ndamushimira cyane Umusaraba.
