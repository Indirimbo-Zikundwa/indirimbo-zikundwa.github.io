#287

Yes’Azaganz’ ibihugu byose

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Yes’Azaganz’ ibihugu byose, iyo biva bikagera. Ndetse n’iyi si yos’izab’ Ubwami bwe, Ari We Mwami w’abami.

Couplet 2

Bazamusenga badacogora; Bazamusingiz’ iteka. Izina rye rizababer’ impumuro, ikwire mw’is’ isandare.

Couplet 3

Twihute, twamamaz’ibye hose, mu moko yose yo mw isi, ngw’indimi zose zizavug’ Ishimwe rye, zemere yukw’ar’ Umwami.

Couplet 4

Ku Ngoma ye, n’Amahirwe masa: Imboh’ Arazibobora, kand’abakene na b’akabakenura, indushy’ Akaziruhumu.

Couplet 5

Nimuze mwese, mwa byaremwe mwe mwubah’ Umwami w’abami; Namwe Bamarayika, turirimbane, tut’Urakaz’ Uhimbazwe.
