#110

Twari twafashwe mpiri

Umuco

Couplet 1

Twari twafashwe mpiri, mu gihugu kitar’ic’ iwacu; twaranyazwe ibirwanishwa, twari nka basinziriye bupfe.

Refrain

Yesu Uwanesheje, Yaramaze kuvuza inzamba Urugamba rwaratanguye, atuj’imbere tukamukurikira n’Umwami w’Agaheta nta wigeze Amunesha.

Couplet 2

Umumarayika yaramanutse, aje kudukangura twese; avuz’ inzamba mu gicugu, inzamba yo gutangur’ urugamba.

Couplet 4
