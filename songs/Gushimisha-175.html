#175

Mwunger’Udukunda

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Mwunger’Udukunda,—Jy’uturagira, n’uduhora hafi,—Twatiny’iki se? Tugukurikire, iy’Ujya hose, naho haba habi—cyangwa se heza.

Couplet 2

Mwunger’Udukunda,—Tuz’Ijwi ryawe, Uduhamagaye,—Turanezerwa kand’iy’ Uduhannye, turabyemera. Tur’Intama zawe:—Utwiharire.

Couplet 3

Wavuy’Amaraso,—Mwungeri Mwiza, kugira ngw’Intama—Uducungire. Washyize kuri twe Ikimenyetso, ni cyo Mwuka Wera,—Udutuyemo.

Couplet 4

Mwunger’Uturinde,—Uturengere amasega mabi—Ye kutwegera. Nta cy’azadutwara, kuk’Ukomeye. Turikumwe, byose—Biranesheka.
