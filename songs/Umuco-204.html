#204

Benshi birukira

Umuco

Couplet 1

Benshi birukira kwinjira, mu masengero yubatswe, n’amaboko y’abana b’abantu, bakishimira ibiboneka ./.Bikabanezereza, bakavyishimira./.

Refrain

Injira vuba injira (vuba), mu Rusengero rw’Imana (Yera), ruri Ahera, ahera cane, ahatabonwa n’abinyama (n’amaraso), ni kubw’Ubuntu oh! Ni kubw’Ubuntu, injira injira, ni kubw’Ubuntu.

Couplet 2

Benshi birukira gusengera, mu mashengero yubatswe, n’amaboko y’abana b’abantu, ariko ntibamenye yuko ./.Abaseng’ Imana bose bayisengera muri Mpwemu./.

Refrain

Injira vuba injira (vuba), mu Rusengero rw’Imana (Yera), ruri Ahera, ahera cane, ahatabonwa n’abinyama (n’amaraso), ni kubw’Ubuntu oh! Ni kubw’Ubuntu, injira injira, ni kubw’Ubuntu.

Couplet 3

Umwami wacu Yesu Yavuz’ati: Ishengero ryanje ryubatse, ku guhishurirwa na Mpwemu, none natwe twese twubake ./.Kur’ urwo Rutare, ku guhishurirwa./.

Refrain

Injira vuba injira (vuba), mu Rusengero rw’Imana (Yera), ruri Ahera, ahera cane, ahatabonwa n’abinyama (n’amaraso), ni kubw’Ubuntu oh! Ni kubw’Ubuntu, injira injira, ni kubw’Ubuntu.
