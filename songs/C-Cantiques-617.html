#617

Nzambi wanyi, ndinyemena ku-udi

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Nzambi wanyi, ndinyemena Ku-udi, kuitabuji bua meme kufuishibua bundu, Unsungile bua Buakane Buebe Teleja Nzambi wanyi milombo yanyi.

Refrain

{Nzambi wanyi, Muimpe Tshiakumulekela to} x2 Nzambi wanyi Muimpe, Muimpa be Mushema.

Couplet 2

Nzambi Wewe ke Tshisokomenu tshianyi, Ndi ngamba ne Wewe Udi Nzambi wanyi, Hanga nyemena Kudi Ungakidile, Ndi bianyi mukoko wa mu Tshidilu Tshiebe

Refrain

{Nzambi wanyi, Muimpe Tshiakumulekela to} x2 Nzambi wanyi Muimpe, Muimpa be Mushema.

Couplet 3

Nzambi wanyi Udi Tshinyemenu tshianyi Bukola buanyi Ngwewe, Ntshiena ntshina tshintu, Udi Dikuatshisha dianyi mu makenga, Didi kadiyi dihanga Tshiendelele

Refrain

{Nzambi wanyi, Muimpe Tshiakumulekela to} x2 Nzambi wanyi Muimpe, Muimpa be Mushema.

Couplet 4

Bulelela Nzambi udi Muimpe Mushema, Ne Bienzedi Biebe bidi binkemesha, Ndi munkatshi mua dikatshila Buimpe Buebe, Ne Malu audi wenza adi ankemesha

Refrain

{Nzambi wanyi, Muimpe Tshiakumulekela to} x2 Nzambi wanyi Muimpe, Muimpa be Mushema.

Couplet 5

Nzambi wanyi ndi musue bua Wewe wikale, Tshisokomenu tshianyi ne Dikisha dianyi, Nansha mpankuata malu tshiakukulekela to, Bualu Udi Ntshinkula mu mianda yonso

Refrain

{Nzambi wanyi, Muimpe Tshiakumulekela to} x2 Nzambi wanyi Muimpe, Muimpa be Mushema.
