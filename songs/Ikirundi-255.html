#255

Yesu Kristo Yarazutse

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yesu Kristo Yarazutse, Tumushime, tuti Haleluya! Uyu musi tunezerwe, Tumushime, tuti Haleluya! Kera ku musaraba Ni ho Yesu yatubabarijwe Ngw ab’ impongano y’ ivyaha vyacu Haleluya, muze tumuhimbaze.

Couplet 2

Yes’Umwami wo mw’ Ijuru, Tumushime, tuti Haleluya! Tumushimish’ indirimbo, Kukw ar’ Umukiza w’abo mw isi, Yarabambg’ arahambga Kugira ngw adukurekw ivyaha, None yaranesheje rwa rupfu, Haleluya, muze tumuhimbaze.

Couplet 3

Ivyo Yesu yababajwe Tubimushimire, Haleluya! Ni vyo vyonyene vyatumye Tugir’ ubugingo budashira; None yimye mw ijuru, Ni h’ashimwa n’abamarayika, Natwe dushimane na bo, tuti Haleluya, muze tumuhimbaze.
