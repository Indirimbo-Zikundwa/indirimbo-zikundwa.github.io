#24

Unyigishe ugusenga mpore nsegera mu mwuka

Impimbano· Fr René

Couplet 1

Unyigishe ugusenga, mpore nsegera mu Mwuka. Ntibibe uko nshaka, Ugushaka kwawe kuze. Inzira zanjye zose zibe izawe, Umfate ukuboko. Unyigishe ugusenga, mpore nsegera mu Mwuka.

Couplet 2

Unyigishe ugusenga, nkuko wari i Gestemani. Ati Data Byashoboka Igikombe kikandenga, Ntibibe nkuko nshaka Ubushake bwawe buze. Unyigishe ugusenga, nkuko wari i Gestemani.

Couplet 3

Unyigishe ugusenga, mbashe kunesha iby’ ikigihe. Nubwo bizitse benshi, Abawe udufate ukuboko. Intambara zo ziriho, Wavuze kubana natwe. Unyigishe ugusenga, mbashe kunesha iby’ikigihe.

Couplet 4

Unyigishe ugusenga, ntihagir’ ibinter’ ubwoba. Ntunkure kurugamba, ahubwo Ubane nanjye. Nubwo benshi bakuvuyeho, banejejwe n’iby’ isi. Unyigishe ugusenga, ntihagir’ ibinter’ ubwoba.

Couplet 5

Unyigishe ugusenga, kuko ukuza kwawe kuje. Sinibagirwe iby’iwacu, i Sodomu aho nihabi. Nubwo benshi bahakunda, ntibakibon’ Ubwami Bwera. Unyigishe ugusenga, kuko Ukuza kwawe kuje.

Couplet 6

Unyigishe ugusenga, Mwami Wanjye nkunezeze. Unyigishe ugusenga, Mwami Wanjye Mpore nesha. Unyigishe ugusenga, Mwami Wanjye neshe Intambara. Unyigishe ugusenga, Mwami Wanjye nzabane nawe.
