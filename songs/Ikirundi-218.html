#218

Kubana na Yesu! Ibihe vyose

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Kubana na Yesu! Ibihe vyos’ iryo Jambo rivug’ Ubugingo bgacu budashira. Mw’isi nd’ingenzi, Si ho nzohora, Kuko ndi mu rugendo Runjana mw’ijuru.

Couplet 2

Urugo rw’ Imana Hamwe ndaruraba Nk’ ururi hafi y’ amaso Kubgo kwizigira. Birankumbuza Ko noshikayo, Ni ho mwandu w’ abera, Yerusalemu nsha.

Couplet 3

Paulo yaravuze, Ati Tuzobana N’Umukam’ ibihe vyose; N’ ijambo ry’Imana. Rinshikirize None Mukiza, Tubane n’ ino mw’isi, Uhor’ untabara.

Couplet 4

Ni twamana Mwami, Aho nzocikana, Urupfu ruzonyinjiza Aho batagipfa. Nzoca nkumenya Nk’ uk’ unzi Mwami, Ndakwiratir’ imbere Ko tuzohorana.
