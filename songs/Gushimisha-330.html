Wowe Man’Ikiranuka

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Wowe, Man’Ikiranuka, turashim’ Izina ryawe kuk’Uduhay’ Udukiza. Ujy’utuyobor’i Wawe.

Couplet 2

Waduhay’Umwami Yesu, Aza kutwigisha neza ibir’i Wawe mw’Ijuru. Ngo tukuyoboke neza.

Couplet 3

Wow’Udusabir’iteka, Ube mu mitima yacu; Natwe tujye tugukunda, Kuk’Ur’ Umuvunyi wacu.

Couplet 4

Uhimbazwe, Mwami Yesu: Twibuka k’Uzagaruka kudushaka, twe n’abacu, ng’Uduh’ Ubugingo bushya.
