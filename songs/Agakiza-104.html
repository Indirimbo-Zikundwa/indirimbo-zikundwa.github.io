#104

Yesu ni wowe Mucyo

Agakiza

Couplet 1

Yesu, ni wowe Mucyo, ni wowe nshima kuko Wankunze. Mwami, byose Wakoze kutwitangira ndabigushima.

Refrain

Yesu, Ur’Amahoro yacu, kuko Watuberey’Inshungu.

Couplet 2

Mwami, ndanezerewe kukuririmba kuko Wankunze. Yesu, ko mez’Umfashe, nanjye ngukunde, nkuririmbire.

Refrain

Yesu, Ur’Amahoro yacu, kuko Watuberey’Inshungu.

Couplet 3

Yesu, ngwino tubane, ngwin’Unyobore mu Nzira yawe. Kandi ni wowe Nzira, ur’Ubugingo ndetse n’Ukuri.

Refrain

Yesu, Ur’Amahoro yacu, kuko Watuberey’Inshungu.

Couplet 4

Nzi ko har’Igihugu cy’Umucyo mwinshi ku bakijijwe. Ndetse icyo Gihugu n’icy’Izahabu zitatse neza.

Refrain

Yesu, Ur’Amahoro yacu, kuko Watuberey’Inshungu.
