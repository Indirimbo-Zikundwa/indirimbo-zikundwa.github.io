#247

Unyigishe Mukiza, Iby’Umusaraba

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Unyigishe Mukiza, Iby’Umusaraba n’icyatumy’Uza mw’isi, Ukatubambirwa Umenyeshe umumaro w’umunyabyah’ umwe, Umbwire n’igiciro Wanshungurishije!

Couplet 2

Unyigishe n’impamvu Y’isok’ itukura yo mu rubavu rwawe, urwo bacumise, ari jye gusa mw’isi: Wacumuy’ Imana, Uba Warayavuye ngw’amber’ Impongano.

Couplet 3

Unyerek’Urukundo rutarondoreka rwamanutse i kuzimu ku bwacu abihebe. Uyu mutim’ ukonje wuzuy’ inarijye, Uwatsemwo Urukundo ruv’i Gologota.

Couplet 4

Unyigishe, Mukiza: ndi umunyabyaha pe. Icyantabara, Mwami n’Ubuntu bwonyine. Yesu ni jy’ugukennye: Mu bo Wapfiriye b’abanyabyaha babi, ni jyewe ukabije.

Couplet 5

Mukiza wanjye, Yesu ni Wowe nizeye maz’ubw’ Umpamagaye, ndakwiyeguriye. Non’ubw’ Unyakiriye, njye nshim’ Imbabazi na rwa Rukund’ Unkunda, mbivuge ndinde mfa.
