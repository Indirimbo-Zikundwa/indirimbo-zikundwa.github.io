#19

Ndiyo Dhamana Yesu wangu

Tenzi za Rohoni

Couplet 1

Ndiyo dhamana, Yesu wangu; Hunipa furaha za Mbingu; Mrithi wa Wokovu wake Nimezawa kwa Roho yake.

Refrain

Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu, Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu.

Couplet 2

Kumsalimu moyo wangu, Mara namwona raha yangu; Aniletea malaika, Wananilinda, taokoka.

Refrain

Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu, Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu.

Couplet 3

Sina Kinyume, nashukuru, Mchana kutwa huja kwangu; Usiku kucha kuna nuru; Mwokozi wangu; ndimi huru.

Refrain

Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu, Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu.

Couplet 4

Hali na mali anitwaa! Mara namwona anifaa, Nami nangoja kwa subira; Akiniita, nije mara.

Refrain

Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu, Habari njema, raha yangu Yesu ndiye Mwokozi wangu.
