#87

Rukundo rwawe Mwami sinarurondora

Impimbano· Sr Henriette

Couplet 1

Rukundo rwawe Mwami sinarurondora, rurenz’iyi si yose rusumba n’Ijuru. Indimi zose z’isi ntirwarurimba, isanzure ry’Ijuru ntirwarikwirwamo.

Refrain

Reka njye nduririmba rwansaby’ umutima, rundutir’ ubutunzi n’ubwiza bw’iyi si. Mutima wanjye tuza wabony’ Umukunzi, ishimir’ Ineza Ye iraguhagije.

Couplet 2

Wandeheje nkibona Umucyo w’Ijambo, no kwicar’ imbere Ye byampay’ Ubuzima. Amahor’ arut’uk’ umuntu yayavuga niy’atuma ndirimba haleluy’iteka.

Refrain

Couplet 3

Uko bukeye ndushaho kumva nkumbuye, kubon’ Ubwiza bw’Uwo Wankunze ntamuzi. Icyo nzi ni kimwe nuk’Ageze hafi ndabon’ ibimenyetso bidashidikanwa.

Couplet 4
