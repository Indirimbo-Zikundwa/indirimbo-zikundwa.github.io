#152

Meme tshivua

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Meme tshivua mwa kudisungila, Yesu Nkayende ngwa kuenza bionso; Nenditambisha bua tshinyi tshianyi? Bionso Yesu ke ngudi muenza!

Refrain

Yesu ngwanyi tshiendelela, Ndi munanga too ne tshiendelela!

Couplet 2

Nganyi mukuabo uvua mua kulua Kuitaba bua kufua muaba wanyi Ne kumfutshila mabanza onso Bu muakenza bo kudi Yesu?

Refrain

Yesu ngwanyi tshiendelela, Ndi munanga too ne tshiendelela!

Couplet 3

Yesu udi Musungidi wanyi, Yeye mene udi Nzambi wanyi; Nyuma muimpe udi undombola, Tshilobo tshianyi, Yesu mene.

Refrain

Yesu ngwanyi tshiendelela, Ndi munanga too ne tshiendelela!

Couplet 4

Yesu udi Mfumu wa bamfumu Ne Mukalenga wa bakalenga, Tshisokomenu tshia basungula, Nzambi munene wa bukole.

Refrain

Yesu ngwanyi tshiendelela, Ndi munanga too ne tshiendelela!
