#160

Ee mara ngapi tunasumbuka

Nyimbo za Mungu

Couplet 1

Ee mara ngapi tunasumbuka na roho inalegea, Tunazungushwa na giza kubwa, tunaogopa sana? Sauti tamu ya Bwana Yesu tunasikia kusema: “Mutoto wangu ninakupenda, sitakuacha daima.”

Refrain

{kwa kila wakati, kwa kila pahali, Aliahidi kuchunga, hataniacha daima.} x2

Couplet 2

Mupenzi, umesahau mbio sauti hii ya mapendo Ya Bwana Yesu aliyetoa uzima wake kwawe? “Mutoto unayechoka sana, kumbuka sitakuacha; Niliahidi nitakuchunga na kuwa nawe daima.”

Refrain

{kwa kila wakati, kwa kila pahali, Aliahidi kuchunga, hataniacha daima.} x2

Couplet 3

Halafu, ndugu, ufarijiwe katika kila matata, Mwokozi wako anakupenda, hatakuacha kamwe; Unyoshe kwake mukono wako, utamukuta karibu, Faraja tamu kumusikia: “Sitakuacha daima.”

Refrain

{kwa kila wakati, kwa kila pahali, Aliahidi kuchunga, hataniacha daima.} x2
