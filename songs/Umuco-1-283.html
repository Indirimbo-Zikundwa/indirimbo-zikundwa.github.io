#283

Ivy’umuntu buntu gusa

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Ivy umuntu buntu gusa, Vyose biba ar’ubusa.

Couplet 2

Ijambo ry’Imana rivuga: Nta ciza kiri mu muntu.

Couplet 3

N’ubuntu twagiriwe gusa, Nta ciza twari dufise.

Couplet 4

Ibibogaboga muri We, Nivyo twahawe n’Umwami.

Couplet 5

Hashimw’ Izina ry’Umukiza, Ni We yaducunguye.

Couplet 6

Nta rindi zina dukeneye, Atar’iry’Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 7

Nta rindi shimwe dukeneye, Atar’iry’Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 8

Nta cubahiro dukeneye, Atar’ic’Umwami Yesu.

Couplet 9

Nta n’ibikorwa dukeneye, Bidakorewe mu Mwami.

Couplet 10

Nta n’amajambo dukeneye, Atavugirwa mu Mwami.

Couplet 11

Twese nitwicishe bugufi, Umwami ashirwe hejuru.

Couplet 12

Twese nitub’abavukana, Umwami azoturongora.

Couplet 13

Nta mpembo y’isi dukeneye, Tuzahemberwa mw’Ijuru.

Couplet 14

Uko dutama mur’iyi si, Ni ko tuzogororerwa.

Couplet 15

Tunih’iminiho itovugwa, Twipfuza ko Umwami yoza.

Couplet 16

Twemeje kub’abaiulüjwe, Tuzaruhuka mw’iju,tu.

Couplet 17

Twemeje guheb’umubiri, N’ibiwunezereza.

Couplet 18

Twemeje kugwanya kamere, N’ivyo gahahamira.

Couplet 19

Dusavy’Umukiz adufashe, Kugwan’intambara nziza.

Couplet 20

Dusavy’Uhoraho Adufashe, Tuze tugere mw’Ijuru.
