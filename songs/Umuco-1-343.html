#343

Uhoraho ni we bwirukiro

Umuco 1

Refrain

[Trad. en Fr., voir Cant. n°461]

Couplet 1

Uhoraho ni We Bwirukiro, Nta wundi mbona ko yokiza, Umuntu buntu, kukw’igikorwa, Catanguriye ku musaraba.

Refrain

Uhoraho ni We Nkingi, Uwo ni We Rufatiro, Ndanezerewe kuk’Umukiza, Yam’arind’ abicisha bugufi.

Couplet 2

Uhoraho ni We nihishamwo, Kuko ni We Yansezeraniye, Ndamwisunze ni ho na wa mwansi, Atazoshobora kunzimiza.

Couplet 3

N’aho ngendera kw’isi yuzuye, Imiruho, jewe sintinya, N’Umwami wanje yayiciyemwo, Ni W’andemesh’ ijor’umurango.

Couplet 4

Ni We Mukunz’andutira bose, Uwo ni We Kristo yabambwe, Kugira ngo ninjire mw’ihanga, Ni ryo hanga yiguz’amaraso.

Couplet 5

Umwami wanje ni W’angwanira, Iy’urugamba rukomeye, Ndazi yukw’ataco jewe nzoba, Kuk’Uwaneshej’ari Muzima.
