#340

Yesu Mucunguzi wanje ndaj’i waw’uyu musi

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yesu Mucunguzi wanje ndaj’i waw’uyu musi. Amaraso antunganye Y’Umwagazi w’Intama.

Refrain

Yes’ Ashimwe kand’ Ashimwe, Icubahiro kibe Ic’Umwana uw’Intama, Dushim’ Izina ryiwe.

Couplet 2

Kera nashimitse cane Ngo mbone umugisha. None ndakwihaye Mwami, Yesu Mwana w’Intama.

Refrain

Yes’ Ashimwe kand’ Ashimwe, Icubahiro kibe Ic’Umwana uw’Intama, Dushim’ Izina ryiwe.

Couplet 3

Nzobarir’ abantu bose Agakiza ka Yesu. Umukiza yaduhaye Agakiza k’ ubuntu.

Refrain

Yes’ Ashimwe kand’ Ashimwe, Icubahiro kibe Ic’Umwana uw’Intama, Dushim’ Izina ryiwe.

Couplet 4

Nzovuga can’ ivya Yesu, N’ ubutumwa nta soni: Kuko yankijij’ umwansi, Ndamushim’ arakiza.

Refrain

Yes’ Ashimwe kand’ Ashimwe, Icubahiro kibe Ic’Umwana uw’Intama, Dushim’ Izina ryiwe.

Couplet 5

Ndamushim’ Uwankijije, N’ ugukiz’ arakiza. Yes’ ashimwe n’ Umukiza, Ububasha n’ ubgiwe.

Refrain

Yes’ Ashimwe kand’ Ashimwe, Icubahiro kibe Ic’Umwana uw’Intama, Dushim’ Izina ryiwe.
