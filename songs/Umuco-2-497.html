#497

N’igihe gito intambara zikarangira

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

N’igihe gito Intambara zose, zo mur’iyi si zikarangira; Tukibanira n’Umukiza Yesu, Ibihe bidashira mw’Ijuru.

Refrain

{Yerusalemu we Musha, Ur’umugwa mwiza w’Abizera.} x2

Couplet 2

Ni naco gituma tudatinya gushingir’ intah’iri Jambo; Uwihanganir’ ibigeragezo, Ni w’azokwambikw’ Igitsibo.

Refrain

{Yerusalemu we Musha, Ur’umugwa mwiza w’Abizera.} x2

Couplet 3

Ijambo twumva mur’iki gihe, N’iritegur’ imitima yacu; Turyumvire kandi turyizere, Ni ho tuzoronk’ Ubugingo.

Refrain

{Yerusalemu we Musha, Ur’umugwa mwiza w’Abizera.} x2

Couplet 4

Turi ku Rugamba rukomeye, Turi mu kurangiz’ Urugendo; Uhoraho Yatanguy’ igikorwa, kandi Ni W’Azogiheraheza.

Refrain

{Yerusalemu we Musha, Ur’umugwa mwiza w’Abizera.} x2

Couplet 5

Shengero ry’Imana twihangane, Yes’Ari hafi yo kutujana; Azob’ Ahagaze mu bicu, Ati: “Nimuz’abo Napfiriye”.

Refrain

{Yerusalemu we Musha, Ur’umugwa mwiza w’Abizera.} x2
