#109

Bansantu tuelayi mbila

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Bansantu tuelayi mbila ya disanka, Tutumbisha Kilisto Mukalenge, Wakafua ne wakabika ku lufu, Wakatamba lufu bukole mene.

Refrain

Dina dia Yesu ditumbishibua kashidi Anu Kilisto ngudi Nzambi tshiendelele.

Couplet 2

Tuimbilayi Mukalenge wa butumbi, Wakafua bua kutuvuija ne muoyo Ne wakabika bua kutubingisha; Lelu tudi ne muoyo bualu buandi.

Refrain

Dina dia Yesu ditumbishibua kashidi Anu Kilisto ngudi Nzambi tshiendelele.

Couplet 3

Kilisto Dinanga dia tshiendelele, Didi ditamba bintu bionso mene; Kuikala nende kudi ditalala, Nekudi dikisha dia tshiendelele.

Refrain

Dina dia Yesu ditumbishibua kashidi Anu Kilisto ngudi Nzambi tshiendelele.

Couplet 4

Kilisto ngudi muakane tshishiki, Ngudi mukumbanyina kutumbisha; Bua Yesu udi umwe wa butumbi, Tumutumbishayi ne muoyo umwe.

Refrain

Dina dia Yesu ditumbishibua kashidi Anu Kilisto ngudi Nzambi tshiendelele.
