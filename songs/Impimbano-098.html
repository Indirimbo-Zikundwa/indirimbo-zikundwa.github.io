#98

Unyumve Mwami wanje je ndakunyotewe

Impimbano· Fr Salem

Couplet 1

Unyumve Mwami wanje, je ndakunyotewe. Hari igikorwa cawe, gishitse muri je.

Refrain

{Mpindurir’imero ngo nse nawe Mukiza.} x2

Couplet 2

Dukizwa n’ukwizera, sikubw’ ibikorwa. Yesu ku Musaraba, Yazira icanje caha.

Refrain

{Nyonger’ukwizera, ngo mbeho nk’uk’ Ugomba.} x2

Couplet 3

Nshaka kukunezera, nkakwizera Wewe. Ubuzima bw’Imana bungaragaremwo.

Refrain

{Nyonger’ukwizera, ngo mbeho nk’uk’ Ugomba.} x2

Couplet 4

Reka ngusabe Mwami, Urukundo rwawe. Bikumvikan’ Iwawe, nkabibamwo nanje.

Refrain

{Nyonger’urukundo, Mukiza Yesu-Kristo. Mpembura Mwami wanje, Mbwirako turi kumwe.} x2
