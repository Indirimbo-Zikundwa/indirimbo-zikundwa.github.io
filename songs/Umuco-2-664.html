#664

Ndagusavye mukiza Yesu

Umuco 2

Couplet 1

Ndagusavye Mukiza Yesu, Wumve uku gutaka kwanje, Kukw’ Indirimb’ idudubiza, Mu mutima wuzuy’ Ishimwe.

Refrain

Watanz’ Amaraso ku bwanje, Ku Musaraba i Gologota, Unkura ibwina kwa Satani, Mw’itanure ryak’umuriro.

Couplet 2

Kuko narajimijwe cane, Njakarir’ Umwansi Satani, Maze Uc’Umfat’ Ukuboko, Unshikura mu vyara vyiwe.

Refrain

Reka nsuk’uyu munezero, Wuzuye mu mutima wanje, Ndirimbe nitera hejuru, Narazutse mva mu bapfuye.

Couplet 3

Baragow’abakozi babi, bazimiza abana bato, Kuko bazoboherw’ urusyo, rwo kw’izosi bagwe mu nyenga.

Refrain

Satani yabahumy’amaso, Nta n’udusoni bagifise, bitobekeranij’ umwampi, mu mutima batabizi.

Couplet 4

Har’amadini menshi cane, anyuranya rwose n’Ijambo, Mbega bazohungira hehe, Yes’Avuz’ati: “Sindabazi”?

Refrain

«Twirukany’amadayimoni, twasenga no mw’Izina ryawe, twakoz’ ibimenyetso vyinshi» Ati: «Mviraho, Sindabazi».

Couplet 5

Umuco w’Ukuri waratse, Wakira mur’iyi si yose, Ushir’ ahabona ikinyoma, Satani aramaramara.

Refrain

Mbeg’abawubonye ni ba nde? Ni mwebwe Abatoranijwe, Nay’abana. bo kuranduka, bazokwama bari mu Mwiza.

Couplet 6

Nezerwa Mugeni Wakunzwe, Kuk’uri hafi yo gutaha, Ikibanza kirateguwe, N’Igitsibo Kirateguwe.

Refrain

Haleluya Yesu n’Uwera, N’Iman’ Ihishur’ Amabanga, N’Umwunger’ Az’Intama Ziwe, Iziwe zose zikamumenya.
