#89

Imana yera Imana y’ubumwe

Umuco

Couplet 1

Imana Yera, Imana y’Ubumwe ikorera mu vyicishije bugufi, mu Mashengero yose ar’indwi Yabonywe n’abicishije bugufi.

Refrain

Nshimir’ Imana ingene Yakoze ndayishimira Urukundo Yadukunze; ndayishimira kukw’ Idahindagurika, n’Ubuntu Yagiriye Ishengero.

Couplet 2

Uhereye mu gihe ca mbere, Ikorera mubitaboneka; abana bayo nibo bayimenya, bayibonera mukwicisha bugufi.

Refrain

Nshimir’ Imana ingene Yakoze ndayishimira Urukundo Yadukunze; ndayishimira kukw’ Idahindagurika, n’Ubuntu Yagiriye Ishengero.

Couplet 3

Mur’ivyo bihe vyose vyabayeho, Yiyerekanye mu buryo bwinshi; ariko Yabonywe n’ababona, kandi Yumviswe n’abumva.

Refrain

Nshimir’ Imana ingene Yakoze ndayishimira Urukundo Yadukunze; ndayishimira kukw’ Idahindagurika, n’Ubuntu Yagiriye Ishengero.

Couplet 4

Abakuru n’abahambaye, ntibigeze banayimenya, bayishakira mu biri hejuru, nay’Ikorera mu vyicishije bugufi.

Refrain

Nshimir’ Imana ingene Yakoze ndayishimira Urukundo Yadukunze; ndayishimira kukw’ Idahindagurika, n’Ubuntu Yagiriye Ishengero.

Couplet 5

Iki gihe cacu kibi, abantu bara pfuy’imitima; biyita ko batunze vyose, ariko ntibazi igihe ic’ari co.

Refrain

Nshimir’ Imana ingene Yakoze ndayishimira Urukundo Yadukunze; ndayishimira kukw’ Idahindagurika, n’Ubuntu Yagiriye Ishengero.

Couplet 6

Abizera ehe bugira buce, ku banyakibi ehe bugira bwire; eh’umunsi w’Umuco uraje, ku banyakubi umwiza uraje.

Refrain

Nshimir’ Imana ingene Yakoze ndayishimira Urukundo Yadukunze; ndayishimira kukw’ Idahindagurika, n’Ubuntu Yagiriye Ishengero.
