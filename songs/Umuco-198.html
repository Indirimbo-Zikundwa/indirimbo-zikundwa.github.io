#198

Hari imburi y’uhoraho

Umuco

Couplet 1

Har’imburi, imburi y’Uhoraho, kandi yama iza ngo twiyubare, kukw’ inyuma, inyuma y’imburi ./.Hama haba gucirwakw’ iteka ku bantu batumviy’imburi./.

Refrain

Ntac’Imana ikora, itaburiy’ abana bayo. Nta c’Imana ikora, itabwiy’ abasavyi bayo. Arikw’ Imana Ishimwe cane, kuk’ Urufatiro rw’Imana Nzima; rwanditseko ikimenyetso, ng’Uhoraho araz’ abiwe ./.Kandi ng’ uwuvug’ Izina ryiwe, yitandukanye n’ivyanduye./.
