#61

Mpwem’ arakubgira, wumv’ ijwi ryiwe

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mpwem’ arakubgira, wumv’ ijwi ryiwe, ut’ ubgoro bgawe, raba ku giti. Mpwem’ arakugomba, nta c’umwankira, wari wacumuye, ngwino kwa Yesu.

Refrain

Uhoraho Abazigame, Abarabe neza imisi yose; Abamurikishiriz’ Umuco wera, namwe mumwizere ntimutenzure.

Couplet 2

Ngwahan’ ico nguha, uze ntutinye, Akir’ ukunesha, waracunguwe. Mpwem’ arakwinginga, ntumubabaze, indirimbo yaw’ ishikire Yesu.

Refrain

Uhoraho Abazigame, Abarabe neza imisi yose; Abamurikishiriz’ Umuco wera, namwe mumwizere ntimutenzure.

Couplet 3

Mu mutima wawe were derere; Ub’ Urusengero rw’ Imana Yera. Mpwem’ arakwongera kwizera kwinshi, agukomereze gukund’ Imana.

Refrain

Uhoraho Abazigame, Abarabe neza imisi yose; Abamurikishiriz’ Umuco wera, namwe mumwizere ntimutenzure.
