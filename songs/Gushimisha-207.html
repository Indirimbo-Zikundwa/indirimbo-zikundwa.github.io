#207

Uwab’atinyutse Ibyago byose

Gushimisha

Couplet 1

Uwab’atinyutse Ibyago byose, naz’akurikire—Umwami Yesu! Nta cyamurekesha—Iyo migambi ye yo guca mu Nzira —Ijya mw’Ijuru!

Couplet 2

Ntabw’azacogozwa n’inshamugongo. Abazimubwira—nibo bazagwa! Abanzi be bose—nti bamugarura munzir’ ahisemo—Ijya mw’Ijuru.

Couplet 3

Mwam’ujy’undindisha—Umwuka wawe, mbone kuzaragwa kubahw’iteka. Have wa bwoba we—nimunyihorere! Namaramaje pe—Kujya mw’Ijuru.
