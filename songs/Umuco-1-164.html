Mwenedata mukundwa

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Mwene Data mukundwa, We wahamagawe n’Uhoraho, Imana y’Aburahamu, Niyo yagusezeraniye, Isezerano ry’intahava, Izobana nawe hose.

Refrain

Ntiwibagir’Iyagusezeraniye, Ntiwibagire n’Isezerano ryawe, Uhorah’ari kumwe nawe hose, Inzira z’Uhoraho n’igihome, Gikingira Umugororotsi.

Refrain

Ntiwibagir’Iyagusezeraniye, Ntiwibagire n’Isezerano ryawe, Uhorah’ari kumwe nawe hose, Inzira z’Uhoraho n’igihome, Gikingira Umugororotsi.
