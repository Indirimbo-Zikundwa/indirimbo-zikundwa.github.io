#242

Nukuimbila mwanta wami

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Nukuimbila Mwanta wami Yesu Kamina kalala kakusakila: N’akwaKristu kushimuna nami, Neli yuma yejima yayiwahi.

Couplet 2

Kwejim’atenaŋa ŋovu yindi, Nimaana, ninyidimu yindi yonsu; Chakeŋayi chinayikaŋanyi Neli yuma yejima yayiwahi.

Couplet 3

Yesu wafwili nakuŋamwina Kukaluŋa kachinana nikufwa; Natamini, waŋanakeneni: Neli yuma yejima yayiwahi.

Couplet 4

Nadiwili luwi nimazu indi, Hela namuumbili, waŋamwinini, Kulonda nakeŋili mwiwulu: Neli yuma yejima yayiwahi.

Couplet 5

Chaasayi Satana nyivu yindi Yankunyi niyawulobu anjahi, Namwakula, ami yami nami, Neli yuma yejima yayiwahi.

Couplet 6

Chinakaya kudi Mwanta mwiwulu Nakemba kamina kakusakila, Kana kakakulu kinembili, Neli yuma yejima yayiwahi.
