#27

Umanuri inanga yawe uririmbe

Impimbano· Fr René & Fr Siméon

Couplet 1

Umanuri inanga yawe uririmbe, ucurange kandi unezerewe. Shima kandi uhimbaze, Uwagukuye mugihungu cyuzuyemo umubabaro.

Refrain

Ndaririmba Halleluya, nshima Imana yo Mwijuru. Ndacurangishi inanga yanjye Ishimwe ry’Uwiteka, nshima kandi mpimbaza Uwankuye mugihugu cyuzuyemo umubabaro.

Couplet 2

Dor’ikintera Mwami kugushima iteka ryose, Wanyujujemo Amahoro asendeye. Wanyujuje umunezero ump’ Ubuntu bw’ Agakiza, mu mutima wanjye hararimba.

Refrain

Ndaririmba Halleluya, nshima Imana yo Mwijuru. Ndacurangishi inanga yanjye Ishimwe ry’Uwiteka, nshima kandi mpimbaza Uwankuye mugihugu cyuzuyemo umubabaro.

Couplet 3

Rwose mbuz’uko gushima mbony’ ukuntu ubana natwe, mbony’ ukuntu usubiza amasengesho. Ariko sinihagana nzagushima iteka ryose, Ur’Imana yacu iteka Mukiza.

Refrain

Ooh!! Ndaririmba Halleluya, nshima Imana yo Mwijuru. Ndacurangishi inanga yanjye Ishimwe ry’Uwiteka, nshima kandi mpimbaza Uwankuye mugihugu cyuzuyemo umubabaro.
