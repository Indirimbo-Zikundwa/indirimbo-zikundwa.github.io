#167A

Yesu ndi nkumpala

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Yesu, ndi nkumpala kuebe, Tshiena ne bukole buanyi; Ndi ndomba mutshima muimpe, Unkuatshishe lelu.

Refrain

{Unkuatshishe lelu,} x2 Ndi musue Wewe nkayebe Unkuatshishe lelu.

Couplet 2

Yesu, wewe udi kuonso, Udi apa katataka; Shala netu tshiendelela, Unkuatshishe lelu.

Refrain

{Unkuatshishe lelu,} x2 Ndi musue Wewe nkayebe Unkuatshishe lelu.

Couplet 3

Malu mabi a Satana Adiku matuku onso; Ntungatshina dikenga diau, Unkuatshishe lelu.

Refrain

{Unkuatshishe lelu,} x2 Ndi musue Wewe nkayebe Unkuatshishe lelu.

Couplet 4

Ndi nkutendelela mpidieu, Ndi ndila bikole kuudi; Yesu, kupidi muan’ebe, Unkuatshishe lelu.

Refrain

{Unkuatshishe lelu,} x2 Ndi musue Wewe nkayebe Unkuatshishe lelu.
