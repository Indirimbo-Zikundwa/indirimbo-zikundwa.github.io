#120

Mw’Ijur’ imbere y’ Imana, mfis’ Umunt’ Amvugira

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Mw’Ijur’ imbere y’ Imana, mfis’ Umunt’ Amvugira, n’Umuherezi Mukuru, kandi yitwa Rukundo. Nzi ko mu Mutima wiwe handitsw’ izina ryanje; kukw amvugira nta Mwansi yobasha kumunnyaga.

Couplet 2

Iyo Satan’ anyeretse ivyaha vyanje vyinshi, agomba ko nihebura, ndangamiza mw’ Ijuru, ndabay’ Umukiza wanje Yankuyekw’ ivyo vyaha; Yarantsindanishirije ngo simbe ngipfa rubi.

Couplet 3

Uhorah’ Umucamanza Ambonyekw’ Amaraso, ntancir’ Urubanza rubi, Anyit’ ugororotse. Yaranyiguze nd’ uwiwe, Niwe Bugingo bganje: Kukw’ Adapfa singipfuye, twe na Yesu tur’umwe.
