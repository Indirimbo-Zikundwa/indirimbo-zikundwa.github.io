Waducishije mu nzira

Umuco 1

Couplet 1

Waducishije mu Nzira igoye, Kuko nta wundi yari bwayicemwo, Ariko Wewe warayiciyemwo, Nanje nzoyicamwo nezerewe.

Refrain

Kukw’ivyo navyo biva ku Mana, Umujanama w’Igitangaza, Uhambaye mu Bwenge, Uhoraho Nyen’ingabo.

Couplet 2

Refrain

Refrain
