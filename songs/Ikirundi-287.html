#287

Yes’Azoganz’ ibihugu vyose

Ikirundi

Couplet 1

Yes’Azoganz’ ibihugu vyose Iyo biva bikagera, iyi si yos’ izocik’ Ubgami bgiwe, ni We Mwam’ aganz’ abami.

Couplet 2

Bazomusenga badahumeka, Bazomutazira cane, Izina ryiwe rizoba nk’ imibavu Itamiran’ isi yose.

Couplet 3

Ni twihute tujan’ ubutumwa Mu moko yose yo mw isi, Ngw indimi zose zize zivug’ ishinwe Ry’ Umukiza wacu Yesu.

Couplet 4

Mu bgami bgiwe har’ imigisha: Imbohe zirabohorwa, Kand’ abarushe ni Yes’ abaruhura Amar’ abor’ ubukene.

Couplet 5

Ni tuve has’ ivyaremwe vyose, Duh’ Umwam’ icubahiro, Muririmbane natwe. bamarayika, Tuti n’Ahimbazwe, amen!
