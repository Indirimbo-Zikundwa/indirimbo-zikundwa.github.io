#17

Unatembea njia gani?

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Unatembea njia gani? Waza we’ ndugu, waza leo. Unasimama ‘pande gani? Waza we’ ndugu, waza leo.

Refrain

Waza we’ ndugu, waza we’dada, Waza we’ ndugu, waza kweli. Waza sasa, waza leo; Waza we’ ndugu, waza leo.

Couplet 2

Njia ni moja ya Uzima, Hakuna njia ‘ngine tena; Usifwate njia ya lufu, Pokea Yesu hapa sasa.

Refrain

Waza we’ ndugu, waza we’dada, Waza we’ ndugu, waza kweli. Waza sasa, waza leo; Waza we’ ndugu, waza leo.

Couplet 3

Waza myaka ulipoteza Kufwata njia ya dunia; Yesu ni mbele yako sasa, Yeye ni Njia ya Uzima.

Refrain

Waza we’ ndugu, waza we’dada, Waza we’ ndugu, waza kweli. Waza sasa, waza leo; Waza we’ ndugu, waza leo.

Couplet 4

Usisahau Njia Yake, Waza we’ ndugu waza leo; Anapiga hodi, fungua, Yesu yuko tu anakuita.

Refrain

Waza we’ ndugu, waza we’dada, Waza we’ ndugu, waza kweli. Waza sasa, waza leo; Waza we’ ndugu, waza leo.
