#318B

Lesa e kacema wandi

Coll. des Cantiques

Couplet 1

Lesa e Kacema wandi, Nshakakabile pe; Ku mulemfwe alentwala, Ku menshi ya bumi.

Couplet 2

Umutima ambwesesha, Mu lwendo angafwa; Mu nshila sha bololoke, Pe Shina Lyakwe fye.

Couplet 3

‘Lintu nkenda mu mfwa ine, Nshakatine kantu; Pantu imwe mulensunga, No kusansamusha.

Couplet 4

‘Cintamba mwanungikila, Mu cinso ca babi; Umutwe wandi mwansuba, ‘Nkombo shaisula.

Couplet 5

‘Busuma bweka no luse, Fyakulakonka pe; Na mu ŋanda ya kwa Lesa, Nkekalililamo.
